A folio's specifics written into platform code. It works for exactly one
consumer and silently damages the rest. Ask of every change: **does this
name, assume, or default to one folio?**

**Archived 2026-09-19** (bean `folio-assistant-4kiw`). Superseded by

naming one folio, and the three-destination table (platform / the folio's
tree / the folio's config) whose third row is the one that gets collapsed.
Retained as a node; injected into no prompt.
