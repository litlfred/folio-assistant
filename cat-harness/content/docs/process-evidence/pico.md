**Population, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome.**

WHO guideline development states the question this way before any retrieval, and
the reason is auditability rather than ceremony: without a PICO, *"we looked for
evidence"* is not a claim anybody can check. With one, the search is
reproducible — a second reader can run it and say whether the same body of
evidence comes back.

The PICO is carried on the recommendation, not discarded after the search. It is
what a later reader needs in order to tell whether new evidence bears on this
recommendation or a different one.
