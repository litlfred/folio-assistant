**The first step looks inward, not outward.**

Before any search, the author reads what the document, DAK or IG *already* says
on this question. A new recommendation that contradicts a sibling recommendation
in the same artefact is a defect that no amount of external evidence will
reveal — the sources can all be impeccable and the guideline still incoherent.

This is also the cheapest step by far, and it is the one most often skipped.
