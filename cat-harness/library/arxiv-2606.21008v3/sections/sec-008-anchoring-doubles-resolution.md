The bootstrap opens with a raw pass — every portfolio scored by every other model with no anchor,
averaged leave-self-out, 95% bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples; Efron & Tibshirani, 1993). It
supplies the baseline: the top-ranked portfolio, claude-opus-4.5’s, whose first archetype is Figure

between a leading eight and a trailing four more than doubles relative to the spread of the means,
while ranks within either band stay unresolved.
