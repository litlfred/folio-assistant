Conditional independence is a structural assumption about the residual errors of the measurements
and is not guaranteed by high predictive accuracy of each imperfect measurement. We therefore
first recommend explicitly designing multiple measurements to make the assumption as plausible as
possible (Section 5.1). We then propose a diagnostic tool for the conditional independence assumption
(Section 5.2). We finally propose a direct test of the assumption and a bias-correction estimator in
a special case when a small amount of validation data is available (Section 5.3).
