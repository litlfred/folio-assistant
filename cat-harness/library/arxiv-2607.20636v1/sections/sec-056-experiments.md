Finally, we conducted simulations to assess the effectiveness of financial supplements in
overcoming pessimism traps in our theoretical model. We study the first setting, with
a single group and correct action known to government. The primary objectives of our
experiments were to evaluate the impact of financial supplements on the proportion of
correct cascades, analyze the magnitude of the subsidies, and understand the scalability
of interventions across population sizes.
