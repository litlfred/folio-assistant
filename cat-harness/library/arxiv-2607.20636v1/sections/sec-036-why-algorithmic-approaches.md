In this chapter, we introduce our perspective on applying algorithmic tools to studying
problems in social epistemology. We start by laying out our view of theoretical modeling
and the role it can (and cannot) play in the study of social problems. We engage with
literature on the philosophy of models and draw on our own experiences to present our
argument, and we include recommendations for how to approach social modeling. Then,
we introduce the broad social epistemological problem we consider in this part.
