C.3.1
Purpose
To ensure that when matching licenses and exceptions to the SPDX License List, there is an appropriate balance between matching
against the substantive text and disregarding parts of the text that do not alter the substantive text or legal meaning. Further
guidelines of what can be disregarded or considered replaceable for purposes of matching are listed below here and in the subsequent
specific guidelines. A conservative approach is taken in regard to rules relating to disregarded or replaceable text.
