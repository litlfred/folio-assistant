To maximize domain and workflow diversity, we used a community-driven, open-source contribution
model. In total, 142 contributors from academia and industry submitted 400 candidate tasks. We
counted submissions that included the full task specification–instruction, environment, oracle, and
verifier–along with contributor-assessed difficulty ratings. From this pool, maintainers curated the
final evaluated set used in the main experiments.
