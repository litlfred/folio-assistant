Summary
Specifies the safety risk level.
Description
Lists the different general safety risk levels that can be used to describe the general safety risk of an AI system.
Using categorization according to the EU general risk assessment methodology129. The methodology implements Article 20 of
Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and is intended to assist authorities when they assess general product safety compliance.
