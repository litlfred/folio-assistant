Summary
Records the results of general safety risk assessment of the AI system.
Description
Records the results of general safety risk assessment of the AI system.
Using categorization according to the EU general risk assessment methodology128. The methodology implements Article 20 of
Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and is intended to assist authorities when they assess general product safety compliance.
It is important to note that this categorization differs from the one proposed in the EU AI Act’s provisional agreement.
