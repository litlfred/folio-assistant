Summary
Indicates whether a relationship is known to be complete, incomplete, or if no assertion is made with respect to relationship
completeness.
Description
RelationshipCompleteness indicates whether the provided relationship is known to be complete, known to be incomplete, or if no
assertion is made by the relationship creator.
Metadata
https://spdx.org/rdf/3.0.1/terms/Core/RelationshipCompleteness
Name:
RelationshipCompleteness
Entries
complete The relationship is known to be exhaustive.
incomplete The relationship is known not to be exhaustive.
noAssertion No assertion can be made about the completeness of the relationship.
