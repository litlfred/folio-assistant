and macro environment of an organization, moreover, it is difficult to use qualitatively listed factors in 
decision making. The qualitative examination of the internal and external factors can only be a beginning for 
an in-depth analyze in planning process.  The expanding literature reviewing SWOT method shows that 
referring only SWOT is insufficient in strategic planing. Academic researches on the subject suggest that the 
effectiveness of SWOT can be improved with using qualitative and quantitative techniques together. A 
number of scholars have proposed new analytical methods to combine with SWOT and some scholars 
suggest alternative methodologies to it.  
There is a considerable amount of academic study on SWOT, but little attention has been paid to 
understand the historical emergence, advantages and  limitations of the method. This article which also 
presents current methodological changes to the technique seeks to reveal a clear understanding of SWOT, by 
examining historical background of SWOT, the components of it, advantages, disadvantages and limitations 
of the technique.
