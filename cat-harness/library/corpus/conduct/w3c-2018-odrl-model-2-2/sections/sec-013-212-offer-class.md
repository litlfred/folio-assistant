An ODRL Policy of subclass Offer represents Rules that are being offered from assigner Parties. An Offer is typically used to
make available Policies to a wider audience, but does not grant any Rules.
An ODRL Policy of subclass Offer:
MUST have one assigner property value (of type Party) to indicate the functional role in the same Rules.
