An Action MAY include the refinement property to indicate a Constraint/Logical Constraint that narrows the semantics of the
Action operation directly. To meet this condition of narrower semantics for the Action, all of the Constraints/Logical Constraints
referenced by the refinement property MUST be used as generating a satisfied state.
