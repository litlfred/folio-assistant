Our approach provides a clear positive impact of reduced
frontier-model inference cost and energy use with minimal
tradeoff in the overall performance. However, our proposed
approach of utilizing partial trajectories can be misused to
bypass safety-focused frontier models for malicious pur-
poses. Considering multiple objectives for routing, such
as considering the safety of the given task together during
routing, is an important and interesting direction for future
work.
