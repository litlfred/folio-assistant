2.1
Autonomous Agent
An autonomous agent is a computational entity designed for independent and effective operation
in dynamic environments [98]. Its essential attributes are:
• Autonomy: Independently manages its actions and internal state without external controls.
• Perception: Detects the changes in the surrounding environment through sensory mechanisms.
• Intelligence and Goal-Driven: Aims for specific goals using domain-specific knowledge and
problem-solving abilities.
• Social Ability: Can interact with humans or other agents, manages social relationships to
achieve goals.
• Learning Capabilities: Continuously adapts, learns, and integrates new knowledge and experi-
ences.
