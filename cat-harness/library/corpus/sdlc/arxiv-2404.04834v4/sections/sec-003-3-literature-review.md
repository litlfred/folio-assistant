To summarize, this study makes the following key contributions:
• We conduct a systematic review of 71 recent primary studies on the application of LMA systems
in software engineering.
• We perform two case studies to illustrate the current capabilities and limitations of LMA systems.
• We identify the key research gaps and propose a structured research agenda that outlines potential
future directions and opportunities to advance LMA systems for software engineering tasks.
