Previous research has laid the groundwork for the exploration of LMA systems in software en-
gineering, yet this domain remains in its nascent stages, with many critical challenges awaiting
resolution. In this section, we outline our perspective on these challenges and suggest research
questions that could advance this burgeoning field. As illustrated in Figure 4, we envision two
phases for the development of LMA systems in software engineering. We discuss each of these
phases below and suggest a series of research questions that could form the basis of future research
projects.
