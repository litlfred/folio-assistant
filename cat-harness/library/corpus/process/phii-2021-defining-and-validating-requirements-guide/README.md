- **Core Methodology Summary:**
  - **Requirement Statement Grammar (pp. 1–2):** Functional requirements are anchored to business process activities and phrased as *"The system must or should..."*, whereas non-functional requirements specify system properties/constraints (performance, security, reliability, maintainability).
  - **Priority Weighting (p. 2):** Assigns each requirement a three-level priority scale — **High, Medium, Low** — corresponding to **Must-have, Should-have, Nice-to-have** when trade-offs are needed.
  - **Nine Quality Criteria for Requirements (pp. 3–4):** *Correct*, *Unambiguous*, *Complete*, *Consistent*, *Ranked for importance and/or stability*, *Verifiable*, *Modifiable*, *Traceable*, and *Comprehensible*.
  - **Three Validation Techniques (p. 5):**
    1. **Desk checking:** Individual review of the requirements specification against business processes and quality criteria.
    2. **Walkthrough:** Facilitated step-by-step group traversal of task flows and requirements with SMEs.
    3. **Peer review:** Independent structured inspection by peers to catch ambiguities, omissions, and inconsistencies.
