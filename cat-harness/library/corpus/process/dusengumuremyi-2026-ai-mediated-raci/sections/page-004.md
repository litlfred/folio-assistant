387% ROI over 24 months.

4.4. Sub-Saharan African Mandatory Protocol
Finding 4: Comparative analysis of 12 peer
organizations in sub-Saharan Africa (no structured
RACI) showed chronic responsibility ambiguity. The
paper mandates:

Mandatory Implementation Protocol:

Phase 0 (Assessment): Audit latency (>48 hrs) and
dispute frequency (>0.5/decision).

Phase 1 (Digital RACI): 90-day non-AI digital
RACT baseline.

Phase 2 (AI-Mediated): Deploy AI agent with NLP,
API integrations; 8-hour boardroom training.

Phase 3 (Defensibility Lock): Predictive alerts for
decay, omission, overload.

Phase 4 (Audit & Iterate): Monthly defensibility
audit, target >70% detection.

5. Discussion

5.1. Novelty Contribution

This paper makes three novel contributions aligned
with its Title and Keywords:

First, it introduces and empirically validates the AI-
Mediated RACI Matrix for accountability
verification and ex-ante defensibility.

Second, it provides the first known comparative
quantification of human vs. Al RACI performance
on real-time operational efficiency, error-free
sensemaking, management effectiveness,

and organisational defensibility.
