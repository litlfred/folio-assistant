5.2. Reconciling with Prior Literature

While prior IB research emphasized institutional
voids (Khanna & Palepu, 2010) and relational
contracting (Dyer & Singh, 2018), this paper argues
such voids are self-perpetuated by absence of
structured accountability tools. The AlI-Mediated
RACI Matrix is a technology leapfrog (Lee et al.,

2018) applied to governance infrastructure.

5.3. Limitations and Future Research

Three limitations: (1) single-case design limits
generalizability; (2) author’s insider role may
introduce bias; (3) AI performance depends on data
quality. Future research should conduct multi-case
replication and develop lightweight offline versions

for low-infrastructure settings.
