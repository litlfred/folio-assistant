- **Core Methodology Summary:**
  - Demonstrates the end-to-end application of PHII's Collaborative Requirements Development Methodology (CRDM) to a clinical and public health domain (Maternal Health Information Systems in low-resource settings).
  - **Process Framework (Table 1, p. 7 / PDF p. 8):** Organizes 20 health-service business processes across 4 functional columns (*Patient Administration*, *Patient Care*, *Patient Fees and Accounting*, *Planning, Management & Surveillance*) and selects 10 priority processes for detailed modeling.
  - **Task Flow Diagrams & Narratives (Appendix 1, pp. 10–19 / PDF pp. 11–20):** Models each priority process with swimlane task-flow diagrams across pools (*Facility*, *Community*) and actor lanes (*Nurse/Midwife*, *Skilled Birth Attendant*, *Community Health Worker*, *Woman*), paired with numbered activity narratives.
  - **Functional User Requirements & General System Requirements (Appendices 2–3, pp. 20–35 / PDF pp. 21–36):** Maps each numbered task-flow activity to specific functional user requirements (`"The system must or should..."`) and complements them with cross-cutting system requirements (audit, security, reporting, interoperability).
