General Process Notes 
Objective: The identification of a pregnant woman, 
so she can be followed by the appropriate health 
provider.
There are 2 primary ways a woman may be 
identified:  she may present herself, or she may be 
identified by a community health worker (CHW).
1. Woman suspects she is pregnant
The woman knows the signs of pregnancy and 
suspects she is pregnant.
2. Seek advice from community health worker?
The woman may seek the advice of the CHW as her 
initial entrance into the health system.
3. Woman goes directly to health center?
The woman may already know the process and 
decide to go directly to the health center for 
antenatal care (ANC).
4. Register into health system
The woman is identified in the health center as a 
pregnant woman to be followed.  This is typically 
includes entry into some kind of ANC register.
5.Community screening
The CHW as part of her normal activities screens 
for women who are potentially pregnant
7. Woman counselled and advised to go to 
Health Center
The CHW counsels the woman and explains she 
should seek medical care to ensure the health of 
herself and her baby. The CHW may also provide 
her with details of clinic hours and directions.
8. Woman agrees to go to Health Center for 
ANC?
The woman will decide if she wants to seek ANC 
care at this time.  If not the CHW will continue to 
follow up.

General Process Notes 
Objective: Initiate record of patient visit and validate 
Patient identification and insurance coverage
1.Arrive at clinic
The patient arrives at the service delivery point. 
Service delivery could include scheduled sessions at 
health centers/clinic, single visits to health center/
clinic, follow up appointments, etc. 
2. Search patient name and record
Retrieve patient record through identifying 
information collected. A search will need to be done 
first on all patients in order to avoid duplicates
3. New patient?
Identify whether the patient has an existing record or 
is a first-time user.
4. Enter patient record
Create a patient record in the system for new Patients.
Record demographic information with proof of 
identification. 
5. Validate/update demographic information
Display patient information that can be edited for 
updates so the clerk can vverify identification of 
patient.
6. Is payment required? 
Does the patient need to pay for services? They may 
have insurance, or the service may be free of charge, or 
they may be required to pay in advance.
