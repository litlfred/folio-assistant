- **Core Methodology Summary:**
  - Defines CRDM as a process-centric methodology grounded in business process management that brings multi-disciplinary subject matter experts (SMEs) together to map business processes and derive information system requirements.
  - Organizes requirements engineering around three concentration areas:
    1. **Business Process Analysis (think — *How do we do our work now?*):** Documenting current-state operations via a high-level **Business Process Matrix** (inputs, activities, outputs, actors, business rules, issues/opportunities) and **Task Flow Diagrams** (swimlane diagrams showing sequences of tasks across actors).
    2. **Business Process Redesign (rethink — *How should we do our work?*):** Identifying bottlenecks, redundancies, policy/procedure constraints, and opportunities to streamline workflows before automating them.
    3. **Requirements Definition (describe — *How can an information system support our work?*):** Translating each activity in the redesigned process into clear, concise, testable functional requirements.
  - Links directly to the companion EHR Toolkit guide [`phii-2021-defining-and-validating-requirements-guide`](../phii-2021-defining-and-validating-requirements-guide/) (`https://phii.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Defining-and-Validating-Requirements-Guide_0.pdf`).
