The Harvard Library bibliographic dataset serves as a conve-
nient but imperfect baseline. Subject headings in a production
ILS may reflect varying levels of cataloging (full, core, mini-
mal), copy cataloging from diverse sources with differing sub-
ject indexing practices, legacy headings predating current SHM
instructions, and batch processing or automated enrichment.
The baseline headings should therefore be understood as one
professional interpretation rather than a gold standard.
