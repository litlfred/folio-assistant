RQ1: (Performance of Fine-tuning) Does the optimization of fine-tuning correlate with better UI wireframe
generations? For RQ1, we evaluate the effectiveness of fine-tuning the LLMs, compared with widely-used in-context
learning baseline approaches (e.g., zero-shot and few-shot).
RQ2: (Performance of Variations) Do the variations of the LLMs result in better UI wireframes? For RQ2, we
present the comparison among the variations of GPT models (e.g., Curie, Babbage, Ada) to demonstrate the performance
of using Turbo as the model.
