Evaluation of LLMs is a broad and rapidly growing field of research [15] ranging from very
general assessments of capabilities or intelligence to very task specific performance results
(Fig. 5).
However, while researchers have developed evaluations for related fields, there is limited
literature assessing open-weight LLMs in a standardised way across a variety of public health
specific tasks and data.
Figure 5: LLM Evaluation Spectrum. In this paper we focus on a combination of domain and
task specific LLM evaluations within public health in order to inform our understanding of where
LLMs may be successfully deployed within the field. The area marked by the (*) - denotes how our
evaluations compare to others.
