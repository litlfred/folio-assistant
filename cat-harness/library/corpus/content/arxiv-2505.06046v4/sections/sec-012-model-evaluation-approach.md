To understand the level of knowledge current open-weight and proprietary LLMs have about UK
Government public health guidance, we assess 24 models on PubHealthBench, including: GPT-
4.5 (OpenAI, 2025a), Claude-Sonnet-3.7 (Anthropic, 2025), Gemma-3 (Team, 2025), o1 (OpenAI,
2024), Phi-4 (Abdin et al., 2024), OLMo-2 (OLMo et al., 2025), and Llama-3.3 (Grattafiori et al.,
2024).
