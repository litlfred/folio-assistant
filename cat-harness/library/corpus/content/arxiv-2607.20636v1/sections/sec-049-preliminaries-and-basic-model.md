We study two settings in this chapter: first, we look at a single sequence of T agents
deciding whether to take action A or B; second, we will expand this to k parallel sequences
of agents, where the best action (between A and B) differs group-to-group. Here, we define
the signal and sequence dynamics for a single sequence; in Section 6.4, we flesh out the
setting with k parallel groups. We take T to be finite, but our analysis extends to infinite
T.
