1.Introduction

The international business (IB) literature has long
recognized that the boardroom's ability to translate
strategic intent into error-free sensemaking of
strategic decision-making and real-time operational
efficiency is the central differentiator between
transient success and sustained market leadership
(Buckley & Ghauri, 2021; Verbeke & Kano, 2022).
Yet, a persistent vulnerability gate remains: the
human-mediated assignment of Responsibility,

Accountability, Consultation, and Information
(RACD in real-time, high-stakes environments. In the
absence of a dynamic, error-correcting mechanism,
organizations face escalating risk windows, disputes,
shortcomings, loss doors, and structural weaknesses,
detected only ex-post, when mitigation becomes
impossible or prohibitively costly. This directly
impacts management effectiveness and

organisational defensibility.

This paper addresses a critical, unexamined gap: the
transformation of the static Human RACI matrix into
an Al-Mediated RACI Matrix, due is a real-time
governance tool for operational efficiency, error-free
sensemaking, and organizational defensibility. While
prior research has explored AI in strategic decision-
making (Metcalf et al., 2019) and operational
automation (Brynjolfsson & McAfee, 2017), no study
has systematically integrated the RACI framework
with machine learning-based sensemaking for pre-

emptive risk closure.

The author brings a unique, ground-level perspective.
During a master's program in International Business
in the Russian Federation, the author reflected on
direct professional experience within a multinational
organization operating in a persistently developing
sub-Saharan African market. The observed reality

was stark: before AI, a human-mediated RACI matrix

was the boardroom's primary tool for allocating smart
accountability, balancing inputs, and achieving real-
time operational quality. Yet, the wider sub-Saharan
African organizational landscape, characterized by
self-hindering patterns, unresolved disputes, and
vulnerability cycles, had not taken a single step
toward adopting even this manual tool, let alone its
Al-augmented successor. This paper is a direct

response to that failure.

Research Objectives:

1. To conceptualize and operationalize the Al-
Mediated RACI Matrix.

2.To empirically compare human-mediated vs. AI-
mediated RACI on real-time operational efficiency,
error-free sensemaking, and management
effectiveness.

3. To quantify the framework's impact on
organizational defensibility (ex-ante risk mitigation).
4. To provide a mandatory, actionable protocol for
IB firms, particularly in developing regions.

5. This paper proceeds as follows. Section 2 reviews
theoretical foundations. Section 3 details
methodology. Section 4 presents findings. Section 5
discusses novelty, limitations, and the mandatory

protocol. Section 6 concludes with implications.
