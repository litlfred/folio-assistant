- **Study Design:** Quantitative corpus analysis of **$n = 126$ real-world BPMN diagrams** across **3 industry/academic cohorts** (**60 consulting project diagrams**, **36 web-published diagrams**, and **30 professional seminar diagrams**) coded against all **50 BPMN 1.x specification constructs** (Section 3, p. 5 / LNCS p. 469).
- **What Was Measured:** Construct occurrence frequency, vocabulary size per diagram (number of distinct BPMN construct types used), and statistical distribution across cohorts.
- **Numeric Thresholds & Key Findings:**
  - **Practical Vocabulary Size Limit (`~9 constructs per diagram`):** Across all 126 models, practitioners use an average of just **~9 distinct BPMN constructs per model**:
    - **Consulting models ($n = 60$):** mean **$9.0$ constructs** (SD $3.44$, min $2$, max $20$, Section 4, p. 5 / LNCS p. 469).
    - **Web models ($n = 36$):** mean **$8.78$ constructs** (SD $3.79$, min $3$, max $17$, p. 5 / LNCS p. 469).
    - **Seminar models ($n = 30$):** mean **$8.70$ constructs** (SD $2.49$, min $4$, max $14$, p. 5 / LNCS p. 469).
    - **Typical Working Range:** **$6	ext{ to }12$ distinct construct types** per model (pp. 11–12 / LNCS pp. 475–476).
  - **Core Construct Concentration (Fig. 3, p. 7 / LNCS p. 471):**
    - Only **4 constructs** appear in $> 50\%$ of diagrams (**Task: 99%**, **Sequence Flow: 98%**, **End Event: 80%**, **Start Event: 75%**, followed by **XOR Data-Based Gateway: 69%** and **Pool/Lane: 58–60%**).
    - **9 of the 50 BPMN constructs ($18\%$)** were **never used once** in any of the 126 models, and **32 constructs ($64\%$)** appeared in fewer than $10\%$ of diagrams (pp. 6–8 / LNCS pp. 470–472).
