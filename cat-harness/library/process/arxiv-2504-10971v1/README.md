- **Study Design:** PRISMA Systematic Literature Review screening **991 records** down to **$n = 48$ primary studies** evaluating business process visualizations across user roles, cognitive profiles, notations (BPMN, Petri nets, EPCs, process trees, 3D/VR, tabular/textual), and visual modifications.
- **What Was Measured:** Comparative effectiveness of visual representations and secondary notation cues (layout, colour highlighting, decomposition, flow direction, animation/interactivity) on task accuracy, completion time, and mental effort.
- **Numeric Thresholds & Key Findings:**
  - Synthesizes empirical evidence across **48 primary studies** showing that secondary notation (layout, syntax/colour highlighting, orthogonal routing, consistent LR/TB flow direction, and modular decomposition) systematically improves comprehension effectiveness across both novices and experts (Sections 3–4, pp. 4–14).
