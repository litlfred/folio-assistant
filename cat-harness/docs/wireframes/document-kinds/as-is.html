Document kinds — smart-base

A document kind is a named structure of sections that a document authored with a harness follows: fixed (exactly these sections) or semi-fixed (these required, others allowed). Every kind and section names its sources.

Digital Adaptation Kit (DAK) dak

The L2 layer of a WHO SMART Guideline: the ten components that make a guideline's recommendations operational, ahead of the L3 FHIR IG that implements them. Fixed: a DAK has exactly these components, in this order.
