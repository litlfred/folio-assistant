Adjudicator
cat-harness/glossary/glossary-ledger.json#role/adjudicator		 Settles a criterion whose reviewer entries disagree, and records WHY. Inherits `reviewer` because an adjudicator is a reviewer with one added entitlement — the `adjudication` permission — and the separation is the point: a reviewer reports, an adjudicator settles, and a process where every reviewer could settle has no way to say who decided. The KNOWLEDGE spreads (several roles carry the `adjudication` skill, because each has to tell an adjudicable disagreement from a complaint); the ENTITLEMENT does not, and lives on the actor as a permission.

Deep researcher
cat-harness/glossary/glossary-ledger.json#role/deep-researcher		 Answers a question FROM a corpus already held, and is answerable for the answer. Distinct from `librarian`, which decides what to capture, and from `ingestion-agent`, which derives structure from what was captured: both produce the corpus, and this role consumes it. Added 2026-09-23 with the `doc-researcher` methodology, because the accountability it names — somebody who must stand behind a synthesis and its citations — belonged to no declared role. THE LANE IT BINDS IS DRAWN IN ANOTHER INSTANCE — the `Deep researcher` lane of `folio-assistant-core/processes/deep-document-research.bpmn`, which names this role with `<folio:role ref>`. It carried a `lanes[]` here for about an hour on 2026-09-23 before #1168 landed and inverted the relation: a lane names its role, and a role does not list its lanes, because a general node never names its users. The binding is therefore stated once, on the lane, and this role says nothing about where it is drawn.
