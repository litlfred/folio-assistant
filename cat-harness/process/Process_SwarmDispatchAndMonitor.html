descriptionBinds or cold-resumes any multi-agent swarm on a parent Swarm Work Item (epic or milestone in beans, for example a repository Stability epic, a proof-closure epic, or a literature-audit epic) carrying measurable exit criteria (EC-1..EC-k), structured child queues (completed, active, queued, idle-backlog), and a live Cold-Start Handover Runbook, then dispatches isolated-worktree workers under a per-swarm spawn gate and supervises them through Process_SwarmMonitorSweep until the epic drains or halts.
enforcementstrict

Task_InitOrClaimSwarmEpic

  • owner-decision cat-harness#1397 2026-11-24
    Owner directives (2026-11-24, PR #1397): starting any swarm binds to a parent Swarm Work Item (epic or milestone in beans, parameterized over any swarm goal — with 'Stability' as one concrete example alongside research, proof-closure, detangling, literature-audit, and outreach swarms) carrying explicit acceptance criteria / end state, structured child work-item queues, user-selectable on-demand vs periodic status reporting, and a live Cold-Start Handover Runbook kept synced on every check-in through Process_SwarmMonitorSweep so any successor coordinator or merge manager can take over cold when a session stalls.

Swarm_Decompose

  • owner-decision 2026-11-28 owner
    Owner directive, 2026-11-28: research-agent queues stay specialized by deep mathematical or technical niche so related follow-up questions route to the same specialist, with cross-fertilization fan-out across multiple specialists when an interface connection is suspected (Process_ResearchDelegationAndNicheRouting).
