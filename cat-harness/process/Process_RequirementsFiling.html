descriptionHow a proposal in docs/proposals/<slug>.md is promoted into docs/requirements/<slug>.md when its feature ships, or how an existing filed requirement is amended in place, superseded by a new requirement (status: superseded + supersededBy: req:<new-slug>), or retired (status: retired) without deleting historical records. Every filed requirement keeps its flat path docs/requirements/<slug>.md (id: req:<slug>) and is organised in docs/requirements/index.md under its canonical concern group from code-lists/concern-group.json, verified by check:requirements, and signed off by the Business Analyst / Owner.
