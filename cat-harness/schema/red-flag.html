Description

RED FLAG taxonomy, adversarial review sidecar schema, and recorded override path.

What is a RED FLAG?

A RED FLAG is a finding the reviewer asserts would block the merge. It reuses the two existing axes in schemas/sdlc/qa-review.ts: - FindingSeverity (critical | major | minor: what kind of breakage) - FindingWeight (blocking | suggestion | praise: what the reviewer asks of the gate)

A RED FLAG is weight: "blocking" with a category from a closed taxonomy: security, data-loss, correctness, false-green, provenance, scope-breach, irreversible-action, licence.

Evidence is strictly REQUIRED for any blocking finding: a finding without reproducible observation cannot be blocking (it is demoted to suggestion).

Warn-only review

The review warns; it does not block. An open red flag is evaluated by the gate as would-have-blocked rather than holding the merge, which keeps the taxonomy and the blocking weight and records the data needed to decide on promoting it to a gate later.

The human override path

A red flag is NEVER deleted. An override is a recorded decision by a human with standing (who, when, why, and finding ID). The finding's status becomes overridden with a full resolution record; the gate passes on resolved or overridden and warns/blocks on open / would-have-blocked.
