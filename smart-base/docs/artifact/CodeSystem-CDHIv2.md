## Classification of Digital Health Interventions v2

`CodeSystem/CDHIv2`

CodeSystem for the Classification of Digital Interventions, Services and Applications in Health (CDISAH), second edition (2023). ISBN 978-92-4-008194-9. Organised into four groups based on the primary user: Persons Healthcare providers Health management and support personnel Data services New categories vs v1: 1.4.4, 1.6.2, 1.8, 2.5.6, 2.11, 3.1.5, 3.5.7, 3.5.8, 3.8, 4.3.5, 4.4.2, 4.4.3, 4.5. See ConceptMap CDHIv1toCDHIv2 for the full mapping from the first edition.
