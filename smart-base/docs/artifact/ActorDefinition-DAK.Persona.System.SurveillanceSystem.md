## Public Health and Disease Surveillance System

`ActorDefinition/DAK.Persona.System.SurveillanceSystem`

A digital system for detecting, monitoring, investigating, and responding to disease outbreaks and public health threats. The surveillance system supports DHIs including: Notification of public health events from point of diagnosis (3.3.1) Transmit health event alerts to specific population group(s) (1.1.1) Map location of health event (4.3.2) Data synthesis and visualizations for outbreak response (4.1.3) Automated analysis of data to generate predictions (4.1.4) Services and Application Type : E2 — Public health and disease surveillance systems
