Examples 
2 
This appendix records selected national, subnational and regional implementations, together with 
3 
the standards-based tools and technologies on which they draw, to illustrate approaches that 
4 
countries have taken in building the shared digital resources described in this guidance. Each 
5 
example is intended to help ministries of health, digital public infrastructure authorities and 
6 
implementation teams recognise patterns relevant to their own setting, and to see how the 
7 
components articulated in Section 3.5 and Appendix C have been realised in practice across a range 
8 
of health system contexts and levels of maturity.  
9 
Note on the naming of systems.  The systems, tools and national programmes named in this 
10 
appendix are cited descriptively, to illustrate approaches adopted by countries at a point in time and 
11 
are drawn from publicly available sources. Naming reflects each country's own implementation choice. 
12 
Their inclusion does not constitute endorsement, recommendation or an assessment of suitability by 
13 
WHO or ITU for any particular context, and no ranking or comparative evaluation between named 
14 
systems is intended or implied. The transferable observations offered under each example hold 
15 
independently of any specific product. 
16 
The tools and technologies listed in Section D.1 are software components, servers and applications 
17 
that may be used to implement or support the capabilities described in this guidance; their inclusion 
18 
does not imply that any of them constitutes shared infrastructure or digital public infrastructure in its 
19 
own right. Several are commonly deployed at facility or programme level rather than as national shared 
20 
infrastructure, and whether a given capability functions as shared infrastructure is determined by how a 
21 
country deploys and governs it, not by the tool itself.
