No country designing or implementing DPI-H begins from a blank slate. Every health system 
14 
carries a legacy, including programme-specific applications built around particular funding 
15 
priorities, national health information systems of varying capability, patient registries that sit in 
16 
silos, and data still maintained in spreadsheets for reporting. The reference architecture, while 
17 
ideal in outlook, recognises this legacy and aims to help countries understand which existing 
18 
investments can be preserved, which should be upgraded to conform to open standards, which 
19 
can be integrated into shared infrastructure, and which should be retired in favour of shared 
20 
components that serve the whole system more efficiently. 
21 
 
22 
This chapter provides practical guidance for moving from the current state toward REDDHI — 
23 
described in the preceding chapter not as a fixed destination to be reached and completed but 
24 
as a continuously maturing direction of travel — through a structured, phased approach adapted 
25 
to each country’s context, capability, and starting conditions.
