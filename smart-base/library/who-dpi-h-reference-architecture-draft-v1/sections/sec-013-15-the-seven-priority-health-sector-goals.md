The reference architecture is organised around seven priority health sector goals* that represent 
424 
the areas where digital infrastructure can most meaningfully improve health system 
425 
performance. These goals are not isolated ambitions but mutually reinforcing outcomes that 
426 
compound in value as the underlying infrastructure matures. 
427 
 
428 
At the heart of the architecture is a simple but consequential principle: that data about a person, 
429 
a facility, a product, or a transaction should be captured once, governed well, and made 
430 
available — with appropriate consent and permissions — wherever it is needed across the 
431 
health system. "Governed well" carries as much weight here as "captured once": the value of 
432 
shared data depends on its trustworthiness, quality, and security, which in turn rest on sound 
433 
data governance — consistent standards applied across the entire data journey, from capture 
434 
through to use. In practice, this principle reshapes everyday work. A health worker would no 
435 
longer be required to re-enter the same patient demographics at every encounter; a clinical 
436 
finding from one visit would inform the next; a prescription recorded at a clinic can be verified at 
437 
a pharmacy.

440

programmes, each drawing on the same underlying data but compiled separately by hand.

The same well-governed foundation compounds in value as it is reused. It allows a health 
450 
authority to see, close to real time, where its workforce is and what it is qualified to do; it lets a 
451 
patient's clinical context follow them from a community health worker to a clinic to a hospital, so 
452 
that care is coordinated rather than repeated; it enables medicines to be directed to where 
453 
demand and capacity confirm they are needed; and it allows financing to be verified against real 
454 
service delivery and directed toward the populations that need it most. When a disease 
455 
outbreak emerges or a climate event strains the system, the same surveillance and data 
456 
exchange capabilities provide early signals. Each of these is reflected in the seven goals set out 
457 
below — but their common thread is that none requires a separate, parallel system. They draw 
458 
on the same shared foundation, which is precisely what allows their value to accumulate.
