For Ministries of Finance, the most persuasive case is grounded in outcomes they already track: 
556 
improved budget execution, reduced leakage, timely provider payments, traceable health 
557 
expenditure, and the efficiency gains that follow from replacing fragmented administrative 
558 
systems with shared infrastructure. Framed this way, DPI-H reads as capital investment in 
559 
health system infrastructure rather than a technology modernisation project — and that 
560 
reframing is what justifies financing it as infrastructure: through multi-year, recurrent budget 
561 
lines and domestic cost-recovery mechanisms, rather than the project-based donor cycles that 
562 
treat digital infrastructure as a passing line item.42 Ministries of Finance are best engaged not as 
563 
a final approval step but as strategic partners in the long-term financing of shared services.
