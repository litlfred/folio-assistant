3.4.2 Governance principles 
249 
The following principles should guide the governance design of any national DPI-H deployment: 
250 
• Subsidiarity. Decision authority should sit at the lowest level at which it can be exercised 
251 
effectively. Operational decisions should not require strategic deliberation, and strategic 
252 
decisions should not be delegated to operational bodies.
