2. DPI Authorities: This reference architecture shows how the health sector can become 
145 
major users of your foundational DPI services while explaining what health-specific 
146 
requirements they need to build on top of your infrastructure. 
147 
3. Enterprise Architects and Technical Teams: This reference architecture provides 
148 
TOGAF-based methodology, formal architecture patterns, and systematic requirements 
149 
frameworks to support coherent planning and long-term scalability across the health 
150 
ecosystem. It also provides a clear, standards-based blueprint that guides system 
151 
design, integration, and validation, enabling interoperable and scalable implementations.  
152 
4. Development Partners and Donors: This reference architecture provides a shared 
153 
framework to guide coordinated investments, reduce duplication, and ensure funded 
154 
solutions are interoperable, scalable, and aligned with country priorities. It demonstrates 
155 
how DPI can strengthen transparency and accountability by being embedded into health 
156 
financing and public financial management processes. It also highlights how health-
157 
specific capabilities integrate with broader DPI to support multiple health goals 
158 
simultaneously, enabling more strategic investments and avoiding siloed, unsustainable 
159 
approaches.

informed evaluation of solutions.
