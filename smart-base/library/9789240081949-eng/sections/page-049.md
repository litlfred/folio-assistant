SERVICES AND 
APPLICATION TYPES
The digital services and application types represent the types of software, information and 
communications technology (ICT) systems and services or communication channels that 
deliver or execute the digital health intervention(s) and health content. The classification 
categorizes them into 5 representations within the Digital Health Architecture. Definitions 
and additional descriptors are included to support the appropriate mapping and 
categorizing of digital solutions.
