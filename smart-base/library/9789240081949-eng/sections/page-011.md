The classification of DISAH also highlights 
functionalities that fit within various Services and 
Application types, such as Logistics Management 
Information Systems (LMIS) or Electronic Medical 
Records (EMR). Services and Application types 
represent the types of digital applications, 
information systems and services designed to deliver 
one or more digital health interventions. A digital 
health intervention such as “notify stock levels of 
health commodities” would fit into the Application 
type, LMIS. Linking digital health interventions to 
services or application types is critical as these 
serve as the starting point for interoperability 
considerations.
