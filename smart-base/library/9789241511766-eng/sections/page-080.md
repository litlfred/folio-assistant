Case–control
■
■Retrospective studies in

■Can be relatively inexpensive

alternatives
■
■Results can be

Ecological correlational 
studies
■
■Look for associations between 
exposures and outcomes in

individuals

■Can be less expensive than 
alternatives

outcome in individuals
■
■Can be difficult to 
control for confounding
Case report
■
■Report of an event, unusual

study designs
■
■Can be used to spur 
subsequent research

■Least publishable unit in

Case-series reports
■
■Aggregates individual cases in

■
■Can be used to spur 
subsequent research
■
■Difficult to publish in the
