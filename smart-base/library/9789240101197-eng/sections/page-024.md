The DHSC vision provides a guiding beacon by summarizing the ideal future state of health 
supply chain digitalization and how to achieve it. Strategic goals guide the country’s actions and 
decisions to achieve the vision. They translate to strategic objectives that are specific, measurable, 
achievable, relevant and time-bound. The goals should link to the DHSC implementation activities.
