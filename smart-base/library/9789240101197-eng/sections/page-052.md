Budget dependence on political

and outcomes
	»
Budget for DHSC initiatives at the start of a government’s term. 
	»
If initiatives require time beyond political events that might affect the budget, consider options for 
scaling back or phasing, depending on the context.
	»
Budget rigidity that creates an 
obstacle to accommodating 
unforeseen but necessary 
changes (due to factors related to 
technological advances, regulatory 
changes, staffing, etc.)
	»
Ensure that budgets account for potential changes and include a buffer.
	»
