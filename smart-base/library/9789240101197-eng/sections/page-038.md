The following governance options for an architecture that 
is based on supply chain design assume SCISMM Level 5 
maturity.
	
→Architecture based on supply chain design: 
centralized management. Countries that manage 
their health supply chain centrally can adopt this 
approach, which resembles the general framework 
depicted earlier in Fig. 14. All health supply chain 
services, including those used by district and service 
delivery point facilities, are centrally managed using 
this approach (Fig. 17). This approach is suitable if 
central warehouses manage when and how much 
inventory to push to downstream facilities.
