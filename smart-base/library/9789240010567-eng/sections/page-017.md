STEPS

STEPS

architecture blueprint for the design of the digital 
health implementations.
+	Identify validated open standards to ensure 
data exchange, systems integration and future 
proofing of digital health implementations. 
STEPS
+	Monitor your implementation to ensure digital 
implementations are functioning as intended 
and having the desired effect.

within the overall health system.
STEPS

the implementation context.

standards for the future state.
STEPS

implementations.
