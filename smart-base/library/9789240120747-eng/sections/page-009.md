vii
Glossary
Active 
pharmacovigilance
Proactive measures that are taken to detect adverse events (1).
Adverse drug 
reactions
Harmful, unintended reactions to medicines that occur at doses normally 
used for treatment (2).
Business process
A set of related activities or tasks performed together to achieve the 
objectives of the health programme area, such as registration, counselling 
and referrals (3).
Data element
A unit of data that has a specific and precise meaning (4).
Digitization
The process of converting and organizing data from paper records into a 
digital format for easier entry, storage and retrieval. Digitization is the initial 
step towards digital transformation (5, 6).
Digitalization
The process of digitally automating and simplifying processes to streamline 
individual health programmes and reducing manual efforts for greater 
efficiency. Digitalization is the second step towards digital transformation
(5, 6).
Digital 
transformation
The process of digitally optimizing and driving systems-level changes 
by leveraging innovation, analytics and feedback mechanisms to 
improve person-centred health systems. Digital technologies are used to 
fundamentally change and improve how health services are delivered and 
accessed across all health programme areas (5, 6, 7).
Enabling 
environment
Attitudes, actions, policies and practices that stimulate and support effective 
and efficient functioning of organizations, individuals and programmes. The 
enabling environment includes legal, regulatory and policy frameworks and 
political, sociocultural, institutional and economic factors (3).
End user 
People who will be using the digital system(s).
Enterprise 
architecture
A blueprint of business processes, data, systems and technologies used 
to help implementers design increasingly complex systems to support the 
workflow and roles of people in a large enterprise, such as a health system 
(3).
Evaluation
The systematic and objective assessment of an ongoing or completed 
intervention with the aim of determining the fulfilment of objectives, 
efficiency, effectiveness, impact and sustainability (8).
Falsified medical 
products
Medical products that deliberately or fraudulently misrepresent their identity, 
composition or source. Any consideration related to intellectual property 
rights does not fall within this definition. Such deliberate or fraudulent 
misrepresentation refers to any substitution, adulteration or reproduction of 
an authorized medical product or the manufacture of a medical product that 
is not an authorized product (9).
Features
The characteristics of your system that include look and feel as well as what 
the system can do (e.g. “approval workflow” can be a feature of a product 
master data management solution). 
Functional 
requirements
Capabilities the system must have to meet the end users’ needs and achieve 
tasks within the business process.
