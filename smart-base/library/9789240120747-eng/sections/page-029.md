2. Establish a shared understanding and governance framework
2.5 Analyse and mitigate risk
Digital transformations often face multiple risks 
– technical, financial or political. Conduct a risk 
analysis early in the strategic planning process to 
allow for appropriate mitigation measures to be 
put in place. Examples of risk include: 
•	
Technical risks. System failures, cybersecurity 
threats, interoperability challenges.
•	
Financial risks. Funding shortfalls, changes 
in donor priorities or excessive long-term 
maintenance costs.
•	
Political and stakeholder risks. Shifting 
government priorities, lack of stakeholder 
cooperation or data ownership disputes.
Annex 7 provides a template for risk management 
to identify, assess and prioritize these risks, 
creating mitigation strategies and contingency 
plans.
Successful digital transformation of substandard 
and falsified medical product reporting processes 
depends on collaboration, careful policy 
consideration and proactive governance. By 
taking these strategic steps, countries can lay the 
groundwork for an effective, sustainable digital 
reporting system that ultimately safeguards the 
quality of medicines and protects public health.
After finishing the steps outlined in this chapter, there should be a comprehensive 
current state analysis of the key stakeholders including:
New and/or amended regulatory policies on substandard and falsified medical products
Governance structures and mechanisms established
Strategic goals for reporting of substandard and falsified medical products
