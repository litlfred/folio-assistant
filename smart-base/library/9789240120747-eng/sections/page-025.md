Notes: a Anonymizing the reporting of suspected substandard and falsified medical products is highly valuable because 
it encourages more reports by reducing fear of legal, professional or personal repercussions. It protects vulnerable 
individuals, such as health care professionals, patients or whistleblowers, particularly in environments where retaliation 
is a risk. Anonymity also helps focus investigations on the evidence rather than the identity of the reporter, building 
public trust in the reporting system. However, challenges include difficulties in following up for more details, assessing 
credibility and managing potential false reports.
