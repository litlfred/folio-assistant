6.2 Develop an operations and 
continuous improvement plan
Develop an operations plan to sustain the 
ongoing operations and continuous improvement 
of the reporting application, ensuring its 
reliability and efficiency. The operations plan 
addresses strategies to scale the application, 
including expanding the user base and adding 
new features; as well as the maintenance and 
update strategy to support continued usage by 
existing users. By implementing a comprehensive 
operations and sustainability plan, stakeholders 
can manage resources effectively, maintain 
robust performance, accommodate growth and 
evolving needs, and stay abreast of emerging 
cybersecurity threats.
Establishing an operations and sustainability 
plan is a critical step to ensure the long-term 
success and reliability of the substandard and 
falsified reporting application. This plan should 
include details of stakeholders that will own 
and maintain the reporting application. These 
stakeholders could be internal teams responsible 
for daily operations or external partners 
providing specialized support. Clearly defining 
the roles and responsibilities of each stakeholder 
will ensure accountability and streamline the 
maintenance and update process. Additionally, 
it is crucial to identify the required resources, 
such as maintenance personnel, cybersecurity 
oversight, hardware, network infrastructure, and 
backup power. These resources are fundamental 
to the smooth operation and resilience of the 
application, preventing disruptions due to 
technical failures or power outages.
Recurring knowledge management needs, 
including training, updating job aids, recruitments 
and job rotations, must also be addressed in 
the plan. Continuous training programmes will 
keep the maintenance personnel and other 
stakeholders equipped with the latest skills and 
knowledge to manage the application effectively. 
Updating job aids and recruiting new staff when 
necessary, will help maintain a high level of 
operational efficiency. Job rotations can prevent 
burnout and ensure that team members have a 
well-rounded understanding of the system.
Furthermore, the plan should outline the 
recurring costs for the required resources, 
system subscriptions, knowledge management, 
cybersecurity, M&E and efforts to maintain 
and operate the application. A comprehensive 
financial strategy is essential to cover these 
recurring operational costs, ensuring the 
sustainability of the application. This strategy 
should include budgeting for unexpected 
expenses and exploring potential funding 
sources.
A robust plan for data backups and system 
redundancy must be devised to mitigate the 
impact of system failures. Regular data backups 
will safeguard against data loss, while system 
redundancy will ensure that the application 
remains functional even if one component fails. 
Implementing a change management and 
governance process for new requirements arising 
from evolving needs will keep the application 
relevant and responsive to changing demands. 
This process should include mechanisms for 
evaluating and prioritizing new requirements, as 
well as a structured approach to implementing 
changes.
