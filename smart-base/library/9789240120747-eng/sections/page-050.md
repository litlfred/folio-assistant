Evaluate the deployment options, including 
public, private or hybrid cloud, as well as on-
premises solutions. Choose technology solutions 
that are cost-effective, reliable and easily scalable. 
Ensure that the chosen technology aligns with 
the overall enterprise architecture and integrates 
seamlessly with existing systems.
Tip: It is important to recognize that 
although open-source applications may 
be free, implementing them can still incur 
costs. Factors such as required features, 
time, effort, cost and national IT and data 
sovereignty policies should be considered 
when choosing the appropriate option 
as long-term maintenance costs could 
potentially be higher depending on the 
context and capacity available.
This is a critical decision that significantly impacts 
the application's functionality and efficiency, and 
it should fit within the enterprise architecture 
outlined in Section 3.2. See Table 15 for various 
options to consider, each with its unique 
advantages and constraints.
