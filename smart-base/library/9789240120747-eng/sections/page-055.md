Table 17. Steps in developing a comprehensive M&E plan
Step 1
Establish an underlying framework that guides the M&E process. The framework 
should outline how the reporting application is expected to work and what 
outcomes it aims to achieve. It serves as a blueprint for the entire M&E process, 
ensuring that all activities are aligned with the stated goals.
Step 2
Clearly identifying evidence needs and evaluation objectives is essential. 
Determine what data are required to evaluate the reporting application accurately 
and what the objectives of the evaluation are. This step involves setting clear, 
measurable goals that the M&E activities will seek to achieve, such as improving 
substandard and falsified reporting rates or reducing substandard and falsified 
medical product incidence rates. Link the objectives with the timing and stage of 
the substandard and falsified reporting application’s maturity.
Step 3
Design the process that will guide the M&E activities. This involves selecting the 
appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques to collect and analyse data. The 
design should be robust, ensuring that it can provide reliable and valid results that 
can inform decision-making.
Step 4
Determine who will be responsible for carrying out the M&E activities. This involves 
identifying and training personnel who have the necessary skills and expertise 
to conduct M&E activities effectively. It may also involve partnering with external 
organizations or experts to ensure the evaluations are carried out to the highest 
standards.
Step 5
Timing and resource allocation are critical components of an effective M&E 
plan. Create a timeline that outlines the different stages of the implementation 
and operations when M&E activities will be carried out, ensuring they align with 
the substandard and falsified reporting application’s implementation schedule. 
Additionally, resources must be allocated to support these activities, including 
funding, personnel and data collection tools.
Step 6
All the above details must be summarized into an M&E implementation plan. 
This plan will outline the steps and processes for carrying out M&E activities, 
detailing how data will be collected, analysed and reported. It should also include 
mechanisms for reviewing and updating the M&E plan to ensure it remains 
relevant and effective over time.
