Digital transformation handbook for reporting substandard and falsified medical products
Similarly, the following illustrative questions 
can help with the evaluation needs across the 
substandard and falsified reporting application’s 
life cycle (Table 16). 
Table 16. Evaluation needs across the substandard and falsified reporting application life cycle: sample 
questions
Usability
Is the substandard and falsified reporting application usable by the 
targeted end user(s), and does it fit within their workflow?
How steep is the learning curve before a user can demonstrate proficient 
use of the reporting application?
What are the rates of error – in using the system or in workflows – as a 
result of system use/misuse?
Efficacy
Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed processes 
(e.g. time between event X and response Y) in a research or controlled 
setting such as a pilot?
Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed outcomes 
(e.g. increased reporting by health care professionals) in a research or 
controlled setting such as a pilot?
Effectiveness
Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed processes 
(e.g. time between event X and response Y) in a non-research setting?
Has the substandard and falsified reporting application changed outcomes 
(e.g. increased reporting by health care professionals) in a non-research 
setting?
Cost
Has the substandard and falsified reporting application reduced costs 
associated with substandard and falsified reporting or substandard and 
falsified medical product incidences in general?
Has the substandard and falsified reporting application introduced costs 
that are commensurate with benefits provided?
