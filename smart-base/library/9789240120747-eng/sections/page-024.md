Barriers. Understanding the barriers to reporting 
substandard and falsified medical products 
is crucial for the effective implementation of 
reporting systems. Identify these barriers and 
understand the reasons behind them. This 
ensures that the design and deployment of 
reporting tools are tailored to meet the needs 
of NMRAs, health care professionals and other 
stakeholders. This understanding is vital for 
creating a robust and efficient reporting system 
that can mitigate the challenges faced by users, 
thereby enhancing the overall effectiveness of the 
reporting process. Various potential barriers are 
outlined in Table 5 along with potential solutions 
that can be considered and evaluated within the 
country context when developing the reporting 
solution. Further analysis of barriers is provided in 
Annex 6.

Confirm whether there is a standard reporting form for substandard
