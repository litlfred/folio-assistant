A comprehensive assessment of the current state and the enabling environment is necessary before 
implementing a digital reporting system for substandard and falsified medical products. This chapter 
outlines the areas that should be analysed and the factors to consider. The inputs needed to inform this 
step and the anticipated outputs of this phase are listed below.
1.1 Analyse key stakeholders
To implement an effective digital substandard 
and falsified reporting solution, it is essential to 
identify and analyse stakeholders. Stakeholders 
include government officials, health care 
providers, funding organizations, the medical 
product industry, technology vendors and 
implementers, civil society and other partners. 
The analysis should outline their roles in 
substandard and falsified medical products, 
pharmacovigilance and digital health. It should 
help mobilize support and engage key players 
by examining responsibilities, information 
flows and power dynamics. Consultations 
with NMRAs, ministries of health, civil society, 
industry associations, health care professional 
associations, funders and other relevant partners 
are valuable to ensure a comprehensive analysis. 
Creating a stakeholder matrix to outline 
responsibilities, interests and influence will 
help in better coordination and expectation 
management. The sample questions provided 
in Table 2 facilitate comprehensive stakeholder 
analysis across various dimensions. See the RACI 
(responsible, accountable, consulted, informed) 
matrix example (Annex 4).

1.2 Assess the digital readiness
Different levels of preparedness for deploying 
digital technologies will influence the design, 
implementation and adoption of reporting 
apps. As one of the first steps, a digital 
readiness assessment should be conducted 
to ensure solutions are tailored to the existing 
infrastructure, capacity and context. The outputs 
from the assessment should inform the necessary 
resources, enabling environment, budget and 
funding support for planning, implementation 
and operationalization of the substandard and 
falsified reporting system.
