1. Assess the current state and enabling environment
Table 3. Assessing the technology infrastructure
Assessing network 
coverage, connec­
tivity and electricity
Obtain or calculate the World Economic Forum Network Readiness Score.
Obtain the availability of electricity at the facility level, including the 
availability for 8 hours a day.
Obtain the percentage of health care facilities with internet access.
Obtain the number of mobile phone users per health care facility.
Guiding questions
What is the affordability and quality of internet across the country, especially 
in urban versus rural areas? Do they vary across provinces or regions?
Do the existing 3G and 4G stations sufficiently cover the population?
What is the number of mobile broadband users and internet subscriptions?
Does the prevalence of smartphones exceed access to computers with wired 
internet connections?
Hardware 
and software 
availability
Obtain the proportion of health care facilities with functioning and accessible 
mobile devices.
Assess in-country data storage centres, servers or cloud-based storage 
availability.
Explore the national hardware inventory.
Explore the inventory of systems and platforms.
Explore penetration of computers and high-speed internet, mobile phones 
and other smart devices in health care facilities and the general public.
Infrastructure
What national, state and regional computing infrastructure exists across the 
country, including components such as server farms, data centres, support 
systems and personnel, etc.?
Can existing infrastructure components scale up to support broader national 
use?
Have there been large-scale e-health or other information and 
communication technology (ICT) infrastructure projects that have failed or 
stalled? If so, what were the primary reasons?
Security
Are there robust cybersecurity measures in place to protect digital 
infrastructure, systems and data?
Is there a comprehensive incident response plan in case of security 
breaches?
Are regular security audits conducted to identify vulnerabilities and mitigate 
risks?
Are there laws and regulations in place to address cybercrime and protect 
against unauthorized access?
Privacy
Are there legal frameworks and regulations in place to safeguard individuals' 
privacy rights?
Is there awareness among citizens about their privacy rights and how their 
data are being used?
Are organizations required to obtain consent before collecting and 
processing personal data?
Is there a designated authority responsible for overseeing privacy-related 
issues?
Data ownership
Are there clear laws and regulations defining data ownership rights?
Do individuals have control over their personal data and the ability to access, 
modify or delete it?
Are there mechanisms in place to ensure organizations do not unlawfully 
claim ownership of user-generated data?
Are there guidelines for data sharing and collaboration between different 
entities while respecting data ownership?
