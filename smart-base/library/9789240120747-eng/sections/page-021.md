Table 2. Stakeholder analysis: sample questions
Roles and 
responsibilities
What is this stakeholder’s current role in managing or reporting 
substandard and falsified medical products?
How does this stakeholder contribute to pharmacovigilance or digital 
health efforts?
What decisions can this stakeholder make regarding digital substandard 
and falsified reporting systems?
Are they implementers, advisors, funders, regulators or end users?
Information flows 
and interactions
What data does this stakeholder generate, use or depend on related to 
substandard and falsified products?
Who do they share this information with, and how frequently?
Are there any existing digital platforms or tools they use that must 
integrate with the substandard and falsified reporting system?
Influence and 
power
How much influence does this stakeholder have over policy, funding or 
system design decisions?
Can this stakeholder help or hinder the adoption of the digital reporting 
system?
Who do they influence or rely on for decision-making?
Interests and 
expectations
What are this stakeholder’s main concerns or interests in relation to 
substandard and falsified reporting?
What outcomes would they expect or value from this system?
Are there any incentives or risks for them in engaging with this initiative?
Engagement and 
collaboration
Have they been engaged in similar initiatives before? If so, what worked 
well or poorly?
What is the best way to engage them – technical working groups, one-on-
one meetings, formal memoranda of understanding?
Are there any other partners or intermediaries they trust and collaborate 
with?
Capacity and 
resources
Does this stakeholder have the technical, financial or human resource 
capacity to contribute to or implement the system?
Will they need support, training or tools to fulfil their responsibilities?
