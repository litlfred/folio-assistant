Once you have defined the future state and the requirements for the substandard and falsified 
reporting application, the next step is to implement the system. This chapter provides guidance on 
planning the implementation, including the creation of a detailed budget.
Effective planning is critical for the successful 
implementation of the substandard and falsified 
reporting application. This process involves 
a structured approach to developing the 
implementation plan, budgeting for all phases 
of the project and executing key steps to ensure 
sustainability and scale. The steps outlined in 
Table 14 serve as a practical roadmap to guide 
implementers through this process.
