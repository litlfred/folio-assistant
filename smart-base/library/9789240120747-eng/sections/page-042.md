4.4 Outline data requirements
A data dictionary is a repository of information 
about the data elements such as description, 
data type and other characteristics. A data 
dictionary will be a useful input to designing the 
solution and facilitating understanding across 
multiple stakeholders. Data dictionaries should 
utilize terminology standards (e.g. International 
Classification of Diseases [ICD-11], SNOMED-GPS) 
and syntactic interoperability standards (e.g. HL7 
FHIR). These standards ensure consistent and 
high-quality data exchange across various digital 
systems, regardless of the time, health topics, 
or different health care providers and facilities 
involved.
The data elements that health care professionals 
will collect will be defined in the data dictionary 
and included in the reporting application in 
appropriate activities of the workflow. A sample 
list of data elements is provided in Table 11 and 
can be tailored to the country’s context.
4.5 Gather requirements
Functional and non-functional requirements 
outline what the reporting application needs to do 
and how it should perform. These requirements 
guide developers and stakeholders in designing, 
developing and deploying the application. They 
define the application's purpose, features, 
functionality and behaviour. 
Illustrative functional and non-functional 
requirements are provided in Tables 12 and 13 
as a starting point. This can be adapted and 
expanded to meet the needs of the country and 
the specific context.
