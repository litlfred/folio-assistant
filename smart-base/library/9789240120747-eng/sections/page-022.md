1.3 Assess current substandard and 
falsified reporting practices
Current practices. Conduct an analysis of 
current practices, challenges and barriers faced 
in reporting substandard and falsified medical 
products. The outputs of the analysis should 
be used to develop optimal business processes 
and workflows within the application. It is 
important to examine substandard and falsified 
reporting procedures and their challenges, 
alongside pharmacovigilance procedures to 
identify potential opportunities for synergy. The 
integration of these processes can enhance their 
mutual effectiveness in reporting. As part of 
this analysis key pharmacovigilance structures, 
systems, procedures and mechanisms within the 
country should be identified. This could include 
the existence of basic infrastructure to enable 
pharmacovigilance operations and reporting, 
policy and regulatory frameworks, and the 
collection, analysis and evaluation of adverse drug 
reactions. 
•	
Data collection tools. Evaluate the current 
data collection tools and processes for 
pharmacovigilance and substandard 
and falsified medical products reporting. 
This typically involves a reporting form 
that gathers all essential information 
on a suspected adverse drug reaction 
or substandard and falsified medical 
product. Various tools are used to deploy 
these reporting forms across countries, 
including electronic or digital forms (such 
as applications or web-based forms), paper-
based forms or a combination of both. 
•	
Digital tools. Conduct a landscape analysis 
to identify existing or previously used 
software applications and other tools related 
to medical products or similar use (e.g. 
pharmacovigilance reporting tools) as well as 
related digital technologies. Identify current 
registry systems such as product registry 
and facility registry and if an interoperability 
layer exists. This inventory aids in identifying 
opportunities for reuse of existing 
applications and the required interoperability 
across systems. In some countries digital tools 
may already be in use for pharmacovigilance 
activities. If digital tools are already in 
use for pharmacovigilance reporting, it is 
advantageous to incorporate substandard 
and falsified reporting into existing 
pharmacovigilance applications. Assess 
how the tools are currently utilized, including 
the information flow from the receipt of 
the report to the integration of the data
