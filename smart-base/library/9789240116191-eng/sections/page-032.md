Instituting data management and governance policies 
23
Institutionalise product master data governance 
policies and procedures.
STEP 4
POTENTIAL RISKS
MITIGATION
The variety and volume 
of data attributes raise 
concerns regarding privacy, 
confidentiality, and data 
sensitivity. These concerns can 
make data sources reluctant to 
share information with multiple 
and diverse stakeholders.
To address these concerns, procedures and systems should be developed to 
categorize data attributes based on their level of sensitivity. Access should then 
be controlled according to these sensitivity categories, ensuring appropriate data 
protection while enabling secure data sharing.

Establish a master data governance group 
composed of all relevant stakeholders to 
provide leadership and guidance in managing 
product master data additions and changes.
•	 The governance group should support 
the overall governance and planning of 
product master data standardization, 
capture, and dissemination activities, and 
provide leadership and oversight for all data 
management efforts.
•	 The master data governance group can 
be integrated into existing governance 
structures or steering committees.
Promote the integration and consistent use 
of the established master data management 
procedures, coding schemes, data definitions, 
data structures, and governance policies 
across all health supply chain processes, 
systems, and organizations.
