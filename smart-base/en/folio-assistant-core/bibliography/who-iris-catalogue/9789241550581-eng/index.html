Abstract
This guideline provides comprehensive, evidence-based recommendations to support countries in delivering high-quality, person-centred HIV testing services (HTS) across diverse populations and settings. Updating and consolidating previous WHO guidance issued in 2015 and 2016, it incorporates new evidence on HIV self-testing, social network-based HIV testing approaches, western blotting, and strategic service delivery to strengthen progress towards global HIV targets. Developed using the GRADE methodology and informed by systematic reviews, modelling studies and expert consultation, the guideline aims to improve the effectiveness, efficiency and quality of HIV testing while promoting timely linkage to prevention, treatment and care. The publication addresses the full continuum of HIV testing services, including demand creation, pre- and post-test services, facility- and community-based testing, implementation considerations for priority populations, strategic planning, diagnostic testing strategies, quality assurance and programme monitoring. It provides updated recommendations on HIV self-testing, provider-assisted referral, social network-based approaches and the use of dual HIV/syphilis rapid diagnostic tests, while emphasizing the WHO 5Cs of HIV testing services: consent, confidentiality, counselling, correct results and connection. Intended for policy-makers, national programme managers, health-care providers and implementing partners, the guideline supports the expansion of equitable, evidence-informed HIV testing services to improve diagnosis and strengthen national HIV responses.
