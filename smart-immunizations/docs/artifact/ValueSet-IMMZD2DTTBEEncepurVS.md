## IMMZD2DTTBEEncepur ValueSet for Decision Table

`ValueSet/IMMZD2DTTBEEncepurVS`

ValueSet IMMZD2DTTBEEncepur for IMMZ.D2.DT.TBE.Encepur. Business rule: Determine if the client is due for a tick-borne encephalitis (TBE) vaccination according to the national immunization schedule Trigger: IMMZ.D2 Determine required vaccination(s) if any Table: Encepur schedule (In areas where the disease is highly endemic, implying that there is a high individual risk of infection, WHO recommends that vaccination be offered to all age groups, including children )
