Four lanes, because four different kinds of actor are involved and conflating
them is how accountability gets lost:

| lane | who | owns |
|---|---|---|
| Author / guideline developer | person | the PICO, the appraisal, the grade |
| Evidence agent | system | retrieval across the three classes |
| Trusted registries and observatories | **external** | whether a citation resolves |
| Work plan — beans | shared | every gap and every unverified citation |

The third lane is external on purpose. **Authority is not something the folio
can assert about itself**; it is a fact resolved at the publisher. Drawing that
lane inside the system boundary would make "authoritative" a self-assessment.
