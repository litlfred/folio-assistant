It runs inside the editing loop, between claiming the bean and drafting the
change — see [Editing and the HCI validation
gate](publication-workflow.html#editing-and-the-hci-validation-gate). That
position is the whole point: **the evidence is gathered before the sentence is
written, not assembled afterwards to support one.**

A recommendation drafted first and evidenced second is a literature search for
confirmation. The step order is what makes it not that.

It is a *call activity*, so it has its own file, its own lanes and its own
diagram, and the editing diagram stays readable. It also has nothing to do for
content that carries no recommendations — a mathematics folio has none — and is
skipped there rather than satisfied vacuously.
