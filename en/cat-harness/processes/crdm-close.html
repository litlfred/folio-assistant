CRDM close-out

Process_CRDM_Close · strict · 5 step(s)

The stakeholders sign off, the BA confirms every criterion is met, the feature lands on main through the merge-to-main sub-process, and only then does the agent close the issue. Never the other way round: an agent must never assume completion.

How it connects

  • Called by: CRDM requirements
  • Calls: Adjudication, Merge to main

Land the feature’s PRs
Call_MergeToMain		Agentcalls Merge to main
merge-to-main		Phase 6’s “explicit confirmation before merging to main”, made a step that can refuse. Once per pull request the feature still has open, after the sign-off is recorded and before the issue is closed: an issue is not closed on a feature that is not on main. merge-to-main.bpmn reads each head’s evidence live, establishes that the owner authorised THAT merge (a stakeholder sign-off or the BA’s confirmation is acceptance of the feature, not authorisation to merge it, unless the owner’s own words say so), records the authorisation, merges, and confirms the base. A PR the owner holds or refuses stays open, and A_Close then waits: the BA’s authorisation to close does not cover a feature that did not land. An increment the owner asked to land earlier in Phase 6 went through the same sub-process at that time.
