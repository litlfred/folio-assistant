J. Issue a Verifiable Digital Health Certificate (shared block B4)

PHW.J · strict (defaulted) · 7 step(s)

Shared block B4 of the BPMN reuse proposal (Q-BPMN-1 A, 2026-11-01): one issuing process for every certificate type. The certificate profile is a data input (a logical model canonical, for example the DDCC core data set in who.ddcc, the ICVP model in smart-icvp, or DVCMin in this IG), so a trust domain or jurisdiction calls this process instead of copying its steps. Steps are taken from smart-icvp’s ‘Issue Digital ICVP Card from EIR Data’, ‘Immunize & Issue Digital ICVP Card’ and ‘Digitize Existing Physical ICVP Card’ diagrams, which share them. The issuer’s signing key is registered with the trust anchor beforehand (smart-trust onboarding; [ITI-YY1] in process B). Call it from another IG with bpmn:import namespace http://smart.who.int/trust-phw/bpmn and calledElement PHW.J (Q-BPMN-2 A).

Lanes — who acts

lanerolewhat it does here
Certificate Holder (person / Personal Health Wallet)—The person the certificate is about, and the wallet that keeps it. smart-icvp: ‘Traveller’.
Certificate Issuer (health worker or issuing system)—The health worker or system that records the health event and asks for issuance. smart-icvp: ‘Actor’ and ‘National Health Authority’.
Certificate Issuance Service—Signs the certificate with a key whose public part the trust anchor publishes. smart-icvp: ‘Certificate Issuance Service’.

Add Consent Directives to a Shared or Issued DocumentOptional (owner, 2026-11-07): before the certificate is signed, process M adds the consent directives it is issued under, explicit (PHWExplicitConsent, recorded by process L) or implied (PHWImpliedConsent, the trust domain’s default policy), as consent entries listed in the Advance Directives section. The copy in the certificate is evidence for a receiver; the Consent Registry copy is the one enforced.
