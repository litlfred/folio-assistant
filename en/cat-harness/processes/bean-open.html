Open the bean

Process_BeanOpen · strict · 4 step(s)

The opening half of every bean’s life: check before you create, find out whose it is, and claim it before any work starts — or leave it alone when it is someone else’s. Called, never run on its own: the caller is the process about to do the work, and it calls this where the bean becomes its own.

The caller’s own gateway reads which end this reached. Claimed means the bean is in progress and the caller works it; not yours means a sibling holds it, nothing was written, and the caller stops. Process_BeanClose is the other half, called before each end the caller reaches after a claim.
