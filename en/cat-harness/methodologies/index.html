The three states are different facts and are kept apart deliberately. Source held means an evidence: reference resolves to a document in a declared library — you can open it from this checkout. Cited, not ingested means the node names an origin and nobody has fetched it; that is an open question, reported by check:methodology-evidence and gated by nothing.
