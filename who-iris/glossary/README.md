# glossary

The WHO glossary: folio-glossary/v1 schemes of terms the WHO publications use under more than one name -- a HOUSE rule about wording, like the style-guide voices under skills/voices/ (bean ftu0). An alternative label is recorded only where a publication STATES the equivalence, and each term cites that sentence in this instance's own library; a count or a latent-semantic cosine is never the evidence. The scheme's source is the who-guideline-development voice, so its terms are minted in this instance's namespace. Rendered on the glossary/ page and checked by check:glossary, both of which read every instance's declared glossary directory.

Part of [WHO IRIS](../README.md) 0.1.0, declared as `glossary`, holding `glossary`.
