# who-iris-scripts

`gen-iris-pages.ts` and its tests -- the IRIS-SPECIFIC code: the replica of iris.who.int, themed as IRIS. The generic catalogue checker and cover generator moved to folio-assistant-core/scripts/ on 2026-09-30 (bean eayu). `gen-iris-pages.ts` is the instance-local generator `wjfu` cites as the precedent for every other one here (*"instance-local generator, static HTML, committed, gated"*). Cited as a model by another instance's bean while being undeclared itself, which is the sharpest form of the `ylj7` defect: a directory can be the corpus's own worked example and still be invisible to every consumer that walks declarations.

Part of [WHO IRIS](../README.md) 0.1.0, declared as `who-iris-scripts`, holding `code`.
