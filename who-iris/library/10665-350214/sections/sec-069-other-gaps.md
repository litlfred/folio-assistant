The impact of integrating nurturing care interventions 
into service delivery for infants and young children 
exposed to HIV needs to be better understood.  
The impact of caregiver support interventions  may also 
need to be further explored. Finally, more research on 
integrated services is needed, especially to document 
the impact of HIV services when integrated into routine 
child services.
