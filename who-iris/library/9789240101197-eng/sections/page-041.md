Analytics services synthesize the aggregated supply chain data to answer 
questions such as:
•	 Based on consumption patterns, how can we optimize inventory to 
minimize stockouts?
•	 Are the planned supplies sufficient, and will they arrive in time to meet the 
projected demand?
•	 How accurate are the forecasts compared with actual demand/
consumption?
•	 Are products being delivered on time and in full quantities to the right 
locations?
•	 Is there any inventory wastage or leakage, and if so, what are the reasons?
•	 Are any items at risk of expiry, and where could that stock be moved to?
•	 How often and at which facilities are stockouts occurring?
•	 How are the suppliers performing in terms of quality and on-time delivery?
