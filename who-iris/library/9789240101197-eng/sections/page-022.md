Identify and engage all relevant stakeholders
1
Step
Identifying and engaging all relevant stakeholders involves recognizing everyone who has an 
interest or role in the DHSC implementation and actively involving them in the process. This 
ensures that diverse perspectives are considered, fosters collaboration and gains support for 
successful DHSC outcomes.
