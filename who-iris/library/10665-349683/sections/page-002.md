CONTEXT

opportunities to realise sexual and reproductive health and rights for all, including key and vulnerable populations,
and to reimagine and enhance equitable access and quality coverage of SRH services. Delivery of comprehensive SRHR
interventions throughout the life course is an essential component of UHC.
