Create and adopt processes and metrics for monitoring and 
evaluating the effectiveness of the global strategy. This component 
calls for the continuous assessment of whether the global strategy 
on digital health has been true to its purpose and effective in 
supporting countries.
The Secretariat will measure the effectiveness of the global strategy 
from data collected globally and will report findings to 
Member States.
