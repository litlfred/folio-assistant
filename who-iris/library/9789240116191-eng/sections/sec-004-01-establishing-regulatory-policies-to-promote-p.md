policies to promote 
product master data 
standardization
Manufacturers and distributors of health products are the 
source of truth for product master data. Ensuring that 
manufacturers and distributors consistently provide 
product master data in a standardized and uniform format 
is crucial for enabling interoperability and leveraging 
automation through barcode scanning.
Regulatory organizations play a key role in establishing 
policies and legislation that mandate and enforce 
requirements for manufacturers and distributors to supply 
standardized product master data. This chapter outlines the 
steps that Ministries of Health and national drug regulatory 
authorities can take to establish appropriate policies and 
legislation to support and promote the use of standardized 
product master data.
