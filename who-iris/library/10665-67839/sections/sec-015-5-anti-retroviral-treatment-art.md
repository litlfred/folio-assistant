ANTI-RETROVIRAL TREATMENT (ART)
Anti-retroviral treatment 
(ART)
5 
5.1
The Brazilian experience demonstrates that effective AIDS treatment is feasible in a
middle-income country - Brazil’s GNP per capita for 1999 was $4790.  It has been
shown that it can alter the natural history of coinfections, including TB.  Whether the
same results can be repeated in countries with significantly higher HIV prevalence
rates, and lower incomes, remains to be seen.
