5
Key questions
Priority outcomes
3.	 Antenatal anti-D immunoglobulin administration
C.3: For non-sensitized RhD-negative pregnant women (P), is routine prophylactic antenatal anti-D 
administration (I) compared with no anti-D (C) effective for preventing Rhesus alloimmunization (O) 
and improving outcomes?
•	 Maternal alloimmunization
•	 Fetal/newborn morbidity (haemolytic disease of the newborn)
•	 Fetal/newborn mortality
4.	 Preventive anthelminthic treatment
C.4: For pregnant women (P), does prophylactic anthelminthic treatment (I) compared with no 
intervention (C) improve maternal and perinatal outcomes (O)?
•	 Maternal morbidity (anaemia)
•	 Maternal satisfaction 
•	 Fetal/newborn morbidity (SGA, low birth weight, preterm birth)
•	 Fetal/newborn mortality
5.	 Tetanus toxoid vaccination
C.5: For pregnant women (P), does vaccination against tetanus (I) compared with no vaccination (C) 
reduce morbidity and improve outcomes (O)?
•	 Maternal satisfaction
•	 Side-effects
•	 Fetal/newborn morbidity (infection)
•	 Fetal/newborn mortality
