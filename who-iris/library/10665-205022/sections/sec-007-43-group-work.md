Group work 
Participants developed a generic plan for the establishment of a national 
external quality assessment scheme for CD4 T lymphocytes enumeration. The 
draft plan emphasized on responsibility of the National Programme Officer to 
institutionalize national EQAS, development of infrastructure for EQAS, 
training of human resource, logistics for shipment of material with or without 
stabilizers, continuous collaboration between the organizer of the scheme as 
well as the participating units and a continuing endeavour towards quality 
improvement.
