Objectives 
The objectives of the workshop were to: 
(1) Review the status of enumeration of CD4 T lymphocytes in high 
HIV-burden Member countries of the SEA Region; 
(2) Orient on new, economical and field-friendly technologies for CD4 
enumeration; 
(3) Provide hands-on training on selected technologies including the 
interpretation of results, and 
(4) Identify a mechanism for external quality assessment scheme.
