For WHO 
The following actions were recommended for WHO:  
(1) WHO should develop guidelines and standard operating procedure 
for CD4 T lymphocytes enumeration. 
(2) WHO should support the expansion of Regional CD4 T 
lymphocytes EQAS to Sri Lanka, Myanmar, and Nepal in addition to 
India, Indonesia and Thailand where it is already in operation. 
(3) WHO should develop criteria for clinical application of CD4 counts 
in paediatric population.
