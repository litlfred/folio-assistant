PrEP provides an opportunity for regular screening for common STIs, including syphilis, gonorrhoeae and chlamydial 
infections. Where possible, programmes should expand and implement relevant national guidelines on STI screening 
among people starting or taking PrEP.
Additional guidance on STI screening can be found in the WHO manual Laboratory diagnosis of sexually transmitted 
infections, including human immunodeficiency virus (29).
