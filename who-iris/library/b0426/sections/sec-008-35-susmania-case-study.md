Susmania case study 
A case study based on data from a fictional country ‘Susmania’ was 
conducted in order to: 
(1) 
Gain practical experience in filling NHA tables. 
(2) 
Be able to select relevant information from NHA questionnaires. 
Report of Bi-Regional Workshop on National Health Accounts  
Page 6 
Participants were first introduced to the country through a situational 
analysis that focused on health and health financing issues. The practical 
NHA exercise was in three parts and participants worked in groups assisted 
by resource persons. Time was set aside for more elaborate discussions on 
completion of each agreement.
