WHO SEARO is committed to pushing the NHA agenda forward in the 
South East Asia Region. The workshop was an important step in this 
direction and contributed to the development of a critical mass for NHA 
development and advocacy in countries. The workshop also provided the 
opportunity to pilot and adapted global curriculum at the Regional level 
and, also, to identify a set of core Regional resource persons for further 
activities, particularly at the country level.

Importantly, was not regarded as an end of capacity-building but, 
rather, the initiation of an exercise which is to be followed-up by country 
support in institutionalising NHA and, critically, to link evidence to policy.
