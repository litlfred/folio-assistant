Concluding remarks 
Dr Anton Fric made the closing remarks by reiterating the fact that the NHA 
is an important policy tool for health systems development and expressed 
the hope that participants are convinced that NHA is possible and that they 
would take the initiative to advocate for it in their countries. He also 
assured participants of assistance from the Regional Office and support to 
countries that would carry forward this crucial agenda. He thanked the 
participants for their active participation and dynamic involvement 
throughout the workshop.
