The Health System Challenge framework provides an overview of needs and challenges 
faced in health systems that reduce optimal performance. They describe overarching 
problems faced by health systems which can be addressed through implementing digital 
health interventions. Program planners can use them as a starting point to drill down to the 
specific bottlenecks (or pain points) that they seek to improve within a health programme 
process, which then enables the selection of appropriate digital health interventions.
