This option provides follow-up care to the infant for a 
minimum of 8 months after interruption of ART. Where 
possible, both EID (qualitative) and VL (quantitative) tests 
should be performed at 4 weeks, 4 months, and 8 months 
after treatment interruption.

breastfeeding and carry out the national infant testing 
schedule for HIV-exposed infants to ensure appropriate
