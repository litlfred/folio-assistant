The recently introduced weekly smallpox epidemic reporting system has
been established. Ail the 32 Vaccination Inspectors have been instructed to
submit weekly smallpox epidemic reports of their areas regularly, and in time.
Even 'NIL' reports are being insisted upon. The Medical Superintendent of

the 1.D. Hospital, Delhi, is contacted by telephone daily regarding any
admission of a smallpox case. — :
