DDCC:VS core data set guidance laid out in this document may be leveraged to generate artefacts 
conformant with other initiatives such as the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) 
Guidelines on visible digital seals (“VDS-NC”) for travel-related health proofs (16) and the European 
Union (EU) EU Digital COVID Certificate (17). Additional technical details can be found on the DDCC 
vaccination certificate implementation guide available at: WorldHealthOrganization.github.io/ddcc.
