Notes: 
1.	
All indicators should also be disaggregated by facility type (e.g. referral hospital, district hospital, health centre, etc.) and managing 
authority/facility ownership (public, private, NGO, etc.).
2.	
Quality-related indicators are also found in other indicator groups (some may require special data collection methods):
a.	
Mortality: selected mortality indicators, e.g. CFRs, may reflect quality of care in facilities.
b.	
Morbidity: admissions for certain diagnoses (e.g. hypertension, diabetes, chronic lung disease) may reflect inadequate care in 
PHC facilities. Re-admissions for certain diagnoses (e.g. postoperative infections) may reflect inadequate inpatient care.
c.	
Health service resources: availability of appropriate inputs are a prerequisite for quality services.
