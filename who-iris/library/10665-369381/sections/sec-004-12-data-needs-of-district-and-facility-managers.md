District and facility managers are responsible for ensuring that the health services under their management are 
appropriately delivered. This may include ensuring that:  
	y	
district health services are in line with national and subnational policies, priorities and standards;
	y	
district health services quickly detect and respond to unusual events and changing needs; 
	y	
all segments of the district population have access to the health services they need, at the required standards 
of quality;
	y	
district health services achieve required targets; and 
	y	
the resources needed to provide the services are available, equitably distributed and efficiently used. 
To perform these functions, managers need information. They must have access to the data regularly reported 
through the RHIS and the capacity to analyse and interpret it. To interpret RHIS data within its context, managers 
must also assess this data in relation to information from other sources.
1	 The term “hospital” is used in this document as a generic term to describe all facilities that have inpatient services and that report on admis-
sions, discharges and deaths. It is recognized that the precise naming of facilities may differ among countries.
