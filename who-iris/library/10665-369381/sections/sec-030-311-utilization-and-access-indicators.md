Notes: All indicators should also be disaggregated by facility type (e.g. district hospital, health centre, etc.) and managing authority/
facility ownership (public, private, NGO, etc.).
At facility level, numerator-only indicators (without a population-based denominator) will typically be used.
1	 “Hospital discharge rate” is often used to express the inpatient discharge rate; “discharge rate” is preferred to “admission rate”.
