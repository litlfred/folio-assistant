This operational handbook was prepared by Avinash Kanchar with inputs from Dennis Falzon 
and Matteo Zignol under the overall direction of Tereza Kasaeva, Director of the WHO Global TB 
Programme. The WHO Global TB Programme gratefully acknowledges the contributions of all experts 
and reviewers involved in the production of the latest update of the WHO guidelines on TB preventive 
treatment on which this handbook is based, as well as other contributors.
