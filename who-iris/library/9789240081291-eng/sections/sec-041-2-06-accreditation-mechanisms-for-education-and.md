training institutions and their programmes 
Indicator name
Existence of national and/or subnational mechanisms for accreditation of health workforce  
education and training institutions and their programmes (Yes/Partial/No)
Disaggregation
By health workforce education and training programme
Definition
The following questions should guide a response to this indicator: 
•	 	Have national and/or subnational mechanisms for accreditation of health workforce education  
and training institutions and their programmes been established? 
•	 	Are national and/or subnational mechanisms for accreditation of health workforce education  
and training institutions and their programmes compulsory?
•	 	Are there national and/or subnational mechanisms for accreditation of health workforce  
education and training institutions and their programmes that are not compulsory?
•	 	If established, do national and/or subnational mechanisms for accreditation of health workforce 
education and training institutions and their programmes take into account national education 
plans for the health workforce?
Glossary
•	 	Accreditation
•	 	Health workforce education and training programme
•	 	Health workforce education and training institution
