Retention
The progress in reducing subnational inequities mentioned above relies on the capacity of 
countries to retain HCWs in rural, remote and other underserved areas. Retention challenges 
can be observed in the HLM when a misalignment exists with two phenomena occurring at the 
same time: unemployment of HCWs while several jobs are available and posts remain vacant. 
Incentives are sometimes used as a mechanism to improve retention of HCWs in rural or hard-
to-reach areas.
