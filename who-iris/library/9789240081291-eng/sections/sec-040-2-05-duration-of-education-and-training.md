Indicator name
Duration of health workforce education and training programmes, in years
Disaggregation
By health workforce education and training programme 
Definition
Duration of health workforce education and training is the number of years required to complete  
a full curriculum for each health workforce education and training programme. This duration does  
not include specializations or time to obtain additional, optional, certifications.
