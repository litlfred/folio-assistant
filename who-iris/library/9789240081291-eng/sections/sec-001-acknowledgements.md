The National Health Workforce Accounts (NHWA) Handbook was developed under the direction of the World 
Health Organization (WHO) Health Workforce Department to support the implementation of the Global Strategy 
on Human Resources for Health: Workforce 2030.
The development of the NHWA Handbook version 2 was coordinated by the Data, Evidence and Knowledge 
Management unit of the Health Workforce Department: Mourtala Abdou Illou, Mathieu Boniol, Khassoum Diallo, 
Teena Kunjumen, Maria Sarah Nabaggala, Tapas Sadasivan Nair, Amani Siyam. Special thanks to WHO colleagues
