Indicator name
Percentage of active health workers by sex
Numerator
Number of active health workers in sex categories
Denominator
Total number of active male and female health workers, defined in headcounts
Disaggregation
By occupation
Definition
Percentage of active health workers in the given sex category.
Sex group corresponds to male or female health workers.
