Indicator name
Ratio of unfilled posts to total number of funded posts
Numerator
Number of funded full-time posts that have not been filled for at least twelve months
Denominator
Total number of funded full-time posts (filled and unfilled)
Disaggregation
By occupation
Definition
Ratio of unfilled posts to total number of posts.
By limiting the count of unfilled posts for at least twelve months, this indicator enables to
distinguish between a systematic job vacancy situation versus potential rapid turnover of staff.
