Dimension
Education expenditure
Indicator name
Expenditure per graduate of health workforce education and training programme
Numerator
Total expenditure on health workforce education
Denominator
Total number of graduates from health workforce education and training programmes
Disaggregation
By health workforce education and training programme, by health workforce education and
training programme and institution ownership (public/private)
Definition
Expenditure in US$ on health workforce education per graduate.
The disaggregation by programme enables to identify inequities across programmes.
The disaggregation by institution ownership enables to distinguish between publicly
and privately owned education institutions.
Glossary

• Graduate

Annual
