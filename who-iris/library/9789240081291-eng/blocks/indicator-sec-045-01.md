Indicator name
Average entry-level wage and salary excluding social contributions
Disaggregation
By occupation, by occupation and public/private
Definition
Average wage or salary in US$ received by health workers when entering the active health
labour market, excluding social contributions.
Disaggregation by public vs private source is suggested, although often only the source from
the public sector is available.
