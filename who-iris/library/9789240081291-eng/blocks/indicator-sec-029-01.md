Indicator name
Percentage of active health workers by their place of birth
Numerator
Number of active health workers by place of birth
Denominator
Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts
Disaggregation
By occupation
Definition
Percentage of active health workers by their place of birth.
Place of birth is defined as national-born or foreign-born.
This indicator will capture the information on the health workers coming from abroad.
