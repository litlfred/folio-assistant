7 Recommended timings to apply the questionnaire

The questionnaire should, as a minimum, be first applied 4 weeks - 6 weeks after birth, when
the infant is due to visit the health centre for HIV testing. However, it is recommended to
conduct interviews at two-week intervals in the first two months of life, where this is
acceptable and feasible. Then the questionnaire should be applied every month for the first 6
months, and every 3 months after 6 months of age.
