5.3. Clarify stakeholder roles

Stakeholders may be characterized by the role, contribution, level of influence and interest that
each is likely to have in the development of a national eHealth vision. Understanding these
aspects will enable strategic engagement of key groups at the right points in the process

(Figure 7, Table 7).
