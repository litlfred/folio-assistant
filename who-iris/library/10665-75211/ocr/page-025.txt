(Table 4).

© Support and empower required change, implementation

Strategy and Enabling environment Ensure a responsive strategy and plan for the national eHealth

¢ Align financing with priorities; donor, government and private-

Legislation, policy Enabling environment © Adopt national policies and legislation in priority areas; review

* Create a legal and enforcement environment to establish trust
and protection for consumers and industry in eHealth practice

Workforce Enabling environment ¢ Make eHealth knowledge and skills available through internal
expertise, technical cooperation or the private sector.
Build national, regional and specialized networks for eHealth

Establish eHealth education and training programmes for health

Standards and Enabling environment © Introduce standards that enable consistent and accurate
interoperability collection and exchange of health information across health
systems and services.

Infrastructure ICT environment © Form the foundations for electronic information exchange
across geographical and health-sector boundaries. This includes
the physical infrastructure (e.g. networks), core services and
applications that underpin a national eHealth environment.

Services and ICT environment Provide tangible means for enabling services and systems; access

applications to, and exchange and management of information and content.
Users include the general public, patients, providers, insurance,
and others. The means may be supplied by government or
commercially.
