eHealth outcomes What will be achieved or changed through using eHealth, and how will

¢ Improving the information flows within the health sector

eHealth vision High-level statement that describes the strategic benefits and outcomes
for the country in general or for the health system and population
through the strategic changes to health system and services introduced
by eHealth (eHealth outcomes).

National eHealth The national eHealth environment is made up of eHealth components

environment representing the enabling and foundation elements for eHealth as well
as technical capabilities that form together an ‘ecosystem’ for eHealth in
a country.

eHealth components The building blocks of a national eHealth environment, which will allow
the eHealth outcomes to be achieved. They describe what is needed to
be introduced or strengthened to achieve the eHealth vision in terms of:
