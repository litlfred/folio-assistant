information protection for example. They should be noted as they are identified, and considered
when analysing that specific component.

9.5 Standards and interoperability

Objective

The step identifies the eHealth standards and interoperability components required to enable
the consistent and accurate collection and exchange of health information across geographical
and health-sector boundaries (Table 21). Without these components, health information cannot
be collected consistently, will be open to misinterpretation, and will be difficult or impossible to
share due to incompatibilities in data structures and terminologies.
