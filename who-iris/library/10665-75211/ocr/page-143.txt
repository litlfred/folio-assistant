National eHealth Toolkit * Part 2: Developing a national eHealth action plan

Approach

This step requires internal analysis supported by consultation with relevant stakeholders to
explore their views on priorities for the action plan and to seek their guidance regarding the
options for improving alignment with these priorities (Table 9).

Consultation with stakeholders (including potential funders) will reconfirm stakeholder
expectations and assess options for refining the action plan to improve alignment. Consultation
plays an important role in gaining the involvement of stakeholders in the development of the
action plan, thus beginning the process of building their understanding of and support for the

plan.
