method
The Toolkit method is based on experience and lessons from countries that have already 
developed a national strategy (Figure 4). A successful outcome requires effective leadership, a 
well-managed process and stakeholder engagement. The vision is developed through an iterative 
approach, which ensures it is grounded in the current context, yet is not overly constrained.
First, an initial ‘unconstrained’ vision is drafted, based on a review of health system goals, 
challenges and relevant international trends and best practices. Next, the vision is refined, 
based on an analysis of the current eHealth environment, including opportunities and gaps. 
Recommendations are drawn up which reflect priorities and resources, in preparation for Part 
2: the development of an implementation plan. This chapter summarizes the steps. Details are 
provided in Chapters 4–12 and a summary table in Annex E.
