Draft recommendations can be prepared either by the steering group 
before the GDG meets to formulate recommendations, or during the meeting by the GDG itself. Under the leadership of the chair, the GDG reviews 
and discusses the GRADE evidence profiles presented by a representative 
of the systematic review team. In addition, the GDG considers other relevant criteria and evidence as discussed above. If the steering group has 
not drafted the evidence-to-decision tables before the GDG meeting, the 
GDG assesses each of the factors in Table 10.1 at its meeting and completes 
the evidence-to-decision tables. If the steering group has drafted these 
tables, they are reviewed at the meeting and each assessment is confirmed 
by the GDG.
The GDG needs to agree on the direction and the strength of the recommendations. WHO recommendations should be formulated by consensus 
(see Chapter 3). If the GDG is in strong disagreement with respect to the 
strength and direction of a recommendation, a conditional recommendation is appropriate.
