THIRTY-NINTH WORLD HEALTH ASSEMBLY 
FOURTH REPORT OF COMMITTEE A 
During the course of its thirteenth meeting held on 16 May 1986, Committee A decided to 
recommend to the Thirty-ninth World Health Assembly the adoption of the attached resolutions 
relating to the following agenda items: 
25. Rational use of drugs 
21• Infant and young child feeding 
28. WHO activities for the prevention and control of acquired immunodeficiency syndrome 
(report by the Director-General)
