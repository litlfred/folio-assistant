Annual review checklist 
Refer to Section 3.12.2 - Have you included the following items for each pre-
qualified device and for each manufacturing site? 
1. A covering letter specifying the device to which the dossier refers. 
 
2. The annual re-evaluation fee in US dollars. 
 
3. Certified photocopy of your annual license renewal(s) if the current license(s) is/are 
due to expire before the review meeting.  
 
4. Certified photocopy of any additional license(s) that have been issued by a GHTF 
founding member since you made your last submission. 
 
5. Certified photocopies of all other time-expiring documentation that you have 
renewed since you made your last submission, including renewal of your quality 
system certification issued by one of the Certification Bodies listed in Table 2. 
 
6. Details of changes to the manufacturing site since you made your last submission.  
 
7. Details of changes to the manufacturing process since you made your last submission. 
 
8. Details of changes to the product since you made your last submission. 
 
9. Details of all adverse events reported to you during the past year. 
 
10. Details of all device failures reported to you during the past year. 
 
11. Progress report on the resolution of any problems reported to you by the PQS 
Secretariat. 
 
12. A list of other matters that you wish to draw to our attention. 
 
13. A correctly labelled and addressed envelope/package. 
 
 
Reminder:  You must submit one dossier for each device. If the device is 
manufactured at more than one manufacturing site, you must submit one dossier for 
each site.
