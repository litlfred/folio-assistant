Preliminary application checklist 
Refer to Section 3.7 - Have you included the following? 
1. A covering letter. 
 
2. A description of each device that you wish to offer for evaluation. 
 
3. Brief details of each of your manufacturing sites. 
 
4. Confirmation of the production capacity of each of your manufacturing sites. 
 
5. A list of the standards to which each device conforms. 
 
6. A brief description of the licensing situation for each device. 
 
7. Twenty clearly labelled samples of each device. If the device is made at more than 
one site, provide samples from each manufacturing site. 
 
8. A correctly labelled and addressed package. 
 
 
Reminder: Your preliminary application can include information about more than 
one device and more than one manufacturing site.
