1.0 Introduction 
This guideline tells you how to make an application to WHO for the pre-qualification 
of single-use injection device for procurement by UN agencies.  Under the WHO’s 
new PQS system, you may offer devices designed either for preventive or for 
therapeutic purposes, provided they comply with the requirements set out in this 
guideline. The document describes what pre-qualification means, it gives a step-by-
step guide to the pre-qualification process, and it outlines how your pre-qualified 
status can be maintained and also how it can be lost.
