The task of helping countries prepare to engage in this effort is enormous, and WHO is
committed to working with its traditional partners – ministries of health, international
agencies, nongovernmental organizations and people living with HIV/AIDS – to make the
prevention of HIV in infants a reality, especially in the hardest hit developing countries.
