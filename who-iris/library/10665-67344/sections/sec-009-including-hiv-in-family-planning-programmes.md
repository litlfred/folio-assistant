Through the efforts of its Department of Reproductive Health and Research, WHO will
build on its long history of supporting countries to improve the quality and coverage of their
family planning and other reproductive health services. It will also continue to advocate for
a shift in emphasis in family planning programmes, with greater attention being given to
simultaneously preventing infection and pregnancy (dual protection). In some cases, this
will entail greater emphasis being placed on the use of condoms, both male and female,
as a primary family planning method.
