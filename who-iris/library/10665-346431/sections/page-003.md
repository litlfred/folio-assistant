Surveillance performance indicators and targets
a. Rate of discarded cases: at least 2 discarded measles or rubella cases per 100 000 population
b. % cases with adequate laboratory investigation: ≥ 80%
c. % origin of infection known: ≥ 80%
d. Rate of viral detection: ≥ 80%

RVC comments, based on 2017 reporting
The Regional Verification Commission for Measles and Rubella Elimination (RVC) concluded that endemic transmission of 
both measles and rubella remained interrupted in Slovakia in 2017 and confirmed that measles and rubella elimination has 
been sustained. Surveillance performance and sensitivity need to be strengthened and better documented.
