Definition and applicability
Contextual definition:
Employment title is a term that describes the occupation held by 
an employee, and is also referred to as job title.
Applicability: 
Applicable to all individuals in the registry.

Notes:

source documents at the time of record creation. The data 
elements listed above will be used for salary/wages and other 
payment processing.
Examples of employment titles include: pharmacist, laboratory 
technician, radiologist, driver, health promotion officer, community 
health worker.
