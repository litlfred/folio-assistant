Definition and applicability
Contextual definition : 
An electronic image of the person in the registry. The definition also 
applies to an electronic copy of the birth certificate, stored as part of 
the person’s documented evidence of birth. 
Applicability :
Applicable to all individuals in the registry. 
Data representation and notes
Data type:
Image
Suggested format:
JPEG
Additional Notes: 
Photograph 1: Photograph of the individual can be placed as part of 
the record and identity management system. 
Photograph 2: Electronic copy of birth certificate can be placed in the 
field.
