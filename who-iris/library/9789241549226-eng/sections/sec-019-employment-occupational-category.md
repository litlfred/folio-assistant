Minimum Data Set for Health Workforce Registry
Definition and applicability
Contextual definition: 
Employee’s detailed occupation, classified under a broader 
category by the legally hiring authority within a country for the 
purposes of organizing employment.
Applicability: 
Applicable only to individuals in the registry who are employed.
