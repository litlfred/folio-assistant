National health workforce accounts – health workforce levels and trends 2026
30
This chapter presents an analysis of the joint impact of population and HWF ageing. First, 
demographic statistics on global population ageing are presented, followed by those of health 
workers. The last section shows how both these phenomena would result in a HWF gap under 
a hypothesis of maintaining current (as in 2024) workforce to population density for each age 
group.
