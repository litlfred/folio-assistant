The National health workforce accounts: health workforce levels and trends 2026 was 
produced by the  Health Workforce Data and Learning Analytics team, WHO Academy, WHO 
headquarters – Teena Kunjumen, Tapas Sadasivan Nair, Mathieu Boniol, and Lennah Etyang – 
under the leadership of Khassoum Diallo, Unit Head a.i., Health Workforce Policies and Data. 
The WHO GIS Centre for Health produced the maps for the report. 
The following staff from the Health Workforce Policies and Data Unit, WHO Academy, WHO 
headquarters reviewed an earlier draft of the report and provided technical inputs: Giorgio 
Cometto, Michelle McIsaac and Pascal Zurn.
Appreciation is due to the National Health Workforce Accounts (NHWA) focal points – both 
current and former – who have been instrumental in the implementation of the NHWA 
in their respective countries and contributed to the reporting and verification of health 
workforce data (Annex 4).
Appreciation is also due to the current and former WHO staff at country offices (WCOs), 
regional offices (ROs) and headquarters, who have contributed to the implementation and 
quality assurance of the NHWA (Annex 5).
Funding
WHO is grateful to all the donors who have provided financial support for the 
conceptualization, development and implementation of the NHWA since its inception. This 
includes the Governments of France, Germany, Ireland, Japan, Norway, Republic of Korea, 
Spain, United States of America (prior to 20 January 2025), and the UHC Partnership (Belgium, 
Canada, European Union, France, Germany, Ireland, Japan, Luxembourg, United Kingdom of 
Great Britain and Northern Ireland, and WHO). This also includes organizations such as the 
Burdett Trust for Nursing, the Gates Foundation, the Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation, 
and Training Programs in Epidemiology and Public Health Interventions Network.
WHO also gratefully acknowledges the financial support from the Spanish Agency for 
International Development Cooperation and the Ministry of Health and Welfare of the 
Republic of Korea towards the development and publication of this report.
