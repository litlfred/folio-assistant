The density of medical doctors, nursing and midwifery personnel, dentists and pharmacists has 
increased by 52% globally, from 44.8 per 10 000 population reported in 2006 to 67.9 reported in 
2025, with the density of nursing and midwifery personnel increasing by nearly 60% (Table 4). 
Data presented in Table 4 are the estimated densities of the SDG 3.c.1 occupations per 10 000 
population based on the data release for the years 2006, 2012, 2021 and 2025 respectively, and 
hence are to be compared with caution as these estimates were always purely based on the

increased. It can be thus inferred that despite the above-mentioned differences in estimation 
methodologies, HWF density has continued to increase globally since 2006. Considering this 
increase in HWF density, it can be implied that the population has better access to health 
workers and, in turn, health services.
