In order to provide a comprehensive overview of the HWF, most of the quantitative indicators 
in the NHWA are disaggregated by occupation. While the NHWA data platform follows the 
structure outlined in the International Standard Classification of Occupations (ISCO-08), it 
includes selected additional health occupations that are collected as part of another initiative 
(the Joint Data Collection on Non-Monetary Health Care Statistics by OECD, Eurostat and WHO 
Regional Office for Europe) and occupations that are globally relevant but not explicitly listed 
in ISCO-08. The list below provides occupational group titles and corresponding ISCO-08 codes 
useful for monitoring the HWF.
