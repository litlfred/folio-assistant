4. Thematic analysis – population and health workforce ageing
Despite these differences, all economic groups are projected to see a growth in their population 
from 2024 to 2030, and an even more pronounced growth of their population aged 65 years 
and more (Table 5). LICs are projected to see a rapid increase in their population with a 17% 
growth over the next 6 years while HICs are expected to experience a 1% increase during 
the same period. In 2024, HICs had the highest share of population aged 65 years and above, 
representing nearly one out of five inhabitants (compared with one out of 10 globally), and this 
population age group is projected to increase in all regions from 831 million inhabitants in 2024 
to 1003 million inhabitants by 2030 (46).
