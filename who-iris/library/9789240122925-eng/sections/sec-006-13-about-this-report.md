This report is the inaugural edition of an annual series on the levels and trends of the health 
and care workforce, based on the data shared by countries to WHO through the NHWA, and 
is intended to provide a snapshot of the current and past situation at the global and regional 
levels. This report is also intended for the NHWA focal points, as requested by them during the 
assessment of the NHWA, to advance their understanding of the HWF situation and embrace 
the pertinence of their data sharing. 
National health workforce accounts – health workforce levels and trends 2026
6
The report presents (i) the progress on NHWA implementation and HWF data availability 
(Chapter 2), (ii) recent statistics from NHWA on HWF stock, geographical distribution and 
trends (Chapter 3), and (iii) analytics on a selected thematic topic which will differ each year 
(Chapter 4). This year’s selected thematic topic is on the ageing of the population and the HWF 
and their implications.
