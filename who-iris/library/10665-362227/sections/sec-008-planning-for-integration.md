Differentiated service delivery (DSD) is a person-centred approach that simplifies and adapts PrEP and STI services to meet 
clients’ needs and situations (2). DSD can ensure that the health system functions efficiently, enable the health system to 
focus resources on those most in need and, most importantly, enhance client outcomes.
Considering the WHO DSD framework (35) and its adaptation for the provision of PrEP (2), when planning for the integration 
of STI services, public health officials need to decide which STI services will be offered, how they will be offered, who will 
offer them and where they will be offered (Fig. 1).
