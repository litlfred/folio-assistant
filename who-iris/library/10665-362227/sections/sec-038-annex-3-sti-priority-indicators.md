aAll unique individuals who have accessed an HIV service (including STI clinics, PrEP services, key population services, HIV testing services,

bDenominator to reflect country guidelines. For some countries, treatment may be offered to those who are suspected of having syphilis, while

cWHO has treatment guidelines for the management of symptomatic infections related to five syndromes: urethral discharge syndrome, vaginal 
discharge syndrome, lower abdominal pain, genital ulcer disease syndrome and anorectal discharge (2).
