A stepwise approach to the integration of STI services with PrEP services should be considered (Fig. 2). This approach should 
be informed by the local context, including epidemiology, availability of resources and acceptability to users and health care 
workers. When the information is available, the cost-effectiveness of the different levels of STI services provided should be 
considered.
Through this stepwise approach, programmes can transition from clinical management of STIs based on signs and symptoms 
to services based on etiologic management and can focus on enhancing access to STI interventions using various service 
delivery models.
