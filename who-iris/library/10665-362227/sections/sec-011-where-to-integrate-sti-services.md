Currently, programmes offer PrEP through a variety of service delivery models. The STI services selected for integration 
should be feasible for the specific model of PrEP delivery as well as aligned with local resources, laws and regulations. 
Annex 2 also provides examples of different combinations of STI services and where and how they can be offered to people 
who use PrEP.
