Of the 13 (2553 participants) studies that evaluated sputum specimens, sensitivity was not estimable 
for two studies (Figure 14). The sensitivities in the remaining 11 studies (2540 participants) included in 
the meta-analysis were between 53% and 100% and the specificities were between 97% and 100%. The 
summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 95.1% (83.1 to 98.7) and the summary specificity was 98.1% (97.0 to 
98.7). The certainty of evidence for both sensitivity and specificity were graded as “High”.  
For a population of 1000 adults and adolescents with pulmonary tuberculosis where 100 have rifampicin 
resistance on pDST or gDST, 113 would have rifampicin resistance detected on LC-aNAAT and 17 (15%) 
would not have rifampicin resistance (false-positives); 887 would be rifampicin sensitive on LC-aNAAT 
and 5 (0.6%) would have rifampicin resistance (false-negatives).
