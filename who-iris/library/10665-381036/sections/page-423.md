The conventional method, SSM followed by Culture/DST (Smear, Culture, and Sensitivity Testing), was 
established as the baseline for comparison. This approach incurred a cost of $212.90 and resulted in a 
QALY of 24.24. 
 
Next, the study evaluated alternative strategies: 
• 
SSM followed by TB-LAMP and DST costed $237.20 with a QALY of 25.02. The ICER was calculated 
to be $29.93 per additional QALY gained compared to the baseline. 
• 
TB-LAMP followed by DST costed $204.20 and a QALY of 25.17. 
• 
SSM followed by Xpert MTB/RIF incurred a higher cost of $277.60 but yielded a QALY of 24.77. 
However, the ICER was considerably higher at $118.70 per additional QALY gained compared to 
the baseline. 
• 
Only Xpert MTB/RIF resulted in a cost of $218.10 and a QALY of 25.07. With an ICER of $5.94 per 
additional QALY gained compared to the baseline, it emerged as a cost-effective option. 
 
These findings suggest that TB-LAMP followed by DST is not only effective but also cost-saving when 
compared to the standard diagnostic approach. Additionally, utilizing Xpert MTB/RIF alone proves to be a 
cost-effective alternative. These results provide valuable insights for healthcare practitioners and 
policymakers in optimizing TB diagnostic strategies while considering cost-effectiveness.
