Appendix 3: Details of ineligible but relevant RCTs 
Characteristics of excluded but relevant RCTs  
These have been summarised in Appendix Table 1 and 2 below. Marcy 2023 (35) included 
2,570 children aged <5 years in a stepped-wedge cRCT comparing the WHO standard of care 
for severe pneumonia (n=1401) to Xpert Ultra (n=1,169). The trial was conducted in 16 
hospitals across six countries in Africa. In the 12-week follow-up period, 91 (7.8%) children 
died in the Xpert Ultra group and 110 (7.9%) died in the standard care group. Forty-one 
participants in the Xpert Ultra were lost to follow-up (3.5%) and 40 (2.9%) were lost to follow-
up in the control group within 12 weeks. A further nine (0.8%) and 11 (0.8%) participants 
withdrew from the Xpert Ultra and control groups respectively. 
 
Mukoka 2023 (36), conducted a two-by-two factorial RCT in Malawi which included 640 HIV 
clinic outpatients aged ≥18 years. Patients were randomised to one of four interventions: 
Xpert MTB/RIF and no chest x-ray (arm one, n=162), Xpert MTB/RIF with a chest x-ray (arm 
two, n=159), Xpert Ultra with no chest x-ray (arm three, n=159), Xpert Ultra with a chest x-
ray (arm four, n=160). All patients had a TB culture performed. Hospitalisations and deaths 
within 56 days were reported, as were the numbers lost to follow-up. There were eight deaths 
(1.3%) in the whole study, but the number of deaths was not reported by the trial arm. The 
composite outcome of hospitalisation and death was highest in the Xpert MTB/RIF and chest 
x-ray group (11/159, 6.9%) and lowest in the Xpert Ultra and no chest x-ray group (5/159, 
3.1%). Numbers lost to follow-up within 56 days were 10 (6.2%), 8 (5.0%), 6 (3.8%) and 9 
(5.6%) in arms one, two, three and four respectively. 
Leukes 2024 (37)—an ongoing trial in Tanzania and Mozambique (Tuberculosis Close the Gap, 
Increase Access, and Provide Adequate Therapy (TB-CAPT) CORE) employed a pragmatic cRCT 
design to assess the implementation of Molbio Truenat MTB Plus and Truenat MTB-RIF Dx
