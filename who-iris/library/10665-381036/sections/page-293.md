Figure 13: Forest plots of study-specific and summary estimates of difference in accuracy between the parallel use of LC-aNAATs combined versus LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples 
alone for tuberculosis in children that are (a) HIV negative or unknown HIV status and (b) CHIV with presumed pulmonary tuberculosis by a CRS. The black squares represent the 
point estimate of one study, the black horizontal line its 95% confidence interval (CI). The QUADAS-C risk-of-bias judgements by study are included on the right. Note that study-
specific 95% CIs sometimes contain impossible values (difference in sensitivity < 0 and difference in specificity > 0). This is because the method used to estimate standard errors 
does not take the dependence between the two tests into account. Abbreviations: N: Sample size, P: Patient Selection, I: Index Test, R: Reference Standard, FT: Flow and Timing.
