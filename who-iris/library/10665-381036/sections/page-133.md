Explanations 
a. Although the specificity ranged from 50-100%, we could explain some of these inconsistencies based on whether decontaminated specimens were used for 
culture inoculation, which could lead to loss of viable bacteria in the process, thus causing a false negative on the culture and decreasing the specificity.  
b. The very wide 95% CI around false positives may lead to different decisions, depending on which confidence limits are assumed. We downgraded one 
level for imprecision. We downgraded one level only, also keeping in mind that culture is an imperfect reference standard and might lead to a decreased 
specificity.
