Figure 5. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study: lymph node 
tuberculosis in lymph node aspirate 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose lymph node tuberculosis in lymph node aspirate in 
adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of lymph node tuberculosis, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 85.3% (73.4 to 92.4) and 
74.1% (63.5 to 82.5) from nine Xpert Ultra studies (445 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity) and very low certainty (specificity) evidence. 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose lymph node tuberculosis in lymph node aspirate in 
adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of lymph node tuberculosis, against a 
composite reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 71.3% (64.3 to 77.4) and 
97.4% (82.3 to 99.7) from six Xpert Ultra studies (461 participants); moderate certainty 
(sensitivity) and very low certainty (specificity) evidence.
