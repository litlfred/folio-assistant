Figure 12. Risk of bias and applicability summary for pulmonary tuberculosis: gastric aspirate and 
composite reference standard 
Seven studies (1,057 participants) evaluating gastric aspirate against a CRS were included with 
sensitivities between 19% and 83% and specificities 91% to 100% (Figure 13). All seven studies were 
included in the meta-analysis. The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 48.8% (31.4 to 63.7) and the 
summary specificity (95% CI) was 98.7% (97.7 to 99.3) (low-certainty (sensitivity) and high-certainty 
(specificity) evidence). 
For a population of 1,000 children where 100 have tuberculosis on CRS, 67 would be Xpert Ultra 
positive; of these, 49 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (true positives) and 12 would not have 
pulmonary tuberculosis (false positives); 939 would be Xpert Ultra negative; of these 888 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 51 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (false-
negatives).

positive; TN: true negative; TP: true positive.
