STANDARD M10, SD Biosensor, Republic of Korea 
The STANDARD M10 is an in vitro molecular diagnosis system that automatically extracts and 
amplifies nucleic acids (DNA, RNA) from the collected samples, and analyses the data from 
amplification in real-time, by combining the processes of isothermal amplification and real-
time PCR in a single platform using all-in-one cartridge systems. There are two variations 
currently available for TB: STANDARD™ M10 MTB/NTM and STANDARD™ M10 MDR-TB. 
These assays are intended for use with the STANDARD M10 system and provide qualitative 
results within 80 minutes from human normal sputum or sputum sediment specimens. 
 
1. STANDARD™ M10 MTB/NTM assay aids in the simultaneous detection of Mycobacterium 
tuberculosis complex (MTBC) and non-tuberculous mycobacteria (NTM) DNA. 
The possible results are: 
• MTBC positive, NTM negative. 
• MTBC positive, NTM positive. 
• MTBC negative, NTM negative. 
• MTBC negative, NTM positive. 
 
2. STANDARD™ M10 MDR-TB assay aids in the simultaneous detection of M. tuberculosis and 
drug resistance against rifampicin (RIF) and isoniazid (INH). 
The possible results are: 
• MTBC positive, both RIF and INH resistance detected. 
• MTBC positive, RIF resistance detected, INH resistance not detected. 
• MTBC positive, INH resistance detected, RIF resistance not detected. 
• MTBC positive, RIF or INH resistance not detected. 
• MTBC negative.
