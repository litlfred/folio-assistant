Description of the articles found in the primary review (specific TBST)  
 
Eight economic evaluations were conducted from middle-income countries (Russia and Brazil), these mostly 
compared DiaskinTest against PPD-TST (Table A2, supplementary). One study met most of the Drummond’s 
checklist factors and reported all the required information. Steffen et al (2020) 12 studied the cost-effectiveness 
of two novel TBST (Diaskintest and C-TST) for people living with HIV (PLHIV) in Brazil compared to PPD-TST and 
QFT-GIT. A Markov model was used to compare single screening strategies of the respective tests. The primary 
outcome used was incremental cost-effectiveness ratio (ICER) per incremental gain in quality-adjusted life 
years (QALY). 
The rest of the articles came from Russia and were primarily focused on children using Diaskintest as an 
alternative strategy to PPD-TST. Most of these studies included cost-effectiveness analyses using the ICER (per 
case averted or diagnosed) as measure of effectiveness. The type of model used, time horizon, discounting 
rate, and sensitivity analyses were either uncertain or not stated in the majority of these articles which might 
bias the results of the studies 13-17.
