Figure 3. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study: tuberculous 
meningitis in cerebrospinal fluid 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose tuberculous meningitis in cerebrospinal fluid in 
adults with signs and symptoms of tuberculous meningitis, against a microbiological 
reference standard? 
LC-aNAAT summary sensitivity and specificity (95%) were 88.2% (83.7 to 91.6) and 96.0% 
(86.8 to 98.9) based on 16 Xpert Ultra studies (1684 participants); high certainty (sensitivity) 
and moderate certainty (specificity) evidence. 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose tuberculous meningitis in cerebrospinal fluid in 
adults with signs and symptoms of tuberculous meningitis, against a composite reference 
standard? 
LC-aNAAT summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 60.3% (50.9 to 69.0) and 
99.2% (98.1 to 99.7) from 10 Xpert Ultra studies (1397 participants); moderate certainty 
(sensitivity) and high certainty (specificity) evidence.
