Incremental accuracy of parallel use of LC-aNAATs combined versus LC-aNAAT on 
respiratory samples alone based on the microbiological reference standard 
For children without HIV or HIV status unknown, incremental difference in sensitivity for parallel use of 
LC-aNAATs combined versus LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples alone ranged from 0.0% [Hanrahan 2018; 
Orikiriza 2021]  to 14.3% [Hasan 2017] between studies. The difference in specificity for parallel use 
versus LC-aNAAT alone ranged from 0% [Donkeng 2022; Hanrahan 2018; Hasan 2017] to -8.0% ([M. P. 
Nicol 2013]). See Figure 8. The pooled difference in sensitivity was 7.1% (95% CrI 3.2 to 13.4; 95% 
prediction interval (PI) 1.8 to 21.3) and -1.7% (95% CrI -3.8 to -0.6; 95% PI -13.1 to -1.7) for specificity. 
See Figure 9. 
For CHIV, the pooled difference in sensitivity was 4.0% (95% CrI 0.6 to 12.9; 95% prediction interval (PI) 
0.4 to 19.3) and -1.9% (95% CrI -3.9 to -0.7; 95% PI -8.6 to -0.3) for specificity. See Figure 10.
