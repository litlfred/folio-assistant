Explanations 
a. Median prevalence (11%; range 6 to 33%) in the included studies were above the range of the three prevalence

one level for inconsistency.

observed sensitivities. We downgraded one level for serious imprecision.

specificities. Of three studies, one study had a wide 95% CI (40%- 100%) for specificity estimation. We downgraded 
one level for serious imprecision.
