Figure 13. Forest plot of pooled difference in sensitivity and specificity (all studies combined) by index test: LF-LAM, LC-aNAAT, and their parallel use.  
The squares represent the pooled sensitivity and specificity, the black horizontal line its 95% credible interval (CrI). The pooled difference in sensitivity and specificity between parallel testing and LC-aNAAT alone 
is indicated by a line connecting two squares. This pooled difference may not correspond to the difference between the pooled single test accuracy estimates (see 3.3.4: Statistical analysis and data synthesis). 
MRS = microbiological reference standard; CRS = composite reference standard.
