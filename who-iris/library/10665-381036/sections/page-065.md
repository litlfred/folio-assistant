Twelve studies (1959 participants) evaluating gastric aspirate against a MRS were included with 
sensitivities between 0% and 100% and specificities between 67% and 100% (Figure 11). All 12 
studies were included in the meta-analysis. The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 69.6% (60.3 to 
77.6) and the summary specificity (95% CI) was 91.0% (82.5 to 95.6) (moderate-certainty (sensitivity 
and specificity) evidence). 
For a population of 1,000 children where 100 have tuberculosis on culture, 151 would be Xpert Ultra 
positive; of these, 70 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (true positives) and 81 would not have 
pulmonary tuberculosis (false positives); 849 would be Xpert Ultra negative; of these 819 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 30 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (false-
negatives).
