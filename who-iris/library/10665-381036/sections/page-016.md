Respiratory specimen types 
Most included studies used sputum for their evaluation of LC-aNAATs. Three studies exclusively 
evaluated Xpert Ultra testing of BAL samples (Chien 2020, Park 2019, Zhang 2021) while one study 
included BAL in addition to sputum samples (Sava 2023).  One study (Penn-Nicholson 2021) pooled 
multiple sputum specimens per participant in the reference laboratory and used unpooled sputum in 
the peripheral labs. Sabi 2018 used the best result from multiple specimens (1 to 4) per participant. Li 
2023 used three sputum samples per person and examined each sample microscopically for acid-fast 
bacilli. The remainder of the three sputum samples were then combined and used for Xpert Ultra and 
liquid culture.
