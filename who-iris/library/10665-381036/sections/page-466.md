Methodological limitations of the studies 
A summary of our assessment for methodological quality can be found in Annex 5. Briefly: 
The two studies30 31 about Xpert Ultra were overall of very good quality having undertaken several steps 
or fairly thorough attempt towards methodological quality across the assessed components. 
Studies about Xpert MTB/RIF or rapid NAATs in general were of overall good quality with more than half 
of them (n=32) having largely undertaken several steps or fairly thorough attempt towards 
methodological quality in the assessed domains. The rest (n=15) mostly undertook a few steps towards 
methodological quality.  
 
Confidence in the review findings 
We identified 19 review findings for all included low complexity NAATs. Of these, we graded 13 as having 
high confidence and 6 as having moderate confidence. Overall, we had high confidence in the review 
findings. Of note, of the 19 review findings, nine also applied to Xpert Ultra. 
A summary of our CERQual26 judgements can be found in Annex 6 and 7. Annex 6 provides judgements 
for evidence on Xpert Ultra. Annex 7 is more detailed and includes evidence for perceptions about Xpert 
MTB/RIF and Xpert Ultra. It builds on findings of our previous Cochrane qualitative review on low 
complexity NAATs,9 and provides supporting and contextual information for decision making in the face 
of little evidence about Xpert Ultra. 
 
Review findings 
This section begins by presenting summarized evidence on the 19 review findings about all included 
NAATs (Xpert Ultra, Xpert MTB/RIF & rapid NAATs) across three overarching categories (Critical aspects 
users’ value, challenges related to those values and concerns for equity and access). A detailed section of 
explanations of the nine review findings identified from Xpert Ultra studies then follows. Review findings 
for all included NAATs can be found in Annex 7.
