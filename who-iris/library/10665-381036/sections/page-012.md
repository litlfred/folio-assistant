Assessment of certainty of the evidence 
Certainty of the evidence was assessed using the GRADE approach for reviews of diagnostic studies 
(Schünemann 2020a; Schünemann 2020b, Mustafa 2024). There are five GRADE domains (referred to 
as factors in GRADEpro) that may decrease the Certainty of the Evidence (CoE). These domains are: 
Risk of bias, Indirectness, Inconsistency, Imprecision, and Publication Bias. Judgments about sensitivity 
and specificity were made separately.  
 
Results 
Search Results: Xpert Ultra 
We identified 1267 research reports for screening from searches of electronic sources. After removing 
duplicate reports (n = 746), we screened the titles and abstracts of 521 unique articles and excluded 
408. Of the remaining 113 reports, three were not retrievable. We performed full-text screening of 
110 reports and excluded a further 79 reports for various reasons (Figure 1). A WHO Public Call for 
diagnostic studies was released on November 30, 2023, to identify unpublished studies that might 
inform this systematic review. Through this we reviewed 8 studies, of which 2 were identified for 
inclusion.  
Studies (i.e., a scientific investigation with unique participants) may be reported in more than one 
publication or in more than one source. We found that among our included reports, two included 
findings from a single study (Nicol 2018, Zar 2019; listed in Table 1 under Zar 2019). Of the remaining 
31 reports (i.e., excluding Nicol 2018 and Zar 2019), one report contributed two studies based on 
inclusion of cross-sectional and prospective cohorts (Mishra 2020a, Mishra 2020b). Thus, there are 33 
reports and 33 studies.
