Author’s conclusions 
 
Regarding positioning tNGS as the first test for drug resistance for patients (PICO 1):  
 
Pooled sensitivity to first line drugs was 88%-96%, with the highest estimate for isoniazid 
and the lowest for pyrazinamide. This is similar to the results obtained from WGS 
sequencing from culture,(10) a routine diagnostic approach already adopted by a number of 
countries. Sensitivity for the flouroquinolones was 94%-96%, which is comparable or higher 
than some results obtained from WGS from culture. This may be because tNGS better 
detects low frequency resistance alleles.(14)
