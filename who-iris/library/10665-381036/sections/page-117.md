Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose pericardial tuberculosis in pericardial fluid in adults 
and adolescents with signs and symptoms of pericardial tuberculosis, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
LC-aNAAT summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 84.0% (73.9 to 90.7) and 
86.6% (79.5 to 91.5) from three Xpert Ultra studies (202 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity and specificity) evidence.
