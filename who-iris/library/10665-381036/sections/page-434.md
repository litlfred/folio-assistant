PLHIV in South Africa using Ultra & LAM or Xpert & LAM compared with stand-alone testing. Further cost-
effectiveness studies across diverse settings and populations would be helpful to strengthen the evidence-
base and to demonstrate for the cost-effectiveness of parallel testing.
