setting, and combined use was the most cost-effective option. The authors concluded that adopting these 
technologies can help low-burden countries meet the WHO End TB Strategy milestones.  
 
TrueNat cost-effectiveness results (n=1) 
One study assessed the cost-effectiveness of TrueNat. In the study by Bhatia et al., 2023, the cost of 
TrueNat testing, derived from a previous analysis, was $16.61 per patient tested (53). The results of the 
study showed that TrueNat is more cost-effective than Xpert. The ICER of TrueNat in relation to Xpert 
MTB was $94.14/DALY averted, and compared to smear microscopy, the ICER was $86.99/DALY averted.
