Index tests  
The individual LC-aNAATs are: 
● GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra;  
● Truenat (Truenat MTB Plus and Truenat MTB‐RIF Dx);  
● STANDARD M10, SD Biosensor;  
● IRON-qPCR, BIONEER 
For ease of reference, we will refer to GeneXpert MTB/RIF Ultra as Xpert Ultra and the two Truenat 
assays as Truenat except where necessary to refer to a specific assay. 
Xpert Ultra, Cepheid, USA 
Xpert Ultra is a NAAT that functions as an automated closed system that performs real-time 
polymerase chain reaction (PCR). Xpert Ultra can be used for diagnosing TB and RR-TB. Xpert Ultra can 
be performed by operators with minimal technical expertise. Index test results are automatically 
generated, and the user is provided with a printable test result as follows. 
·         MTB (M tuberculosis) DETECTED HIGH; RIF (rifampicin) Resistance DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED MEDIUM; RIF Resistance DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED LOW; RIF Resistance DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED VERY LOW; RIF Resistance DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED HIGH; RIF Resistance NOT DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED MEDIUM; RIF Resistance NOT DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED LOW; RIF Resistance NOT DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED VERY LOW; RIF Resistance NOT DETECTED. 
·         MTB DETECTED HIGH; RIF Resistance INDETERMINATE. 
·         MTB DETECTED MEDIUM; RIF Resistance INDETERMINATE. 
·         MTB DETECTED LOW; RIF Resistance INDETERMINATE. 
·         MTB DETECTED VERY LOW; RIF Resistance INDETERMINATE. 
·         MTB Trace DETECTED; RIF Resistance INDETERMINATE. 
·         MTB NOT DETECTED 
·         INVALID (the presence or absence of MTB cannot be determined). 
·         ERROR (the presence or absence of MTB cannot be determined). 
·         NO RESULT (the presence or absence of MTB cannot be determined).  
Xpert Ultra has five semi-quantitative grades based on the lowest cycle threshold (Ct) value from the 
four rpoB probes: trace positive, very low, low, medium, and high. Trace corresponds to the lowest 
bacterial burden for detection of M. tuberculosis (Chakravorty 2017). In this systematic review we 
will consider a trace result to indicate MTB DETECTED. However, rifampicin resistance results will be 
unavailable for participants with trace results because the trace sample is always reported as 
‘INDETERMINATE’ for rifampicin resistance (WHO 2017). 
Truenat MTB Plus and MTB-RIF Dx assays, Molbio Diagnostics, India 
Truenat assays, developed by Molbio diagnostics in Bangalore, India, include Truenat MTB Plus and 
Truenat MTB-RIF Dx. The Truenat and Xpert assays can both detect dead and live bacilli in the test 
sample. Truenat assay performance is similar to that of Xpert assays (Penn-Nicholson 2021). Truenat 
MTB Plus targets the multicopy genes (nrdZ and IS6110) for identifying M. tuberculosis. Truenat MTB 
Plus has four semi-quantitative grades (high, medium, low, and very low) based on colony forming 
units (CFUs) but does not specify the actual count. Truenat MTB-RIF Dx targets the rpoB gene (RNA 
polymerase gene's beta subunit) for detecting rifampicin resistance.
