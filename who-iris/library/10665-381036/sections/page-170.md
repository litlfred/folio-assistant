LC-mNAATs (TB-LAMP, Eiken, Japan) 
TB‐LAMP (tuberculosis loop‐mediated isothermal amplification) is a PCR test that can be used 
as a replacement for smear microscopy for diagnosing TB in resource‐limited settings because 
it requires the same biosafety measures and infrastructure as smear microscopy (16). TB-
LAMP has a rapid turnaround time, resistance to sample matrix inhibitors, high product yield, 
and low cost. TB‐LAMP was reported to have higher sensitivity and lower specificity than 
smear microscopy amongst children, where induced sputum, gastric aspirate, or 
bronchoalveolar lavage fluid was used for the diagnosis of TB (17, 18). Similarly, various 
studies of adults with TB have demonstrated TB‐LAMP to have higher sensitivity and 
specificity than smear microscopy (19, 20). TB-LAMP gives results as negative, positive, or 
indeterminate/invalid according to manufacturer recommendations. TB-LAMP does not 
provide any information on rifampicin resistance or resistance to other TB drugs. 
 
MC-aNAATs (FluoroType XDR-TB assay, Hain Life Science, Germany) 
The FluoroType XDR assay is a qualitative in vitro test for automated detection of the M. 
tuberculosis complex and its resistance to FQ, amikacin (AMK), ethambutol (EMB) and 
linezolid (LZD) from sputum specimens and cultivated samples. Resistance to FQ is done 
through the detection of specific mutations in gyrA and gyrB, while AMK resistance detection 
is done by the detection of specific mutations in rrs. Detection of EMB and LZD resistance is 
done through the identification of specific mutations in embB, rplC and rrl, respectively.
