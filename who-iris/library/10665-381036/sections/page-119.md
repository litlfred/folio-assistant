Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose disseminated tuberculosis in blood in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of disseminated tuberculosis, against a microbiological 
reference standard? 
LC-aNAAT sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 38% and 98.0% from one Xpert Ultra 
study (578 participants); low certainty (sensitivity and specificity) evidence.  
 
Additional analyses 
 
Detection of tuberculous meningitis in adults and adolescents living with 
HIV 
Study estimates of sensitivity and specificity are presented in Figure 21. 
Should LC-aNAAT on cerebrospinal fluid be used to diagnose tuberculous meningitis in 
PLHIV with signs and symptoms of tuberculous meningitis, against a microbiological 
reference standard? 
LC-aNAAT summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 88.5% (76.6 to 94.7) and 
90.6% (86.9 to 93.3) from three Xpert Ultra studies (381 participants); moderate certainty 
(sensitivity) and high certainty (specificity) evidence.
