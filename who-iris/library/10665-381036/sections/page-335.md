3.1.3 Index tests 
The principal focus of this review was to estimate the incremental accuracy of the parallel use of 
respiratory LC-aNAAT and urinary LF-LAM. Here, we define parallel use as follows: both tests are 
conducted simultaneously on the same person, and a positive result on either test implies a positive 
result for the combination. In clinical practice, urinary LF-LAM may be performed first, and the LF-
LAM result is known to those interpreting the LC-aNAAT (though a LC-aNAAT result will often be an 
objective, automatically generated result). Other index tests are urinary LF-LAM alone and 
respiratory LC-aNAAT alone.  
For urinary LF-LAM, at the time of writing, the only commercially available test is Determine TB LAM 
Ag (Abbott, Chicago, IL, USA). We considered Determine TB LAM Ag test results at grade 1 and above 
as positive (on current manufacturer scale of 1 to 4). For studies that used the prior reference scale 
card with band intensities graded on a scale of 1 to 5, we considered the test to be positive at grade 
2 and above, corresponding to the current recommended positivity threshold. We excluded studies 
that did not use a positivity threshold corresponding to the manufacturer's recommendations. The 
SILVAMP TB LAM (Fujifilm, Tokyo, Japan) has been evaluated in several studies [Broger 2019; 
Bjerrum 2020; Huerga 2023b], but demonstrated significant lot-to-lot variability in a large multisite 
clinical study, resulting in the test not being commercialized and not made available for clinical use 
[Székely 2022; Huerga 2023b]. We did not include studies on SILVAMP TB LAM.  
For LC-aNAATs include, at the time of writing, Xpert MTB/RIF and Xpert Ultra, Truenat MTB and MTB 
Plus, STANDARD M10 and IRON-qPCR. LC-aNAATs were eligible for inclusion when performed on 
respiratory samples.  
For the LC-aNAATs in general, results are automatically generated i.e. “DETECTED” for a positive 
result or “NOT DETECTED” for a negative result and it will be indicated if the test result is invalid. In 
the example of Xpert MTB/RIF and Xpert Ultra, the user is provided with a printable test result as 
follows: 
1. MTB (M. tuberculosis) DETECTED; Rif (rifampicin) resistance DETECTED 
2. MTB DETECTED; Rif resistance NOT DETECTED  
3. MTB DETECTED; Rif resistance INDETERMINATE  
4. MTB NOT DETECTED 
5. INVALID/ERROR/NO RESULT (the presence or absence of M. tuberculosis cannot be 
determined). 
Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra incorporates a semi-quantitative classification for results: trace, very low, low, 
moderate, and high. We considered a trace result to mean MTB (M. tuberculosis) DETECTED [World 
Health Organization. 2017].
