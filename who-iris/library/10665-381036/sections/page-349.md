Diagnostic accuracy of parallel use of respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-
LAM based on the microbiological reference standard  
The sensitivity of the parallel use of LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples and LF-LAM on urine ranged 
from 33% to 100% and specificity from 57% to 100%. See Figure 11. The pooled sensitivity of parallel 
testing was 77.5% (95% CrI 73.4 to 81.3) and specificity 89.4% (95% CrI 85.8 to 92.3), (27 studies; 
12,651 participants, 2,368 (18.7%) with tuberculosis).
