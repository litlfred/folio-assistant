Figure 28. Risk of bias and applicability summary for extrapulmonary tuberculosis using lymph node 
tissue or aspirates and a microbiological reference standard  
Two studies (58 participants) were included all with a sensitivity of 100% and specificities between 
33% and 82% (Figure 29). Meta-analysis was not performed due to the small number of cases and 
studies. 
For a population of 1,000 children where 200 have tuberculosis on culture, 342 to 736 would be 
Xpert Ultra positive; of these, 198 to 200  would have lymph node tuberculosis (true positives) and 
144 to 536 would not have lymph node tuberculosis (false positives); 264 to 658  would be Xpert 
Ultra negative; of these264 to 656 would not have lymph node tuberculosis (true negatives) and 0 to 2 
would have lymph node tuberculosis (false-negatives).  
 
Figure 29. Xpert Ultra for extrapulmonary tuberculosis using lymph node and a microbiological 
reference standard. FN: false negative; FP: false positive; TN: true negative; TP: true positive.
