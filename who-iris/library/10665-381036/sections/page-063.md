Figure 8. Risk of bias and applicability summary for pulmonary tuberculosis in CLHIV: sputum 
specimen and composite reference standard  
 
Seven studies (317 participants) evaluating sputum against a CRS in CLHIV were included with 
sensitivities between 0% and 25% and specificities all 100% (Figure 9). All seven studies (317 
participants) were included in the meta-analysis (Figure 9). The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 
18.6% (14.2 to 24.0) and the summary specificity (95% CI) was 100.0% (95.2 to 100.0) (moderate-
certainty (sensitivity and specificity) evidence).
