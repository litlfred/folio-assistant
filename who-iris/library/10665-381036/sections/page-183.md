Findings of the synthesis 
Primary outcomes 
Both studies did not report our primary outcomes of all-cause mortality and cure.  
 
Secondary outcomes 
Both studies did not report LTFU. 
 
Time to TB diagnosis 
Cattamanchi 2021 (32) was the only study to report time to TB diagnosis and calculated an 
adjusted geometric mean ratio (0.49, 95% CI: 0.39−0.62) (Figure 4). The adjusted geometric 
mean ratio suggested that the mean time to TB diagnosis was less for those who followed the 
multicomponent diagnostic strategy (which included Xpert Ultra), compared to routine care 
(smear microscopy).

Time to TB treatment 
Both studies reported the time to TB treatment initiation (32) (32, 33, 35). Cattamanchi 2021 
reported time to treatment as an adjusted geometric mean ratio (Figure 5) (32). The adjusted 
geometric mean ratio suggests that the mean time to TB treatment was less for those who 
followed the multicomponent diagnostic strategy (which included Xpert Ultra), compared to 
routine care (0.35, 95% CI: 0.21−0.56).
