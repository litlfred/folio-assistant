Xpert cost analyses (n=12) 
 
Twelve studies assessed the cost per test of Xpert (54–65). Most of these studies (46%) were done in 
lower-middle-income countries. Most studies (92%) were done from a provider perspective, and only 
one was done from a societal perspective. The studies were conducted in various settings, and Xpert 
was compared to several different comparators. Table 4 outlines the study characteristics of the 12 
costing studies with details on the country of analysis, study population, type of TB disease, study 
setting, intervention, comparator, and analysis perspective.  
 
Of note, the costing studies took a heterogenous approach in terms of which cost components were 
included in the unit test cost estimates (i.e.: consumables, equipment, human resources, overhead, 
transport etc.) The cost components included in each of the 12 costing studies is outlines in Table 5.   
Most costing studies included human resources, equipment, and consumables costs.
