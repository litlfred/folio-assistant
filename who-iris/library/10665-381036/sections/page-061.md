Figure 4. Risk of bias and applicability summary for pulmonary tuberculosis: sputum specimen and 
composite reference standard  
 
Eleven studies (2,824 participants) evaluating sputum against a CRS were included with sensitivities 
between 17% and 63% and specificities all 100% (Figure 5). Ten studies (2,823 participants) were 
included in the meta-analysis. The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 23.5% (20.3 to 27.0) and the 
summary specificity (95% CI) was 100.0% (99.7 to 100.0) (moderate-certainty (sensitivity and 
specificity) evidence). 
For a population of 1,000 children where 100 have tuberculosis on CRS, 24 would be Xpert Ultra 
positive; of these, 24 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (true positives) and 0 would not have 
pulmonary tuberculosis (false positives); 976 would be Xpert Ultra negative; of these 900 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 76 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (false-
negatives).
