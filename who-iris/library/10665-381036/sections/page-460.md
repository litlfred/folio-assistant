Three review authors (EO, MM and PK) independently performed data extraction in duplicate using the 
Covidence platform.17 We resolved any disagreements by discussion and consensus. The platform for data 
extraction was designed and piloted on at least two studies before use. We extracted data findings in a 
narrative form including themes and supporting quotations. 
 
We extracted data about: 
Study characteristics:  the first author, publication year, journal, language, participant group (type of and 
cadre of user), setting (country, rural/urban, type of health facility). 
 
Intervention: type and category of test, the role of the test, testing strategy or test algorithm involved, 
Program or facility where the test is used, sample collection and transport, health worker involved in 
administering the test, implementation process (workflow), implementation considerations (reports on 
costs, access, equity). 
 
Research methods:  study objective, if study conception was independent of those designing, 
implementing and evaluating the intervention, method of data collection and analysis, framework used, 
and outcomes (reported perspectives, experiences, barriers and facilitators and related themes), 
reporting of ethical clearance, reflexivity and reported conflicts of interest. 
 
Quality assessment 
 
Three review authors (EO, MM and PK), using a predesigned and pretested form, independently assessed 
included studies and resolved any disagreements by discussion and consensus. Team members did not 
assess the methodological quality of any study they had authored. 
 
We used the Evidence for Policy and Practice Information and coordinating Centre tool (EPPI-Centre 
tool)21 to assess the quality or methodological limitations of included studies across five domains. These 
domains included rigor in sampling, rigor in data collection, rigor in data-analysis, extent to which findings 
are grounded in the data and breadth and depth of findings.  Based on a pre-defined criteria availed by 
the EPPI Centre tool, we scored the domains as yes, a fairly thorough attempt was made; yes, several 
steps were taken; yes, a few steps were taken; and no, not at all/can’t tell. We reported our assessments 
in a methodological limitations table. We did not exclude studies due to our assessment of methodological
