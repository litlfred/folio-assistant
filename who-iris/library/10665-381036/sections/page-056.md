Methodological quality 
Studies evaluating Xpert Ultra on sputum specimens (15 studies)  
In the patient selection domain, for MRS, we judged the risk of bias as low for most studies (13/15, 
86.7%) and high for two studies (2/15, 13.3%) because in one study participants with definitive 
tuberculosis were excluded (Liu 2021) and in the other, the sampling method was retrospective and by 
convenience (Enimil 2022). Regarding applicability, we judged low concern for most studies (12/15, 
80%). We judged high concern for two studies (2/15, 13.3%) because participants were enrolled 
exclusively as inpatients in tertiary care centers.  We judged unclear concern for one study (1/15, 
6.7%) because the setting was undefined.  
  
In the index test domain, we judged the risk of bias as low for all studies (100%) because Xpert Ultra 
test results are automatically generated, and the user is provided with printable test results (thus there 
is no room for subjective interpretation of test results) and the threshold for positivity is pre-specified.  
Regarding applicability, we judged low concern for all studies, which used standard protocols for 
Xpert Ultra testing of sputum (100%). 
In the reference standard domain, for MRS, we judged the risk of bias as low for 11/15 studies (73%), 
and unclear for four because it was unclear how many cultures were performed. Regarding 
applicability for studies using culture, we judged low concern for most (13/15, 86.7 %) and unclear 
for two studies because in one study only solid culture was used and in the other, it was unclear if 
culture was performed on sputum or gastric aspirate specimens. For the CRS, we judged the risk of 
bias as low for 4/12 studies (33%); unclear for three studies (25%) because it was unclear if Xpert 
Ultra on respiratory specimens was included in the CRS; and high for 5/12 studies (41.7%), because 
Xpert Ultra on respiratory specimens was included in the CRS.  
 
In the flow and timing domain, for MRS, we judged a low risk of bias for most studies (15/16, 93.8%)
