Results 
Results of the search 
Our search yielded 1,068 articles, including one RCT which was identified from the WHO 
public call. Seven duplicates were removed. We screened 1,061 titles and abstracts and 
excluded 991 studies. We retrieved and screened the full text of the remaining 70 studies 
against the eligibility criteria. We excluded 68 studies due to reasons outlined in Figure 1. A 
list of the excluded studies is provided in Appendix 2 with further details in Appendix 3 about 
three potentially relevant studies excluded because the comparators were ineligible.  Two 
studies met our inclusion criteria, an individual RCT (iRCT) and a cRCT.
