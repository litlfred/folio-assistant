Search Results: STANDARD M10 and other index tests 
We screened 34 titles and abstracts and excluded 32. We assessed the full texts of two articles and 
found none eligible for this review (Figure 3). 
 
Figure 3.  Study flow diagram for STANDARD M10 and other new LC-aNAATs 
 
Characteristics of included studies 
There were 36 included studies: 31 that only evaluated Xpert Ultra (including one study based on two 
reports—Nicol 2018 and Zar 2019—and two studies extracted from a single report—Mishra 2020a & 
2020b), three studies that only evaluated Truenat MTB Plus (Gomathi 2020, Jose 2024, Ngangue 
2022), and two studies that evaluated both tests (Penn-Nicholson 2021, Theron 2024). Key 
characteristics of the studies are described in Table 1 (Xpert Ultra) and Table 2 (Truenat MTB Plus). 
Thirty-four studies assessed LC-aNAATs (Xpert Ultra, Truenat MTB Plus) for detection of PTB using a 
microbiological reference standard. One multicentre study (Penn-Nicholson 2021) compared the 
accuracy of Truenat MTB Plus (multiple countries) and Xpert Ultra (only in Peru). Theron 2024 
evaluated both Xpert Ultra and Truenat MTB Plus in South Africa. 
 
Adolescents (10-14 years) were included in 12 studies, of which 11 evaluated Xpert Ultra and one 
study (Jose 2024) evaluated Truenat MTB Plus. Seven of these studies included adolescents and 
children (this review only included the data from adolescents) and five studies included adolescents 
and adults. Detection of rifampicin resistance was assessed in 11 studies including two studies on 
Truenat MTB-RIF Dx (Gomathi 2020a; Penn Nicholson 2021).
