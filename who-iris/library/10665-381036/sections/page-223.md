Should LC-mNAATs on respiratory samples be used to diagnose smear-negative 
pulmonary TB in adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of pulmonary TB, 
against a microbiological reference standard? 
Twenty-two (11422 participants) studies evaluated sputum specimens. The sensitivities ranged 
between 24% and 100% and the specificities between 74% and 100% (Figure 11). The summary 
sensitivity (95% CI) was 59.9% (50.7 to 68.4) and the summary specificity was 97.9% (96.4 to 98.7). 
 
For a population of 1000 smear-negative adults and adolescents where 100 have pulmonary TB on 
culture, 79 would be LC-mNAAT-positive and 19 (24%) would not have TB (false-positives); 921 would 
be LC-mNAAT-negative and 40 (4%) would have TB (false-negatives).
