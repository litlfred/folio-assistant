Description of the studies 
 
Studies about Xpert Ultra 
These two studies were conducted in Africa (Kenya and Swaziland)30 and Eastern Europe (Belarus),31 
countries identified as high burden TB settings. Mwaura et al was a focused group discussion conducted 
in a TB stakeholder workshop in two countries. Sereda et al conducted interviews among health care 
workers in a hospital. This study reported findings about Xpert MTB/RIF test but stated that all study sites 
utilized MTB/RIF Ultra cartridges.31 These studies included a variety of providers (Clinicians, laboratory 
managers and technicians, TB program coordinators or program officers, and non-governmental 
organization representatives) but few end users (TB patient advocates from local organizations). Mwaura 
et al30 reported that Xpert Ultra was the initial diagnostic test for TB in Kenya and Swaziland  whereas 
Sereda31 only mentioned that Xpert Ultra was the preferred test for presumptive TB patients with COVID-
19.  These studies did not report if settings were urban or rural. No evidence was identified about 
perspectives of parallel testing in these identified studies.
