Assessing the Quality of Evidence:  
The quality of reporting for complete economic evaluation studies included in the review were assessed 
using a modified version of the Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards (CHEERS) 
checklist (18). As some items do not apply to partial economic evaluation or costing studies, we assessed 
these studies using a version of the CHEERS checklist, which focused on appropriate items for assessing 
costing studies.  An evidence profile was included in the results, showing the quality of reporting for each 
study.  The checklists are presented in the Appendix.    
 
Missing Data: 
We contacted study authors for missing or unclear data. We recorded and tracked where data was 
requested, attempts to reach authors, and their responses. If missing data could not be obtained, we used 
the available data and discussed the potential impact of missing information in the report discussion.  
 
Statistical Analysis:  
We performed a narrative summary describing the outcomes of interest for all classes on NAATs included 
in the review. 
 
Heterogeneity and Subgroup Analysis: 
Given the anticipated sparse data and the significant heterogeneity in economic evaluations across 
diverse settings, a meta-analysis of costing and economic data was not performed. However, where 
studies were deemed methodologically similar (i.e.: similar outcomes) we calculated average unit test 
cost and range of unit test costs. 
 
The included studies were also summarized according to general characteristics, including the age of the 
included population (paediatric vs. adult), HIV status, type of TB disease (drug-sensitive vs. drug-resistant 
TB), level of care (outpatient vs. inpatient), and the type of sample tested.
