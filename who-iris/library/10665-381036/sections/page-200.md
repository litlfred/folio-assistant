Findings 
All-cause mortality 
Appendix Figure 1 shows the forest plot for all-cause mortality. Only Marcy 2023 reported all-
cause mortality. Note that raw numbers were not used to calculate risk ratios because 
dividing numbers by the design effect is not an appropriate adjustment for results of stepped-
wedge trials (21). Instead, the adjusted odds ratios as calculated by the study authors are 
presented. In the intention-to-treat analysis, there was no statistical evidence of an effect 
(0.99, 95% CI: 0.60−1.63). In the per-protocol analysis, Xpert Ultra reduced the odds of all-
cause mortality in children compared to the control (0.85, 95% CI: 0.51−1.42).

(N – number of participants included for the outcome; ITT – intention to treat; PP – per 
protocol) 
 
Lost-to-follow-up 
Numbers lost to follow-up were reported in Marcy 2023 (35) but an effect size (accounting 
for the study design) was not estimated so we are unable to illustrate the result on a forest 
plot.  Mukoka 2023 also reported numbers lost to follow-up. 
 
Time to TB treatment 
Marcy 2023 reported hazard ratios for time to TB treatment initiation (35). The adjusted 
hazard ratio comparing time to TB treatment initiation for children randomised to receive 
Xpert Ultra plus WHO standard care for pneumonia compared to standard care was 1.26 (95% 
CI: 0.69−2.30) (Appendix Figure 2). This suggests that Xpert Ultra improves the time to TB 
treatment initiation.
