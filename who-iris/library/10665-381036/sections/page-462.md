Analysis 
 
We used a thematic approach to analyze and synthesize our findings.23 24 Guided by the conceptual model 
and framework of review findings of our previous review,11 we utilized deductive and inductive 
approaches grounded in the data to code and extract themes. EO and MM extracted themes from all 
sampled studies using an excel worksheet adding identified codes and themes across the previously 
identified review findings and adding emerging themes or findings to the framework. The identified codes 
and themes were finalized upon discussion with team members.   
 
We presented our review findings narratively grouping them into three overarching categories; Critical 
aspects users’ value; Challenges reported to realizing those values (feasibility and acceptability concerns) 
and Concerns for access and equity.
