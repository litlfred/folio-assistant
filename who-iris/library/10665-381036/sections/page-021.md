Methodological quality 
Figure 4 summarises the results of the risk of bias and applicability assessment for each of the 
included studies.  
The following studies were assessed as “high” risk of bias and/or applicability concern: 
● Aguilera-Alonso 2022 - Patient selection, applicability concern; Reference standard 
applicability concern based on recruitment of participants exclusively from tertiary referral 
hospitals and lack of speciation of mycobacteria isolated in culture. 
● Jose 2024 - Reference standard, high risk of bias; Patient selection, applicability concern 
based on the reference standard was not blinded and participants were recruited from the 
inpatient setting of a tertiary care hospital.  
● Liu 2021- Patient selection, applicability concern based on inclusion criteria. Clinical 
symptoms were not an inclusion criterion, whereas radiographic abnormalities were.  
● MacLean 2023 - Patient selection, high risk of bias as study enrolled a convenience sample 
and excluded those with recent COVID-19.  
● Mishra 2020b - Patient selection, high risk of bias as the cohort was exclusively people with a 
history of treated TB in the past 2 years.  
● Opota 2019 - Patient selection, applicability concern based on exclusive inclusion of 
hospitalized patients (tertiary hospital) with the goal of determining the need for airborne 
isolation. 
● Park 2019 - Patient selection, applicability concern based on recruitment of inpatients 
exclusively from tertiary referral hospital.  
● Piersimoni 2019 - Patient selection, applicability concern based on recruitment of inpatients 
from tertiary referral hospital (<10% of participants were outpatients)  
● Wang 2019 - Patient selection, high risk of bias; Patient selection, applicability concern based 
on pre-selection for smear-negative participants and exclusive enrolment of inpatients at 
tertiary referral hospital.
