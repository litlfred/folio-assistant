Conclusions 
The use of Xpert Ultra may decrease the time to TB diagnosis and improve the time to TB 
treatment initiation. However, we found that compared to smear microscopy or culture, 
there is limited evidence with moderate certainty of evidence to confidently conclude 
that Xpert Ultra has an impact on patient-important outcomes.
