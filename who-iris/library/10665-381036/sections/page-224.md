Detection of extrapulmonary tuberculosis 
Methodological quality  
Of the three studies included for extrapulmonary TB (Figure 12), we judged two studies to have an 
unclear risk of bias due to a lack of information about blinding to the index test results. One study 
was judged to have an unclear applicability concern in the reference standard domain because of a 
lack of information on speciation.
