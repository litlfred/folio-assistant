Should LC-mNAATs on pleural fluid be used to diagnose pleural TB in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of pleural TB, against a microbiological reference 
standard? 
Two studies (292 participants; 37 cases) assessed the accuracy of LC-mNAATs for detecting pleural 
TB using pleural fluid and a microbiological reference standard (Figure 16). Estimated sensitivities 
were 48% and 75% and estimated specificities were 89% and 96%. Danfack 2024a also provided data 
for pleural TB using pleural biopsy and pleural pus (Figure 16).

Should LC-mNAATs on synovial fluid be used to diagnose bone or joint TB in adults 
and adolescents with signs and symptoms of bone or joint TB, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
One study (5 participants; 1 case) assessed the accuracy of LC-mNAATs for detecting bone or joint TB 
using synovial fluid and a microbiological reference standard (Figure 17). Estimated sensitivity and 
specificity were both 100%.
