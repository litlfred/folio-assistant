Risk of bias assessment 
Two review authors (JO and LW) independently assessed the risk of bias in the included 
studies per outcome. We used the revised Cochrane Risk of Bias Excel tool (RoB 2.0—version 
of 22 August 2019) for individual RCTs (27) and its variant (RoB 2.0—version of 18 March 2021) 
for cRCTs (28). Any disagreements were resolved by discussion between the reviewers or by 
consulting a senior review author (EO) where necessary.  
 
Data analysis and synthesis 
Two review authors (YT and KS) conducted analyses using Stata version 17. We used risk ratios 
(when the 2x2 data were available) odds ratios and hazard ratios or mean geometric ratios to 
summarise binary and time-to-event outcomes respectively. For cRCTs, we presented 
adjusted effect measures as calculated by the study authors if an adjustment of raw numbers 
by dividing by the design effect was not possible.  
 
We were unable to conduct meta-analyses due to heterogeneity in outcomes and populations 
(adults versus children) in the few included studies. Therefore, individual study results were 
presented on forest plots and described narratively. We had planned to assess small study 
effects and publication bias using funnel plots, but we did not do so because less than 10 
studies were included in the review (29).
