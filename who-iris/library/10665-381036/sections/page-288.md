Incremental diagnostic accuracy of parallel use of LC-aNAATs combined versus 
LC-aNAAT on stool alone based on the microbiological reference standard 
In children without HIV or HIV status unknown, incremental difference in sensitivity for parallel use of 
LC-aNAATs versus LC-aNAAT on stool alone ranged from 6.7% [Chabala 2024] to 31.7% [Jaganath 2024] 
between studies. The difference in specificity ranged from 0% [Donkeng 2022; Orikiriza 2021] to -13.0% 
[M. P. Nicol 2013]. See Figure 11.  The pooled difference in sensitivity was 23.5% (95% CrI 5.8 to 40.3; PI 
8.0 to 46.9) and −4.1% (95% CrI −10.5 to 0; 95% PI -25.0 to -0.4) for specificity. See Figure 9. 
In CHIV, the pooled difference in sensitivity was 8.5% (95% CrI 2.4 to 20.9; PI 1.5 to 31.8) and −1.4% 
(95% CrI -3.3 to -0.4; 95% PI -8.5 to -0.2) for specificity. See Figure 10.
