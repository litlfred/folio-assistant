Figure 7. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study: lymph node 
tuberculosis in lymph node tissue 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose lymph node tuberculosis in lymph node tissue in 
adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of lymph node tuberculosis, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 96.5% (84.7 to 99.3) and 
79.4% (65.4 to 88.8) from eight Xpert Ultra studies (578 participants); moderate certainty 
(sensitivity) and very low certainty (specificity) evidence. 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose lymph node tuberculosis in lymph node tissue in 
adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of lymph node tuberculosis, against a 
composite reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 61.5% (47.1 to 74.2) and 
96.7% (91.5 to 98.7) from three Xpert Ultra studies (229 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity and specificity) evidence.
