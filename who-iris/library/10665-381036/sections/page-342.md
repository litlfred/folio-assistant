4. RESULTS 
4.1 Results of the search 
We identified and screened a total of 457 records for inclusion in this systematic review. Of these, 
we assessed 130 full-text reports against our inclusion criteria. We excluded 99 records and provided 
reasons.  
Figure 1 shows the flow of studies in the review. We recorded the excluded studies (n=99) and the 
reasons for their exclusion. We included 31 unique studies: all studies provided data comparing a 
parallel strategy of respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM to any of the tests alone for detection of 
tuberculosis. Of the 31 studies, we could extract data directly from the study publication for two 
studies [J. Peter 2015a; Shah 2014]. On request, authors provided data re-analysed for the purpose 
of this review for 15 studies [Åhsberg 2023; Bjerrum 2015; Boloko 2022; Bonnet 2023; de 
Vasconcellos 2021; Huerga 2023a; Kebede 2021; Liu 2022; Reeve 2023; Shapiro 2022; Spooner 2022; 
Stead 2023; Szekely 2022; Van Hoving 2019] and shared individual participant data for three studies 
[Benjamin 2019; Broger-Cohort4 2020; Gils 2023]. In 11 studies [Calligaro 2017; Garcia 2020; Gupta-
Wright 2018; Huerga 2017a; Huerga 2021; Huerga 2019; Huerga 2020; Kasaro 2020; Lawn 2017b; 
J.G. Peter 2016; Yoon 2019], authors accepted to have summary data re-analysed for the purpose of 
this review based on the individual participant data shared for the meta-analyses published by 
Broger et al. [Broger 2023a].
