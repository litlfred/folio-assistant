Figure 11. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study: pleural tuberculosis in 
pleural tissue 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose pleural tuberculosis in pleural tissue in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of pleural tuberculosis, against a microbiological 
reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs sensitivities were 80% and 100% and specificities were 75% and 86% from two 
Xpert Ultra studies (105 participants); low certainty (sensitivity) and very low certainty 
(specificity) evidence.  
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose pleural tuberculosis in pleural tissue in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of pleural tuberculosis, against a composite reference 
standard? 
LC-aNAATs sensitivities were 54% and 81% and specificities were 94% and 97% from two 
Xpert Ultra studies (105 participants); low certainty (sensitivity) and very low certainty 
(specificity) evidence.
