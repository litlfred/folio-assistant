Outcome measures 
Views, perceptions, experiences, attitudes of providers (health workers, managers or policy makers) and 
recipients (patients).  We identified user perspectives about: 
• 
The use of NAATs/classes of NAATs 
• 
Important uncertainty about or variability in how much people value the main outcomes  
• 
The acceptability of the intervention to key stakeholders 
• 
The feasibility of the intervention to implement 
• 
The impact on health equity
