Results 
 
Identification, screening, eligibility, and inclusion 
 
For the primary search (systematic review 1) on cost-effectiveness of novel skin tests, 367 records were 
identified for full text screening (103 written in English/Chinese, and 264 in Russian) of which only 8 were 
relevant to the research question (only one of those written in English and 7 in Russian) (See PRISMA flow in 
Figure 1). For the secondary search on cost-effectiveness of PPD-TST or IGRA tests, 56 out of 407 records were 
chosen for full-text screening and 29 papers were selected for analysis (see PRISMA flow in Figure 2).
