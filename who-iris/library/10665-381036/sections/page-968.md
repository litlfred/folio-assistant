$10,586.6 for DiaskinTest, being highly cost-effective, compared to TST (ICER=$49,523.9, ICER=$40,641, 
respectively) (Table A2 for studies details). The difference in ICERs between these two DiaskinTest studies is 
that Solodun et al 201716 included costs for chest radiography and additional tests in the costing scheme.
