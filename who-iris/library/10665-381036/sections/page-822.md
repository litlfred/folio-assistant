9. ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS 
 
There are a number of individuals whom we wish to thank for their support in completing this work. 
Firstly, we would like to thank Donna Hesson for her help with designing our search strategy and for 
being an altogether great resource. Donna is one of the Public Health Informationists at Johns Hopkins 
University, Bloomberg School of Public Health. Secondly, we want to thank several members of FIND, 
for providing their technical expertise, as well as for making themselves available to consult whenever 
we had questions. Thirdly, we want to thank the authors from several of the manuscripts that were 
included in our systematic review. Our conversations were instrumental in better understanding their 
methodology, and some of the cost considerations with tNGS.
