5.3 Summary of main results of PICO 2 
___________________________________________________________________________ 
See Appendix 22 and 23. Summary table, comparative accuracy for parallel use of LC-aNAATs 
combined and LF-LAM versus LC-aNAATs combined alone using a MRS, presented for all 
participants, by subgroups, and sensitivity analyses. 
___________________________________________________________________________ 
Based on six studies included in this meta-analysis for a microbiological reference standard (653 
participant, tuberculosis prevalence 6.6% [43/653]), the estimated diagnostic accuracy of the 
parallel use of LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples and stool and LF-LAM is 
• 
Pooled sensitivity: 77.6% (59.9 to 89.6) |Pooled specificity: 83.9% (73.9 to 90.4) 
➔ For a population of 1,000 children where 400 have tuberculosis on culture, 408 would test 
positive using the parallel testing strategy; of these, 311 would have pulmonary 
tuberculosis (true positives) and 97 would not have pulmonary tuberculosis (false 
positives); 529 would test negative by the parallel testing strategy; of these 503 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 89 would have pulmonary tuberculosis 
(false-negatives). 
Compared to LC-aNAATs combined alone, difference in accuracy is: 
• 
Pooled difference in sensitivity: 6.9% (1.5 to 20.1) 
• 
Pooled difference in specificity: -10.2% (-19.6 to -4.9) 
➔ For a population of 1,000 children where 400 have tuberculosis on culture, compared to 
testing with LC-aNAATs combined alone, 89 children would additionally test positive with 
the parallel strategy; of these, 28 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (true positives) and 
61 would not have pulmonary tuberculosis (false positives); compared to testing with LC-
aNAATs combined alone, 89 children less would test negative; of these, 61 would not have 
pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 28 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (false- 
negatives). 
Based on six studies included in this meta-analysis for a composite reference standard (674 
participant, tuberculosis prevalence 42.4% [286/674]), the estimated diagnostic accuracy of the 
parallel use of LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples and stool and LF-LAM is 
• 
Pooled sensitivity: 30.1% (13.2 to 54.9) | Pooled specificity: 83.3% (69.6 to 90.2) 
➔ For a population of 1,000 children where 400 have tuberculosis on culture, 220 would test 
positive using the parallel testing strategy; of these, 120 would have pulmonary 
tuberculosis (true positives) and 100 would not have pulmonary tuberculosis (false 
positives); 780 would test negative by the parallel testing strategy; of these 500 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 280 would have pulmonary tuberculosis 
(false-negatives).
