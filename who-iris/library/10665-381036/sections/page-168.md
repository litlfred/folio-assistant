LC-aNAATs 
Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra, Cepheid, USA 
For ease of reference, we refer to Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra as Xpert Ultra. Xpert Ultra is a NAAT 
that functions as an automated closed system that performs real-time polymerase chain 
reaction (PCR). Xpert Ultra can be used for diagnosing TB and RR-TB. Xpert Ultra can be 
performed by operators with minimal technical expertise (11). For the detection of PTB, a 
Cochrane review reported a summary sensitivity and specificity (95% credible interval (CrI)) 
of Xpert Ultra against culture to be 90.9% (86.2-94.7) and 95.6% (93.0-97.4) based on 7 
studies (2834 participants; high-certainty evidence) (12). For rifampicin resistance detection, 
the summary sensitivity and specificity from the review were 94.9% (95% CrI: 88.9-97.9) and 
99.1% (95% CrI: 97.7-99.8) (5 studies, 921 participants; high-certainty evidence). 
 
Truenat MTB Plus and MTB-RIF Dx assays, Molbio Diagnostics, India 
Truenat assays include Truenat MTB, Truenat MTB Plus, and Truenat MTB‐RIF Dx. However, 
Truenat MTB will be excluded from this class because WHO received official information from 
Molbio Diagnostics in 2024 that the Truenat MTB assay will no longer be sold in the 
international market. Truenat MTB Plus is semi‐quantitative and gives four grades (high, 
medium, low, and very low) based on CFUs but does not specify the actual count. A 
multicentre trial of diagnostic accuracy for the detection of PTB reported sensitivity (95% 
confidence interval (CI) of 80% (75%- 84%) for Truenat MTB Plus (13). Truenat MTB‐RIF Dx 
targets the rpoB gene (RNA polymerase gene’s beta subunit) for detecting rifampicin 
resistance (14, 15). 
 
The possible results for Truenat MTB Plus are listed below. 
• MTB DETECTED 
• MTB NOT DETECTED 
• INVALID 
• ERROR 
The possible results for Truenat MTB‐RIF Dx are listed below. 
• RIF Resistance DETECTED
