Detailed review findings for Xpert Ultra 
Summary of Qualitative Findings table: Findings 1-2, Annex 6 
 
Finding 1: Compared to existing tests/sputum microscopy, healthcare professionals appreciate the 
rapidity and accuracy of Xpert Ultra results, especially in hard to diagnose patients, ability to detect 
drug resistance, as well its benefits outweighing harms (High confidence; Mwaura 2020,30 Sereda 
202231).  
Providers valued the ability of Xpert Ultra in leading to more diagnosis and treatment of patients 
(Sereda 2022).31 More so, its increased sensitivity in detecting TB considering that Xpert MTB/RIF’s 
accuracy in the hard to diagnose patients was suboptimal. They downplayed its reduced specificity 
compared to Xpert MTB/RIF citing that considering benefits vs harms the effects of overtreating TB due 
to false positives not as dire as the consequences of missing TB. Respondents anticipate that the short 
turnaround times for Xpert Ultra would add to its robustness in weathering power outages.30 
“In the lab with each test we have a measure of uncertainty . . .so to me, it is just as the normal routine 
like all other tests that we do. So having [the sensitivity] being so nice, we go for it.” … “yeah, we accept 
it because we know most of our people will benefit from it.” (Mwaura 2020)  
“As a practitioner, I would support a more differentiated approach. We do need to conduct TB screening 
among patients with COVID-19, that’s without doubt” (Sereda 2022) 
 
Finding 2: Existing GeneXpert platform aids in implementation of new NAATs and integration of 
testing for other diseases such as COVID-19 (Moderate confidence; Sereda 202231) 
The presence of existing resources and platforms was appreciated in easing the introduction and use of 
Xpert Ultra, especially for diagnosing other diseases, limiting need for additional funding and training of 
health workers. 
“...TB hospitals do not need additional training to implement Xpert MTB/RIF [Ultra] testing among 
patients with COVID-19. There is no need for additional funding. With this intervention, we will use 
resources more efficiently.” (Representative of the National TB Programme) (Sereda 2022) 
 
 
Challenges reported to realizing those values 
 
Summary  
Findings on Xpert MTB/RIF demonstrated health system challenges including underutilization and delays 
in the diagnostic pathway due to poor sample quality, insufficient resources and maintenance, lack of 
functional data connectivity systems or record systems, inefficient patient flows, unavailability of 
updated clinical guidelines and poor ownership and accountability of the tests by health facilities. 
Overreliance on test results rather than clinical judgement and lack of data driven implementation
