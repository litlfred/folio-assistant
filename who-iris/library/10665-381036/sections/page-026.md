Description of studies with lower specificity:  
Mishra 2020b: Participants were exclusively persons with a history of treated TB in the past two years. 
Likely, many of these participants would have had residual DNA from M. tuberculosis leading to a 
positive NAAT and negative TB culture.  
 
Wang 2019: This study had a high rate of Xpert Ultra positive/culture-negative results. Participants with 
suspected PTB had one sputum that underwent MGIT testing. This study exclusively recruited persons 
who were smear-negative and 50% reported a prior history of tuberculosis. The high number of 
participants with a prior history of TB (time since prior episode not reported), may have led to false 
positive NAAT results.
