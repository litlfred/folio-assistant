Comparative accuracy in children with advanced HIV disease 
We identified three studies that reported data for children with advanced HIV disease involving 272 
participants, 19 (7.0%) with tuberculosis. The sensitivity estimates of the parallel use of LC-aNAATs 
combined and LF-LAM ranged from 60.0% [Olbrich 2024] to 100% [Jaganath 2024] and specificity 
estimates ranged from 58.5% [Jaganath 2024] to 91.7% [LaCourse 2018]. 
Comparative accuracy stratified by CHIV with CD 4 ≤200/microliter 
We identified four studies that reported data for children with advanced HIV disease involving 164 
participants, 14 (8.5%) with tuberculosis. The sensitivity estimates of the parallel use of LC-aNAATs 
combined and LF-LAM ranged from 60.0% [Olbrich 2024] to 100% [Jaganath 2024] and specificity 
estimates ranged from 58.5% [Jaganath 2024] to 95.0% [LaCourse 2018].
