3.3.3. Cost Estimates and sensitivity analysis: 
In the base case the per-test cost of was $22.48 (range: $16.74 - $49.40) for LT NAAT and $18.66 (range: 
$11.66 - $45.40) for HT NAAT after adjusting for inflation (Table 10). In this study the cost of equipment 
was fixed at $100,000 with test kit price of $13 and $9 for LT and HT NAAT respectively and batch size of 
24 patient samples for each NAAT. The main difference for the per-test cost is attributable to differences 
in the test kit cost. 
 
In sensitivity analysis, offering testing service 6 days was more cost-effective than once weekly testing. 
Equipment price, durability, and TB-specific workload significantly impacted per-test costs. Analysis 
showed per-test costs were sensitive to equipment utilization up to 5,000 annual tests, stabilizing 
between 5,000 and 8,000. Beyond 8,000, HT NAAT was more cost-effective due to lower test kit prices. 
However, if LT NAAT equipment costs were significantly lower (50% less) than HT NAAT and HT NAAT 
equipment less durable, LT NAAT could be cheaper, especially at lower testing volumes. 
 
3.3.4. Quality of included study: 
Our quality assessment tools for health economic studies were used to evaluate the quality of included 
manuscript. This tool contains 18 questions and was adapted from a modified CHEERS, as described 
earlier. The quality score obtained was 17 out of 18, representing a score of approximately 94%.
