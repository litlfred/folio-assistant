Discussion 
Summary of findings 
This review assessed the impact of LC-aNAATs, LC-mNAATs and MC-aNAATs on patient 
outcomes in people being investigated for TB. We included two RCTs published between 2021 
and 2023 (one iRCT and one pragmatic cRCT) assessing the impact of Xpert Ultra, a type of 
LC-aNAAT (32, 33). Both studies involved adult participants.  
 
Data were available for two of the three secondary outcomes of interest and none for the 
two primary outcomes (all-cause mortality and cure). Time to diagnosis was reduced for 
those tested using Xpert Ultra compared to routine care (adjusted geometric mean ratio: 
0.49, 95% CI: 0.39−0.62) (32). Esmail 2023 reported a substantial effect of Xpert Ultra on time 
to treatment (33). The hazard ratio comparing time to TB treatment initiation, including 
treatment initiated as a result of a positive culture test, was 2.30 (95% CI: 1.0−5.1) (33). 
Cattamanchi 2021 reported time to treatment as an adjusted geometric mean ratio (65%, 
95% CI: 44%−79%) (32), suggesting that the use of Xpert Ultra improves the time to TB 
treatment initiation when compared to smear microscopy. 
 
Other evidence 
We identified three trials that were excluded from the review because the comparator used 
was not smear or culture (details in Appendix 3) (35-37). Marcy 2023 reported all-cause 
mortality. In the intention-to-treat analysis, there was no statistical evidence of an effect 
(0.99, 95% CI: 0.60−1.63) (35). The hazard ratio reported by Marcy 2023 suggests that Xpert 
Ultra plus WHO standard care for pneumonia improves the time to TB treatment initiation 
compared to standard care (35). Both Marcy 2023 and Mukoka 2023 reported numbers lost 
to follow-up (35, 36). Leukes 2024—an ongoing trial evaluating Truenat MTB Plus reported 
on time to TB treatment initiation in its preliminary analysis (37). 
 
Strengths and limitations 
A strength of our review is an extensive search of the literature, including grey literature 
sources, by an information specialist and a WHO public call for data. We also mitigated bias 
by having two review authors conduct study selection, data extraction and risk of bias 
assessment independently. However, we found only two trials assessing the impact of one 
LC-aNAAT (Xpert Ultra). We did not identify RCTs of LC-mNAATs and MC-aNAATs.
