LC-aNAAT for the detection of rifampicin resistance had a summary sensitivity of 95.1% (83.1 to 
98.7) and summary specificity of 98.1% (97.0 to 98.7) based on 11 studies. Two of the included

● 
We found no eligible studies that evaluated rifampicin resistance testing among PLHIV. 
● 
The number of adolescents included in this systematic review was small: there were at least 160 
adolescents among whom 35 had TB by MRS. 
● 
Since our last systematic review in 2020, there were relatively few studies identified in the 
literature that evaluated Xpert Ultra as the index test for respiratory specimens. This may be 
consistent with acceptance of Xpert Ultra as a standard of care test for the detection of TB using 
respiratory samples. 
 
 
 
Implications for research  
Future studies should assess the diagnostic accuracy of Truenat MTB Plus compared with other rapid 
tests for tuberculosis and drug resistance, especially in difficult-to-diagnose groups, i.e. children, 
PLHIV, and those with extrapulmonary tuberculosis. There continues to be a need for research and 
guidance on the interpretation of Xpert Ultra trace positive results in different clinical settings and 
populations.
