Explanations 
a. Some concerns for risk of bias in four domains; timing of identification, deviations from intended intervention, measurement of the outcome, selection of the reported result. 
Participants were screened for recruitment after randomization and there was no information regarding measurement of time to diagnosis and treatment per arm. The trial 
protocol did not prespecify the analysis methods for time to diagnosis and time to treatment. 
b. Only one study included for this outcome with large sample size of 10,644. Confidence interval not broad ; Effect estimate was Geometric mean ratio 0.49 (95% CI 0.39 to 
0.62) 
c. Low risk of bias across all domains for Esmail et al. 
d. Two trials included, One trial had 10, 644 participants and the other 584 participants. The reported confidence intervals for both studies are not broad and do not cross the null
