4.6 Sensitivity analyses 
When excluding studies at high of bias (QUADAS-C) and with high or unclear concerns regarding 
applicability (QUADAS-2), the pooled difference in sensitivity increased to 10.0% (95% CrI 4.9 to 17.6) 
and pooled difference in specificity to -10.8 (95% CrI -20.6 to -5.4) (8 studies, 4,304 participants). See 
Table 17.  
When excluding participants with at least one unavailable or non-determinate index test result (1.966 
participants), pooled difference in sensitivity decreased to 4.4% (95% CrI 2.8 to 6.5) while pooled 
difference in specificity was -6.9% (95% CrI -9.1 to -4.5) (27 studies, 10,685 participants).
