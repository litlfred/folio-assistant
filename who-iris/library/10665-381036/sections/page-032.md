Detection of rifampicin resistance 
Should LC-aNAATs on respiratory samples be used to diagnose rifampicin 
resistance in adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms or screened positive 
for pulmonary TB, microbiological reference standard? 
The methodological quality of the 13 included studies is summarised in Figure 13. 
Gomathi 2020 was assessed as having applicability concerns related to patient selection 
due to use of frozen samples that were tested in a central lab. See explanations on page 
19 for the other studies with high risk of bias and/or applicability concerns.
