Findings  
Should LC-mNAATs be used to diagnose lymph node TB in lymph node tissue 
in children with signs and symptoms of lymph node TB, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
One study (nine participants) assessed the accuracy of LC-mNAATs for detecting lymph node TB 
using lymph node tissue and a microbiological reference standard (Figure 6). Estimated sensitivity 
and specificity were 100%. 
 
 
Figure 6. Forest plot of LC-mNAATs for detection of extrapulmonary TB 
FN: false negative; FP: false positive; TN: true negative; TP: true positive.  
Discussion 
Evidence on the diagnostic accuracy of TB-LAMP for detection of pulmonary and extrapulmonary TB 
in children is limited due to few and small studies. Adequately powered studies evaluating TB-LAMP 
in children are needed.
