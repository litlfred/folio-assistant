Figure 18. Risk of bias and applicability summary for pulmonary tuberculosis: stool and 
microbiological reference standard 
 
Ten studies (2885 participants) evaluating stool against a MRS of culture or Xpert Ultra on sputum or 
gastric aspirate were included with sensitivities between 26% and 100% and specificities between 
89% and 100% (Figure 19). All ten studies were included in the meta-analysis. The summary 
sensitivity (95% CI) was 68.0% (50.3 to 81.7) and the summary specificity (95% CI) was 98.2% (96.3 
to 99.1) (moderate-certainty (sensitivity) and high-certainty (specificity) evidence). We had access to 
data from three studies (1002 participants, 35 cases) with an MRS of culture alone on gastric or 
sputum specimens. The summary sensitivity (95% CI) of Xpert Ultra on stool in these studies was 
72.5% (60.0 to 82.0) and the summary specificity (95% CI) was 98.0% (96.0 to 98.7).  
 
For a population of 1,000 children where 100 have tuberculosis on culture, 82 would be Xpert Ultra 
positive; of these, 68 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (true positives) and 17 would not have 
pulmonary tuberculosis (false positives); 918 would be Xpert Ultra negative; of these 883 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 32 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (false-
negatives).
