Selection criteria 
We included cross-sectional studies, cohort studies, and randomised controlled trials (RCTs) that 
provided data on the diagnostic accuracy of the index tests against the reference standards of culture 
(microbiological reference standard, MRS) and/or a composite reference standard (CRS) when used 
on respiratory specimens for PTB detection in adolescents and adults. We included similar study types 
that provided data on the accuracy of the index tests against the reference standards of culture-based 
drug susceptibility testing with or without whole genome sequencing for rifampicin resistance.  
Only studies that enrolled adolescents (10 - 14 years of age) and adults (>15 years of age) were eligible. 
We included only participants that had the index test performed on respiratory samples (i.e., 
expectorated sputum, induced sputum, bronchial alveolar lavage (BAL)/bronchial wash, or tracheal 
aspirate). For the evaluation of TB detection, we included studies that evaluated the index tests in 
people with signs and symptoms of PTB, i.e. persons with presumptive PTB. We excluded studies 
designed to find people with active TB in community settings which screened persons irrespective of 
symptoms, as well as drug resistance surveys.  
For TB detection, we included studies that reported data comparing the index test(s) to an acceptable 
reference standard from which we could extract true positive (TP), true negative (TN), false positive 
(FP), and false negative (FN) values. As per the primary analysis in this study, Xpert Ultra trace positive 
results were considered to be positive for the detection of M. tuberculosis. For the detection of 
rifampicin resistance, we included studies that allowed estimation of sensitivity and specificity (i.e., if 
no rifampicin resistance was detected by the reference test in any of the specimens, then the study 
was excluded). 
Index tests 
Only design-locked marketed test technologies were considered. The individual LC-aNAAT test 
technologies are:  Xpert Ultra; Truenat MTB Plus; Truenat MTB-RIF Dx; STANDARD M10, SD Biosensor; 
and IRON-qPCR, Bioneer. 
Target conditions 
To be eligible for inclusion, studies needed to address diagnosis of PTB or RR-TB or both using a 
respiratory sample, including sputum (expectorated or induced), BAL/bronchial wash, or tracheal 
aspirate. Pleural specimens, gastric aspirates, oropharyngeal specimens, and nasopharyngeal swabs 
were excluded from this review. Participants who had received 7 or more days of TB treatment at the 
time of specimen collection were excluded. 
Reference standards 
Tuberculosis detection 
We included two reference standards: culture (microbiologic, MRS) and composite reference standard 
(CRS).
