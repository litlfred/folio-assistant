Table 4. CRS Definitions 
Study 
CRS Definition 
Aguilera-Alonso 
2022 
Adolescents; CRS definition: (1) Confirmed TB: >1 respiratory specimen positive for MTB 
(culture or PCR); (2) Unconfirmed TB: without bacteriological evidence who met >1 (if 
immunological evidence of MTB) or 2 (immunological evidence absent) criteria: 
symptoms/signs suggestive of TB, CXR consistent with TB, close TB exposure, response to 
TB treatment. Immunological evidence of MTB infection defined as positive TST or IGRA. 
Berhanu 2018 
Adult patients with clinical diagnosis of TB by X ray or other method. 
Chien 2020 
Adolescents with either bacteriologically confirmed TB (culture-confirmed TB) or diagnosed 
with active TB by a clinician who decided to give the patient a full course of TB treatment. 
Jaganath 2021 
Adolescents with confirmed intrathoracic TB (positive culture) or unconfirmed TB (negative 
cultures with signs and symptoms of TB and clinical evidence of TB including response to 
anti-TB treatment). 
Opota 2019 
Primarily adults although at least one participant was 15 years of age. CRS based on “clinical 
and radiological findings.” 
Park 2019 
Adolescents and adult participants had 3 month follow up with clinical data including any 
imaging. The final diagnosis of each patient was determined by consensus among 3 
respiratory consultants masked to the Xpert Ultra results. CRS included category 1: culture 
confirmed TB directly from the BAL or EBUS sample or category 2: highly probable TB with 
clinical and radiological features suggestive of TB, response to TB treatment and cytological 
evidence of granulomatous disease if available. 
Sabi 2018 
Adolescent participants with CRS based on sputum culture confirmed TB, highly probable 
TB (CXR consistent with TB, histology/cytology typical for TB, or fluorescent/acid-fast bacilli 
on microscopy), or probable TB (clinically suspected TB without objective findings as 
above). 
Wang 2022 
Primarily adults although adolescents included. CRS defined as: Definite pulmonary TB- 
microbiologically confirmed TB, with positive smear and/or culture outcome excluding 
nontuberculous mycobacteria; Probable TB, defined as smear and culture negative but 
patient was clinically diagnosed with TB based on clinical findings, radiologic imaging, other 
molecular tests, or response to empirical anti-TB treatment. 
Zar 2019 
Adolescents; CRS: microbiologic evidence or "unconfirmed TB" based on a clinical diagnosis 
Zhang 2021 
Adults who met the CRS definition of TB were: 1) Confirmed PTB: BAL samples positive for 
MTB cultures; 2) Suspected PTB: BAL samples culture negative, but clinical symptoms and 
radiological findings suggestive of PTB, and response to anti-TB therapy.
