5. DISCUSSION  
This systematic review summarizes the evidence from 31 fully paired comparative accuracy studies on 
the parallel use of respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM for detection of tuberculosis disease in adults 
and adolescents with HIV presenting with signs and symptoms of tuberculosis or screen positive, 
compared to each test alone. 
We identified three randomized controlled trials that evaluated the impact of a parallel testing strategy 
using respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM versus a strategy with respiratory LC-aNAAT alone on 
mortality and other patient-important outcomes.

The diagnostic accuracy of the parallel use of respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM against a 
microbiological reference standard is:  
• Sensitivity 77.5% (95% CrI 73.4 to 81.3) and specificity 89.4% (95% CrI 85.8 to 92.3)  
Compared to respiratory LC-aNAAT alone, difference in accuracy is: 
o Sensitivity difference 6.7% (95% CrI 3.8 to 10.7)  
o Specificity difference -6.8% (95% CrI -9.5 to -4.7) 
 
The diagnostic accuracy of the parallel use of respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM against a 
composite reference standard is:  
• 
Sensitivity 67.6% (95% CrI 59.9 to 74.6) and specificity 96.2% (95% CrI 92.8 to 98.1) 
Compared to respiratory LC-aNAAT alone, difference in accuracy is: 
o Sensitivity difference 16.0% (95% CrI 10.7 to 22.9)  
o Specificity difference 3.5% (95% CrI -6.6 to -1.7)

For the subgroup of participants with CD4 ≤ 200 cells/mm3, the diagnostic accuracy of the parallel use of 
respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM using a microbiological reference standard is:  
• 
Sensitivity 78.7% (95% CrI 72.9 to 83.8) and specificity 85.6% (95% CrI 78.5 to 90.8) 
Compared to respiratory LC-aNAAT alone, difference in accuracy is: 
o Sensitivity difference 9.2% (95% CrI 4.0 to 17.4)  
o Specificity difference -9.4% (95% CrI -14.7 to -5.6) 
For the subgroup of participants with advanced HIV, the diagnostic accuracy of the parallel use of 
respiratory LC-aNAAT and urine LF-LAM using a microbiological reference standard is:   
• 
Sensitivity 81.9% (95% CrI 75.5 to 87.6) and specificity 87.1% (95% CrI 78.4 to 92.8) 
Compared to respiratory LC-aNAAT alone, difference in accuracy is: 
o Sensitivity difference 8.1% (95% CrI 3.8 to 14.0) 
o Specificity difference -8.4% (95% CrI -15.2 to -4.2) 
 
For the subgroup of seriously ill, the diagnostic accuracy of the parallel use of respiratory LC-aNAAT and 
urine LF-LAM using a microbiological reference standard is:   
• 
Sensitivity 78.7% (95% CrI 62.8 to 90.7) and specificity 79.7% (95% CrI 68.7 to 88.5) 
Compared to respiratory LC-aNAAT alone, difference in accuracy is: 
o Sensitivity difference 13.3% (95% CrI 4.4 to 25.4)
