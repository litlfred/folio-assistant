2.2. Study Population & Specimen Type: 
The study population may include any of the following:  
 
All people who screen positive, with signs and symptoms of TB, or with abnormal chest X-rays, people 
living with HIV (PLHIV), and children with signs or symptoms of TB. Specimen types may include respiratory 
or non-respiratory isolates, including stool and urine. 
 
Comparator: 
We were not restricting by diagnostic comparator or microbiological reference standards. Comparator 
groups may include individuals who underwent other forms of diagnostic testing for a diagnosis of DR-TB 
or no testing at all. The former may include conventional phenotypic DST performed on liquid or solid 
cultures from respiratory specimens, but it is not limited to it.  
 
The Outcome of Interest: 
Key economic outcomes of interest to be extracted from included studies were any of the following: 
• Any component cost associated with the NAATs unit test and/or implementation/scale-up 
costs. 
• Unit test costs or costs per patient for each diagnostic strategy, including cost per patient 
tested. 
• Natural cost-effectiveness outcomes, including cost per patient diagnosed. 
• Incremental cost-effectiveness ratio (ICER) (e.g., incremental cost per case diagnosed, life 
year saved, or other intermediate outcomes or health utilities: Disability adjusted life years 
(DALYs) averted/ Quality adjusted life years (QALYs) gained). 
• Budget impact outcomes and estimates.
