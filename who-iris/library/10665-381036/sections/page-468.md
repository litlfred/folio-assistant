Providers’ acceptability of Xpert Ultra seemed high but there was uncertainty about its accuracy, 
potentially leading to reduced trust and litigation should there be a false diagnosis. Like Xpert MTB/RIF,

Detailed review findings for Xpert Ultra 
Summary of Qualitative Findings table: Findings 3-7, Annex 6 
  
Finding 3: Patients can be reluctant to test for TB/MDR-TB because of stigma related to the 
association of TB with low socioeconomic status (High confidence; Sereda 202231). 
Health providers mentioned that refusal to test among patients was related to the perception that TB 
testing was for socially deprived populations. 
“Some people are concerned. They consider COVID-19 as a relatively “good disease” while TB is a “bad 
disease” (Sereda 2022). 
Finding 4: Diagnostic delays are accumulated because of various health system factors such as staff 
shortages, weak triage workflows at in and out-patient settings, poor/delayed sample transport and 
safety (High confidence; Sereda 202231). 
Rapid turnaround time is a key advantage of new NAATs tests however this benefit could be hampered 
by health system weaknesses that introduce diagnostic delays. 
“…However, there is one problem, they don’t have enough trained staff. Sputum collection is not a 
routine procedure there. Secondly, they often don’t have enough sputum collection rooms. Plus, 
logistics………. if we transport them to the sputum collection room, which is located in another building 
block, and back, we could make them worse”. … Sputum collection is not a routine procedure there… 
transportation of a dangerous biological sample, considering COVID-19 and TB, is another issue.” (Sereda 
2022). 
Finding 5: The lack of sufficient resources and of ensuring maintenance (i.e. shortages/stock-outs; 
unreliable logistics; lack of funding, electricity or power back up systems leads to underutilization and 
negatively influences uptake and impact of Xpert Ultra (High confidence; Mwaura 202030, Sereda 
202231).  
The challenges facing Xpert MTB/RIF of erratic power supply and supply chain deficiencies and 
associated stock outs could also affect the implementation of Xpert Ultra, but the introduction of the 
new test could provide an opportunity to strengthen existing infrastructure. 
“...if the Ultra cartridge [comes] to the ground, probably they would be able to have a wide scope on how 
they are going to implement, in terms of the rolling out of these cartridges to the facilities to make sure 
that they are really there. Because definitely, as usual, when any product comes, it comes in a big supply. 
. .” (Mwaura 2020) 
“TB resources are underused. Xpert MTB/RIF cartridges and anti-TB drugs have limited shelf-life. We can 
waste resources if a decrease in TB detection will continue” (Sereda 2022) 
Finding 6: The introduction of Xpert Ultra increases workload of laboratory staff if added onto existing 
work without adjusting staffing arrangements or training workforce, decreasing motivation of health 
workers who may be hesitant to accept testing with Xpert Ultra (High confidence; Sereda 202231). 
The health workforce could lose motivation to work with increasing demand for Xpert testing services 
especially if they are not trained in the required skills in collecting specimens and handling increased 
workload. “Of course, we have to scale up [Xpert MTB/RIF testing among patients with COVID19] in 
other hospitals. However, there is one problem, they don’t have enough trained staff” (Sereda 2022)
