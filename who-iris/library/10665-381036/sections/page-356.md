Comparative accuracy in participants screening positive for tuberculosis  
Six studies reported data for participants screening positive for tuberculosis involving 1,901 participants, 
346 with tuberculosis (18%). Participants were reported screening positive by chest Xray by [Åhsberg 
2023; Huerga 2023a; Olbrich 2023; Van Hoving 2019], by CRP [Reeve 2023] or both Chest Xray or CRP 
[Shapiro 2022]. Among participants screening positive, the pooled sensitivity of parallel testing was 
80.8% (95% CrI 67.2 to 90.2) and specificity 84.5% (95% CrI 72.0 to 91.9), respectively (Table 12). The 
pooled difference in sensitivity and pooled difference in specificity between parallel testing and LC-
aNAAT was 7.3% (95% CrI 2.1 to 18.0; 95% PI 0.5 to 46.9) and -9.1% (95% CrI -19.8 to -3.8; 95% PI -50.6 
to -0.9).

Comparative accuracy in participants with advanced HIV  
Twelve studies reported data for participants with advanced HIV involving 2945 participants, 525 with 
tuberculosis (18%). Among participants with advanced HIV, the pooled sensitivity of parallel testing was 
81.9% (95% CrI 75.5 to 87.6) and specificity 87.1% (95% CrI 78.4 to 92.8), respectively (Table 13). The 
pooled difference in sensitivity and pooled difference in specificity between parallel testing and LC-
aNAAT was 8.1% (95% CrI 3.8 to 14.0; 95% PI 1.1 to 36.3) and -8.4% (95% CrI -15.2 to -4.2; 95% PI -49.4 
to -0.8).

Comparative accuracy for seriously ill participants 
Six studies reported data for seriously ill participants [Åhsberg 2023; Huerga 2023a; Kebede 2021; 
Olbrich 2023; Shapiro 2022; Van Hoving 2019] involving 577 participants, 166 with tuberculosis (29%).  
Among seriously ill participants, the pooled sensitivity of parallel testing was 78.7% (95% CrI 62.8 to 
90.7) and specificity 79.7% (95% CrI (68.7 to 88.5), respectively (Table 14). The pooled difference in 
sensitivity and pooled difference in specificity between parallel testing and LC-aNAAT was 13.3% (95% 
CrI 4.4 to 25.4; 95% PI 2.0 to 42.3) and -14.4% (95% CrI -24.2 to -7.4; 95% PI -44.2 to -3.0).
