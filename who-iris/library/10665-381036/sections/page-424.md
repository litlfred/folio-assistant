Sohn et al. (2019) conducted a cost and cost-affordability analysis of TB-LAMP and Xpert MTB/RIF, when 
deployed in peripheral laboratories in Malawi and Vietnam (80). Affordability was determined by 
calculating the percentage of nationwide implementation's total first-year operational costs within the 
reported annual national budget for tuberculosis (TB) in 2014. The analysis used a budgeting table from 
the WHO Xpert implementation manual, estimated annual testing volumes, and categorized laboratories 
into low, medium, and high workload categories. 
 
To implement TB-LAMP in Malawi, it would require a budget allocation of 34% of the National 
Tuberculosis Program's annual budget, spread across 160 laboratories. Meanwhile, GeneXpert receives 
97% of the NTP's annual budget in those same labs. In Vietnam, the budget allocation for TB-LAMP is 151% 
of the NTP's annual budget, spread across 920 labs. GeneXpert, on the other hand, receives 435% of the 
NTP's annual budget in those same labs.
