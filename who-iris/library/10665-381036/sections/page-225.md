Should LC-mNAATs on cerebrospinal fluid be used to diagnose TB meningitis in 
adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of TB meningitis, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
Two studies (70 participants; 3 cases) assessed the accuracy of LC-mNAATs for detecting TB 
meningitis using CSF and a microbiological reference standard (Figure 14). Estimated sensitivity and 
specificity were both 100% in one study and 0% and 97% in the other study; very low certainty 
(sensitivity) and low certainty (specificity) evidence.
