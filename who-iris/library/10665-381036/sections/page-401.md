Quality assessment 
 
Two reviewers independently evaluated the reporting quality of each included study, and any 
disagreement was resolved by discussion. An 18-item modified Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation 
Reporting Standards (CHEERS) statement was used. The CHEERS statement is not a tool for assessing the 
quality of methods and does not provide an overall score. Instead, it assesses the quality of reporting. 
However, we estimated an overall score from the sum of items reported by each study compared to those 
indicated in the modified checklist. Each item that was met was rated as ‘yes’ with a score of 1 or ‘no’ with 
0. 
Supplementary table 1 and 2 presents the reporting quality of the included cost-effectiveness analyses. 
For Xpert cost-effectiveness studies the quality of reporting was generally very good, with quality ratings 
from 83-96%. For Ultra, all the studies were also of high quality with ratings from 83-100%. The reporting 
of the study on TrueNat was also of high quality, with 89% of the items on the checklist reported.
