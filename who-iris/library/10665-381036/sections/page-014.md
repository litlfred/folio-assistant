Search Results: Truenat 
We identified 1175 research articles for screening from searches of electronic sources. After removing 
duplicate studies (n=524), we screened the titles and abstracts of 651 unique articles and excluded 
617. We identified four reports through citation searching and WHO call for data. We performed full-
text screening of 38 articles and included five reports of which four contributed to the meta-analysis 
of Truenat MTB Plus and two included for the meta-analysis of rifampicin resistance (Figure 2).
