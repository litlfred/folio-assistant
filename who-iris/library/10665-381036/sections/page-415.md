3.2.  Systematic review of LC-mNAAT (TB-LAMP): 
 
3.2.1. Summary of Findings: 
After eliminating 638 duplicates, our search found 1,990 studies. Six studies were included in the final 
systematic review, as seen in Figure 2. Studies that did not involve people with TB, used LAMP as a 
diagnostic intervention, or did not contain cost data were excluded.  
 
Of the six included studies, one performed a cost-utility analysis (41), and two performed a cost-
affordability analysis (79,80). The three other studies (81–83) estimated the cost of TB-LAMP.  
 
Figure 2. PRISMA diagram for LC-mNAATs 
 
3.2.2. General characteristics of included full economic evaluation studies  
All included studies were conducted in low- to middle-income countries. Specifically, two studies were 
conducted in Thailand (41,82), one in Malawi (83), one in Malawi and Vietnam (80), and another in India
