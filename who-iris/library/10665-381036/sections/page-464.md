EO is a public health physician and methodologist. She has 12 years' experience in evidence synthesis 
specializing in methodology, systematic reviews, and meta-analysis of diagnostic tests. She has conducted 
diagnostic test accuracy and qualitative systematic reviews on tuberculosis tests, some of which have 
informed WHO guidelines on tuberculosis tests. She is also an academic editor with the Cochrane 
Infectious Disease Group. 
 
PK has experience with qualitative evidence synthesis for tuberculosis diagnostics. She has co-authored a 
qualitative review of diagnostic tests which informed WHO guidelines on tuberculosis tests. Her views on 
tuberculosis diagnostics may have been influenced by being a healthcare worker involved in a 
multidisciplinary review of management of people with MDR tuberculosis. 
 
MM has experience with qualitative evidence synthesis for tuberculosis diagnostics and pre-treatment 
loss to follow-up for tuberculosis. Her views on tuberculosis diagnostics may have been influenced by 
being a PhD researcher investigating strategies for pre-treatment loss to follow-up for tuberculosis.  
 
NE has conducted a qualitative evidence synthesis and interview studies about user perspectives of 
several TB diagnostics, including low complexity NAATs which informed WHO guidelines for diagnostic 
tests for tuberculosis. She has also conducted a range of primary studies in India's and South Africa's 
health systems examining challenges to diagnosing and diagnostic processes at point-of-care. She has also 
undertaken studies on the attempts of innovating and implementing point-of-care diagnostics for
