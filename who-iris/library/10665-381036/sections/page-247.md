Should LC-mNAATs on respiratory samples be used to diagnose pulmonary TB 
in children with signs and symptoms of pulmonary TB, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
Three studies assessed the accuracy of LC-mNAATs for detecting pulmonary TB using respiratory 
samples (sputum, BAL and tracheal aspirate) and a microbiological reference standard (Figure 4). 
The sensitivities were between 60% and 100% and the specificities were between 95% and 100% (62 
participants including 8 with pulmonary TB); very low certainty (sensitivity) and low certainty 
(specificity) evidence.
