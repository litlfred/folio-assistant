Figure 13. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study: 
genitourinary tuberculosis in urine 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose genitourinary tuberculosis in urine in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of genitourinary tuberculosis, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs sensitivities were all 100% (3 studies) and specificities were between 28% and 
100% (6 studies, 232 participants); very low certainty (sensitivity and specificity) evidence.  
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose genitourinary tuberculosis in urine in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of genitourinary tuberculosis, against a composite 
reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 23.0% (14.7 to 34.1) and 
98.9% (89.7 to 99.9) from three Xpert Ultra studies (693 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity) and moderate certainty (specificity) evidence.
