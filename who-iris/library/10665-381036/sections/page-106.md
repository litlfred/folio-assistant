As none of the Truenat MTB plus studies were included in the class level analyses, only Xpert Ultra data were 
included. Dashes indicate where there were insufficient data to perform a meta-analysis. Estimates of sensitivity, 
specificity and predictive values are presented as percentages. 
CI: confidence interval; CRS: composite reference standard; EPTB: extrapulmonary tuberculosis; MRS: 
microbiological reference standard; PLHIV: people living with HIV.   
 
 
The three Truenat MTB Plus studies evaluated several forms of extrapulmonary tuberculosis. 
Sharma 2021 and Sharma 2023 provided accuracy estimates for cerebrospinal fluid and 
lymph node aspirate against a microbiological reference standard. Jose 2024 provided 
accuracy estimates for cerebrospinal fluid, lymph node aspirate, lymph node biopsy, 
peritoneal fluid, intestinal biopsy, omentum, peritoneal tissue, bone marrow, synovial fluid, 
synovial tissue, pus and urine against a microbiological reference standard. Lymph node fluid 
and cerebrospinal fluid were also evaluated against a composite reference standard.
