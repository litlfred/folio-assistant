1.2.2 Index test, LC-aNAATs 
The Xpert MTB/RIF [Cepheid 2023a] and Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra (Xpert Ultra) [Cepheid 2023b] assays 
simultaneously detect M tuberculosis complex and rifampicin resistance in a single step, where most 
of the sample processing and the amplification process are combined in a closed system. Xpert 
MTB/RIF and Xpert Ultra cartridges are used with the GeneXpert system. Xpert MTB/RIF and Xpert 
Ultra provides a result within two hours after starting the test, with minimal hands‐on technical time 
[Cepheid 2023b]. A Cochrane Review on the diagnostic accuracy of Xpert MTB/RIF for pulmonary 
tuberculosis found a pooled sensitivity of 85% (95% CrI 82 to 88) and specificity of 98% (95% CrI 97 
to 98) [Horne 2019]. As the Xpert MTB/RIF sensitivity decreased in people with HIV to 81% (95% CrI 
75 to 86) [Horne 2019], the Xpert Ultra was designed to detect the M. tuberculosis complex with 
greater sensitivity. Among people with HIV, a comparative review found pooled sensitivity was 
87.6% (95% CrI 75.4 to 94.1) for Xpert Ultra versus 74.9% (95% CrI 58.7 to 86.2) for Xpert MTB/RIF; 
pooled specificity was 92.8% (95% CrI 82.3 to 97.0) for Xpert Ultra versus 99.7% (95% CrI 98.6 to 
100.0) for Xpert MTB/RIF [Zifodya 2021]. Xpert assays can also be used to diagnose extrapulmonary 
tuberculosis, where sensitivity varies across different extrapulmonary sampling sites being highest in 
cerebrospinal fluid [Kohli 2021a].    
Truenat MTB and Truenat MTB Plus are developed by Molbio diagnostics for automated detection of 
the M. tuberculosis complex with both sample processing and amplification in one step and results 
obtained within one hour [Diagnostics 2023a, 2023b]. Truenat MTB is a quantitative test that gives 
actual colony-forming units (CFUs) per milliliter count, while Truenat MTB Plus is semi-quantitative 
and gives four grades (high, medium, low, and very low) based on CFUs but does not specify the 
actual count [Diagnostics 2023a, 2023b]. One multicentre trial evaluating the diagnostic accuracy of 
these assays for pulmonary tuberculosis reported 73% sensitivity and 98% specificity for Truenat 
MTB; 80% sensitivity and 96% specificity for Truenat MTB Plus  [Penn-Nicholson 2021]. 
STANDARD M10 and IRON-qPCR are both validated as fully automated platforms with all-in-one 
cartridge for nucleic acid extraction and amplification. Results are available within one hour. IRON-
qPCR technology includes targets for M. tuberculosis complex (and resistance mutations for 
isoniazid, rifampicin and fluroquinolones) to be run on sputum or on isolates (culture growth) 
[BIONEER. 2022]. STANDARD M10 requires a simple sputum pre-treatment process but can include 
cartridges for simultaneous detection of M. tuberculosis and non-tuberculous mycobacteria and 
drug-resistance against rifampicin and isoniazid [SD BIOSENSOR. 2021].  
We will refer to the Truenat MTB and MTB Plus assays as “Truenat” and Xpert MTB/RIF and Xpert 
Ultra as “Xpert” unless it is necessary to distinguish between specific tests.
