Characteristics of included studies 
Key characteristics of the four included studies for detection of pulmonary TB are described in Table 
1. One of the studies also assessed extrapulmonary TB (Promsena 2022). All studies were cross-
sectional in design and assessed TB-LAMP using a microbiological reference standard in central 
laboratories. No study used a composite reference standard. None of the studies reported non-
determinate test results.

Detection of pulmonary tuberculosis 
 
Methodological quality 
Figure 2 summarises the results of the risk of bias and applicability assessment for each of the 
included studies for detection of pulmonary TB. Bojang 2016 was judged to have unclear risk of bias 
in the reference standard domain due to lack of information about blinding and in the flow and 
timing domain due to lack of clarity about the number of patients recruited and analysed. We judged 
Promsena 2022 to have high applicability concern as children were recruited from an in-patient 
setting in a tertiary care hospital.
