Kaso & Hailu, 2021 compared Xpert to smear, and the ICER was $28 per additional case diagnosed (44). 
 
Cadavid et al., 2022 compared Xpert to smear and culture in adults & children and found Xpert to be more 
cost-effective with an ICER of $1017 compared to $1039 for culture (29).  
 
Hickey et al., 2022, compared one or two Xpert tests to smear and culture. One Xpert was found to be 
cost-saving at $6811 per patient (31). 
 
Xpert cost-effectiveness studies with health utilities outcomes (n=4) 
Khumsri et al., 2020, compared Xpert to smear, and Xpert dominated as it was less costly and more 
effective (46). 
 
Sedrak et al., 2022, compared Xpert with smear, culture, and clinical diagnosis. Xpert was cost-effective, 
with an ICER of $1854/ DALY averted (47). 
 
Keim-Malpass et al., 2023 assessed parallel LAM & Xpert testing in PLHIV with Xpert, LAM and empiric TB 
treatment (45). However, the authors did not conduct an incremental analysis of the strategies. The ICERs 
were $4269/QALY gained for LAM & Xpert, $2683/QALY gained for LAM, and $2021/QALY gained for 
Xpert. Empiric treatment was the most cost-effective strategy. 
 
Chitpim et al., 2022, compared sequential testing, including Xpert as the initial test, to smear and culture 
DST, smear and Xpert, smear and TB LAMP and TB LAMP as the initial test (41).  TB LAMP was the dominant 
strategy. The ICERs for the other strategies were $119/QALY for smear and Xpert, $6/QALY for initial 
Xpert, and $30/QALY for smear and TB LAMP.
