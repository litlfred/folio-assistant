Methods 
This review was written following the recommended methodology described in the Cochrane 
Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions (23) and the PRISMA 2020 statement (24). 
The protocol for the review is available on the Open Source Repository. 
 
Criteria for considering studies for this review 
 
Types of studies 
We included cluster and individually randomised controlled trials (RCTs) that met our 
inclusion criteria and provided data on the outcomes of interest.  
 
Types of participants 
We included people with presumptive TB. Based on the WHO definition of age groups, we 
included children aged <10 years and adults and adolescents aged ≥10 years.  
 
Types of interventions 
Intervention 
Diagnostic strategies that use the design-locked marketed test technologies listed below 
within each class. 
• LC-aNAATs: Xpert Ultra; Truenat MTB Plus, Truenat MTB-RIF Dx; STANDARD M10; 
IRON-qPCR 
• LC-mNAATs: TB-LAMP 
• MC-aNAATs: FluoroType XDR-TB 
Control 
Diagnostic strategies that used smear microscopy and/or culture. 
 
Types of outcome measures 
Primary outcomes 
• All‐cause mortality from the time of the first diagnostic test to the end of trial follow‐
up 
• Cure 
Secondary outcomes 
• Proportion of participants lost to follow-up 
• Time to diagnosis
