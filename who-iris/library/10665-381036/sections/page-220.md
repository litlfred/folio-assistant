Should LC-mNAATs on respiratory samples be used to diagnose pulmonary TB in 
adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of pulmonary TB, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
Twenty-six (18297 participants) studies assessed diagnostic accuracy using sputum specimens. The 
sensitivities were between 55% and 100% and the specificities were between 70% and 100% (Figure 
4). The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 84.1% (78.3 to 88.6) and the summary specificity was 
96.1% (94.2 to 97.4); high certainty (sensitivity and specificity) evidence.  
 
For a population of 1000 adults and adolescents where 100 have pulmonary TB on culture, 119 would 
be LC-mNAAT-positive and 35 (29%) would not have TB (false-positives); 881 would be LC-mNAAT-
negative and 16 (2%) would have TB (false-negatives).
