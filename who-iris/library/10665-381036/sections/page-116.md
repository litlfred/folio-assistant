The methodological quality assessment results are summarised in Figure 15 and study 
estimates of sensitivity and specificity are presented in Figure 16 for detection of bone or 
joint tuberculosis in bone or joint fluid. 
 
Figure 15. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study: bone or joint 
tuberculosis in bone of joint fluid 
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose bone or joint tuberculosis in bone or joint aspirate 
in adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of bone or joint tuberculosis, against a 
microbiological reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 96.6% (87.2 to 99.1) and 
91.1% (80.8 to 96.2) from three Xpert Ultra studies (126 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity and specificity) evidence.  
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose bone or joint tuberculosis in bone or joint aspirate 
in adults and adolescents with signs and symptoms of bone or joint tuberculosis, against a 
composite reference standard? 
LC-aNAAT sensitivity and specificity were 90.0% and 97.0% from one Xpert Ultra study 
(145 participants); moderate certainty (sensitivity) and very low certainty (specificity) 
evidence.
