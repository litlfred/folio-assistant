Appendix 3. Additional forest plots  
Appendix Figures 1 to 11 summarise currently available data for LC-aNAATs by brand and subgroups. 
Two studies (Penn-Nicholson 2021 and Theron 2024) compared the accuracy of Xpert Ultra and 
Truenat MTB Plus. Note that for the class level meta-analyses of LC-aNAATs presented in Table 3, to 
avoid double counting, we selected the data for Truenat MTB Plus for inclusion in the meta-analyses 
because Truenat assays were the index tests evaluated in the studies.          
 
Appendix Figure 1. Forest plot of LC-aNAATs for pulmonary TB by brand: sputum 
specimen and microbiological reference standard 
 For each participant, Sabi 2018 tested between 1 and 4 sputum samples using Ultra and culture. The 
"maximal" result for each type of test was used as the per person result. Li 2023 used 3 sputum samples per 
person and examined each sample microscopically for acid-fast bacilli. The remainder of the three sputum 
samples were then combined and used for Xpert Ultra and liquid culture.
