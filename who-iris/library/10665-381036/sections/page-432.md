LC-nNAAT discussion: 
 
The present systematic review provides an in-depth analysis of the economic and cost-effectiveness of 
implementing TB-LAMP in diverse settings. Through a range of economic analyses, including cost-utility, 
cost-benefit, and cost-affordability assessments, this study contributes valuable insights into the potential 
role of TB-LAMP in tuberculosis diagnostics.  
 
The reviewed studies reveal that factors such as batch scenarios and larger settings influence the per-test 
costs, with the cost per test decreasing in specific scenarios. Testing volumes, location, and operational 
parameters can also impact the cost. Notably, the cost-utility analysis by Chitpim et al. (2022) positioned 
TB-LAMP favorably in terms of cost-effectiveness compared to other diagnostic algorithms (41).  
 
Furthermore, the cost-benefit analysis by Donkeng‑Donfack et al. (2022) in challenging settings such as 
prisons and refugee camps provided evidence supporting the combined use of TB-LAMP and GeneXpert 
MTB/RIF (79). The significant reduction in turnaround time and cost benefits observed from the second 
year of implementation underscores the practical advantages of this diagnostic algorithm in resource-
constrained environments.  
 
Sohn et al.'s (2019) cost and cost-affordability analysis in peripheral laboratories in Malawi and Vietnam 
highlighted the economic considerations of implementing TB-LAMP and Xpert MTB/RIF (80). The study 
showed that per-test costs for TB-LAMP were lower than those of Xpert MTB/RIF. However, the potential 
financial burden of widespread implementation underscored the importance of cost-effectiveness 
assessments in shaping diagnostic strategies. 
 
It is worth noting that the reviewed studies exhibit some limitations, such as variations in settings, sample 
sources, and comparators, which may influence the generalizability of findings. Additionally, the 
affordability analysis underscores the financial implications of nationwide implementation, suggesting the 
need for careful budgetary planning and allocation. Furthermore, it is important to note that the recent 
decrease in the price of TB-LAMP ($6) by the Global Drug Facility (GDF) may have an impact on the results 
of economic evaluations, potentially enhancing the cost-effectiveness and affordability of implementing 
TB-LAMP in diverse settings.
