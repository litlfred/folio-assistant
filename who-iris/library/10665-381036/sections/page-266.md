3. METHODS 
This review was based on a protocol, which was informed by methodology used in previous reviews and 
other protocols in the field [Inbaraj 2023; Zifodya 2021; Kohli 2021; Shapiro 2021; Bjerrum 2019b; Shah 
2016b].  
3.1. Criteria for considering studies for this review 
3.1.1. Types of studies 
For children who were HIV negative or with unknown HIV status, we included comparative diagnostic 
accuracy studies that evaluated the accuracy of the parallel use of LC-aNAATs on a respiratory specimen 
and LC-aNAAT on stool, and the accuracy of one of the tests used alone, in the same population (Table 
1).  
For children with HIV, we included comparative studies that evaluated the accuracy of parallel use of LC-
aNAAT on respiratory samples and stool (LC-aNAAT combined) and LF-LAM versus each one of the tests 
used alone, in the population (Table 1).
