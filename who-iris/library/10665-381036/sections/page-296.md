4.4.3 Subgroup analyses using a microbiological reference standard 
The diagnostic accuracy of the parallel use of LC-aNAAT combined, LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples, 
and LC-aNAAT on stool in children without HIV or HIV status unknown is shown for the following 
subgroups against a MRS in Appendix 16: inpatient setting; symptomatic participants; age 0-4 versus 5-9 
years; sample type NPA versus other respiratory samples; severe acute malnutrition present.   
4.4.4 Non‐determinate results 
Six studies reported invalid results for either LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples or stool in children 
without HIV or HIV status unknown [Babo 2023; Hanrahan 2018; Hasan 2017; Kabir 2021; Olbrich 2024; 
Orikiriza 2021]. 
LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples 
Four studies [Babo 2023; Hanrahan 2018; M. P. Nicol 2013; Orikiriza 2021] reported invalid results, 
ranging from 0.3% (1/371; [Babo 2023]) to 1.3% (2/160; [Hanrahan 2018]). Three studies observed zero 
invalid results [Olbrich 2024; Hasan 2017; Kabir 2021]. 
LC-aNAAT on stool 
Four studies [Babo 2023; Hanrahan 2018; Kabir 2021; Olbrich 2024] reported invalid results, ranging 
from 0.2% (1/447; [Kabir 2021]) to 13.2% (15/114; [Hanrahan 2018]). Two studies observed zero invalid 
results [Orikiriza 2021; Hasan 2017]. 
4.4.5 Sensitivity analyses 
In children without HIV or HIV status unknown, when excluding studies at high or unclear risk of bias 
(QUADAS-C) and with high or unclear concerns regarding applicability (QUADAS-2; 6 studies, 1,516 
participants), the pooled difference in sensitivity comparing the accuracy of parallel testing to LC-aNAAT 
on respiratory samples alone increased to 8.0% (95% CrI 3.5 to 15.8) and pooled difference in specificity 
to -2.3 (95% CrI -5.3 to -0.6). The pooled difference in sensitivity comparing the accuracy of parallel 
testing to LC-aNAAT on stool alone increased to 24.2% (95% CrI 14.7 to 36.7) and pooled difference in 
specificity to -5.5 % (95% CrI -10.4 to -2.3).   
When excluding participants with at least one unavailable or non-determinate index test result (8 
studies, 1,474 participants), pooled difference in sensitivity comparing the accuracy of parallel testing to 
LC-aNAAT on respiratory samples decreased to 5.3% (95% CrI2. 0 to 11.6) while pooled difference in 
specificity was -1.4% (95% CrI -3.5 to -0.4). The pooled difference in sensitivity comparing the accuracy 
of parallel testing to LC-aNAAT on stool alone increased to 16.0% (95% CrI 8.3 to 26.8) and pooled 
difference in specificity to -4.2% (95% CrI -7.9 to -1.8). See in Appendix 17.
