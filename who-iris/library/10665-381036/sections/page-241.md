Objectives  
1. To assess the diagnostic accuracy of LC-mNAATs for detection of pulmonary and 
extrapulmonary TB by the site of disease in children with presumptive TB.  
2. To investigate the potential sources of heterogeneity. For detection of pulmonary TB, we 
aimed to investigate the effect of smear microscopy status (smear positive/smear negative), 
HIV status (positive/negative), and the history of TB on test accuracy. 
Methods 
Methods are described in detail in the generic protocol for six diagnostic accuracy reviews that will 
provide evidence to inform the 2024 update to WHO policy guideline on tests for TB detection. The 
protocol is available on the Open Source Repository. 
 
Search methods 
Electronic searches  
The Cochrane Infectious Diseases Group Information Specialist (Vittoria Lutje (VL)) performed 
literature searches between 2nd and 3rd October 2023, without language restrictions, in the following 
databases, using the search terms described in Appendix 1: Cochrane Central Register of Controlled 
Trials (CENTRAL), included in the Cochrane Library, issue 10, October 2023; MEDLINE (Ovid); Embase 
(Ovid); Science Citation Index and Biosis previews (ISI Web of Knowledge); WHO Global Index Medicus; 
and SCOPUS (Elsevier). VL searched ClinicalTrials.gov (clinicaltrials.gov/) and the WHO Clinical Trials 
Registry platform (www.who.int/trialsearch) to identify ongoing trials.  
Searching other resources 
The review authors examined the reference lists of included articles and relevant review articles 
identified through electronic searches. VL also searched for relevant dissertations in ProQuest 
Dissertations & Theses A&I. The review authors searched for information on ongoing and unpublished 
studies from experts working on new diagnostics for TB such as STOP TB Partnership’s New Diagnostic 
Working Group and FIND (the global alliance for diagnostics). A WHO public call was made between 
December 2023 and 15th February 2024 for ongoing and unpublished studies from manufacturers and 
researchers. 
 
Selection criteria 
Types of studies  
We included cross-sectional and cohort studies of the diagnostic accuracy of TB-LAMP against culture 
or composite (or both) reference standards. We included studies that reported the number of true 
positives (TP), true negatives (TN), false positives (FP), and false negatives (FN) or provided statistics 
that enabled their derivation. We excluded studies with a multiple-group design (i.e. diagnostic case 
control) because these studies may lead to biased estimates of diagnostic accuracy. We used abstracts 
to identify published or unpublished studies. 
Participants  
We included studies that evaluated the index tests for pulmonary TB and extrapulmonary TB in 
children presumed to have TB. We considered studies from all types of health facilities and all 
laboratory levels (peripheral, intermediate, and central) from all countries. We included studies that
