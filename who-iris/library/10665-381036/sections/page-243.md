Results of the search 
We identified 2806 research articles for screening from searches of electronic sources and screened 
the titles and abstracts of 1138 unique articles. Of the 1138 articles, we excluded 988. We performed 
full-text screening of 151 articles. We included four studies for pulmonary TB and one of them for both 
pulmonary and extrapulmonary TB. Figure 1 shows the flow of studies through the screening and 
selection process.
