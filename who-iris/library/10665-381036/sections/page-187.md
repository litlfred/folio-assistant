Implications for future research 
There is a need for high-quality trials that assess the impact of LC-aNAATs, LC-mNAATs and 
MC-aNAATs against relevant comparators on important patient outcomes to confidently 
inform policy decisions.  Studies included in this review were conducted within the WHO 
African region. Evidence from other WHO regions with high TB incidence such as South-East 
Asia and Western Pacific regions will help generalise findings on the impact of LC-aNAATs (38). 
Research on the impact of the three classes of NAATs on all-cause mortality and cure is 
needed. 
 
Conclusion 
There is insufficient evidence of the impact of LC-aNAATs and none for LC-mNAATs and MC-
aNAATs on patient outcomes in people being investigated for TB. There was no available 
evidence of the effect of LC-aNAATs relative to smear or culture on all-cause mortality, 
cure and LTFU. The use of Xpert Ultra, an LC-aNAAT, may decrease time to TB diagnosis 
and improve time to TB treatment initiation. However, we found that compared to smear 
microscopy or culture, there is limited evidence with moderate certainty of evidence to 
confidently conclude that Xpert Ultra has an impact on these outcomes.
