3.1. Systematic review of LC aNAATs (Xpert, Ultra and TrueNat) 
 
Summary of Findings: 
A total of 1534 studies were identified in the original search, after removing duplicates, 736 potentially 
relevant studies were screened. Of these, 107 were assigned for full-text review and were evaluated 
against the inclusion and exclusion criteria, and 29 studies were included in the final systematic review 
(Figure 1). Of the 29 included studies, 22 (76%) assessed Xpert, six (21%) Ultra, and one study (3%) 
evaluated TrueNat. Ten of the studies evaluating Xpert were cost-effectiveness analyses, and 12 were 
cost analyses. All the included Ultra studies and the study evaluating TrueNat were cost-effectiveness 
analyses.
