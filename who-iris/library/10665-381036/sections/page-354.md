4.4.3 Subgroup analyses based on the microbiological reference standard 
The comparative diagnostic accuracy for the parallel use of respiratory LC-aNAAT and LF-LAM versus any 
of the tests alone is shown for the following subgroups: symptomatic versus asymptomatic, CD4 cell 
count ≤ 200 versus > 200, and inpatients versus outpatients. We also show results limited to the 
following subgroups: screening positive for tuberculosis, with advanced HIV, and seriously ill. See 
Appendix 11: Summary table, comparative accuracy for parallel use (LC-aNAAT and LF-LAM) versus LC-
aNAAT alone by subgroup. 
Comparative accuracy stratified by symptomatic versus asymptomatic 
participants  
We identified 23 studies that reported data for participants with symptoms suggestive of tuberculosis 
involving 8,644 participants, 1,557 (18%) with tuberculosis. Most studies defined symptomatic 
participants based on a positive WHO four-symptoms screen, but some included prolonged cough or 
being seriously ill. Among symptomatic participants, the pooled sensitivity of parallel testing was 78.5% 
(95% CrI 73.8 to 82.8) and specificity 89.3% (95% CrI 85.1 to 92.6), respectively (Table 9). The pooled 
difference in sensitivity and pooled difference in specificity between parallel testing and LC-aNAAT was 
7.0% (95% CrI 3.8 to 11.7; 95% PI 0.5 to 48.2) and -6.8% (95% CrI -10.1 to -4.4; 95% PI -36.6 to -0.9). 
Eight studies reported data for asymptomatic participants, involving 1,370 participants, 55 (4%) with 
tuberculosis. Asymptomatic participants would have been enrolled based on low CD4 cell count, 
admission to hospital, initiation of antiretroviral therapy. Among asymptomatic participants, the pooled 
sensitivity of parallel testing was 53.5% (95% CrI 34.8 to 71.7) and specificity 96.1% (95% CrI 92.7 to 
98.2%) respectively (Table 2). The pooled difference in sensitivity and pooled difference in specificity 
between parallel testing and LC-aNAAT was 4.9% (95% CrI 1.0 to 14.9; 95% PI 0.6 to 24.1) and -2.5% 
(95% CrI -5.4 to -0.9; 95% PI -16.6 to -0.3).
