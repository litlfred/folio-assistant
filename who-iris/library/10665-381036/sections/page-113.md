Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose pleural tuberculosis in pleural fluid in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of pleural tuberculosis, against a microbiological 
reference standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 74.0% (60.8 to 83.9) and 
88.1% (78.8 to 93.6) from 13 Xpert Ultra studies (1041 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity) and very low certainty (specificity) evidence.  
 
Should LC-aNAATs be used to diagnose pleural tuberculosis in pleural fluid in adults and 
adolescents with signs and symptoms of pleural tuberculosis, against a composite reference 
standard? 
LC-aNAATs summary sensitivity and specificity (95% CI) were 43.6% (32.8 to 55.0) and 
99.2% (95.2 to 99.9) from seven Xpert Ultra studies (667 participants); low certainty 
(sensitivity and specificity) evidence.
