Figure 2. Risk of bias and applicability summary for each study 
 
Findings 
Main results are presented below with additional forest plots provided in Appendix 2 and summary 
of findings tables in Appendix 3. Figure 3 summarises the sensitivity and specificity of the studies 
included across and for each specimen type. Four studies (314 participants) assessed diagnostic 
accuracy using different specimen types. The sensitivities were between 50% and 100% and the 
specificities were between 67% and 100%. Given the mix of specimen types and paucity of data, we 
did not perform a meta-analysis to combine results across studies.
