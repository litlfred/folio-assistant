The sensitivities in the 10 studies (2127 participants) were between 0% and 96% and the specificities 
were between 96% and 100% (Figure 8). The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 69.9% (52.7 to 82.9) and 
the summary specificity was 98.8% (96.2 to 99.7).  
 
For a population of 1000 adults and adolescents where 100 have pulmonary tuberculosis on a composite 
reference standard, 80 would be LC-aNAAT-positive and 10 (13%) would not have tuberculosis (false-
positives); 920 would be LC-aNAAT-negative and 30 (3%) would have tuberculosis (false-negatives). 
 
 
Figure 8. Forest plot of LC-aNAATs for detection of pulmonary TB using a composite 
reference standard. Index test in all studies is Xpert Ultra. 
 
Studies are sorted on the plot by sensitivity (low to high) and specificity. FN: false negative; FP: false positive; 
TN: true negative; TP: true positive. All nine studies evaluated Xpert Ultra.
