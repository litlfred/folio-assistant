Methodological quality assessment 
Figure 2 summarises the results of the risk of bias assessment for one outcome from an iRCT. 
There was a low risk of bias across all domains for Esmail 2023 for the time to treatment 
outcome (33).  
 
Figure 2: Summary of risk of bias assessment for iRCT per outcome 
 
For Cattamanchi 2021 there were some risk of bias concerns for both outcomes because 
participants were screened for recruitment after randomisation (Figure 3) (32) . In addition, 
blinding was not feasible due to the nature of the intervention. No information was provided 
regarding deviations from the intended interventions. Information regarding the 
measurement of time to treatment and diagnosis per arm was also lacking. Lastly, the trial 
protocol did not pre-specify the analysis methods for time to treatment and diagnosis 
outcomes (34).
