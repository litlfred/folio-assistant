Figure 14. Risk of bias and applicability summary for pulmonary tuberculosis: nasopharyngeal 
aspirate and microbiological reference standard 
 
Seven studies (1,355 participants) evaluating nasopharyngeal aspirate against a MRS were included 
with sensitivities between 33% and 67% and specificities between 50% and 99% (Figure 15). Six 
studies (1,353) were included in a meta-analysis. The summary sensitivity (95% CI) was 46.2% (34.9 
to 57.9) and the summary specificity (95% CI) was 97.5% (95.1 to 98.7) (moderate-certainty 
(sensitivity) and high-certainty (specificity) evidence. 
 
For a population of 1,000 children where 100 have tuberculosis on culture, 68 would be Xpert Ultra 
positive; of these, 46 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (true positives) and 22 would not have 
pulmonary tuberculosis (false positives); 932 would be Xpert Ultra negative; of these 878 would not 
have pulmonary tuberculosis (true negatives) and 54 would have pulmonary tuberculosis (false-
negatives).
