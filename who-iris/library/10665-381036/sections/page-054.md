Study Identification and Characteristics 
For pulmonary and extrapulmonary tuberculosis detection, we identified 23 studies that evaluated the 
diagnostic accuracy of Xpert Ultra in sputum, gastric aspirate, nasopharyngeal aspirate, stool or 
extrapulmonary specimens. Table 1 describes the study characteristics, and the PRISMA diagram is 
displayed in Figure 1 (Page 2021). 
 
Figure 1. PRISMA Diagram 
 
Of the 23 studies included, 14 studies (61%) took place in high tuberculosis burden and 14 (61%) in 
high TB/HIV burden countries, with two studies (9%) performed in multiple settings of varying 
tuberculosis burdens. Of the 23 studies, 21 (91%) evaluated Xpert Ultra for pulmonary tuberculosis 
and 4 (17%) for extrapulmonary tuberculosis using MRS as a reference standard, and 15 studies 
(65%) evaluated Xpert Ultra against a CRS, all evaluated Xpert Ultra against an MRS.
