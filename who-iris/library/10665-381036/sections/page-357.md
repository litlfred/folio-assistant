4.4.4. Additional analyses 
Nine studies reported data for Xpert Ultra as LC-aNAAT involving 5,752 participants, 658 with 
tuberculosis (11%).  The pooled sensitivity of parallel testing was 77.1% (95% CrI (66.4 to 85.8) and 
specificity 88.5% (95% CrI 82.0 to 93.1), respectively (Table 15). The pooled difference in sensitivity and 
pooled difference in specificity between parallel testing and LC-aNAAT was 7.5% (95% CrI 3.0 to 15.2; 
95% PI 0.7 to 44.2) and -7.4% (95% CrI -13.1 to -3.8; 95% PI -36.6 to -1.1).
