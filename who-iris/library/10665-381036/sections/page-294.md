Incremental accuracy of the parallel use of LC-aNAATs combined versus LC-
aNAAT on stool alone based on the composite reference standard 
For children without HIV or HIV status unknown, within studies, incremental difference in sensitivity for 
parallel use of LC-aNAATs combined versus LC-aNAAT on stool alone ranged from 4.1% [Orikiriza 2021] 
to 31.8% [Chabala 2024]. The difference in specificity for parallel use of LC-aNAATs versus LC-aNAAT on 
stool alone was 0.0% for all studies [Ainan 2021; Babo 2023; Chabala 2024; Donkeng 2022; Hanrahan 
2018; Kabir 2021; M. P. Nicol 2013; Olbrich 2024; Orikiriza 2021; Wobudeya 2024] except for one with -
19.0% [Jaganath 2024]. See Figure 14. The pooled difference in accuracy of the parallel use of LC-
aNAATs versus LC-aNAAT on stool alone was 10.5% (95% CrI 6.9 to 15.0; PI 3.1 to 29.3) for sensitivity 
and −0.1% (95% CrI -0.7 to 0; PI -25.9 to 0) for specificity. See Figure 9. 
In CHIV, the pooled difference in accuracy of the parallel use of LC-aNAATs versus LC-aNAAT on stool 
alone was 8.5% (95% CrI 2.4 to 20.9; PI 0.9 to 11.4) for sensitivity and −1.4% (95% CrI -3.3 to -0.4; PI -9.1 
to 0) for specificity. See Figure 10.
