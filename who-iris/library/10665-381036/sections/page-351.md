Figure 12. Forest plot of study-specific estimates of difference in sensitivity and specificity between the parallel use of LC-aNAAT and LF-LAM versus LC-aNAAT alone for tuberculosis in adults and adolescents 
with HIV, based on the microbiological reference standard. The black squares represent the point estimate of one study, the black horizontal line its 95% confidence interval (CI). The QUADAS-C risk-of-bias 
judgements by study are included on the right. Note that study-specific 95% CIs sometimes contain impossible values (difference in sensitivity < 0 and difference in specificity > 0). This is because the method 
used to estimate standard errors does not take the dependence between the two tests into account.
