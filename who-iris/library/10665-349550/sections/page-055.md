5. Path to elimination: recognizing progress towards EMTCT in countries with a high burden of HIV, syphilis and HBV
A country seeking certification on the PTE for HIV and syphilis follows the same procedure as 
a country requesting validation of EMTCT. See Chapter 7 and the Governance guidance for the 
validation of elimination of mother-to-child transmission of HIV and syphilis (14) for details on  
the validation process.
