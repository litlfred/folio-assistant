6.4. Assessing ANC EMTCT service coverage in non-public settings
In addition to public sector health services, PMTCT services may be provided by private, 
nongovernmental organizations, faith-based organizations, non-health sector institutions, such 
as health services in correctional facilities and some other non-public entities. Ideally, national 
programme monitoring systems should include data from the non-public sectors. Thus, public and 
non-public data should not be separately reported for achievement of validation criteria. However, 
there should be evidence in the report that non-public data are included.
In some countries, it is also common for pregnant women to seek ANC and/or delivery care in both 
the public and non-public sectors. While it is often harder to capture data outside of the public 
sector, or to properly account for women who utilize services in both the public and non-public 
sectors (mixed usage), it is expected that data on the performance of these service indicators be 
provided to validation teams for assessment, if available. Evaluation of EMTCT services in the non-
public sector should be part of the programme assessment. This includes services such as ANC, HIV, 
syphilis and HBV testing, prevention and treatment. Evaluation should assess access, delivery and 
payment and how it compares with the public sector.
