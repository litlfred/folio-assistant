A1.1. Clinical follow-up of infants and young children exposed  
to HIV
The diagnosis of HIV infection in HIV-exposed infants and young children is complicated by the 
variable sensitivities of assays, ongoing HIV exposure (in utero, intrapartum and postpartum via 
breastfeeding) and the presence of maternal antibody for up to 12–15 months. For EMTCT, HIV-
exposed infants and young children should be followed for an appropriate period to determine  
their final infection status.
