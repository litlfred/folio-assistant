# 4. Determine the requirements

It is essential to thoroughly understand how users will engage with the system, the processes they will execute, and the data they will handle within the application before proceeding with the development and implementation of the digital reporting system. This section provides an overview of the key considerations for developing comprehensive requirements that will facilitate successful user adoption of the application.

| Inputs | Outputs | Resources (see page 50) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| • National health strategy, digital health strategy and health policies and scope for the reporting application<br>• Health information system, privacy, security and data use policies OR new and/or amended regulatory policies on substandard and falsified medical products<br>• Digital health enterprise architecture blueprint and high-level requirements for meeting interoperability standards (new or revised)<br>• Current state analysis report including infrastructure readiness OR situational analysis report, including digital systems landscape analysis<br>• Standard operating procedures for reporting substandard and falsified medical products<br>• Existing paper forms<br>• End user and key stakeholder interviews and insights<br>• Digital investment roadmap for the substandard and falsified reporting solution<br>• Governance structures and mechanisms updated and/or established<br>• Strategic goals for reporting of substandard and falsified medical products<br>• Stakeholder matrix | Documentation requirements including:<br>• User personas<br>• User scenarios<br>• As-is and to-be business processes<br>• Data dictionary<br>• Decision logic algorithms<br>• Functional and non-functional requirements<br>• List of indicators and calculation method | • Digital implementation investment guide (DIIG)<br>• Digital adaptation kits<br>• Human-centred approach for health<br>• User persona template (Annex 2)<br>• Key user personas (Table 7)<br>• Business process modelling and notation (Annex 3)<br>• Reference data elements (Section 4.4)<br>• Reference requirements (Section 4.5) |
