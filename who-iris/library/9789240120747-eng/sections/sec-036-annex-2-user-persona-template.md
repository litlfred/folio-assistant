## Annex 2. User persona template

A user persona template/worksheet that summarizes each and every type of end user (e.g. pharmacists, doctors, nurses, dentists, midwives, paramedics, laboratory technicians, public health workers, veterinarians and pharmacy technicians) should be completed.

| Section | Prompts and sample entries |
| --- | --- |
| **Persona occupation (e.g. nurse)** | — |
| **Demographics** | • Gender<br>• Age<br>• Community of origin<br>• Language(s) used<br>• Education level |
| **Expected roles and responsibilities** | • Daily<br>• Weekly<br>• Monthly |
| **Behaviour patterns** | • Regularly prescribes medications<br>• Uses electronic health records daily<br>• Attends medical conferences and workshops<br>• Dispenses or administers medications and monitors patient reactions<br>• Uses mobile apps for quick reference and reporting<br>• Participates in community health programmes<br>• Uses existing pharmacy dispensing/stock software |
| **Context description** | • Do they own their own mobile device? If yes, what kind?<br>• Level of familiarity with digital tools?<br>• What kind of environment is this persona working in?<br>• Rural or urban?<br>• Internet connectivity?<br>• Availability of electricity and water?<br>• Homogenous or heterogenous population? |
| **Challenges/pain points** | *Note: It is beneficial to include quotes given directly from interviews for the persona you are creating.*<br>• What are the routine challenges faced by this user?<br>• Sufficient training and performance monitoring?<br>• Workload/time constraints due to busy schedule?<br>• Complexity of existing reporting system?<br>• Lack of feedback on reported issues?<br>• Limited access to easy-to-use reporting tools?<br>• Too many reporting forms to complete? |
| **What does success look like** | *Note: This does not have to be strictly professional success. An overall perception of success can greatly impact performance in a professional setting. Sometimes the target health worker occupational group is a group of volunteers, so providing health services may not be their priority.*<br>• What are their motivations? Commitment to patient care, professional responsibility?<br>• Complete coverage of health care service providers?<br>• What indicators are they measured on?<br>• What are their performance management metrics?<br>• User-friendly (intuitive and easy navigation) platform?<br>• Availability in multiple devices (phone, tablet, desktop)?<br>• Feedback mechanism?<br>• Seamlessly integrated with other existing systems? |
