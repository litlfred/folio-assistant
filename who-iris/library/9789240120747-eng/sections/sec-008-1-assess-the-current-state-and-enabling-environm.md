# 1. Assess the current state and enabling environment

A comprehensive assessment of the current state and the enabling environment is necessary before implementing a digital reporting system for substandard and falsified medical products. This chapter outlines the areas that should be analysed and the factors to consider. The inputs needed to inform this step and the anticipated outputs of this phase are listed below.

| Inputs | Outputs | Resources (see page 50) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| • National health strategy, digital health strategy and architecture blueprint<br>• National health, data and other applicable policies applicable to substandard and falsified medical products<br>• Standard operating procedures for reporting substandard and falsified medical products<br>• List of existing and planned digital and paper reporting systems for substandard and falsified medical products<br>• Metrics on reporting such as reporting rates<br>• List of stakeholders<br>• List of existing digital health and supply chain systems | • Situational analysis report, including digital systems landscape analysis<br>• Stakeholder matrix | • Global digital health monitor<br>• HIS stages of continuous improvement toolkit<br>• Supply chain information system maturity model<br>• Digital transformation handbook for health supply chain architecture |
