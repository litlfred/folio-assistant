6.11.1	
Member States should urge vaccine manufacturers to set aside a portion of 
each production cycle of pandemic influenza vaccine for use by developing 
countries; and 
6.11.2	
The Director-General, consulting Member States and the Advisory Group, 
will convene an expert group to continue to develop international mechanisms, including existing ones, for the production and distribution of 
influenza vaccines on the basis of public health risk and needs during a 
pandemic, for consideration by the World Health Assembly in 2010.
