2.	 Objective
The objective of the Pandemic Influenza Preparedness Framework is to improve 
pandemic influenza preparedness and response, and strengthen the protection 
against the pandemic influenza by improving and strengthening the WHO global 
influenza surveillance and response system (“WHO GISRS”), with the objective 
of a fair, transparent, equitable, efficient, effective system for, on an equal footing: 
(i)	
the sharing of H5N1 and other influenza viruses with human pandemic 
potential; and
(ii)	
access to vaccines and sharing of other benefits.
