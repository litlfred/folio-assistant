6.6.1	
Upon request, Member States with advanced laboratory and influenza 
surveillance capacity are urged to continue to work with WHO and other 
Member States, particularly developing countries, to develop national laboratory and influenza surveillance capacity, including: 
(i)	
to conduct early detection, isolation and characterization of viruses; 
(ii)	 to participate in pandemic risk assessment and response;
(iii)	 to develop research capacity related to influenza; 
(iv)	 to achieve technical qualifications for consideration of laboratories 
as National Influenza Centres, WHO H5 Reference Laboratories and 
WHO Collaborating Centres on Influenza.
