When CAD is implemented, local calibration is advised to customize the score thresholds to the 
requirements of the programme (see the operational handbook for more details about how this can 
be done) (7). After this initial calibration, ongoing monitoring and analysis of CAD performance should 
be carried out to assess the correlation with human readers’ interpretations and with bacteriological 
confirmation, and with the proportion of images read as abnormal and requiring further investigation.
