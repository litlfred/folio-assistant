As in Experi-
ment 1, the best results were obtained with 10 units

The protection afforded by 14 x 0.075 ml of pooled
vaccine alone was also significant, while the mortal-
ity rates in the case of duck-embryo and HEP Flury

Serum alone (25 units of serum L or 10 units of
serum PIC) failed to protect animals, confirming
our earlier observations.
The results indicate that even when given in
identical
doses,
pooled
phenolized
vaccine
was
superior to the other two vaccines, whether given
before or after infection, alone or with different
doses of serum. The dose of duck-embryo vaccine
used in this experiment corresponded to five times
that recommended for human treatment.
