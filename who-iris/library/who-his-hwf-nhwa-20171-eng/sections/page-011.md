How is the NHWA
handbook structured?
Each of the 10 modules contains a set of indicators with 
declared policy relevance. These include both numeric and 
capability indicators that can provide information on regulation and other mechanisms related to the health workforce, 
and the status of the HRH monitoring and management 
system.
