2.
National
Regional and Global
Relevance: Why would a 
country engage in NHWA?
NHWA is relevant for national, regional and global stakeholders. In particular, it  can enable countries to develop 
evidence-based policies and plans for their health workforce 
and better understand and present their health workforce 
(HWF) data.
