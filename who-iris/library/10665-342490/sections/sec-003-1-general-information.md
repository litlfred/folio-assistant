GENERAL INFORMATION 
 Vaccine: Janssen Ad26.COV2.S COVID-19 vaccine, multi-dose vial. Each vial contains 5 doses of 0.5 ml 
 Schedule: One dose of 0.5 ml 
 Age indication: 18 years of age and older 
 
 Route and site of administration: Intramuscular, in the deltoid muscle of the upper arm
