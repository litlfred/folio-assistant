• 
Frozen vaccine vials may be thawed in the refrigerator (between +2 °C to +8 °C) This will take approximately 2 hours for a single 
vial and 12 hours for a carton of 10 vials. 

 NOTE: Do NOT refreeze thawed vaccine.
