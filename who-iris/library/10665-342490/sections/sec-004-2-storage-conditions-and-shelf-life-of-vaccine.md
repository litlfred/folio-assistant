STORAGE CONDITIONS AND SHELF LIFE OF VACCINE 
STORAGE CONDITIONS SHELF LIFE 
 
Unopened vaccine vials: 
Freezer: -25 °C to -15 °C 
24 months by expiry date 
• Prior to use, avoid vaccine exposure to direct sunlight and 
ultraviolet light. 
• Do NOT refreeze the vaccine, once thawed. 
Refrigerator: +2 °C to +8 °C 
Up to 4.5 months  
(not exceeding expiry date) 
Opened vial (after first puncture of the vial) *: 
• During use, vaccines can be handled in room light conditions.  
• Punctured vaccine vials should be discarded within 6 hours 
after first puncturing OR at the end of the immunization 
session, whichever comes first. 
Refrigerator: +2 °C to +8°C 
Up to 6 hours 
*Write on the vial the date and time of first opening (first puncturing of the vial).  

 NOTE: Keep the vials in the original carton in order to protect from the light and to record the expiry date for the different 
storage conditions, if applicable. Update the expiry date on the outer carton when moving unopened vial from the freezer to 
+2 °C to +8 °C storage. Cross out the original expiry date.  (Updated expiry date: up to 3 months at +2 °C to +8 ° C, not exceeding 
the original expiry date). The vaccine should be used or discarded by the updated expiry date.
