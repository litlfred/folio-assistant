MATERIAL NEEDED FOR VACCINATION AND SUPPLIES 
• 
Syringe for vaccine administration (0.5 ml or 1 ml syringe with 0.1 ml graduations) with 23-gauge  
(or narrower) needle 
• 
Sterile single-use antiseptic swabs 
• 
Safety box for sharps disposal 
• 
Medical treatment kits to manage allergic reactions in the event of an acute anaphylactic reaction 
• 
Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for vaccinators and recipients (gown; gloves; medical mask, face shield, goggles) as per the 
national infection prevention and control guidelines 
• 
Hand hygiene (alcohol-based hand rub or running water and soap) and surface cleaning agents
