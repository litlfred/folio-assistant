Treating uncomplicated P. falciparum malaria in special risk groups
First trimester of pregnancy
■
■Treat pregnant women with uncomplicated P. falciparum malaria during the first trimester 
with 7 days of quinine + clindamycin. (Strong recommendation). Source
Pregnant and breastfeeding women
■
■In women who are pregnant or breastfeeding, consider weekly chemoprophylaxis with 
chloroquine until delivery and breastfeeding are completed, then, on the basis of G6PD 
status, treat with primaquine to prevent future relapse. (Conditional recommendation, 
moderate-quality evidence). Source
