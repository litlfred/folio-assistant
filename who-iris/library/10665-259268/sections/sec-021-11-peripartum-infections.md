■A simple regimen such as ampicillin and once-daily gentamicin is recommended as first-
line antibiotics for the treatment of chorioamnionitis. (Conditional recommendation based 
on very low-quality evidence). Source
■
■A combination of clindamycin and gentamicin is recommended as first-line antibiotics for 
the treatment of postpartum endometritis. (Conditional recommendation based on very low-
quality evidence). Source
