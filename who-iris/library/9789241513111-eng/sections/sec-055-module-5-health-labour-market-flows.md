Module summary
Module 5 addresses the entries into and exits from the health labour market, and labour market 
imbalances. Indicators on entries differentiate the results of domestic efforts and dependency on foreign 
health workers; in addition, both voluntary and involuntary exits are monitored. The selected indicators 
relate to the GSHRH target on the reduction of barriers to access to health services, through the creation, 
filling and sustaining of jobs in the health and social care sectors.
