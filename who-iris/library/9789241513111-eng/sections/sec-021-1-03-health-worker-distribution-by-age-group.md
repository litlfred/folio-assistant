Indicator name
Percentage of active health workers in different age groups
Numerator
Number of active health workers in a specific age group
Denominator
Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts
Disaggregation
By occupation
Definition
Percentage of active health workers in the given age groups, by occupation.
Age groups considered are the following: > 25, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55–64, ≤ 65.
