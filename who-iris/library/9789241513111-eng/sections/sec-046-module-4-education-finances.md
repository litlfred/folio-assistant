Education finances 
Module summary
This module addresses financing of and investment in health workforce education and training. It provides 
a companion to Module 7 – Health workforce spending and remuneration. The module maps and tracks 
expenditure on health workforce education and training, and provides information on the cost of health 
workforce development.
