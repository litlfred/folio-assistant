Numerator
Not applicable

Not applicable
Disaggregation
Not applicable
Definition
This indicator is related to the collection of flow data for labour market entry indicators in Module 05 – 
Health Labour Market Flows. 
This indicator supports the GSHRH Global Milestone 4.1: By 2020, all countries will have made progress 
to establish registries to track health workforce stock, education, distribution, flows, demand, capacity 
and remuneration.
The following questions should guide a response to this indicator: 
1.	 Is there a system that provides information about the health workforce?
2.	 If yes, does this system provide information on the inflows of the health labour market?
