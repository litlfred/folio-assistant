Indicator name
National and/or subnational standards for social accountability in accreditation mechanisms are 
effectively implemented (Yes/No/Partly)
Numerator
Not applicable
Denominator
Not applicable
Disaggregation
By health workforce education and training programme
Definition
The following questions should guide a response to this indicator: 
1.	 Do national and/or subnational mechanisms for accreditation of health workforce education  
and training institutions and their programmes require compulsory reporting on  
implementation of national or subnational standards on social accountability?
2.	 Do the communities served by the health workforce education and training institutions 
participate in the decision-making of these institutions? 
3.	 Do students learn and train in the communities that the health workforce education and  
training institution serves (community placements)? 
4.	 Do health workforce education and training institutions measure their impact on the  
health system and populations they serve?
