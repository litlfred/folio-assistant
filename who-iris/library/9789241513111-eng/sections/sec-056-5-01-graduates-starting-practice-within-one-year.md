Indicator name
Ratio of previous year graduates who started practice to total number of previous year graduates
Numerator
Number of previous year graduates who started practice within one year after graduation
Denominator
Number of previous year graduates
Disaggregation
By occupation, by occupation and sex
Definition
Ratio of previous year graduates who started practice within one year after graduation to total number 
of previous year graduates, by occupation.
For physicians, graduates who started internship/residency training after graduation are included in the 
list of graduates who started practice.
