Ambulatory care provision refers to individuals and organizations that deliver personal health-
care services on an outpatient basis. This includes diagnosis, observation, consultation, treatment, 
intervention, rehabilitation services, and advanced medical technology and procedures even when 
provided outside of hospitals.
