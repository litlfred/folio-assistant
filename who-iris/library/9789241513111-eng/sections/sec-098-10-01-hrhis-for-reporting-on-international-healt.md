Dimension: HRHIS for international reporting
Abbreviated name
HRHIS for reporting on International Health Regulations
10– 01
Indicator name
Ability of HRHIS to generate information to report on International Health Regulations (Yes/No/Partly)
Numerator
Not applicable
Denominator
Not applicable
Disaggregation
Not applicable
Definition
This indicator will help assess whether the HRHIS has the capacity to report on IHR and submit core 
indicators to the WHO Secretariat annually, for example on indicator 08_08.
Glossary
International Health Regulations (2005)
Data reporting frequency 
Annual

•	 	Professional chambers
