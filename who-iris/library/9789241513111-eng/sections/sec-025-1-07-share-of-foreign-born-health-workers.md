Indicator name
Percentage of active foreign-born health workers
Numerator
Number of active foreign-born health workers
Denominator
Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts

Definition
This indicator will capture the information on health workforce coming from abroad.

•	 	Population census data (data mainly available on place of birth, not place of training)
