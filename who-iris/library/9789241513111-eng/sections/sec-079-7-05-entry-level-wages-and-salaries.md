7– 05
Indicator name
Average entry-level wage and salary excluding social contributions
Numerator
Not applicable
Denominator
Not applicable
Disaggregation
By occupation, by facility ownership, by occupation and facility ownership
Definition
Average wage or salary received by health workers when entering the active health labour market, 
excluding social contributions.
Disaggregation by facility ownership is suggested to distinguish between public versus private wages 
and salaries.
Glossary
•	 	Active health worker
•	 	Occupation
•	 	Remuneration
