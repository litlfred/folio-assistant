Definition
This indicator is related to the collection of distribution data for labour market stock indicators in Module 
01 – Active Health Workforce Stock. 
This indicator supports the GSHRH Global Milestone 4.1: By 2020, all countries will have made progress 
to establish registries to track health workforce stock, education, distribution, flows, demand, capacity 
and remuneration. It also supports the achievement of health emergency preparedness under SDG 
3.D.1.
The following question should guide a response to this indicator: Is there a health workforce information 
system that provides information about the health workforce with geocoded information on its place of 
activity?
