Apart from the benefits at national level, the NHWA can also ease the comparability of the HWF 
landscape at regional level and globally. Through country reporting and international compar-
isons, the NHWA may facilitate cross-country capacity-building, information and knowledge 
exchange, as well as more sophisticated research about future trends of health workforces 
within and across systems.
