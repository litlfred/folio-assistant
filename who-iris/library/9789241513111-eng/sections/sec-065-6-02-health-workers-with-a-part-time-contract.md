Dimension: Working time
Indicator name
Percentage of employed health workers with a part-time contract 
Numerator
Number of active health workers who have an employment contract that is below the national standard 
working hours, defined in headcounts
Denominator
Total number of active health workers, defined in headcounts
Disaggregation
By occupation, by occupation and facility ownership
Definition
Ratio of employed health workers with a part-time contract to the total health workforce.
