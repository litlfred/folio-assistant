Module summary
This module contains indicators on skill mix composition of the health workforce. It builds upon the other 
module indicators by decomposing the structure of the workforce by sector and occupational group. The 
last two indicators provide information on the availability of human resources to implement the International 
Health Regulations (WHO 2005a) and the existence of an applied epidemiology training programme.
