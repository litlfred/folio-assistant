HIGHLIGHTS
•	Of the 65 IPPP PC recipient countries in the 
2022-23 biennium, 37 (59%) now have a plan 
based on WHO Guidance, of which five updated 
their plan in 2022. 
•	Countries are developing their pandemic plans 
in a step-wise approach requiring consistent 
multi-stakeholder engagement. Nine multi-
sectoral planning workshops were conducted 
in six countries from three regions to assess 
preparedness and response capacities to push 
forward their planning process. 
•	Five countries exercised their IPPPs in 2022 
focusing on outbreak response and multi-
sectoral coordination. Two of these exercises 
were supported by a simulation exercise 
package developed by WHO to test national 
procedures and policies on multisectoral 
coordination, RCCE, and triggers for decision-
making. These exercises will support the iterative 
improvements of emergency preparedness and 
response protocols taking into account lessons 
from the COVID-19 response. 
•	In complementary activities and to update WHO 
guidance for future pandemic preparedness, 
WHO is developing a respiratory module 
as part of the Preparedness and Resilience 
for Emerging Threats initiative. Within this 
module, updated guidance on preparedness 
for a respiratory pathogen pandemic including 
influenza, has been drafted. Complementing 
this is the development of a checklist aimed at 
supporting national authorities to develop and/
or revise existing pandemic preparedness plans. 
This checklist builds on the WHO checklist for 
pandemic influenza risk and impact management: 
building capacity for pandemic response and 
lessons learned from the COVID-19 pandemic. 
In addition, a simulation exercise package, a 
framework for resilient surveillance of respiratory 
viruses, and a partners engagement forum 
will complement efforts to strengthen global 
coherence and coordination.
