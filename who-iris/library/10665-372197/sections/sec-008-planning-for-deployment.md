HIGHLIGHTS
HIGHLIGHTS
•	As part of a foresight project initiated to 
estimate national, regional, and global 
needs for pandemic influenza preparedness 
products (vaccines, antivirals, diagnostics, 
and therapeutics), a series of workshops and 
expert interviews were held. Together these 
helped to further develop the scenarios that 
will result in quantitative needs estimates. 
Participants in these workshops discussed 
opportunities and threats in relation to WHO 
strategies, and how to achieve public health 
goals for pandemic preparedness. They also 
outlined the key decisions that WHO would 
need to make based on the resulting needs 
estimates and in consideration of planning 
assumptions that align with different public 
health goals. A report summarizing this body 
of work is being developed. 
•	The guidance on the developing and updating 
national deployment and vaccination plans 
for influenza and other respiratory viruses 
is currently being revised incorporating 
lessons learned from the country planning 
and deployment of COVID-19 vaccines. It has 
recently been published for public comment 
through which further revisions will be made. 
•	Updates to this guidance were presented 
to 11 countries at a regional meeting in 
the Americas with the aim of linking the 
development of National deployment and 
vaccination plan (NDVP) to pandemic 
influenza preparedness planning. 
•	WHO participated in five missions in two 
regions supporting six countries with the 
aim of strengthening seasonal influenza 
vaccination programmes and policies. 
Additionally, one of these missions allowed 
for the review of the mRNA technology 
transfer programme including in the 
context of influenza vaccine production. 
The strengthening of seasonal influenza 
vaccination programmes provides a critical 
foundation for strengthening pandemic 
influenza preparedness.
