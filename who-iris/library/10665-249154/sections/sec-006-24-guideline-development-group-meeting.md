Following the initial GDG meeting on TB-LAMP in 2012, a GDG meeting was convened by WHO 
in January 2016 to review the evidence from 20 new studies, of which 13 studies conducted 
in 11 countries met the inclusion criteria. The guideline methodologist participated in the initial 
planning and scoping and in the development of the key questions for the GDG meeting but did 
not participate in the meeting.
