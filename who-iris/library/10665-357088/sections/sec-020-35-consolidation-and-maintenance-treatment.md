The 2018 WHO recommendations for consolidation and maintenance treatment for cryptococcal 
disease remain unchanged (16).
Consolidation (2018 recommendation)
Recommendation  
Fluconazole (800 mg daily for adults, 6–12 mg/kg per day for children and adolescents up to a maximum of 800 mg 
daily) is recommended for the consolidation phase (for eight weeks following the induction phase).  
Strong recommendation; low-certainty evidence
Maintenance (2018 recommendation)
Fluconazole (200 mg daily for adults, 6 mg/kg per day for adolescents and children) is recommended for the 
maintenance phase until immune reconstitution (CD4 >200 cells/mm3) and suppression of viral loads on ART.  
Strong recommendation; high-certainty evidence
