ART-experienced people who develop cryptococcal meningitis should be evaluated for 
potential underlying ART treatment failure, ideally through confirmation by HIV viral load. 
ART switches should be deferred by four weeks following an amphotericin B–based induction 
regimen or 4–6 weeks following a fluconazole-based induction regimen.
