The objective of these guidelines is to provide updated, evidence-informed recommendations 
for treating adults, adolescents and children living with HIV who have cryptococcal disease. 
These guidelines are aimed at HIV programme managers, policy-makers, national treatment 
health care professionals and people living with HIV.
