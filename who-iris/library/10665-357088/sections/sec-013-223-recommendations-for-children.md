These recommendations apply to adults and adolescents with advanced HIV disease. The 
decision not to extend these recommendations to children was based on the recognition that 
cryptococcal disease in this age group is rare, even in countries with high incidence (4,10).
These 2022 guidelines provide a new recommendation for the preferred antifungal regimen 
for the induction phase of treatment.
