Studies can be carried out to fill gaps in information on the following subjects:
Structural factors:

— availability of media infrastructure;

— existing policies/legislation/practice in matters such as selling blood, HIV
testing, prostitution, IV drug use, men having sex with men;

— existing media policy regarding dissemination of messages about sex,
condoms and drug use;

— networks and associations such as NGOs and community organizations to
reach more vulnerable populations, and

— availability of trained human resources for IEC activities.
Personal factors:
— who is at risk;

— existing sexual or drug injecting behaviours and what are the desirable
changes;

— factors which might facilitate or inhibit changes;

— who are the influencers for different groups;

— access to media, and media habits (viewing/listening/reading); and

— access to and use of health services, particularly STD treatment and condoms.
