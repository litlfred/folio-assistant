HIV prevention among PrEP users will contribute to equitable health outcomes by sustaining 
their health and the health of their sexual partners. Access to PrEP also provides opportunities 
for access to sexual and reproductive health services addressing multiple health needs. 
Considering the evidence reviewed and its discussions, the GDG noted that offering CAB-LA as 
an additional PrEP option could increase equity by reaching more individuals who could benefit 
from PrEP and who would prefer injectable PrEP over other options. However, the GDG noted 
that inequality in health outcomes could be exacerbated globally through differences in access 
to CAB-LA between and within countries.
