Acknowledgements 
This operational handbook was prepared by Sabine Verkuijl, Annemieke Brands, Kerri Viney and 
Tiziana Masini, under the guidance of Farai Mavhunga and the overall direction of Tereza Kasaeva, 
Director of the World Health Organization (WHO) Global Tuberculosis (TB) Programme.
The WHO Global TB Programme gratefully acknowledges the contributions of all experts who were 
involved in the production of the operational handbook. Unless otherwise specified, those listed below 
work in the WHO Global TB Programme.

Kerri Viney and Tiziana Masini.
