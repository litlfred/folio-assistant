Children can present with TB disease at any age, but most commonly between the ages of 1 and 
4 years in high TB burden settings. Children who develop TB disease usually do so within a year of 
TB infection. The presentation of TB in children is an indicator of recent and ongoing transmission of 
Mycobacterium tuberculosis in the community (4).
Infants and young children, especially those aged under 2 years, are at higher risk of developing 
disseminated disease and tuberculosis meningitis (TBM), which are associated with high morbidity and 
mortality (4). Adolescents with TB usually present with infectious TB disease, as typically seen in adults

from paediatric to adult health service provision (5, 6).
