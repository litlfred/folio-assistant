The target audience for this handbook includes NTPs and other child health programmes that provide 
care for children with or at risk of TB, including maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health 
programmes, HIV services, and PHC programmes. The handbook also targets paediatricians and 
other health care workers (HCWs) in the public and private sectors, school health services, civil society 
and community-based organizations, and health care educators.
