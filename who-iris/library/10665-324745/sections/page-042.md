This indicator will track the proportion of viral load results that 
were returned to sites within one month of the sample being 
taken. This will allow programmes to track the receipt of test 
results at sites. This can be examined during service and data 
quality assessments and/or during routine site visits.
Laboratory equipment and maintenance should be monitored 
closely, since this will affect the processing and return of 
viral load results. For example, it is important for laboratories 
to document the duration of any breakdown in viral load 
instruments and the reasons for the breakdowns..
