In contrast to adults, cryptococcal disease is 
rare, especially among children younger than 
five years of age. In a laboratory-based survey 
performed in South Africa, the incidence of 
cryptococcal disease was estimated to be 47 per 
100 000 children living with HIV in 2007 (26), 
and within two trial cohorts of children living 
with HIV, no cases of cryptococcal disease were 
reported for children younger than five years 
(27). Screening for cryptococcal antigen and pre-
emptive therapy is therefore only recommended 
for adolescents.
