Additional counselling and support are needed 
with earlier antiretroviral therapy initiation. 
Counselling tools have been updated to reflect 
newer optimal regimens and formulations. 
Adolescents generally face challenges in 
adhering to medication, and adolescent-
friendly services, including enhanced adherence 
counselling, adolescent peer counsellors and 
support groups, may be especially valuable.
