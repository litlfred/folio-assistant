■Ensure that protocols and standard operating procedures are in place for obtaining and 
transporting specimens from young children. Training and expertise in paediatric phlebotomy 
and ordering urine bags, gastric washings, sputum induction and stool collection and 
processing need to be considered.
	
■Explore the capacity for antibiotic resistance surveillance to ensure that the optimal first-line 
treatment for severe bacterial infections is being used.
