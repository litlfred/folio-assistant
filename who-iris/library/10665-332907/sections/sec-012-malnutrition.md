Severe acute malnutrition is common at HIV 
diagnosis and carries a high risk of mortality. 
The availability of malnutrition treatment 
programmes and commodities, including ready-
to-use therapeutic food where appropriate, can 
be considered as essential components of HIV 
treatment programmes for children.
