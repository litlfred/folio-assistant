■Health-care providers should be sensitized on child-specific issues such as growth monitoring 
and other routine child health interventions.
	
■Training and capacity building for staff should be implemented in sites where these 
recommendations are to be implemented.
	
■Ensure that the advanced HIV disease package is included in any curriculum of training on HIV 
related to children or adolescents.
	
■Uptake of TB screening specific to children is challenging. Ensure appropriate adaptation of 
existing screening tools for the advanced HIV disease package that focus on the different ways 
that TB can present in children.
	
■Limited access to new diagnostics should not prevent clinical diagnosis and treatment. A 
negative test should not prevent treatment initiation if TB is strongly clinically suspected. 
Clinical diagnosis will remain necessary for many children.
