HPV vaccines
HPV vaccines have the potential to strengthen
interactions among different health services and to
provide a new way of preventing a common cancer
on a very large scale, as well as reducing the more
widespread burden of genital dysplasia and warts.
Some of the key features of the vaccines are dis-
cussed below.
