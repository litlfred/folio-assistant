WHO Editorial Style Manual

Alphabetical arrangement

In lists of names and addresses, indexes, alphabetical reference lists,
etc., the following general rules should be followed:

¢ When the first component of a family name is a particle such as
“de”, “Le”, “al-”, “Van” or “von”, the first letter of the particle
should normally be used for alphabetization, unless established
usage or another specific reason dictates otherwise.

© Names beginning with the prefix “Mac” or “Mc” should be

arranged as if the letter “a” were present in all cases.

° When Chinese personal names are written in the traditional way,
the single-syllable family name comes first and should be used for
alphabetizing (e.g. Dr Hu Ching-Li should be listed under “H”).

¢ In the alphabetization of chemical names, Greek letters and
italicized prefixes should be ignored.
