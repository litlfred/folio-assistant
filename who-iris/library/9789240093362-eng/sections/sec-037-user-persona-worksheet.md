ANNEX 4.
User persona worksheet

A user persona worksheet that summarizes each and every type of end-user (e.g. community health worker, district health officer, health service user) should be completed.

| Section | Prompts |
| --- | --- |
| **Persona title (e.g. nurse)** | |
| **Name, photo and type of persona** | • Name (real or illustrative)<br>• Photo or illustration of persona (helps with visualization and storytelling)<br>• Health worker occupational group |
| **Demographics** | • Gender<br>• Age<br>• Community of origin<br>• Language(s) used |
| **Expected roles and responsibilities** | • Daily<br>• Weekly<br>• Monthly |
| **Actual roles and responsibilities** | • Daily, weekly, monthly<br>• How does this person spend their time?<br>• What is a typical day |
| **Context description** | • Do they own their own mobile device? If yes, what kind?<br>• Level of familiarity with digital tools?<br>• What kind of environment is this persona working in?<br>• Rural or urban?<br>• Internet connectivity?<br>• Availability of electricity and water?<br>• Homogenous or heterogenous population?<br>• Distance to nearest referral facility? |
| **Challenges** | *Note: It is beneficial to include quotes given directly from interviews for the persona you are creating*<br>• What are the routine challenges faced by this user?<br>• Long distances travelled without reliable mode of transport?<br>• Sufficient training and performance monitoring?<br>• Workload? |
| **What does success look like** | *Note: This does not have to be strictly professional success. An overall perception of success can greatly impact performance in a professional setting. Sometimes the target health worker occupational group is a group of volunteers, so providing health services may not be their priority.*<br>• What are their motivations?<br>• Complete coverage of health service users?<br>• Being able to afford food for children?<br>• Being promoted?<br>• Others?<br>• What indicators are they measured on?<br>• What are their performance management metrics? |
