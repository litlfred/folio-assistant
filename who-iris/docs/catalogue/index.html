The catalogue, as a graph

The replica pages show what IRIS looks like. This one shows what this catalogue knows — and, more usefully, what it records that it does not know.

The gap is the point, not a backlog. This instance models 235 node(s) against 273,559 items across 1,057,223 files upstream. Cataloguing by reference means recording that something exists without holding it, so a small number here is the design rather than a shortfall.
