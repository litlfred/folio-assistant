    Module schemas/library/recording

    A recorded meeting or talk as a library entry, and its transcripts. Bean folio-assistant-1r0p.

    Two records:

    • recording.json (folio-recording/v1) says what the recording is: its title, date and spoken language, and which bytes were looked at (source, the same _tech_meta block every rung writes). It is the entry's kind marker, as referenced.json is for a recorded source.
    • transcript/<lang>.json (folio-transcript/v1) holds one transcript per language: the source-language transcript a recogniser produced, and each translation of it.

    A transcript is a narrative

    A recogniser's output is somebody's account of the speech, not the speech, so a transcript carries the narrative review state (schemas/content/narrative.ts): who drafted it, and whether a human has confirmed it. The narrative's rules are applied by parsing the transcript AS a narrative (its segments joined), so they are not restated here and cannot drift: anything written names its drafter, an agent names its model, and only a human confirms.

    The recogniser is recorded, not inferred

    A source-language transcript names the recogniser that produced it: the tool, its version, the model file and that file's sha256. The same audio through a different model is a different transcript, and the comparison the audio-transcription skill asks for is only meaningful when each side says what produced it.

    A translation says what it was translated from

    translated_from names the source-language transcript's language and the sha256 of that file's segments, so a translation of a transcript that has since been corrected reads as stale rather than current. Segment timings are the source's, one for one: a translation re-times nothing.
