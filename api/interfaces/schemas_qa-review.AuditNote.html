    Interface AuditNote

    Why a decision was made, or why a finding in it was not acted on.

    Required on every Decision and on every overrule of a mechanical finding. The rationale is prose in the author's own voice; cites is what makes it checkable.

    interface AuditNote {
        author: ReviewerRef;
        cites: Citation[];
        id: string;
        proposed_by?: string;
        rationale: string;
        written_at: string;
    }
