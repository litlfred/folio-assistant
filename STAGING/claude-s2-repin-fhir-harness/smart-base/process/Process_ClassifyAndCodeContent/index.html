summaryClassifies and codes L1 extracted statements (recommendations, implementation practices, remarks, and proposed coverage exclusions) and L2 DAK slots (personas, business processes, guideline domains, conditions, interventions, medicines, and GRADE ratings) fol…
descriptionClassifies and codes L1 extracted statements (recommendations, implementation practices, remarks, and proposed coverage exclusions) and L2 DAK slots (personas, business processes, guideline domains, conditions, interventions, medicines, and GRADE ratings) following the terminology-coding methodology and the classify-and-code-content skill (bean folio-assistant-4nfs). The authoring agent runs terminology-lookup across the four-rung reuse-first precedence ladder, records provenance (declared, context, inferred) and candidate status, and stages the result for review. When the L1 extraction proposes exclusions in exclusions.proposed.json, a person in the l1-extraction-reviewer lane reads each proposed exclusion against the printed page (sections/page-NNN.md) and signs or rejects it (owner ruling 3b, 2026-10-12). A person in the terminologist / ontologist reviewer lane then adjudicates the candidate codings, SKOS match precision (exactMatch vs closeMatch), and any Rung 4 local glossary candidates.
