5
Size and spacing
Leave “exclusion zones” around the logos (at least 1cm width distance) around the logos to ensure they 
maintain visual impact.
Also the minimum logo size is 3cm (the smallest size at which the logo is still legible in print and on screen)
For other sizes, no less than 1/4 width of logo.
