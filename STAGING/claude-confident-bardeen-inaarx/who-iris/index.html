IRIS

The primary objective of the Institutional Repository for Information Sharing (IRIS) is to provide free digital access to the scientific and technical publications of the World Health Organization (WHO), including contributions from its Country Offices, Regional Offices, and Headquarters. Additionally, IRIS encompasses the mandates established by the Organization’s Governing Bodies in collaboration with its Member States.
