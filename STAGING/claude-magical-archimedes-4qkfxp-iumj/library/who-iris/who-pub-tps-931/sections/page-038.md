4. Acknowledgements. If the entire table is reproduced with little
or no change from another source, this should be acknowledged
in a statement such as:

Reprinted, by permission of the publisher, from Crompton DWT
et al., eds. Ascariasis and its public health significance. London,
Taylor & Francis, 1985.

When editing tables, ensure that each one is internally consistent
(check, for example, for errors in adding up columns of figures or
calculating percentages). Every table must also agree with, and be
referred to in, the body of the text. For typesetting, make sure that
column alignments, rules and typeface requirements are clearly marked
and that footnotes cannot be confused with running text.

Some of these points are illustrated in the example below; the precise
format for the title and use of boldface and italic will depend on the
style adopted for the publication in question.
