A

actor

A concrete participant, human, agentic or mechanical, that persists across every process and takes on roles. Which login is which actor is the data store's to know.

Folio Assistant platform terms · https://litlfred.github.io/folio-assistant/folio-assistant-core/ns#glossary/platform/actor · source cat-harness/skills/folio-core/role-model.md

associated harness

A harness this one knows by name, site and repository, and never loads, builds or copies.

permission

One ODRL rule allowing an assignee an action, optionally on a target and within constraints.

  • exactMatch: www.w3.org/ns/odrl/2/Permission
policy

Which Actor may do which action, and where, written as W3C ODRL 2.2 permission and prohibition rules scoped by Process, Task and Role.

  • exactMatch: www.w3.org/ns/odrl/2/Policy
