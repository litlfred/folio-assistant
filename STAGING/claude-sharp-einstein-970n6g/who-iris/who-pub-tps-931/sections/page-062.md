Genetics

Genes and chromosomes

The International Committee on Standardizing Human Gene No-
menclature has developed a system knownas the International System.
for Human Gene Nomenclature, but this is not universally accepted.
Human genes are generally denoted by italicized symbols composed of
capital letters, sometimes with the addition of an arabic numeral (e.g.
CF, G6PD). Lower-case letters are commonly still used in blood-group
symbols (e.g. Fy, Rh). Phenotype symbols are set in roman type (e.g.
ADA 1).

Human chromosomes may be referred to by number or letter (1-22, X,
Y) or by group (A-G).

For bacteria, genotype symbols usually consist of three italic lower-case
letters (e.g. ara} and phenotype symbols of three roman letters, the first
of which is a capital (e.g. Met).
