descriptionOne pass over one L2 DAK component: the agent edits it, runs its checker, renders and stages it with a before/after, and hands it on; review forks off beside that hand-on and never holds it up, because adjudication is QA, not a gate. That is the owner's ruling of 2026-10-14. Called by each authoring track of l2-dak-authoring.bpmn (Process_L2Dak), one call per component pass. The call's inputs, which are the only things that differ between components (skill l2-component-edit-review, "The inputs, per component"): - component: personas and user scenarios (C2, C3), business processes (C4), core data elements and glossary (C5) with their terminology bindings, decision-support and scheduling logic (C6, C7), indicators and requirements (C8, C9); - authoring skill: l2-dak-authoring, bpmn-authoring, dmn-authoring or terminology-management, named on the calling activity, so it joins this process's skills along the call path (review-task.bpmn's scoped composition); - renderer and diff: what the reviewer looks at, before and after; - checker: the mechanical checks the agent runs before asking anyone; - reviewing role: the person who takes on this process's reviewer lane for the call. One process rather than one per component because nothing but those inputs differs: DMN's test cases are its checker, BPMN's picture is its renderer, the glossary's term diff (dak-data-dictionary-diff.ts) is its diff and its binding check its checker, and traceability to L1 is the checker of personas, scenarios and requirements.
