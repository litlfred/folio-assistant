Spelling of words that commonly
cause difficulty

The general rule in WHO is to follow the spelling given in the latest
edition of The concise Oxford dictionary; where it gives alternative
spellings or forms, use the one given first (e.g. fulfil, fulfill) or the one
to which you are referred from an entry giving a variant spelling (e.g.
“tire, US var. of tyre”: use “tyre”).

There are, however, a number of exceptions, owing to established
usage within WHO and to the recommendations of international
nomenclature-setting bodies in a variety of disciplines. This list
therefore gives the preferred WHO spellings of many of the commonly
encountered exceptions; it also includes The concise Oxford dictionary
spellings that should be followed for many words that often cause doubt
or difficulty, and gives some guidance on usage and choice of words.
