Bootstrap terms

المصطلحتسميات بديلةالتعريف
العقدة—ملف، أو جزء من ملف يمكن الوصول إليه بجزء IRI، يُسمّى بمعرّف IRI.
المرجع—تسمية عقدةٍ لعقدة أخرى بواسطة معرّف IRI الخاص بها.
مخطط العقدة—مخطط يمكن التحقق من عقدة وفقه: JSON Schema، أو مخطط خارجي منشور مثل مخطط BPMN.
نوع العقدة—اسم مقترن بمخطط عقدة.
النوع الفرعي—نوع عقدة يتطلّب مخططها كل ما يتطلّبه مخطط نوع عقدة آخر، فكل عقدة تستوفيه تستوفي الآخر أيضاً.
مثيل العقدة—عقدة تسمّي نوع عقدتها في $schema أو في الترويسة، وتستوفي مخطط العقدة لذلك النوع.
نوع الرسم البياني—مجموعة مسمّاة من أنواع العقد.
الإعلان—مستند JSON باسم <name>.json يحدّد اسماً ويسرد مُدخلات الأدلة ومُدخلات الملفات.
الامتداد—حقل في الإعلان، أو في أحد مُدخلاته، غير معرَّف هنا. القارئ الذي لا يتعرّف على الحقل يتجاهله، ويبقى باقي الإعلان على معناه.
الرسم البياني الفرعي—مُدخل دليل في الإعلان: معرّف ودليل وأنواع الرسوم البيانية التي تنتمي إليها أنواع مثيلات العقدة فيه.
الأصل—مُدخل ملف في الإعلان: ملف واحد عن المستودع نفسه، يبيّن حقلُه role الغرضَ منه.
الرسم البياني المعرفي—مخططات العقد ومثيلات العقد والأصول التي يسردها إعلان واحد، بحسب نسخة واحدة من المستودع. وعند نشرها بصيغة JSON-LD، تشكّل عبارات مثيلات العقدة في كل رسم بياني فرعي رسماً بيانياً مسمّى واحداً.
التبعية—مُدخل needs في الإعلان يسمّي رسماً بيانياً معرفياً آخر. يجوز أن تشير المراجع من مثيلات العقدة في هذا الرسم إلى الرسم المسمّى، ولا يجوز العكس أبداً.
المحتوى—مثيلات عقدة تصف موضوع الرسم البياني المعرفي، كالسجلات والمستندات والفهارس ومصطلحاتها، لا طريقة العمل به.
المهمة—وحدة عمل، تُوصَف بما تحتاجه لتبدأ وبما تُنتجه.
الفاعل—شخص أو وكيل أو برنامج قادر على تنفيذ المهام.
المهارة—A Node Instance stating a responsibility an Actor carries out to complete a Task: as natural-language instructions to follow, as a contract to be tested against, or both. (غير مترجَم)
الدور—مثيل عقدة يسمّي دوراً يتولّاه الفاعل لتنفيذ المهام، ويسرد المهارات التي يحتاجها ذلك الدور.
عقدة العملية—عقدة تدفق BPMN: نشاط (مهمة أو نشاط استدعاء) أو بوابة أو حدث.
تدفق التسلسل—وصلة موجّهة في عملية BPMN من عقدة عملية إلى التي تليها.
العملية—مثيل عقدة يحوي عملية BPMN تنسّق المهام: تقع عُقد العملية فيها، المرتبطة بتدفقات التسلسل، في مسارات يسمّي كلٌّ منها دوراً.
الإطار—رسم بياني معرفي تحوي رسومه البيانية الفرعية مهارات أو أدواراً أو عمليات: ما يستخدمه الفاعل للعمل مع رسم بياني معرفي، بما في ذلك هذا الرسم نفسه.

سائر المصطلحات المحرَّرة، أبجدياً

Folio Assistant platform terms (cat-harness)

المصطلحتسميات بديلةالتعريف
إطار مرتبط—إطار يعرفه هذا الإطار باسمه وموقعه ومستودعه، ولا يحمّله ولا يبنيه ولا ينسخه أبداً. وهو العلاقة الرابعة إلى جانب «يعتمد على» (needs) و«يشير إلى» (remoteGraphs) و«يستخدم» (dependencies).
سياسة—أيّ فاعل يجوز له أيّ إجراء وأين، مكتوباً في صورة قواعد إذن وحظر وفق W3C ODRL 2.2 محدّدة النطاق بالعملية والمهمة والدور.
إذن—قاعدة ODRL واحدة تتيح لمُسنَد إليه إجراءً، اختيارياً على هدف وضمن قيود. والإذن بإجراء أوسع يتيح كل إجراء يندرج تحته.
تنفيذ المهمة—تنفيذ واحد لمهمة BPMN يقوم به فاعل في دور وفق سياسة، يُسجَّل نشاطاً وفق W3C PROV-O خطّتُه هي المهمة.
فاعل—مشارك محدّد، بشري أو وكيل ذكي أو آلي، يستمر عبر كل العمليات ويتولّى أدواراً. أما أيّ حساب دخول يقابل أيّ فاعل فذلك من شأن مخزن البيانات.
دور—المسار: شخصية يتقمّصها الفاعل بحكم المسار الذي يعمل فيه، وتحمل المهارات التي تحتاجها أنشطة ذلك المسار.
user story (غير مترجَم)—What a reader acting in a Role is trying to do, told as that role: as a , I want , so that . The story points at its role; the role names no story. _(غير مترجَم)_
test run (غير مترجَم)—One measurement of a Skill: which skill it tests, the cases it exercised, and a hash of the data and a hash of the process that produced the result, so a third party can redo it. Its cases are checked against the skill’s input and output contracts. (غير مترجَم)
voice (غير مترجَم)—A named set of writing rules for prose, active in the roles and processes it names. The voice points at the role it is written for; the role names no voice. (غير مترجَم)
external schema (غير مترجَم)—A record of a specification the platform depends on but does not hold: which authority publishes it, which version is conformed to, and the operative terms used from it. (غير مترجَم)
KG QA report (غير مترجَم)—A committed QA sidecar recording, for one knowledge-graph subject such as a process, role or skill, which of its references fail to resolve under each audit criterion. (غير مترجَم)
todo (غير مترجَم)—One person’s outstanding work, as a content node the folio owns, tagged with the roles, processes and tasks it concerns. Not the agent work plan, which is beans. (غير مترجَم)
bean (غير مترجَم)—One item of the agent work plan, held by the beans CLI in the committed beans/ store, with a status, a parent and the beans it blocks. (غير مترجَم)
JSON Schema (غير مترجَم)—A JSON document that describes the shape of other JSON documents. In the platform, a node schema and a skill’s input and output contracts are each a JSON Schema file. (غير مترجَم)

GDHCN terms

المصطلحتسميات بديلةالتعريف
Business Owner Representative (غير مترجَم)—The individual identified by a GDHCN Participant as having the primary business and programmatic responsibility for the GDHCN Participant for the implementation of the use cases covered by a Trust Domain. (غير مترجَم)
Certificate Authority (غير مترجَم)—An entity that stores, signs, and issues cryptographic certificates and certifies the ownership of a public key by the named subject of the certificate. This allows GDHCN Trust Network Gateway participants to rely on signatures or assertions made in relation to the private key corresponding to the certified public key. Certificate Authorities may be operated by different types of entities, including, but not limited to, a private-sector Certificate Authority, a ministry or agency responsible for ICT/eGovernment services, a Ministry of Health, or another competent authority within the jurisdiction. It is the responsibility of each GDHCN Participant to determine the appropriate arrangement for its Certificate Authority, in accordance with applicable laws and policies within its jurisdiction, to guarantee security and proper data management. Each GDHCN Participant must identify, establish, or liaise with the relevant Certificate Authority, as applicable and impose on the latter all requirements of this document. (غير مترجَم)
Encryption Key Certificate Governance (غير مترجَم)—The process and technical specifications regarding the management and use of encryption keys for Verifiable Digital Health Certificates, Public Key Infrastructure, and for securing connections with Trusted Services and the Trust Network Gateway. (غير مترجَم)
Eligible GDHCN Participant (غير مترجَم)—An Eligible GDHCN Participant is one of the following: • a WHO Member State or Associate Member, or sub-national unit thereof; • a State Party to the International Health Regulations (2005), or sub-national unit thereof; • the United Nations (UN) and other intergovernmental organizations in effective relations with WHO; • a fund, programme, specialized agency, or related organization within the UN system; or • an organization officially delegated by one of the organizations mentioned above that can abide by the GDHCN Terms of Participation and fulfils one or more of the following health service functions: • Public Health Agency; • Health Professions Education Accreditation Agency; • Health Services Licensing Agency; or • Public Health Security Agency. and, once accepted to the GDHCN, after the successful conclusion of the Onboarding Process, will be referred to as GDHCN Participant throughout this document. An Eligible GDHCN Participant that has initiated the Onboarding Process will be referred to as an Eligible GDHCN Applicant throughout this document. (غير مترجَم)
GDHCN Secretariat (غير مترجَم)—The WHO Secretariat serves as the operational and management leadership of the GDHCN, and in this role, is referred to as the GDHCN Secretariat. (غير مترجَم)
GDHCN Trust Network (غير مترجَم)—The GDHCN Trust Network is a Trust Network comprised of the GDHCN Secretariat and GDHCN Participants. The GDHCN Trust Network operationalizes Trust Domains through a Trust Network Gateway by enabling bilateral verification and utilization of Verifiable Digital Health Certificates and the utilization of Trusted Services by GDHCN Participants. (غير مترجَم)
Health Professions Education Accreditation Agency (غير مترجَم)—An organization or organizational unit that is responsible for establishing and/or implementing procedures for accreditation of health professions education institutions. (غير مترجَم)
Health Services Licensing Agency (غير مترجَم)—An organization or organizational unit that is responsible for establishing and/or implementing procedures for licensure, and the licensing of organizations or individuals to provide clinical, health system or public health services within their jurisdiction. Licensing comprises processes through which duly authorized governmental authorities, such as recognized professional organizations, grant permission to an individual or healthcare organization to operate or engage in a medical occupation or profession. (غير مترجَم)
Key Master Representative (غير مترجَم)—The individual identified by a GDHCN Participant as having the primary responsibility for the management of the Public Key Infrastructure, including the generation of any needed public-private key pairs, and the configuration and management of the connections between the GDHCN Participant’s Public Key Infrastructure and the GDHCN Trust Network Gateway. (غير مترجَم)
Legal Representative (غير مترجَم)—The individual designated by a GDHCN Participant as having the primary responsibility for reviewing the requirements related to a Trust Domain on behalf of this GDHCN Participant. This person should be someone that the GDHCN Secretariat can consult in relation to any legal or policy issues that may arise, such as changes to this document or changes to one of its Annexes that defines a Trust Domain. (غير مترجَم)
Letter of Application (غير مترجَم)—A formal application sent via a verifiable and secure channel from an Eligible GDHCN Participant to the GDHCN Secretariat to join the GDHCN Trust Network and participate in a particular Trust Domain. (غير مترجَم)
Onboarding Process (غير مترجَم)—The processes required for an Eligible GDHCN Participant to join the GDHCN Trust Network. At the successful conclusion of the Onboarding Process, the Eligible GDHCN Participant shall be considered a GDHCN Participant. Sometimes simply referred to as “Onboarding”. (غير مترجَم)
Public Health Agency (غير مترجَم)—An organization, or organizational unit, that is responsible for establishing procedures for and/or implementation of activities related to the protection, promotion, and improvement of public health within a specific jurisdiction or domain. May also be referred to as a Ministry of Health, Department of Health, or Public Health Authority. (غير مترجَم)
Public Health Security Agency (غير مترجَم)—An organization, or organizational unit, that is responsible for establishing procedures for and/or implementing required activities, both proactive and reactive, for minimizing the danger and impact of acute public health events that endanger people’s health across geographical regions and international boundaries. (غير مترجَم)
Public Key Infrastructure (غير مترجَم)—A system of hardware, software, policies, procedures, and roles that support the management of public keys and supports authentication, encryption, integrity, or non-repudiation services via a database of digital public keys. (غير مترجَم)
Technical Representative (غير مترجَم)—The individual designated by a GDHCN Participant as having the primary overall responsibility for the security, technical matters and systems infrastructure of this GDHCN Participant for the applicable Trust Domain, including ensuring compliance with technical specifications. (غير مترجَم)
Trust Domain (غير مترجَم)—Consists of: • Defined use cases and business processes related to the utilization of Verifiable Digital Health Certificates; • the open, interoperable technical specifications that identify or define the applicable Trusted Services and Verifiable Digital Health Certificates; and • a set of policy and regulatory standards describing expected behavior of GDHCN Participants in relation to operation of the Trusted Services and utilization of Verifiable Digital Health Certificates (e.g. data minimization, privacy, scope of use). (غير مترجَم)
Trust Network (غير مترجَم)—A Trust Network is a means to authenticate the encryption public keys used by participants within a network to perform encryption services, verify digital signatures, establish secure connections between systems, and otherwise make use of encryption public keys. (غير مترجَم)
Trust Network Gateway (غير مترجَم)—The open-source software and its IT operational infrastructure, utilizing open standards, for a Public Key Infrastructure and metadata management services which is used to operationalize one or more Trust Domains. (غير مترجَم)
Trusted Service (غير مترجَم)—A service (digital or otherwise) related to the issuance, management, verification, exchange, or other relevant processes, of Verifiable Digital Health Certificates which is defined using open, interoperable digital health standards. (غير مترجَم)
Verifiable Digital Health Certificate (غير مترجَم)—A digital representation of a data set comprising a certificate or document, designed for a set of specific clinical or public health use cases which is defined using open, interoperable digital health standards; that contains within, or is associated to, a digital signature which can be verified by the public key of a public-private encryption key pair, and which is issued by a GDHCN Participant. (غير مترجَم)
