summaryReview a rendered folio and publish it, or send it back with changes.
descriptionReview a rendered folio and publish it, or send it back with changes. Shared by every authoring process: authoring-a-document (this instance) and authoring-a-paper (folio-assistant-sci) call it after they validate and render, so review, publish and committee feedback are drawn once (shared block G6 of the BPMN guidance review, owner Q-BR-7 A, 2026-11-01). What differs by folio kind stays with the caller: its writing loop, its own check (the content profile for a document, the Lean build for a paper), and its renderer. It ends one of three ways. `Published`: the caller closes its bean. `Changes asked` and `Feedback in: changes asked`: the caller goes back to its writing loop.
