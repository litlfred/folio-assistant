labelLogger
descriptionA machine that accepts log messages and puts them somewhere a person can read. It receives and records, and takes no part in what happens next. Its skill is its contract, `logger`: record a complete message unchanged and attributed to its actor (owner, 2026-10-16). Composing the message is a different skill, `log-message`, which belongs to whoever is DOING the thing being logged. The Logger is not the author of a log line; it is where the line goes. That distinction is why `log-message` takes `actor` as a required input: the Logger cannot infer who acted, and a log that names the logger rather than the actor records nothing worth having.
