who-iris — documentation

What this harness is, what ingesting it cost, and where the ingested copy is meant to end up. These pages are about the work; the catalogue itself, and the replica rendered from it, are served separately by the library handler.

This is not a copy of IRIS. who-iris models the WHO Institutional Repository for Information Sharing by reference: 13 nodes of a 273,559-item 361.55 GiB repository, of which 3 items are held here. Everything on these pages was transcribed from captures the owner supplied — iris.who.int is not reachable from the environment that renders them, so nothing here was fetched. The figures come from the catalogue rather than from this sentence, which is why they are the same ones the replica shows.
