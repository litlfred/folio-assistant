descriptionHow a data sharing agreement is drafted, negotiated, signed, published with a version, a hash and a catalogue record, and later amended, renewed or ended. It covers the agreement as a document folio only: processes P1, P2 and P14 of the DSA process catalogue. Two entry points: a request for an agreement (P1, then P2), and a change to one in force (P14). An amendment or a renewal with a new end date runs P1 and P2 again and publishes a new version linked to the old one; the old version stays readable. Standards named: ODRL 2.2 (the Agreement), DCAT 3 (catalogue record, distributions, checksums, versions) and DCMI Terms (dcterms:source from the policy to the text). No field is defined here. Steps no skill covers yet are user tasks with a no-skill reason starting "Gap:". Owner's option B13-B, 2026-10-30; folio-assistant#2702. Consent rules (owner, 2026-10-30), which every agreement drawn here keeps. 1. Legal basis: an agreement carries the odrl:obtainConsent duty only when consent is its legal basis; treatment, public-health law and research waivers do not rest on consent (P1.2a, P1.3). 2. Version pinning: a Consent cites the agreement's fixed-version URL and hash (FHIR R4 Consent.policy.uri), and for a computable agreement the ODRL policy uid is that same URL (P2.1, P2.2b, P2.3); an amendment that widens use needs new consent, one that narrows use does not (P14.3). 3. Plain language: every agreement that needs consent publishes a plain-language summary, hashed with the agreement, and that summary is what the person consents to (P1.2b, P2.2a). How a person gives, withdraws or is asked again for consent is the trust layer's, not this diagram's.
