nameMerge and publish a content change
summaryMerging an approved content change on the owner's explicit confirmation and publishing it: remove the staging preview, rebuild the main site and watch it.
descriptionMerging an approved content change on the owner's explicit confirmation and publishing it: remove the staging preview, rebuild the main site and watch it. A change that is not merged keeps its preview. Called from content-change-review after the committee approves. Approving is the committee's judgement and merging is a separate, confirmed act (bean en2d), which is why the merge opens this phase rather than closing the review.
