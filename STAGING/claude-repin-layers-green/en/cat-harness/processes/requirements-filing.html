Requirements filing: promote, organise, trace, supersede, or retire

Process_RequirementsFiling · strict · 8 step(s)

How a proposal in docs/proposals/.md is promoted into docs/requirements/.md when its feature ships, or how an existing filed requirement is amended in place, superseded by a new requirement (status: superseded + supersededBy: req:), or retired (status: retired) without deleting historical records. Every filed requirement keeps its flat path docs/requirements/.md (id: req:) and is organised in docs/requirements/index.md under its canonical concern group from code-lists/concern-group.json, verified by check:requirements, and signed off by the Business Analyst / Owner.
