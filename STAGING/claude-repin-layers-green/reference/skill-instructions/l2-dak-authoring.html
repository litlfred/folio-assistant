l2-dak-authoring

Skill id: l2-dak-authoring · Package: authoring-who-smart-guidelines · Named by l2-dak-authoring.bpmn steps Personas and scenarios, Data dictionary and core data elements, Indicators and requirements, all in the Business analyst lane and each a call into l2-component-edit-review.bpmn, and by dak-l3-ig.bpmn step Map L2 → L3, beside l3-fhir-authoring, before that diagram calls fhir-harness’s generic l3-fhir-pipeline.bpmn.

Author the L2 layer of a WHO SMART Guideline: the human-readable, machine-processable Digital Adaptation Kit that sits between a narrative guideline (L1) and FHIR artefacts (L3).

Sourcing. The DAK component set and the L1–L4 knowledge layers are WHO’s, defined in the SMART Guidelines documentation and realised in WorldHealthOrganization/smart-base. This skill states how to work them in this harness — which lane, which inputs, which tooling — and defers to WHO for what a DAK component must contain. Where the two disagree, WHO is right and this file is stale.

Input and output

The contract is input.schema.json and output.schema.json.

  • In: dakComponent (required), sourceGuideline (required), existingContent, sprintNumber; as stage 4 of smart-guideline-end-to-end, also stage 2’s l1Kg and stage 3’s l2Dependencies (l2-dependency-choice’s output).
  • Out: artifacts (each an l2Artifact: path, type, description), status, validationIssues, stagingLinks. Stage 5 (l3-dependency-choice) takes the artifacts of every pass as its l2Assets.

dakComponent is the unit of work. Author one component per pass; a DAK is completed component by component, and a sprint that touches five half-way is harder to review than one that finishes two.

What L2 is for, and the mistake to avoid

L2 is structured but not yet FHIR. Its job is to be reviewable by a clinical SME who does not read FHIR, while being regular enough that L3 can be derived from it rather than written twice.

So the failure mode is authoring L2 as if it were L3 — reaching for profiles, slicing and invariants at a stage whose reviewer is a clinician. If a decision can only be expressed in FHIR, it belongs in l3-fhir-authoring; if it can be stated as a data element, a decision rule or an indicator, it belongs here.

Business processes and decision logic are the sibling skills bpmn-authoring and dmn-authoring; terminology binding is terminology-management.

Every track is edited, then reviewed alongside

Each of the six tracks (the three above, BPMN, DMN and terminology) is a call activity into Process_L2ComponentEditReview (l2-component-edit-review). In that subprocess the agent:

  1. edits the component with the track’s skill;
  2. runs the component’s checker;
  3. renders and stages it with a before/after;
  4. hands it on with adjudication: pending.

A person in the component’s reviewing role reviews it at the same time, on the staged preview, through cat-harness’s Process_Review. Review forks alongside the hand-on and never blocks it. The renderer, checker, diff and reviewer for each component are in that skill’s table, so they are not repeated here. The clinical SME’s Clinical validation after the join is the whole-DAK reading across components. It does not repeat these per-component reviews.

Tooling

smart-base carries the extractors that read authored L2 artefacts — dd_extractor, dt_extractor, req_extractor, extractpr. Load a checkout rather than vendoring it; smart-base-tools states why and how, and what happens when SMART_BASE_HOME is unset (the skill degrades to skip, it does not report a clean run over a toolchain it never had).

Where this sits

l2-dak-authoring.bpmn carries <cat-harness.processes:policy enforcement="advisory"/>: it is a per-content-type process, and this package owns what “adequate” means in its domain. That is licence to adapt the sequence, not to skip the gate — the base processes it feeds (editing-hci-validation, content-lifecycle) stay strict.

When this skill runs as stage 4 of smart-guideline-end-to-end, clinical validation is that stage’s adjudication. It runs beside the next stage and does not hold it up: the agent goes on to choose L3 dependencies while the L2 assets are reviewed one by one.
