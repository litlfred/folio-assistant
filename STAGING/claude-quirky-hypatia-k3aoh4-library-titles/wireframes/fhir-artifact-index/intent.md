# fhir-artifact-index — as-is intent

## Covers

The kind viewer for the `fhir-artifact-index` graph. One page per instance that holds an index, all generated by Tool `ig-pages` (`fhir-harness/scripts/gen-ig-pages.ts`), and each stamped with `renders:` / `rendered-by: ig-pages` front matter:

- `smart-trust/docs/index.md`, published at `/smart-trust/`
- `smart-base/docs/index.md`, published at `/smart-base/` (with `--summary`)
- `smart-immunizations/docs/index.md`, published at `/smart-immunizations/` (with `--summary`)

The harness tile for each instance's `fhir-artifact-index` graph links the page. Stage C3 of the smart-* separation (#1767).

### How this was drawn

From the committed Markdown that `gen-ig-pages` writes, not from a built site: the proxy refuses `litlfred.github.io`, and there is no just-the-docs gem here. The drawing is of the smart-base page (the `--summary` form). The smart-trust page is the same, minus the harness section and with "— artefact index" in its title.

## Who it is for, and what they need to do

- **Readers:** an IG author or a reviewer who wants to know what an IG publishes, and which of it this checkout holds.
- **Tasks:**
  - see at a glance how many artefacts there are, and how many are held here rather than referenced upstream
  - see where the index came from (which published files it was reconstructed out of)
  - find one artefact by category and reach either its own page or its published representations

## What it must show

1. The IG banner. Its identity (package id, version, canonical) is the index's own, never a chrome borrowed from another IG; a status watermark only when the chrome is this IG's.
2. For a `--summary` page, the instance's harness section first.
3. The census: artefacts indexed, referenced, materialized, IG and FHIR versions.
4. Provenance: the published files each part came from.
5. The sidecar surface (labelled by the caller: "DAK API" for the WHO IGs, "API sidecars" by default).
6. Every artefact, grouped by the IG's own categories, folded.

## Findings

1. **The status tags are colour-only on the default scheme.** `.st-held` (#0d6e5e) and `.st-ref` (#6b5b95) are drawn as text in a bordered pill. On a dark background both are low contrast. The word inside carries the meaning, so this is a contrast finding rather than an information-by-colour-alone one.
2. **The category tables are wide.** Four columns, with canonical URLs and four format links per row. At 390 px they scroll sideways inside their wrapper. The folded `<details>` keeps the page short, but an opened group is hard to read on a phone.
3. **Links out are not marked.** "Published as" links leave the site for the IG's published output; nothing says so.
