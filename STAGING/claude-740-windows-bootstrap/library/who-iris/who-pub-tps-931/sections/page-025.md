WHO Editorial Style Manval

Ifa footnote refers to a quotation, the reference mark should be placed
after the final quotation marks.

Editor’s notes

Notes supplied by an editor, translator or compiler in a work including
original notes by the author must be differentiated from the original
notes. Such notes may be numbered in sequence with the author’s
notes.

Paragraphing and style

The paragraphing of footnotes should be in keeping with the overall
design of the book.. They may be set flush or in regular indented
paragraph style. Each footnote should end with a full stop.
