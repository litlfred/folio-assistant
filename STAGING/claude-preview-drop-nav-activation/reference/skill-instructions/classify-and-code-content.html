classify-and-code-content

Skill id: classify-and-code-content · Package: authoring-who-smart-guidelines · Methodology: terminology-coding · Process: classify-and-code-content.bpmn (Process_ClassifyAndCodeContent) · Tool: terminology-lookup · Bean: folio-assistant-4nfs

An agent proposes classifications, codes, and coverage exclusions; a person in the named reviewer lane judges and signs them. This skill governs all three lanes of classify-and-code-content.bpmn:

  1. authoring-agent lane — identifies classifiable slots in L1/L2 content, runs terminology-lookup down the four-rung reuse-first ladder of terminology-coding, records provenance (declared, context, inferred) and status: "candidate", and stages the candidate codings and any proposed L1 coverage exclusions for human review.
  2. l1-extraction-reviewer lane — a person acting as l1-extraction-reviewer (owner ruling 3b, 2026-10-12) reads each proposed exclusion in exclusions.proposed.json against the printed page in sections/page-NNN.md and either signs it (signedOffBy) or rejects it back to extraction curation.
  3. terminologist (terminologist / ontologist reviewer) lane — a person acting as terminologist reviews the candidate concept codings, SKOS mapping predicates (exactMatch vs closeMatch per ontologist), and proposed local glossary terms (status: "candidate" per glossary-terms), and records the review verdict.

Input and output

  • Input contract: input.schema.json
  • Output contract: output.schema.json

Step 1 — Agent: classify and code slots with terminology-lookup

For each slot in an L1 extraction or L2 component, run the terminology-lookup Tool:

bun run smart-base/scripts/terminology-lookup.ts \
  --slot <condition|intervention|persona|medicine|domain|business-process|recommendation-strength|recommendation-direction|evidence-certainty> \
  --query <term-or-code> \
  [--vocabulary <guide-vocabulary.json>] \
  [--generic <smart-base-generic-personas.json>] \
  [--scheme <scheme-id>] \
  [--out <result.json>]

Follow the reuse-first precedence of terminology-coding §2:

  1. Rung 1 (rung-1-target-guide): reuse the target guide’s own persona or business process from l1-scenario-vocabulary/v1 whenever its scope covers the statement.
  2. Rung 2 (rung-2-smart-base): fall back to smart-base generic personas (DAK.Persona.*), GRADE code-lists/*.json, or the guideline-domain SKOS scheme (clinical, public-health, health-system-strengthening).
  3. Rung 3 (rung-3-external-standard): resolve against the slot’s authoritative external standard (ICD-11, ICHI / CDHIv2, ISCO-08, ATC), distinguishing skos:exactMatch from skos:closeMatch per ontologist.
  4. Rung 4 (rung-4-local-candidate):
    • For an unmatched persona or domain concept, record a local glossary candidate (status: "candidate", source: "extracted") per glossary-terms and emit WARNING <slot>-glossary-candidate.
    • For an unmatched business process, never invent a process: record could-not-determine and emit WARNING process-not-determined.

Step 2 — l1-extraction-reviewer: read proposed exclusions against the page and sign

When recommendation-candidates writes exclusions.proposed.json, every entry carries:

{
  "signerRole": { "role": "l1-extraction-reviewer" }
}

and no signedOffBy field. Until a person in the l1-extraction-reviewer lane signs the entry, l1-coverage counts the sentence as unaccounted and coverage remains below 100 %.

To review a proposed exclusion in Lane_ExtractionReviewer of classify-and-code-content.bpmn:

  1. Open the printed page beside the work list. Locate location (p<page>:s<n>) in library/<doc_id>/sections/page-<NNN>.md (and the corresponding rendered page image when layout is ambiguous).
  2. Read the sentence in place. Verify the surrounding paragraph, heading, and table context — never judge an exclusion from the isolated sentence string alone.
  3. Check the proposed reason against the five closed reasons:
    • background-fact — epidemiology, disease burden, history, or biological fact with no normative action for a guideline user;
    • manufacturer-statement — a product insert or manufacturer specification quoted as context rather than a WHO recommendation;
    • editorial — licence text, imprint, how-to-read-this-document prose, chapter navigation checkpoints, or glossary definitions;
    • research-question — a gap or priority for future research, not a current practice or recommendation;
    • duplicate-of:<id> — a verbatim or near-verbatim repetition of a statement already captured under <id>, verified against <id>’s statement text.
  4. Sign or reject:
    • Sign: if the sentence is genuinely non-normative for the stated reason, record signedOffBy (the reviewer’s handle) and timestamp in the curation / exclusions file and re-run l1-recommendation-candidates so coverage updates.
    • Reject: if the sentence states a recommendation, an implementation practice, or a remark that belongs in L1, reject the exclusion with a reason so the authoring-agent re-curates the location as a node.

Step 3 — terminologist: review candidate codings and glossary candidates

In Lane_Terminologist of classify-and-code-content.bpmn, a person acting as terminologist (equipped with ontologist, glossary-terms, and terminology-management) judges each staged coding:

  1. Scheme and rung check: verify that the slot was coded against the scheme named in terminology-coding §1 and that no higher reuse rung held an applicable concept.
  2. Precision check (exactMatch vs closeMatch): confirm that a narrower or broader concept was not asserted as exactMatch.
  3. Candidate glossary adjudication: for each Rung 4 glossary candidate (status: "candidate"), either promote it to status: "authored" (checking definition quality and skos:broader / skos:closeMatch links), replace it with an existing code from Rungs 1–3, or reject it.
  4. Record verdict: record the review decision (approve, request-changes, or reject) in the mapping (review: {by, at}) or component QaReview.
