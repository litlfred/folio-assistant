titleCI health watcher
descriptionReads whether a workflow is actually working on the default branch, and reports it. A mechanical participant in the CI process: it runs a fixed program and exercises no judgement, so anything needing a decision belongs in another lane. Its three reporting rules are the judgement-free ones — "could not check" is never rendered as green, a red that has not re-run in a week is flagged as possibly stale rather than as an active fire, and a red whose workflow file changed after the failing run is reported superseded. Each is a statement about evidence, not a verdict on what to do about it.
