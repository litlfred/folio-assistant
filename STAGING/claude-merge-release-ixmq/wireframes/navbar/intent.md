# Navbar — as-is intent

## Covers

This is the chrome every just-the-docs visualiser sits inside, the landing page that the harness dividers open, and the folio glass that sits over every page.

- `cat-harness/docs/_includes/title.html`: the sidebar title and theme avatar mark.
- `cat-harness/docs/_includes/nav_footer_custom.html`: the ☰ / × open control, the **Harnesses** dividers and their graph links, and ⌂ home.
- `cat-harness/docs/_includes/harness_details.html`: "What each harness holds" on the page a divider opens.
- `cat-harness/docs/_includes/landing.html`: the collapsed **Stickies** panel.
- `cat-harness/docs/_includes/footer_custom.html`: the no-JS "Open notes" listing and the build stamp.
- `cat-harness/docs/_includes/head_custom.html`: the metas the glass reads (`fa-tiles`, `fa-todo-src`, `fa-zoom-src`, `fa-staging`).
- `cat-harness/docs/index.md`: the landing page.
- `cat-harness/docs/assets/js/docs-ui.js`:
  - `mountActionTiles`: the ◐ / ⊕ / ▦ header controls, the action panel (with its own Settings view) and the search field
  - `mountNavIconRow`: Todos, Beans, Processes, Knowledge graph, More
  - `mountDocumentIndex`: "On this page"
  - `mountInstanceGraphs`: "Folders"
  - `mountGlass`: the "▾ Folio" handle, the sheet, the shelf of cards, the bottom tile strip, and the Todos and Settings panels
- `cat-harness/docs/assets/css/docs-ui.css`: the 56 px strip at rest and the 264 px open state at 800 px and wider, and the glass (`.fa-glass-*`).
- `cat-harness/docs/assets/harness/tiles.json`: the same tile list, published for pages Jekyll did not build.
- Data: `cat-harness/docs/_data/harness.json`, generated by `cat-harness/scripts/harness-tiles.ts`, `graph-tiles.ts` and `sync-docs-harness.ts` (`harnesses`, `navbar.icons`, `navbar.folders`, `tiles`).

### How this was drawn

The Jekyll site still cannot be built here: there is no just-the-docs gem, and the proxy refuses `litlfred.github.io`. The sidebar and landing are drawn from the templates, CSS and generated data, as before.

The glass is new, so it was **rendered**, not only read. Playwright (`/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`, at 1280×800 and 390×844) drove two pages served from `cat-harness/docs` through `page.route`:

1. The committed generated page `cat-harness/library/who-iris/`. It loads `docs-ui.css` and `docs-ui.js` itself. On it I pressed the first row's "Pull out to folio".
2. A static harness page. It carries the metas `head_custom.html` emits, filled from `_data/harness.json`: `fa-tiles`, `fa-todo-src` → `assets/todos/index.json`, `fa-zoom-src` → `assets/semantic-zoom.json`, `fa-fsh-guts-src`, and `fa-staging` empty, then `fa-staging` set. It also carries a bare `.side-bar` / `.main-content` skeleton, and it loads the committed `docs-ui.css`, `themes.css`, `avatars.css` and `docs-ui.js`.

On the harness page I opened the glass, the Todos panel, the glass Settings panel, and ▦ → Settings. The harness has no just-the-docs CSS, so its sidebar and header positions are not evidence. Only the glass, which is fixed and body-level, is drawn from the render.

## Who it is for, and what they need to do

The template comments quote the owner:
- the dividers are *"like a giant tabbed folio"*: *"a thing you REACH FOR"*, not a dashboard
- *"harnesses should be collapsible"*, and on 2026-09-23 *"harnesses start closed"*
- closed means a strip *"icon width thick"*, not gone
- home stays at the bottom
- the glass: *"where are the todo, fsh guts etc tiles on bottom of glass?"*, *"tile to change folio settings … should be able to set opactiy"*, *"library items need avatar (book's)"*, and *"start with the glass being 20% opaque with a blur effect"* (bean `zrvt`)

The declared interaction profile is low-dexterity. That is why the tab bodies are large targets, and why the glass has −/+ buttons beside every chord.

- **Reader:** anyone on any docs page, including a low-dexterity or touch reader.
- **Tasks:**
  - know which instance they are in
  - jump to a harness and to its graphs (library, docs, processes …)
  - reach the harness actions (todos, beans, processes, KG)
  - see the current page's sections
  - get home
  - reach settings, language, QR and search
  - pull their folio down over the page: see what they pulled out (with its avatar), move and size it, pull a todo onto it, reach the declared tiles, and set how the glass looks

## What it must show (read off the sources)

- **Top:** the instance mark and title, the scheme / language / actions controls, and the icon row. The icon row comes from `navbar.icons`: close, todos, beans, processes, kg, launcher.
- **Middle:** "On this page", when the page has at least 2 h2/h3 headings. Then "Folders", 25 entries for cat-harness, with no-viewer kinds rendered as unlinked text. Then the theme's page list. All of these **arrive folded**, with their counts on the summaries.
- **Bottom:** "Harnesses (5)", **folded on arrival**. These are the instantiated harnesses: smart-trust, WHO IRIS, Folio Assistant, C@T Harness, and Bootstrap as the floor. Each divider carries its mark or initial, its label, and its graph links. Unlinked kinds carry a note ("no viewer yet" or "no viewer by design"). ⌂ home comes last.
- **Landing:**
  - Stickies (5), collapsed
  - title, lede, and Get started / Install / View on GitHub
  - one section per harness: description, three stats, viewer links, and "Gaps in this instance's declaration (n)"
  - then the authored guide
- **The glass** (every page), pulled down with "▾ Folio":
  - a see-through sheet: 20 % tint, blurred (`rgba(20,20,24,.2)`, `blur(6px)`)
  - the cards the reader pulled out, each solid, with its avatar (a book's cover, else its kind avatar), its name as a link, and ✥ move, − smaller, + larger and × back to the library view
  - "Your folio is saved in this browser only …"
  - one panel at a time, above the strip
  - the **bottom tile strip**, fixed to the viewport's bottom edge: **Todos** and **Settings**, which are the glass's own chrome, then every declared tile with the `glass` surface, except `todos`. A `staging-only` tile (fsh-guts) is shown only on a STAGING preview.

## Findings

1. **At rest the strip is marks with no labels.** Changed by #1022: the four divider initials are gone, so the harnesses are **not reachable from the strip at all** until the sidebar is opened and "Harnesses" unfolded. That is two actions, and on a touch tablet at 800 px or wider it is ☰ first. What remains is five unlabelled glyphs (▤ ◍ ⇶ ⌘ ▦) and ⌂. Their accessible names exist, but a sighted mouse reader has to hover each one.
2. **Two dividers open the same page.** Folio Assistant and C@T Harness both have `href: "/"`, which is the landing page the reader is already on. The difference only shows as two sections further down that page.
3. **The harness descriptions leak authoring notes and formatting into the landing page.**
   - Folio Assistant's description is a naming rationale: *"NAMED `folio-assistant-checkout` rather than `folio-assistant`…"*, with literal backticks.
   - C@T Harness's description is five newline-separated alternative spellings ("caaat-harness ca&at-harness .c&at-harness c@t-harness"), which run together as one line.
4. **Each harness's navigation now appears in four places.** The graph kinds appear as the divider's links in the sidebar, as "Folders (25)" for cat-harness, as "visualisations you can open" on the landing, and now as the glass's bottom strip (23 tiles, which is the declared-tile list rather than the divider list). They are generated from one declaration, so they agree. But the strip's captions are the tile titles ("Skills — cat-harness", "folio-assist-core-schemas"), and the sidebar's are the kind names ("skills", "schemas"), so the same destination carries two names.
5. **The open sidebar is long even with its caps.** This is eased, not fixed. With every group folded on arrival, the open sidebar is short until the reader unfolds something. Unfolded, it is "On this page" (11), the 27-page nav, and five dividers with up to 25 graph links each. The CSS comment records that the middle region can shrink to an 8rem floor, and the theme's page list is still the region that gets squeezed.
6. **Mobile: the sidebar's own × / ☰ labels are unstyled below 800 px.** This is read off `docs-ui.css`: every `.fa-nav-toggle` / `.fa-nav-close` rule, and every `:has(.fa-nav-open:checked)` rule, is inside `@media (min-width: 50rem)`, and neither #1010 nor #1022 touched them. Only print hides them. I have not seen a just-the-docs render, so whether the theme's own mobile CSS hides the sidebar footer they sit in is unconfirmed.
7. **The "▾ Folio" handle is fixed at top centre on every page** (`z-index: 91`, 52 px tall in the render). At 390 px it sits over the middle of the theme's top bar, where the title is. New and observed: **it also covers the glass's own content.** The sheet's top padding is 3 rem (48 px), less than the handle, and the sheet scrolls under it. At 390 with Settings scrolled, the handle hides the "Glass" and "High contrast" radio labels. The glyph also stays "▾" when the glass is down. Only the accessible name changes, to "Put your folio away".
8. **New: the bottom strip hides most of its tiles, and says nothing about it.** 25 tiles in one row: 11 visible at 1280, 2½ at 390. The rest scroll sideways inside the strip, with no arrow, count or edge fade. On a phone, only Todos, Settings and agent-skills-library are on screen, and library, processes and tools are off it.
9. **New: most declared tiles on the strip wear the same glyph.** In the render, 21 of the 23 declared tiles draw the same generic outline SVG. Only beans and uploads differ. Tiles are told apart by caption only, and several captions wrap to three lines at 88 px ("folio-assistant-sci-library").
10. **New: there are two unrelated "Settings".** ⚙ Settings on the glass sets the glass: theme, avatars, opacity and blur. ▦ Actions → Settings sets the page: scheme, reading preferences, the Discarded fish and Declared kinds. They share a name and a gear, and neither points to the other. A reader who wants the Discarded items and opens the glass Settings, the one now on screen at the bottom of every page, will not find them.

## What PR #1010 (bean `zrvt`, merged) changed

- **The folio glass.** The sheet is now 20 % opaque and blurred by default. Its shelf holds **movable, resizable cards**: ✥ with arrows, Shift+arrows, drag, −/+, and a semantic zoom to avatar-only below 220 px (300 px for a todo). Each card carries a **book avatar**: the library row's declared cover, else the kind avatar from `avatars.css`, never a broken image. Positions persist per reader on the folio entry. The empty line now says how to add something.
- **The bottom tile strip.** It is new, fixed along the viewport's bottom edge. It reads the same `tiles` declaration as the navbar and board (the new `glass` surface), or `assets/harness/tiles.json` on a page Jekyll did not build. This reverses `v0jv`'s "no tiles on the glass" at the owner's word.
- **The todo panel.** ☑ Todos on the strip lists the todo index with a pull-out per row, and a todo pulled out becomes a card. The sidebar's ▤ Todos and the footer's "Open notes (3)" are unchanged.
- **Settings.** It is a **new, separate** ⚙ Settings panel on the glass (theme, avatars, opacity, blur, tidy, defaults). The ▦ Actions → Settings view, where the Discarded (dead-fish) tile lives, is **unchanged**. That is finding 10.
- **The library row.** "Pull out to folio" moved to the row's **first** cell, beside the row's avatar. It is not drawn here: it belongs to the library viewers.

Not from #1010, but merged into this checkout alongside it: #1022 (`ka1p`) folds On this page, Folders and Harnesses on arrival, which took the divider initials out of the strip. #1022 and #1027 (`yunp`) also added the methodology and external-schema viewers. That is why Folders reads 25 and C@T Harness has 25 kinds and 19 viewers, up from 24 and 17.
