summaryExecuting a `test-plan/v1` against a system under test and taking the result to a certification decision: requested by (or for) the system, run by a tester, re-executed in sample by an untainted checker, decided by a certifier through the plan's exit-criteria…
descriptionExecuting a `test-plan/v1` against a system under test and taking the result to a certification decision: requested by (or for) the system, run by a tester, re-executed in sample by an untainted checker, decided by a certifier through the plan's exit-criteria table, and signed through `qa-report-signing.bpmn`. You are in this process when somebody wants a system — a skill, an agent, a tool, a machine, an IG or a process — certified against a named plan. Executing a plan with no certification in view is still this process; it ends at `undetermined` or `refused` without anything being signed. The run, the report and the certification are three records, and they point backwards: the run at the plan, the report at the run, the certification at the report. The certification is a judgement and lives under the declared `attestations` graph, on main; the report is derived and lives on the `qa-reports` branch.
enforcementstrict
