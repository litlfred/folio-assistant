descriptionA REUSABLE SUB-PROCESS, never an entry point. Every diagram in bootstrap that needs something only a person can supply — a choice, a judgement, a step only they have the rights to perform — reaches it through a call activity with calledElement="Process_HumanAgentDiscussion", rather than drawing its own asking. One way of asking, drawn once: a caller that draws its own has a second answer to "how do I ask", free to drift from this one. BEFORE ANY QUESTION, THE ASKER DETERMINES WHERE IT IS: the CONTEXT, the ROLES, and the PROCESS and TASK (or that it is idle). Each is a task, declared below as a precondition of the first question. WHAT CANNOT BE DETERMINED IS ASKED FIRST, with no default; the caller's question waits, counted. THE ORDER IS THE PROCESS: context, then options, then a recommendation and what happens with no answer, then one question. A question the reader cannot answer without opening anything is not ready. A DEFAULT is applied only when the CALLER allows one, and is recorded as assumed, never as the person's answer. Otherwise silence ends as unsettled. SYMMETRIC IN ITS PARTICIPANTS. The answering lane is a person or a sibling agent that already holds the answer; the record says which, because the two are evidence of different weight. NO work-plan element, and isExecutable is false, for the reasons initialize-harness gives.
