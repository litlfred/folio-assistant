Format 5

Lists of short items, usually —but not invariably —of one line or less, may
often follow a brief introductory statement and should then be set out
as follows:

— dash at left-hand margin

— lower-case letter to start

— the second line of longer items blocked to the first tab stop
— no punctuation after any item but the last.

Format 6
This is almost identical with Format 5, except:

— where the text is rendered clearer by punctuation, each item should
end with a comma or a semicolon;

— where some or most of the items run to a second line, the overall
appearance may be improved by punctuating at the end of each
item.

Format 7
Occasionally, the need for particular emphasis demands the use of
bullets in place of the dashes specified in Formats 5 and 6.
