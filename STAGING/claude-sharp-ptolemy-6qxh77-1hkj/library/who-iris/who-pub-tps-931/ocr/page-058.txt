Enzymes

The recommendations (names and numbers) of the latest edition of
Enzyme nomenclature and its supplements should be followed as far as
possible. IUB recommendations also exist for symbolism and terminol-
ogy in enzyme kinetics.

Further reading

Enzyme nomenclature, 1992: recommendations of the Nomenclature Committee of the
International Union of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology on the nomenclature and
classification of enzymes. San Diego, Academic Press, 1992.

Nomenclature Committee of the International Union of Biochemistry. Symbol-
ism and terminology in enzyme kinetics. European journal of biochemistry, 1982,
128:281-291.
