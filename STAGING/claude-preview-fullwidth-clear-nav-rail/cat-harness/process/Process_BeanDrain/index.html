nameDrain the beans
summaryDraining open beans with several agents at once, as part of the SDLC: build one queue grouped by owner, then take a bean, open it, work it, close it, and take the next — refilling from the deepest queue when a drainer's list runs dry, until nothing drainable…
descriptionDraining open beans with several agents at once, as part of the SDLC: build one queue grouped by owner, then take a bean, open it, work it, close it, and take the next — refilling from the deepest queue when a drainer's list runs dry, until nothing drainable is left. You are in this process when you are asked to drain beans, when you spawn drainers, or when you pull from a shared queue next to other agents. Each bean still goes through the ordinary OPEN and CLOSE (Process_BeanOpen, Process_BeanClose); what the drain adds is the queue around them, and the rule that a bean another agent holds is skipped, not worked. The queue is advisory: it says who should take a bean, and only OPEN's claim says who did.
enforcementstrict
