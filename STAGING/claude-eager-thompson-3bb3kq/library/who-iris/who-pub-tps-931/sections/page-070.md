Non-discriminatory language

The following table gives some examples of common discriminatory phrases and
suggestions for alternatives.

Common discriminatory phrases

Useful solutions

Useful general rules

The client is usually the best judge
of the value of his counseiiing

The client is usually the best judge
of the value of counselling

The best judge of the value of
counselling is the client

Delete redundant possessive
pronouns

Rephrase

Man's search for knowledge has led
him into ways of learning that bear
examination

The search for knowledge has led
us into ways of learning that bear
examination

Use the first person plurat

Man, mankind

Man’s achievements

The average man, the man in the
street

Man a project

Manpower

People, humanity, humans, human
beings, humankind

Human achievements

The average person, people in
general

Staff a project, hire personnel,
employ staff

Workforce, personnel

Find alternative words

Subjects were 16 boys and 16 girls.
Each child was to place a car on his
board so that the two boards looked
alike

Each child was to place a car on his
or her board so that the two boards
looked alike

“His or her” is sometimes difficult
to avoid and is acceptable if used
sparingly

Research scientists often neglect
their wives and children

Research scientists often neglect
their spouses and children

Use neutral terms such as spouse
rather than wife or husband

Woman doctor, male nurse

Doctor, nurse

Specify sex only if necessary to the
sense
