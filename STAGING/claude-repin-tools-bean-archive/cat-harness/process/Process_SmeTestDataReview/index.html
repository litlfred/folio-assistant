nameSME Review of Decision Support and Indicator Test Data
summarySME review of fixed test data for clinical or scientific decision support and indicator definitions.
descriptionSME review of fixed test data for clinical or scientific decision support and indicator definitions. It runs on its caller's bean (Process_TestDataCuration calls it), so it opens and closes none: every step that touches the plan notes on it. TWO UNITS OF REVIEW (`SmeCaseVerdictKind`, `$schema: "sme-case-verdict/1.0.0"` in `schemas/sdlc/test-data.ts`): - `decision-support`: the unit of review is a **worked case** (`case`: input state, `decisionLogicRef`, `systemOutput`, optional `expectedGuidance`). - `indicator-definition`: the unit of review is a **cohort plus an expected value** (`cohort`: `indicatorRef`, `populationSize`, `inputs`, `expectedValue`, `computedValue`, optional `period`, `numerator`, `denominator`). JUDGEMENT-ONLY SME LANE. `Lane_SME` binds `clinical-sme`, which is declared `judgementOnly: true` in `scenarios/roles.json`. `Task_SmeJudge` carries no skill reference because no mechanical procedure yields the clinical or scientific answer — that is the whole reason a person is in the lane. ATTRIBUTABLE REVIEWER AND DATE. `Task_RecordCaseVerdict` writes the `sme-case-verdict/1.0.0` node with the SME's attributable human identity (`reviewer: { kind: "human", provider, handle, role }`) and ISO-8601 review date (`reviewedAt`), because an audited review without an attributable reviewer and date is not audited.
