8
WHO WPRO official logos in different languages
WHO WPRO official logos for different country and regional offices within the Western Pacific region.
All WHO logos are provided in different formats: JPEG, EPS, BMP; other formats can be supplied on request.
Do not alter, rotate or modify the logo. It should never be recreated. Do not obstruct the logo. 
Only use the official logo files.
