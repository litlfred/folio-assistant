WHO Editorial Style Manval

List of participants

The list of participants should be headed by the name of the expert
group, irrespective of the form of the title of the report.

The names of members should be listed in alphabetical order, each
followed by the title of the post held, the name of the institute/
university department, and its geographical location. Titles, names of
institutes, etc. should normally be given in English, but if no exact
equivalent exists or in case of doubt, the name should be left in the
original language. The Chairman, Vice-Chairman and Rapporteur
should be identified.

The names of representatives of United Nations bodies and of nongov-
ernmental and intergovernmental organizations in official relations
with WHO should be listed under the heading “Representatives of
other organizations”.

Under the heading “Secretariat” only the secretary and any consult-
ants and temporary advisers who assisted in the preparation of the
meeting should normally be listed. Other names may be included only
under the special circumstances detailed in the WHO Manual, section
IX.1, Annex A, paragraph 160. Acknowledgements of any special
contributions made by people not included in the list of participants
may be included at the end of the report.

The secretary of the meeting is responsible for the correctness of the
details given in the list of participants. However, additional informa-
tion concerning members of expert advisory panels (i-e. all Expert
Committee members and many members of Study Groups and Scien-
tific Groups) can be obtained from the Office of Research Policy and
Strategy Coordination (RPS).

Changes in titles, addresses, country names and WHO unit/division
names that postdate the meeting should not be recorded.
