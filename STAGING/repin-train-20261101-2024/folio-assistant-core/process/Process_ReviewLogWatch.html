nameReview-log watch
summaryOne poll of one drafting review log (a Contributor Feedback sheet) and its folio repository.
descriptionOne poll of one drafting review log (a Contributor Feedback sheet) and its folio repository. A tool decides, with no agent judgement, whether anything a person set in the sheet or any open issue changed since the last poll. Only then does the agent append rows for new issues and do its pass. Skill: review-log-watch.
