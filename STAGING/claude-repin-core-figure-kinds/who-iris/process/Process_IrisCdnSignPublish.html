descriptionTurn a candidate payload into a signed release on Cloudflare: each changed library item gets a detached JWS over its own manifest, and the BagIt bag (RFC 8493) whose tagmanifest pins every item manifest and signature is signed once with a detached JWS (RFC 7515 Appendix F, ES256 per RFC 7518), the key published as a verificationMethod of a did:web DID document whose publicKeyJwk carries an x5c chain to the WHO signing key. Nothing unchanged is re-uploaded or re-signed; an empty diff ends the process with no commit.
enforcementstrict
