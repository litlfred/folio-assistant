descriptionThe BPMN this layer OWNS as a house process, as against `methodologies/processes/`, which holds the executable half of a methodology adopted whole from the corpus. `l2-dak-authoring.bpmn` is the first: moved here from cat-harness/processes/ on 2026-10-01 by owner ruling (issue #1772 item 3), because placement PR1 had moved its `l2-dak-authoring` skill into `skills/content/authoring-who-smart-guidelines/` and the three activities naming it no longer resolved from cat-harness, which cannot reach this layer. Here the skill sits beside the diagram; the remaining refs (`content-plan`, `todo-manager`, `bpmn-authoring`, `dmn-authoring`, `content-review`, `content-validate`, `terminology-management`) point down, through `fhir-harness`. A TOP-LEVEL directory rather than a second file in `methodologies/processes/`, for the reason `folio-assistant-core-processes` gives: that nested shape is a baselined `check:layout-norms` exception, and a house process is not a methodology read out of `library/`. Declared WITH its file in one commit, per bean `dh4f`. `smart-guideline-end-to-end.bpmn` (2026-10-14) is the second: use case to consuming application through the L1 KG, L2 and L3, with adjudication as QA rather than a gate; it names `l2-dak-authoring.bpmn` and `dak-l3-ig.bpmn` for its L2 and L3 stages rather than copying them. `SGAuthoring.DAKLifecycle.bpmn` (2026-10-14) moved here from the IG's `input/bpmn/`, merged with its graphviz `.layout.bpmn` into one file with diagram interchange, so every authoring process has one home; `l2-component-edit-review.bpmn` (2026-10-14) is the subprocess every `l2-dak-authoring.bpmn` track calls: the agent edits one component, checks, renders and stages it, and hands it on, while a person in the component's reviewing role reviews it alongside, never as a gate; its per-component renderer, checker and reviewer are the skill `l2-component-edit-review`'s table. `scripts/render-processes.ts` draws each beside its source; the site that shows them is folio-assistant's just-the-docs build, which mounts this directory.
