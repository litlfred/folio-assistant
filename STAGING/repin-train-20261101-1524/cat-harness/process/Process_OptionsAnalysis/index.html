nameOptions analysis
summaryA decision reached by a NAMED methodology, with its rejected options recorded.
descriptionA decision reached by a NAMED methodology, with its rejected options recorded. Called as a subprocess, never run on its own. WHEN IT APPLIES: the trigger is `opening-brief`'s — IRREVERSIBILITY AND SURPRISE, not size. A reversible choice needs no subprocess; an irreversible one cannot skip it. A subprocess fired on every decision becomes ceremony, and ceremony is how a gate stops being read. WHY IT IS ONE SUBPROCESS AND NOT A BRANCH PER METHODOLOGY: the methodologies are parallel tracks selected by context, and the selection is a judgement made inside A_Select rather than a gateway. A gateway per methodology would assert the choice is computable from data, which is the claim `dmn` says to make only when the criteria recur. WHAT IT DOES NOT DO: it does not decide. It produces the options, their trade-offs and a recommendation; the decision and its authorisation belong to whoever owns the calling step.
