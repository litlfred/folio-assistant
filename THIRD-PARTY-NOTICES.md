# Third-party notices

Material in this repository that carries somebody else's licence, and the notice
that licence requires.

_Nothing at present._ The `@` in `docs/assets/img/icons/cat-mark*.svg` was
briefly built from DejaVu Sans's COMMERCIAL AT outline, which carries the
Bitstream Vera Fonts Copyright and its "include this notice in all copies"
condition. The marks are now drawn from scratch — the ring is an arc, the `a`
is a bowl with a counter, and the ears, the face and the whiskers are original —
so no third-party outline is redistributed and no notice is owed.

This file stays because the next thing we embed will owe one.
