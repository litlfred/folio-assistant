{%- assign h = site.data.harness -%} {%- comment -%} TWO links, so the browser picks by size rather than us picking for it. The unsized link is the full mark, whiskers and all, used wherever there is room — a pinned tab, a bookmark tile, a home-screen icon. The `16x16` link is the mark drawn for small sizes, which is what a tab strip actually shows. The split exists because the whiskers are `stroke-width="2.4"` against a 120-unit viewBox: 0.32 px at 16 px and 0.48 px at 24 px. Sub-pixel at 1x, and still under a whole device pixel at 2x, so at tab size they render as haze on the face rather than as whiskers. One link would have meant choosing between losing them everywhere and showing them where they cannot be drawn. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if h.icon.src -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if h.smallIcon.src -%} {%- endif -%}
