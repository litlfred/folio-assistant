Three states, and the third is why this is a template rather than a div: 1. A backdrop for this viewport, with a declared text region → overlay. 2. A backdrop whose region is NOT declared → show the picture, put the words BELOW it. Text positioned by guesswork lands on the cat. 3. No backdrop at all → the words alone.
