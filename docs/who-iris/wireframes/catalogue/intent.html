Catalogue visualiser — as-is intent

Covers: cat-harness/docs/who-iris/catalogue/index.html (declared visualiser who-iris/catalogue). This is the coverage.visualiser[0].ref (title "Catalogue") of the who-iris-catalogue directory (path: catalogue/, read-only, skill: iris-dspace) in who-iris/who-iris.json. The page is a static page generated by who-iris/scripts/gen-iris-pages.ts, with no client-side application ("static HTML, … generated here and committed"). Its figures are read out of who-iris/catalogue/nodes/.

Who it is for: a maintainer of the who-iris instance, or a reviewer of its catalogue. The declaration's _coverage_comment sets it apart from the replica: the replica pages answer "what does IRIS look like", and this page answers "what does this catalogue know, and what does it record that it does not know". The page says so in its lede.

What they need to do:

  • see how many catalogue nodes are materialized (bytes held here), referenced (upstream only) or unknown, against the size of upstream IRIS
  • see what the licence and retention gates say about the held bitstreams, with unknown kept apart from permitted ("collapsing the two would turn an open question into a clearance")
  • scan every node, with its state, kind, title, catalogue path, library id ("held as"), record and bitstream count

What it must show (from the page and the generator): the "INGESTED COPY — not WHO" banner, the replica's WHO-coloured header with the logo omitted "as with all who-pages for now", the replica menu, the breadcrumb, three tables (states, gates, every node), a "generated from" note and the replica footer. "Nothing is greyed out": every row states its own state.

Findings

  1. Horizontal scroll at phone width. At 390 px the document is 752 px wide (scrollWidth 752). The "Every node" table does not wrap or scroll in a container, so the whole page pans sideways, and "held as", "record" and "bitstreams" start off-screen. The three columns that tell a held item from a referenced one are the three a phone user cannot see.
  2. The menu and breadcrumb look like links and are not. The six items in the blue menu and the who-iris • Catalogue crumb are <span>s. The page has 4 links in all: folio-assistant, WHO IRIS, and iris.who.int twice. A reader who taps "Communities & Collections" or "who-iris" gets nothing.
  3. No row leads anywhere. Node titles, catalogue paths and library ids ("held as") are plain text, so the 3 materialized items do not link to their library entries or their replica item pages. A reader who finds a held item here cannot reach it from here.
  4. The node list is not filterable or sortable, and it puts the 10 referenced rows ahead of the 3 materialized ones. At 1280×800 the first materialized row is about two screens down.
  5. About the first 280 px at 1280 (about 510 px at 390) is replica chrome before the h1: banner, wordmark, menu, breadcrumb. At 390 px the h1 starts at about y = 560, and the first table is below the first screen.
  6. The gate verdict counts have no label. The state pills carry their word as well as a tint, so colour is not the only channel. In the gates table, though, each count follows its pill as a bare number ("PERMITTED 3 UNKNOWN 3"). It reads as one run, not as two verdicts with a count each.

Re-drawn 2026-10-11 (who-iris ced6dff; bean kx0p)

as-is.html now draws the page as it is today, on who-iris's own site (catalogue/) and in cat-harness's copy. The six findings:

  1. Horizontal scroll at phone width — fixed. Only the node table scrolls (518 px inside its box); the page does not.
  2. Inert menu and breadcrumb — gone. The page wears the site layout (top band, sidebar), not the replica's chrome.
  3. No row leads anywhere — fixed. Collection and item titles link their replica pages. "Held as" links the library viewer only where the site builds one, so who-iris's own site shows the id as text rather than a dead link.
  4. Not filterable or sortable — fixed (who-iris#40). Held rows come first. A state select and a title-or-id search sit above the table, with "n of N shown", and every header is a sort button. The controls stay hidden without scripts.
  5. Replica chrome before the h1 — gone, with finding 2.
  6. Unlabelled gate counts — fixed. Each count is inside its pill and labelled ("permitted: 3 of 6").

The figures are read from the page built at ced6dff:

  • 13 nodes: 3 materialized, 10 referenced, 0 unknown.
  • Gates: every gate is permitted on all 6 bitstreams, except sourceLoss, which is unknown on 3.
