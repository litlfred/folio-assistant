Research and analysis

Findings gathered to inform a decision, not a proposal of what to build. The sibling Proposals section sets out options and what each costs; this one records what was found out — about other projects, about tools a skill could be satisfied by, about the repository itself — so the proposal that later cites it does not have to redo the looking.

Every page here follows the design-record rules in the placement skill: it is dated, it says what it was true of and where each fact came from, and it names what it could not establish. A survey of other projects goes stale faster than anything else in docs/, so read its date before its conclusions.

A finding that changes how agents work does not stay here: its rule moves into the skill it governs, and this page keeps the evidence.
