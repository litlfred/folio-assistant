Who appears in a process?

A lane NAMES the Role its tasks are performed in; it is not itself a Role. The NAME is free text — process-model.ts notes that sixty lanes spell two dozen positions — so the lane’s partitionElementRef (or the legacy <bootstrap.processes:role ref>) is the join that does not depend on spelling, and it is reported separately below rather than merged into the name.
