Online sequence, knot-census, and number-theory database lookup

Skill id: online-sequence-and-knot-databases · Package: authoring-core Processes: processes/content/evidence-gathering-and-provenance.bpmn (Process_EvidenceGatheringAndProvenance), sdlc/processes/research-delegation-and-niche-routing.bpmn (Process_ResearchDelegationAndNicheRouting, in the opt-in sdlc group, so not linked)

Whenever computational or mathematical research produces an integer sequence, a tower of rational series coefficients, a recurring prime-factor denominator pattern, an unrecognised high-precision constant, or a knot/link/manifold invariant, querying the canonical mathematical databases after rescaling to natural geometric or algebraic units is mandatory before asserting novelty or leaving opaque composite denominators in prose or formal declarations.

Why raw-form lookups miss published structure

Series coefficients extracted from perturbation expansions, saddle-point asymptotics, or transfer matrices rarely appear in published catalogues in their raw coordinate gauge:

  1. Unit-gauge obfuscation: an expansion written in bare powers of $\pi$ or a coordinate step $h$ mixes transcendental curvature factors (such as powers of $\sqrt{3}$ or $2\pi$) into the rational prefactors, inflating denominators by extraneous prime powers that hide the intrinsic arithmetic.
  2. Cumulant vs partition-function duality: literature catalogues frequently index the exponential generating series $\exp!\bigl(\sum_{k \ge 1} c_k x^k\bigr) = \sum_{k \ge 0} A_k \frac{x^k}{k!}$ while a saddle or free-energy calculation emits the logged cumulants $c_k$ (or vice versa). Searching only one side misses the published entry completely.
  3. Machine-float PSLQ mirages: running pslq or constant recognition at 53-bit float64 (~15 digits) produces spurious integer relations with three-digit coefficients on almost any four-element basis. Only >= 80 dps lattice reduction with held-out >= 160 dps verification separates genuine identities from coincidence.

§1. OEIS protocol (https://oeis.org/search?fmt=json&q=...) — STRICT

Query the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences via its JSON endpoint, always following redirects (curl -sL):

curl -sL "https://oeis.org/search?fmt=json&q=1,11,697,724351,278392949"

Mandatory five-transform canonicalization before concluding “not in OEIS”

Never query only the raw numerator or denominator list and stop. For any sequence of rational coefficients $(r_k)_{k \ge 1}$, generate and query all five canonical transforms (dropping leading $0$s/$1$s and signs where OEIS conventions vary):

  1. Natural geometric / 1-loop torsion unit rescaling ($r_k \mapsto z_k$): divide out the intrinsic 1-loop Hessian, Reidemeister torsion, discriminant, or period unit $u$ so that $r_k u^{-k} = z_k \in \mathbb{Q}$ lies in the base rationals rather than a quadratic or cyclotomic extension.
  2. Factorial clearing ($k!\,z_k$ and $(2m)!\,z_{2m}$): test exponential generating-function normalisation $k!\,z_k$, separate even and odd sub-towers $z_{2m}, z_{2m+1}$, and check whether $(2m)!\,z_{2m} \in \mathbb{Z}$.
  3. 2-3 torsion / Dedekind $\eta$ denominator stripping ($24^k z_k$ or $D^k z_k$): clear the universal 1-loop divisor ($24 = 2^3 \cdot 3$ from $\eta$-function / $B_2 = 1/6$ / Gaussian Wick contractions, or the minimal geometric integer $D$) and inspect both $\mathrm{num}(D^k z_k)$ and $\mathrm{den}(D^k z_k)$. Check residual prime factors against the von Staudt–Clausen theorem ($p \mid \mathrm{den}(B_{2k}) \iff (p - 1) \mid 2k$, introducing $5, 7, 11, 13, \dots$).
  4. Logged cumulants $\leftrightarrow$ unlogged exponential generating series: always pass both directions through sympy before querying OEIS:
    • Cumulants to moments: $\sum_{k \ge 0} \frac{A_k}{B_k} \frac{v^k}{k!} = \exp!\Bigl(\sum_{k \ge 1} z_k (D v)^k\Bigr)$;
    • Moments to cumulants: $\sum_{k \ge 1} c_k v^k = \ln!\Bigl(1 + \sum_{k \ge 1} M_k \frac{v^k}{k!}\Bigr)$.
  5. Sub-sequences & first differences: query even/odd slices separately and, for integer sequences, check consecutive ratios, prime factorizations, and binomial/Hankel transforms.

Worked case study — Figure-eight knot $4_1$ asymptotic loop coefficients

In the all-orders Kashaev / state-integral saddle expansion of the figure-eight knot $4_1$, the raw loop coefficients in powers of $\pi$ read

\[\omega_1 = \frac{11\sqrt{3}\,\pi}{108},\quad \omega_2 = \frac{2\,\pi^2}{27},\quad \omega_3 = \frac{1081\sqrt{3}\,\pi^3}{21870},\quad \omega_4 = \frac{98\,\pi^4}{729},\quad \dots\]

with opaque composite denominators $108, 27, 21870, 729, 688905, 295245, 26572050, 18600435, 49718962755$. Applying Transforms 1–4 exposes the complete arithmetic origin:

  • Transform 1 (1-loop Reidemeister-torsion unit $u = \frac{2\pi}{3\sqrt{3}}$): writing $\omega_k = z_k u^k$ strips $(3\sqrt{3})^{-k}$ completely, collapsing the sequence into pure rationals \(z_1 = \frac{11}{24},\quad z_2 = \frac{1}{2},\quad z_3 = \frac{1081}{720},\quad z_4 = \frac{49}{8},\quad z_5 = \frac{110011}{3360},\quad z_6 = \frac{51973}{240},\quad z_7 = \frac{32729683}{19200},\quad z_8 = \frac{69709147}{4480},\quad z_9 = \frac{860118209659}{5322240}.\) Here $z_1 = \frac{11}{24}$ splits cleanly into the 1-loop Gaussian Feynman diagram sum $(-\frac{3}{4} + \frac{5}{6}) = \frac{2}{24}$ plus the discrete Euler–Maclaurin boundary/$B_2$ sum $(\frac{3}{2} - \frac{3}{8} - \frac{3}{4}) = \frac{9}{24}$.
  • Transform 2 (even-rung factorial integrality): $(2m)!\,z_{2m} \in \mathbb{Z}$ for every even rung ($1, 147, 155919, 627382323$ at $2m = 2, 4, 6, 8$), while $\mathrm{den}(k!\,z_k)$ is $3$-free for every $k \ge 2, k \neq 3$ (1, 120, 1, 28, 1, 80, 1, 44).
  • Transform 3 ($24^k$ denominator law & Bernoulli primes): for all $k = 1\ldots 9$, $\mathrm{den}(24^k z_k) = (1, 1, 5, 1, 35, 5, 25, 35, 385)$ is coprime to $6$, carrying only von Staudt–Clausen Bernoulli primes $p \ge 5$ ($5, 7, 11$).
  • Transform 4 (unlogged exponential $\to$ exact OEIS hit): expanding $\exp!\bigl(\sum_{k=1}^{9} z_k (24 v)^k\bigr) = \sum_{k=0}^{9} \frac{A_k}{B_k} \frac{v^k}{k!}$ yields
    • Numerators $A_k$: 1, 11, 697, 724351, 278392949, 244284791741, 1140363907117019, 212114205337147471, 367362844229968131557, 44921192873529779078383921 — OEIS A301732;
    • Denominators $B_k$: 1, 1, 1, 5, 5, 7, 35, 5, 5, 275 — OEIS A301733 (Garoufalidis, Quantum Topol. / arXiv:1201.3314; Garoufalidis & Zagier 2023), with $697 - 11^2 = 576 = 24^2 \iff z_2 = \frac{1}{2}$.

§2. Knot, link, and 3-manifold censuses (KnotInfo, LinkInfo, SnapPy, Regina)

Whenever research touches a knot, link, braid closure, or hyperbolic 3-manifold, cross-check all topological, algebraic, and geometric invariants against published censuses rather than hand-transcribing or guessing conventions:

resourcecanonical interfaceinvariants to cross-check
KnotInfo & LinkInfohttps://knotinfo.math.indiana.edu/, https://linkinfo.sitehost.iu.edu/ (CSV/JSON tables & database_knotinfo Python package)Crossing number, braid index & minimum braid word, Alexander / Jones / HOMFLY-PT / Kauffman polynomials (noting variable conventions!), signature, determinant, unknotting number, symmetry type (chiral, reversible, full/negative amphichiral)
SnapPy censusessnappy.Manifold("<name>"), OrientableCuspedCensus, LinkExteriors, HTLinkExteriors (>= 50-80 dps high_precision() / .tetrahedra_shapes('rect'))Complete hyperbolic volume, Chern–Simons invariant ($\bmod\,\pi^2$ / $\bmod\,\frac{1}{2}$), cusp shapes, Neumann–Zagier gluing matrices, Dehn surgery slopes, symmetry group, and canonical ideal triangulation
Snap / SnapPy arithmetic invariants & ReginaSnap trace-field tables, regina 3-manifold triangulation censusInvariant trace field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{tr}\,\Gamma)$ (for example $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ for $4_1$, cubic field of discriminant $-23$ for $5_2$), invariant quaternion algebra, Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm class in $\mathcal{B}(K)$, and cusp field

Convention check (STRICT): always verify orientation, mirror convention ($K$ vs $\overline{K}$ flips $\mathrm{CS}(K)$ and $q \leftrightarrow q^{-1}$ in Jones/HOMFLY-PT), and normalisation (unreduced vs unknot-normalised coloured Jones) on $3_1$ and $4_1$ before ingesting or asserting an identity across a table of knots.

§3. LMFDB (https://www.lmfdb.org/api/) — number fields, $L$-values & modular forms

When exact arithmetic, trace fields, Galois parities, special $L$-values, or $q$-series arise, query the L-functions and Modular Forms Database REST API:

# Example: query number fields of degree 2 and |disc| <= 12 in JSON format
curl -sL "https://www.lmfdb.org/api/nf_fields/?degree=2&_format=json&_limit=10"

Use LMFDB to verify and cite:

  1. Number fields (nf_fields): discriminant, signature $(r_1, r_2)$, ring of integers, class number, fundamental units, regulator, and Galois group — keeping imaginary quadratic trace fields such as $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ (discriminant $-3$, Eisenstein units) cleanly separated from real quadratic sub-expressions $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3})$ arising after factoring out $i = \sqrt{-1}$.
  2. Dirichlet characters & special $L$-values (artin_representations, lfunc_lfunctions): conductor, parity, Gauss sums, and exact algebraic parts of $L(k, \chi)$ (for example $L(2, \chi_{-3}) = \frac{2}{3\sqrt{3}}\,\mathrm{Cl}2(\pi/3)$ and $L(2, \chi{-4}) = G_{\mathrm{Catalan}}$).
  3. Modular, Jacobi & quantum modular forms (mf_newforms, ` mf_hecke_nf`): $q$-expansion coefficients, weight, level, Nebentypus character, CM status, and Eichler–Shimura / Habiro completion data.
  4. Elliptic curves & higher-genus curves (ec_curvedata, hgcwa_passports): conductor, isogeny class, $L$-series, automorphism group passports, and Prym / Jacobian isogeny decompositions.

§4. High-precision constant recognition (mpmath.pslq / identify at >= 80 dps)

Whenever a numerical computation produces an unexplained real or complex constant, asymptotic slope, Stokes multiplier, or subleading intercept:

  1. Precision floor (>= 80 dps, zero float64): compute the target number at >= 80 decimal places (never float64!). Set mp.dps explicitly before constructing any operand.
  2. Structured basis PSLQ (mpmath.pslq): test integer linear relations against a domain-motivated transcendental basis — for example ${1, \pi, \pi^2, \sqrt{3}\,\pi, \mathrm{Cl}2(\pi/3), G{\mathrm{Catalan}}, \zeta(3), \ln 2, \ln 3}$ or logarithmic/polylogarithmic periods of the relevant number field — requiring small integer coefficients ($\max |c_i| \ll 10^{\mathrm{dps}/4}$).
  3. Held-out doubling confirmation (>= 160 dps): re-evaluate both sides of any candidate PSLQ relation at double the working precision (>= 160 dps) and require the residual to drop to < 10^{-150}. Any relation whose residual stalls near 10^{-75} is a lattice-reduction artefact and MUST be discarded.
  4. Database cross-verification: pass every recovered rational ratio $p/q$ through §1 (OEIS + Bernoulli prime factorisation) and §3 (LMFDB $L$-value normalisation), then ingest the primary literature source via evidence-gathering-and-provenance and prove the algebraic skeleton formally where applicable.

§5. Provenance & Knowledge Graph recording (STRICT)

Every hit or structured negative result from §§1–4 MUST leave a durable, checkable record rather than staying in chat prose:

  • OEIS / LMFDB / KnotInfo / SnapPy identifiers: record exact sequence IDs (OEIS A301732, OEIS A301733), LMFDB labels (2.0.3.1), and census names (m004 / 4_1, L6a4) in compute docstrings, *.witness.json metadata, block authorNotes, and formal Lean module docstrings (-- Ref: [...]).
  • Primary paper ingestion: an OEIS or LMFDB entry almost always cites the paper that proved the formula; retrieve and ingest that primary paper into references/ and library/ via evidence-gathering-and-provenance so the theorem is cited to its proof as well as its catalogue ID.
  • Negative search log: if all five OEIS transforms, KnotInfo/SnapPy tables, LMFDB queries, and >= 80 dps PSLQ bases return no match, record the exact transformed query strings and basis vectors tested in the work item or audit note so future sessions do not repeat the search.

Checklist before closing a sequence, constant, or invariant investigation

  • Rescaled raw coefficients into natural 1-loop torsion / geometric units before querying.
  • Tested all five OEIS transforms (curl -sL "https://oeis.org/search?fmt=json&q=..."): raw, unit-rescaled, factorial-cleared $k!\,z_k$, $24^k$ / torsion-cleared, and both logged cumulants and unlogged $\exp(\sum c_k x^k)$.
  • Checked denominator prime factorisations against von Staudt–Clausen Bernoulli primes and stripped extraneous $2, 3, \sqrt{d}$ gauge factors.
  • Cross-checked knot/link/3-manifold invariants (volume, CS, polynomials, symmetry, trace field) against KnotInfo / LinkInfo / SnapPy / Regina with explicit orientation/mirror verification.
  • Queried LMFDB (https://www.lmfdb.org/api/) for number fields, Dirichlet $L$-values, and modular/quantum-modular $q$-series where applicable.
  • Ran mpmath.pslq / identify at >= 80 dps (0 float64) and confirmed any candidate relation at held-out >= 160 dps.
  • Recorded catalogue IDs (OEIS Axxxxxx, LMFDB labels, census IDs) and ingested the primary literature source via evidence-gathering-and-provenance.
