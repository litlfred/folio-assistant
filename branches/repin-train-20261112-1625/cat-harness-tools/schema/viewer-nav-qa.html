Description

The viewer-navbar audit — the common navbar rail as data.

Every page carries the rail unless a harness visualisation explicitly removes it, so a page with no rail is not automatically a defect. The audit therefore records a VERDICT PER PAGE rather than a count, and the count is derived from the verdicts: a count alone cannot say which page regressed, or whether a missing rail was deliberate.

A sidecar rather than a console report

As with kg:audit: committed, the diff tells "unrailed since it was drawn" from "lost its rail in the commit under review".

What this does NOT audit

Whether the rail's links resolve. They cannot be checked from the source tree: the /<instance>/ roots of mounted layers are mount routes mount-instance-docs.ts creates AFTER Jekyll, and /bootstrap/initialization.html is a .md Jekyll renders. check:subgraphs and the site-link tests own that question against the built site; this one owns presence.
