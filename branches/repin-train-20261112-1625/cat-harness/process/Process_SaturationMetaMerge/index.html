nameSaturation meta-merge: land the ready PRs when the runners are full
summaryLanding a batch while the runners are full: cancel everything queued, pause new work, consolidate all ready PRs on one meta-merge branch, skip CI and rendering, and merge it, holding the swarm siblings until it is done.
descriptionLanding a batch while the runners are full: cancel everything queued, pause new work, consolidate all ready PRs on one meta-merge branch, skip CI and rendering, and merge it, holding the swarm siblings until it is done. It repeats across repositories. Skill saturation-meta-merge holds the steps, the safety rules and the record of its first run. This process bypasses merge:guard on purpose, so the owner's card, the PR body and a comment on each member PR are the record.
enforcementstrict
