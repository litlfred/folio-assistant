Voice overlay review

What you are reviewing, and against what

One block, against the rules of the voices the folio has activated — not every voice it ships. The layer’s config (its index.config.json entry) holds the list; an empty list means there is nothing for this skill to do and that is a pass, not a gap.

bun run cat check:voices    # the voices, their rule counts, and that every citation resolves

The rule’s citation is the argument, so open it

Every voice rule carries a source that resolves to a real file — a library/<doc>/sections/<section>.md for a rule read from an ingested document, or a path for a house standard. It also carries the quote the rule was read from.

Open the citation before upholding a finding.

  1. A reviewer who has read the source can say why the rule applies to this block.
  2. The rule may be wrong. A rule derived from a document can misread it. If the quote does not support the rule, the rule is the defect and the work item goes against the voice, not against the block.

Three outcomes, as with the house voice axis

Identical to voice-editorial-review:

  1. The finding stands — the register is wrong for this block. Fix the prose.
  2. The rule does not apply to this content — record it, and consider whether the rule needs appliesTo scoping on the voice. One scoping edit beats many per-block overrules.
  3. The rule applies and this block is an exception — a reviewer entry on the sidecar saying why. It leads the criterion, so the block reads pass with the reasoning attached and nothing is silently rewritten.

A mechanical match is a question

A pattern is a regex over text; it does not know what the block is about. Examples of non-defects it matches:

  • voice-scholarly-default on documentation pages whose register is correctly second-person.
  • voice-editorializing on merely inside a comparative, where the contrast is the block’s claim.
  • voice-editorializing on clearly used as proof economy.

Verify before upholding, and when a rule fires repeatedly across a corpus on the same non-defect, that is evidence about the RULE.

Recording the verdict

Voice findings go on the block’s QA sidecar like any other, through the same writer, so the icon and the published witness agree with what you decided:

bun run pipeline/qa-merge-findings.ts --file findings.json

insertAdjudication places your entry ahead of the script entry, because list[0] is a criterion’s effective verdict. The script’s entry is kept beneath yours, because the disagreement between a checker and a reviewer is information.

Do not

  • Do not uphold a finding you have not traced to its quote. A rule that misreads its source is the failure this design exists to prevent.
  • Do not review against a voice the folio has not activated.
  • Do not delete a script finding from a sidecar. The sweep rewrites its own entries; a hand-removed one returns with no record that anyone looked.
  • Do not resolve a conflict between two active voices in the prose. Both rules stand: name them and let the editor decide.
