A. Record and Manage Patient Privacy Consent

PHW.A · strict (defaulted) · 6 step(s)

Captures, updates, or revokes a patient privacy consent directive (IHE.PCF.consentBasic or IHE.PCF.consentIntermediate) in a PCF Consent Registry using the IHE PCF Access Consent [ITI-108] transaction (Post, Put, Delete). Trust-anchor neutral: applicable across any trust network (including the WHO GDHCN or national/regional trust anchors) with no IUA claim. This is the person-side view of DSA process P9 (individual consent linked to the agreement). It applies only to agreements whose legal basis is consent (rule 1): treatment, public-health law and research waivers do not rest on consent. Where the person has recorded nothing, the jurisdiction’s chosen PCF implicit default policy applies, opt-in (for example Policy-deny) or opt-out (for example Policy-basic-normal or Policy-all-normal) (rule 4) (owner consent rules, 2026-10-30 19:27Z).

Steps

Every one of the 6 step(s) is documented.

steplaneskill / sub-processwhat it does
A1. Select base policy & disclosure scope (PCF Basic / Intermediate Consent)
PHW.A.1		VHL Holder (Patient / Personal Health Wallet)—Holder selects the base policy URI (Consent.policy.uri), permitted purpose of use (PCFPurposeOfUseVS), authorized actors, validity period, and covered clinical data. The Holder is shown the agreement’s plain-language summary, published and hashed with the agreement; that summary is what the person consents to (rule 3). Consent.policy.uri is the agreement’s fixed-version URL, not a moving one (rule 2) (owner consent rules, 2026-10-30 19:27Z).
A2. Construct FHIR Consent & Provenance (IHE.PCF.consentBasic / Intermediate)
PHW.A.2		PCF Consent Recorder (Wallet / VHL Sharer)—Consent Recorder constructs a FHIR Consent resource (status: active \| inactive, scope: patient-privacy, category: LOINC#59284-0) and accompanying IHE.PCF.provenance resource. Consent.policy.uri carries the agreement’s fixed-version URL, so the Consent counts only for that version (rule 2).
A3. Create consent directive [ITI-108] Access Consent: Post
PHW.A.3		PCF Consent Recorder (Wallet / VHL Sharer)—Initiates IHE PCF [ITI-108] Access Consent (POST [base]/Consent or transaction Bundle with Provenance) to record a new consent directive.
A4. Update or revoke directive [ITI-108] Access Consent: Put / Delete
PHW.A.4		PCF Consent Recorder (Wallet / VHL Sharer)—Initiates IHE PCF [ITI-108] Access Consent (PUT [base]/Consent/[id] or DELETE [base]/Consent/[id]) to update or revoke an existing consent directive. Withdrawal sets Consent.status to inactive (PUT); DELETE is for a directive recorded in error. If the agreement is amended to widen use, its new version needs a new Consent; an amendment that narrows use does not (rule 2). Withdrawal ends any cached registry result that holds this Consent at once (rule 4) (owner consent rules, 2026-10-30 19:27Z).
A5. Validate, version & persist Consent + Provenance [ITI-108] Access Consent
PHW.A.5		PCF Consent Registry—Consent Registry validates conformance to IHE.PCF.consentBasic / IHE.PCF.consentIntermediate, assigns Consent.id and Consent.meta.versionId, and persists the resource.
A6. Confirm recorded Consent.id & versionId [ITI-108] Access Consent response
PHW.A.6		VHL Holder (Patient / Personal Health Wallet)—Holder receives confirmation of the recorded or updated Consent.id and Consent.meta.versionId from the [ITI-108] Access Consent response.
