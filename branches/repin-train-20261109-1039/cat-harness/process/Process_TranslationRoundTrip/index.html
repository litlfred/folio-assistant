descriptionDid the meaning survive the translation? The untainted round trip on a sample of one locale's strings, with the two parties kept apart: a back-translator who sees only the target text, and an adjudicator who sees only the source and the back-translation. You are in this process when `multi-locale-translation` or `translation-refresh` calls it, after the deterministic gates have passed. It is the translation instance of `untainted-verification`: the producer (the locale translator) draws the sample and never writes the verdict; the back-translator renders and rules on nothing; the adjudicator rules. `GT_RoundTripQa` in `translation-workflow` is the same check drawn as one step; this diagram draws its parties, because the separation between them is what makes the result a measurement. A first run samples every diagram label, the personas and scenarios, and a seeded random fraction of the rest; a refresh samples only the strings it changed. The third state is drawn: when two separate parties cannot be dispatched, the producer records "could not dispatch" with a reason, and that is never a pass.
