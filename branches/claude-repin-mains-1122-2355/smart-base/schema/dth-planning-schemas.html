Description

Proposed WHO Digital Transformation Handbook (DTH) governance and planning schemas (litlfred/dth-sfp#1 Step P6d, litlfred/dth-phc#1 Step P4b, and litlfred/smart-base#242).

Why these five schemas exist

Across the five WHO Digital Transformation Handbooks — dth-hrh (9789240095687-eng), dth-phc (9789240093362-eng), dth-sc (9789240089600-eng), dth-pc (9789240116191-eng), and dth-sfp (9789240120747-eng) — clinical and operational user personas, user scenarios, business processes, core data elements, decision logic, functional/non-functional requirements, and indicators map directly onto the existing WHO SMART Guidelines DAK block kinds in smart-base/block-kinds/.

However, five recurring governance, stakeholder, barrier-mapping, and risk table structures appear across the DTH corpus and have no fitting DAK logical model. Stepwise planning tables (INPUTS | STEPS | RESOURCES | OUTPUTS | RISKS | MITIGATION) are NOT redefined here: they are DthStepTableSchema in ./dth-step-table.ts (smart-base#258), and the risk register below reuses its DthRiskMitigationRowSchema row:

1. GovernanceRoleSchema (dth-governance-role/v1) — Organisational governance bodies and leadership roles (steering committee, technical working group, advisory council, project champion, coordinator, regulator). Covers SFP §2.3 and PHC Ch 1 §1.1.2. 2. RaciMatrixSchema (dth-raci-matrix/v1) — Responsibility assignment matrices mapping activities and DIIG phases to role IRIs with R | A | C | I codes, distinguishing populated matrices (requiring at least one "A" per activity) from blank worksheet templates. Covers SFP Annex 4, PHC Annex 2, and HRH Ch 3. 3. RiskRegisterSchema (dth-risk-register/v1) — Standalone risk and mitigation registers (populated or blank annex templates) whose rows are DthRiskMitigationRowSchema rows extended with category and ratings, so a register row and a step table's RISKS row are the same thing. Covers SFP Annex 7 and PHC Ch 1–5 registers; PC's inline step risks stay in DthStepTableSchema. 4. BarrierSolutionMapSchema (dth-barrier-map/v1) — Mappings from system/reporting barriers to affected roles, candidate digital/policy solutions, functional requirements, and ArchiMate Motivation assessments. Covers SFP Table 5 & Annex 6 and PHC Table 1. 5. StakeholderAnalysisSchema (dth-stakeholder-analysis/v1) — Stakeholder analysis tables linking multi-sectoral stakeholders to their system role, interest/influence, engagement mechanism, and smart-ra ArchiMate stakeholder/role IRIs. Covers SFP Table 2 and PHC Ch 1 Table 2.

See the companion architect-committee proposal at smart-base/findings/dth-planning-schemas-proposal-2026-10-31.md.
