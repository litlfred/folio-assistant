Description

Shared DTH step-table schema (INPUTS | STEPS | RESOURCES | OUTPUTS | RISKS | MITIGATION), smart-base#242.

Unifies: - Product Catalogue (dth-pc) and Supply Chain (dth-sc) chapter tables (INPUTS / STEPS / RESOURCES / OUTPUTS / RISKS / MITIGATION), - Primary Health Care (dth-phc) chapter/phase panels (Who is involved / Inputs / Tools / Outputs), - Health Workforce Registry (dth-hrh) planning steps, and - Substandard and Falsified Medical Products (dth-sfp) implementation and M&E step tables (Tables 14, 17, Annex 7).

Key invariants (owner, 2026-10-31, smart-base#242): 1. Roles in INPUTS, STEPS, OUTPUTS and RISKS are persona or role IDs (pers:..., actor:..., <HB>-R-nn, DAK.Persona.*), never free text, so BPMN 2.0 swimlanes can be generated mechanically from the table. 2. Steps and outputs carry an optional produces field naming the DAK block kind (persona, business-process, decision-table, data-element, functional-requirement, non-functional-requirement, indicator, etc.) produced by the step. 3. Steps can be flat (steps) or grouped (groups) by chapter or DIIG phase. 4. RISKS and MITIGATION are structured rows with an ownerRoleId that a risk-register block or BPMN governance lane can reference.
