Validate draft MeSH headings against NLM’s authority

Stage three of skill-pipeline-subject-indexing for MeSH: look up every candidate heading of a draft (a preferred heading, an entry term, or a descriptor id, with an optional qualifier) in NLM’s MeSH authority at id.nlm.nih.gov, current year. Each candidate comes back resolved (in the preferred form), qualifier-not-allowed (descriptor kept, qualifier dropped), ambiguous (options listed for a person) or not-found (dropped). It never adds a heading the draft did not propose and never emits one the authority lacks.

Skills it satisfies

A skill states the capability; this Tool is one way to exercise it. Each skill’s declared input and output contract follows, where it has one.

mesh-subject-indexing

Assign Medical Subject Headings (MeSH) to a work as a DRAFT for a person to review, in the four stages of skill-pipeline-subject-indexing: over-generate concepts, filter them by NLM’s own rules, validate every heading against NLM’s MeSH authority with Tool mesh-authority, and write the reviewed list. Never emits a heading the authority does not hold.

Source: folio-assistant-core/skills/library/cataloguing/mesh-subject-indexing.md.

The skill declares no I/O contract (schemas/skills/mesh-subject-indexing/{input,output}.schema.json).

host / errorremedy
id.nlm.nih.govnone: The authority is NLM’s. Without it no candidate can be validated, and the skill emits nothing rather than unvalidated headings.
when to choose it
whenA draft list of MeSH headings from stages one and two of mesh-subject-indexing is ready to be checked against the authority.
limitsChecks existence, preferred form and allowable qualifiers only. It does not judge whether a heading fits the work, and applies none of NLM’s rules on heading count or major topics.
costTwo or three HTTPS requests per candidate to id.nlm.nih.gov; about a second for a short draft.
