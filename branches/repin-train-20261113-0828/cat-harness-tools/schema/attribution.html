Description

Who wrote a piece of derived content.

Text lifted verbatim out of a PDF has no author: provenance: "ingested" says so. A narrative — an image description, a transcript, a translation, a dataset summary — is somebody's account of the thing, and a reader needs to know whether a human curator or a model (and which version) wrote it. Recording it also lets everything a superseded model wrote be re-generated or re-reviewed as a set.

The vocabulary is the QA reviewer's

script | agent | human is the participant vocabulary of schemas/sdlc/block-qa.ts. The constant lives here and is re-exported there, so there is ONE spelling. QaReviewer itself is not reused: its hash fields concern criterion staleness, not authorship.

agent requires a model, structurally

An agent attribution with no model is refused by a schema refinement: it would record that a machine wrote the text while losing the part that lets anyone act on it later.
