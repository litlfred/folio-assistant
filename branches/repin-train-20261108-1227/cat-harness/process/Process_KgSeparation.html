nameA knowledge graph leaves for its own repositories
summaryMove part of a knowledge graph into repositories of its own: the content (files to read, no code) and its tools (the code that writes and checks it) separate as a pair, into two repositories, not one.
descriptionMove part of a knowledge graph into repositories of its own: the content (files to read, no code) and its tools (the code that writes and checks it) separate as a pair, into two repositories, not one. `kg-separation.md` is the practice; this is its order and its gates. Stages 1-3 are `graph-detanglement`, reused rather than restated. EVERY GATEWAY IS A COMMAND. `Tools closed?` is `check:tools-closure` with the content's no-code test and a byte-identity check on the generated files; `Every identifier its file's path?` is `check:node-iris`; `Green alone?` is the standalone rehearsal; `Ready to seed?` is `seed:ready --layer <name> --rehearse`, decided by decisions/seed-readiness-gate.dmn. A check that could not run stops the process rather than reading as clean. A PERSON DECIDES FOUR TIMES, AND THE ADMINISTRATOR LANE SAYS WHERE: whether to separate at all, whether to authorise the extraction, creating the repositories, and the cutover. Nothing is committed to the new repositories before they are created, and the parent keeps its own copy until it consumes the first release. VERSIONS: the pair starts at the content's version at the split, then each is versioned on its own; a tools release lists the content majors it supports. Examples: bootstrap and bootstrap-tools; cat-harness and cat-harness-tools. DRAWN IN PHASES (owner, 2026-11-08): prepare, authorise, move and cut over are each their own diagram, called here; a loop that crosses phases is drawn at this level.

  • owner-decision 2026-10-02

task Task_Seed

  • bean bean folio-assistant-7mwa 2026-11-03
    Without it the declared store is never written, and once the layer stops committing its results no reader finds them (bean folio-assistant-7mwa, 2026-11-03).

userTask Task_Cutover

  • owner-decision 2026-10-06
    It retires the parent's copy, frozen, never refreshed and never rendered, rather than deleting it (owner, 2026-10-06).

task Task_CutoverCheck

  • proposal lessons 15 and 16
    Six conditions, each decided by an existing gate and none assumed: every declared layer mounted at its pin; every path a declaration names resolves (check:declaration-resolution — directories, assets, visualisers, needs); no wiring still names the monorepo (livesAt, a submodule, a reusable workflow, a path into the old checkout — lessons 15 and 16); no import or reference against the layer order; and the site builds from the mounts alone.
