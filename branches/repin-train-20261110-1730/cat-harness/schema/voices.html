Description

Voices — named editorial profiles that overlay the base house voice.

A voice is a named set of editorial rules an agent applies when authoring or reviewing prose. Voices are OVERLAID, not chosen: a folio may activate several, and the union of their rules applies on top of the base house voice in skills/authoring/authoring-core/one-voice-style-guide.md.

A voice is a third axis

Adapters partition block KINDS and profiles nest; a voice does neither. It governs HOW PROSE READS — spelling, punctuation, terminology, register, citation form, and for a design voice the visual conventions around the prose. Two folios can share an adapter and a profile and still speak differently.

Voices are OPT-IN

The default is the empty set, and harness.config.json opts in:

    This is the opposite of criterionProfiles, which defaults to EVERY profile: an unasked-for voice produces confident findings against prose never written to that standard — a wrong FAIL at scale.

    Every rule cites its source

    VoiceRuleSource is REQUIRED on every rule. A plausible rule without the passage it comes from can be plainly wrong about the style guide it claims to follow.
