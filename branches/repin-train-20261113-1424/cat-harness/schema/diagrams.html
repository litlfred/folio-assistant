Description

Derived UML data class diagrams and sequence diagrams from an ArchiMate model (and optional FSH Logical Models and BPMN processes): smart-ra#166 D5.

Owner, 2026-10-31: "i dont really like native UML, rather derived", then "make mechanical tools as parts of skills/processes".

Two kinds of UML diagrams are derived here from the architecture model:

1. Data class diagrams (archimate-data-model/v1) — from DataObject and BusinessObject elements tagged with logical-model (and matched against FSH Logical: definitions when the IG holds them), or connected by data-to-data relationships (Composition, Aggregation, Specialization, Realization, Association) or carrying structured attributes (such as CDE-HWR in 0.2.0 and the Product Master Data hierarchy Product Classification, Generic Product, Trade Item, Regulated Trade Item in 0.3.0-draft.1).

2. Sequence diagrams (archimate-sequences/v1) — from ApplicationInteraction / BusinessInteraction elements and cross-component Flow / Triggering relationship chains in the model's views (and model-wide), plus cross-lane / cross-pool handoffs in BPMN 2.0 processes when provided.
