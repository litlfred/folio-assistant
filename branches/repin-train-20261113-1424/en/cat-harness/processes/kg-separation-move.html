Move the graph into its repositories

Process_KgSeparationMove · strict · 8 step(s)

Creates the repositories, drains the open PRs until the source settles, seeds them, re-points the declaration and verifies a fresh clone, then has the parent consume the result additively, makes the first release and checks every identifier dereferences. Nothing is seeded on an unknown readiness, and a fresh clone that could not run is not a pass. Called by kg-separation and sub-kg-lifecycle (bpmn-authoring: split by phase).

How it connects

  • Called by: A knowledge graph leaves for its own repositories, A sub-KG is staged in place, then leaves for its own repository
