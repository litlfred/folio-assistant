nameFormalized Scientific Publication Authoring
summaryOne mathematical result, from the author's intent to a merged change set.
descriptionOne mathematical result, from the author's intent to a merged change set. The result is a triple: a narrative statement, a narrative proof (both in the block's .md) and a Lean statement and proof (the block's .lean). The Lean kernel shows only that the Lean proof proves the Lean statement. Whether that statement is the theorem the narrative claims is decided separately, by independent reviewers who translate each side without seeing the other and then judge semantic equivalence. Mechanical checks (schema, references, render, Lean, vacuity, witnesses) run before them, so reviewers never spend a reading on a block that does not build. Empirical inputs never enter the triple: a measured value appears only in a marked comparison block, and a number quoted in prose is read from its witness. A result that cannot be closed is kept as a conjecture or as a stated, cited gap; it is never weakened until it passes. Only the author merges.
