Description

A VOICE SKILL — a voice as a first-class member of the knowledge graph.

A voice is a skill, not a data file beside one

A voice tells an actor what to do, is bound to processes by name, and is versioned with the graph — what a skill does. As a skill, its instructions and its rules are the same node, owned by the same layer, so writing guidance cannot drift away from the rules and their citations.

A voice skill lives at <instance>/skills/voices/<id>/, inside the kg graph, and the layer that holds it is the layer that DERIVED it.

Three consumers, and the schema says which field serves which

| process | what it reads | why | |---|---|---| | authoring | instructions, and the rules flagged VoiceSkillRule.counterintuitive | what to know BEFORE drafting — the rules where the instinct is wrong and the retrofit is expensive | | review / QC | every rule with its source | a reviewer upholds a finding by opening the cited page, never by trusting the rule's wording | | QA | patterns, terminology, judgementOnly, VoiceSkillSchema overlaySeverity | the mechanical half, and the weight of the derived overlay criterion |

No field serves two of those by restatement. The instruction body must not enumerate rules. scripts/check-voice-skills.ts enforces it, on four signals — a rule's id, its title, its citation's quote, and both sides of a terminology pair.
