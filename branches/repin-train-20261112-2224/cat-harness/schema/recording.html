Description

A recorded meeting or talk as a library entry, and its transcripts.

Two records:

  • recording.json (folio-recording/v1) says what the recording is: its title, date and spoken language, and which bytes were looked at (source, the same _tech_meta block every rung writes). It is the entry's kind marker, as referenced.json is for a recorded source.
  • transcript/<lang>.json (folio-transcript/v1) holds one transcript per language: the source-language transcript a recogniser produced, and each translation of it.

A transcript is a narrative

A transcript carries the narrative review state (schemas/content/narrative.ts): who drafted it, and whether a human has confirmed it. It is validated by parsing it AS a narrative (its segments joined): anything written names its drafter, an agent names its model, and only a human confirms.

The recogniser is recorded, not inferred

A source-language transcript names the recogniser that produced it: the tool, its version, the model file and that file's sha256. The same audio through a different model is a different transcript.

A translation says what it was translated from

translated_from names the source-language transcript's language and the sha256 of that file's segments, so a translation of a since-corrected transcript reads as stale. Segment timings are the source's, one for one: a translation re-times nothing.
