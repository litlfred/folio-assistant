    Module cat-harness/schemas/external-schema

    A specification this repository DEPENDS ON and does not hold.

    An imported schema (DC, OMG DMN, BPMN, …) is referenced, in the sense materialization.ts means it: we know it exists and WHICH VERSION we conform to, and hold none of its bytes.

    A transcription with no cited edition cannot be checked, cannot be updated deliberately, and cannot tell a reader whether a field it lacks is missing or simply not in that edition — e.g. dublin-core.ts carries the prefix dc but no namespace URI or DCMI version.

    A version is part of the IDENTITY

    {authority, id, version} together, never {authority, id}. BPMN 1.2 and BPMN 2.0 are different specifications that share a name.

    Graph Node

    schema

    Why this lives in cat-harness and not in core

    Its subject is the specifications the platform itself conforms to — BPMN, DMN, DCMI — not any folio's content, and its importers are platform tooling.
