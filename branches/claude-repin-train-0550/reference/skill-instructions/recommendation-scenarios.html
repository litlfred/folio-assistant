recommendation-scenarios

Step 2 of recommendation-extraction: the mechanical step that applies that skill’s Part 2 (the shape of a scenario, and the reuse-first persona, business-process and domain coding of the terminology-coding methodology and classify-and-code-content).

Input and output

  • Input: input.schema.json
  • Output: output.schema.json

In: the L1 graph from recommendation-candidates (or an authored L1 YAML), the target guide’s vocabulary, optionally the generic personas, and the agent’s mapping. Out: scenario-candidates.l2.json, one candidate per recommendation, with warnings for what could not be determined. Every candidate stays candidate until a person reviews it.

Tool

l1-recommendation-scenarios (smart-base/L2/tools/l1-recommendation-scenarios/l1-recommendation-scenarios.ts):

bun run smart-base/scripts/l1-recommendation-scenarios.ts --l1 <l1.json|yaml> --vocabulary <guide.json> [--generic <generic.json>] --mapping <mapping.yaml> --out <dir> [--check]
