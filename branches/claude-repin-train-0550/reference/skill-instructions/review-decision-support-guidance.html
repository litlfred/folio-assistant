Review decision-support guidance

Skill id: review-decision-support-guidance · Package: authoring-who-smart-guidelines · Task Task_Review of act-on-decision-support.bpmn (Process_ActOnDecisionSupport), lane health-worker.

Performed by a person: the health worker, who stays accountable for the care given. Guidance is advice, never an order; declining it is a normal answer, not an exception.

Input and output

The contract is input.schema.json and output.schema.json.

  • In: the guidance, as apply-decision-support returned it (a RequestGroup or RequestOrchestration, or CDS Hooks cards), the subject, and how it is renderedOn.
  • Out: one decisions entry per action: accepted, declined, or modified with the changed resource. An action the health worker did not touch is declined: nothing is carried out on silence. addReason is true only if the health worker tapped “add a reason” (false by default).

The burden is the point

Health workers are overburdened, and this step sits inside their visit.

  • One tap per action: accept or decline. “Accept all” is one tap for the whole proposal.
  • Nothing else is required. A reason is never asked for here; the health worker MAY add one with record-decision-rationale, which runs beside this step and never holds it up.
  • Modify only where the device supports it (a CDS Hooks suggestion, an editable request). It is optional, and a device that cannot offer it offers accept or decline.

Success criteria

  • Every action in the guidance has exactly one decision.
  • The decisions are recorded by who made them and when; nothing in the client’s record has changed yet (the system’s activate-accepted-guidance does that).
