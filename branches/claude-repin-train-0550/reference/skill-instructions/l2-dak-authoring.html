l2-dak-authoring

Skill id: l2-dak-authoring · Package: authoring-who-smart-guidelines · Named by l2-dak-authoring.bpmn steps Personas and scenarios, Data dictionary and core data elements, Indicators and requirements, all in the Business analyst lane and each a call into l2-component-edit-review.bpmn (opt-in smart-base.L2-collab), and by dak-l3-ig.bpmn step Map L2 → L3, beside l3-fhir-authoring.

Author the L2 layer of a WHO SMART Guideline: the human-readable, machine-processable Digital Adaptation Kit between a narrative guideline (L1) and FHIR artefacts (L3).

Sourcing. The DAK component set and the L1–L4 layers are WHO’s, defined in the SMART Guidelines documentation and realised in WorldHealthOrganization/smart-base. This skill says how to work them here (lane, inputs, tooling); what a component must contain is WHO’s, and where they disagree WHO wins.

Input and output

The contract is input.schema.json and output.schema.json.

  • In: dakComponent (required), sourceGuideline (required), existingContent, sprintNumber; as stage 4 of smart-guideline-end-to-end, also stage 2’s l1Kg and stage 3’s l2Dependencies (l2-dependency-choice’s output).
  • Out: artifacts (each an l2Artifact: path, type, description), status, validationIssues, stagingLinks. Stage 5 (l3-dependency-choice) takes the artifacts of every pass as its l2Assets.

dakComponent is the unit of work: author one component per pass, and finish it before starting the next.

What L2 is for, and the mistake to avoid

L2 is structured but not yet FHIR: reviewable by a clinical SME who does not read FHIR, and regular enough that L3 is derived from it.

Do not author L2 as if it were L3 (profiles, slicing, invariants). A decision that can only be expressed in FHIR belongs in l3-fhir-authoring; one that can be stated as a data element, decision rule or indicator belongs here.

Business processes and decision logic are the sibling skills bpmn-authoring and dmn-authoring; terminology binding is terminology-management.

Every track is edited, then reviewed alongside

Each of the six tracks (the three above, BPMN, DMN and terminology) is a call activity into Process_L2ComponentEditReview (l2-component-edit-review). In that subprocess the agent:

  1. edits the component with the track’s skill;
  2. runs the component’s checker;
  3. renders and stages it with a before/after;
  4. hands it on with adjudication: pending.

A person in the component’s reviewing role reviews the staged preview at the same time, through cat-harness’s Process_Review; review never blocks the hand-on. Each component’s renderer, checker, diff and reviewer are in that skill’s table. The clinical SME’s Clinical validation after the join reads the whole DAK across components, without repeating the per-component reviews.

Tooling

smart-base carries the extractors that read authored L2 artefacts — dd_extractor, dt_extractor, req_extractor, extractpr. Load a checkout, do not vendor it (see smart-base-tools). With SMART_BASE_HOME unset the skill reports skip, never a clean run.

Where this sits

l2-dak-authoring.bpmn carries <cat-harness.processes:policy enforcement="advisory"/>: the sequence may be adapted, but the gate may not be skipped, and the base processes it feeds (editing-hci-validation, content-lifecycle) stay strict.

As stage 4 of smart-guideline-end-to-end, clinical validation is the stage’s adjudication and does not hold up the next stage: the agent chooses L3 dependencies while the L2 assets are reviewed.
