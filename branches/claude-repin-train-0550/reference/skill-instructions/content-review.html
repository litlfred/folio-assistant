Content Review

Formal review and approval of validated content before publication.

Responsibilities

  • Review structured domain models before machine-readable encoding begins (phase gate)
  • Review encoded artifacts before publication (phase gate)
  • Assess breaking vs. non-breaking changes
  • Ensure alignment with source material and requirements
  • Approve draft publications for stakeholder circulation
  • Provide final publication release sign-off

Actors

  • Content Reviewer (lead, approval authority)
  • Technical Officer (first-pass review)
  • Domain SME (domain sign-off, supplied by the content-type layer)
  • Programme Manager (governance oversight)

Inputs

  • Validated content artifacts
  • Validation reports
  • Change log / diff from previous version

Outputs

Findings and a decision — two things, not one. See decision-audit and schemas/sdlc/qa-review.ts.

  • Findings — one per observation, each carrying the axis this reviewer can speak on: blocking | suggestion | praise for a person, critical | major | minor for a checker. Neither defaults into the other, and praise has no machine equivalent.
  • A decision — approve | request-changes | reject, naming every finding it weighed and every one it overruled. An overruled finding stays on the record.
  • Audit notes — why this outcome, and why each overruled finding was left. At least one per decision, each citing corpus evidence. Approving over an open critical or blocking finding without overruling it is a reported problem.
