This page lists the roles the business processes of this guide name, and the example people and systems that play them. Every person is fictional.

Roles

RolePlayed byDescription
HolderpersonThe person who holds a Personal Health Wallet, records consent, and presents verifiable health links and certificates. Specializes the ActorDefinition Holder (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
Consent Recorderperson, systemThe person or system that records a consent directive for the holder (IHE PCF Consent Recorder): the wallet itself, or a health worker on the holder’s behalf.
Consent RegistrysystemHolds consent directives and answers IHE PCF [ITI-108] Access Consent. Specializes the ActorDefinition ConsentRegistry (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
VHL SharersystemHolds the health information, generates verifiable health links, and enforces consent when a link is redeemed. Specializes the ActorDefinition Issuer (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
Document SourcesystemThe system that assembles a document about the holder before it is signed: the VHL Sharer when a document is shared, the Certificate Issuance Service when a certificate is issued. Adds the consent directives to it (process M).
VHL ReceiversystemReceives a verifiable health link, checks its signature against the trust list, and retrieves the information. Specializes the ActorDefinition Receiver (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
Trust AnchorsystemPublishes the trust list of keys that participants use to check signatures. GDHCN is one possible anchor among others. Specializes the ActorDefinition TrustNetworkAnchor (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
Certificate Issuerperson, systemA health worker or issuing system that issues a verifiable digital health certificate. Specializes the ActorDefinition Issuer (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
Verifierperson, systemChecks a certificate and its status, with or without a status checking application. Specializes the ActorDefinition Receiver (input/fsh/actors/actors.fsh).
Data Provider AdministratorpersonThe person at a data provider who registers clients under a data sharing agreement.
Data Recipient SystemsystemA server that accesses data under a data sharing agreement (OAuth client) and performs the agreement’s duties.
Provider Authorization ServersystemRegisters clients and decides access under a data sharing agreement (policy decision and enforcement).
AuditorpersonThe person who reviews audit and provenance records under a data sharing agreement.

Example people

Ana (data provider administrator)

Persona: the administrator who registers partner servers under the data sharing agreements her health information exchange signs. Fictional.

Roles: Data Provider Administrator.

Goal. Register only the clients an agreement allows, and revoke them the moment it ends.

Needs

  • The agreement’s terms in a form the server can check
  • One place to suspend a client

Frustrations

  • Agreements that exist only as PDFs

Dr Mei (receiving clinician)

Persona: an emergency physician who treats visitors from other countries and reads their records through verifiable health links. Fictional.

Roles: Verifier.

Goal. Read a visitor’s record quickly and know it is genuine and that she is allowed to read it.

Needs

  • A clear answer: allowed, refused, or unknown
  • A record she can trust, signed by a known issuer

Frustrations

  • Links that fail without saying why

Kofi (auditor)

Persona: an auditor who checks that every access under a data sharing agreement was allowed and its duties were done. Fictional.

Roles: Auditor.

Goal. Show, for any access, which consent and which agreement allowed it.

Needs

  • Provenance that cites the consent and its version
  • Duty checks he can run himself

Frustrations

  • Logs that say who, but not why

Lina (wallet holder)

Persona: a 34-year-old teacher who keeps her health records in a personal health wallet on her phone and travels for work. Fictional.

Roles: Holder, Consent Recorder.

Goal. Share her health information only with the people who need it, for as long as they need it.

Needs

  • To see who can read her record, and to withdraw that at any time
  • One link that works abroad as well as at home
  • To know when her record was read, and by whom

Frustrations

  • Consent forms she cannot read back later
  • Not knowing whether a withdrawal took effect

Nurse Tomás (clinic nurse)

Persona: a nurse at a community clinic who vaccinates, issues certificates, and records consent for patients who ask for help with the wallet. Fictional.

Roles: Certificate Issuer, Consent Recorder.

Goal. Issue certificates that are accepted wherever his patients go, without extra paperwork.

Needs

  • To record a patient’s consent in one step, and show it back to them
  • To know a certificate was issued correctly

Frustrations

  • Patients who were never asked what they agreed to

Example systems

SystemRolesDescription
Home health information exchangeVHL Sharer, Consent Registry, Document SourceThe health information exchange that holds Lina’s record: it records her consent in its Consent Registry, generates her links and enforces consent when a link is redeemed.
Lina’s personal health walletHolder, Consent RecorderThe wallet app on Lina’s phone: records her consent, holds her verifiable health links and certificates, and presents them as QR codes.
Provider authorization serviceProvider Authorization ServerThe authorization server of the health information exchange: registers clients and decides access under data sharing agreements.
Receiving clinic’s record systemVHL Receiver, Data Recipient SystemThe record system of Dr Mei’s clinic: reads verifiable health links, and as a registered client accesses data under a data sharing agreement.
Trust anchor serviceTrust AnchorA trust anchor that publishes the trust list of participants’ keys; GDHCN or another accepted anchor.
