Description

Repository health reports — what the daily sweep under test/health/ wrote.

Like the other committed QA artefacts (*.qa.json, *.script-qa.json, *.kg-qa.json), the file declares what it is with a $schema tag, rather than being typed by its extension or directory.

Why it is not qa-results/v1

qa-results/v1 carries families of findings and a total. It cannot express what a health check is about:

  • A threshold, with the basis it was chosen on. HealthThreshold requires basis, structurally, as schemas/conduct/memory.ts refuses a baseline with no measured.
  • Three states. ok, finding and unknown are different answers, and the third is never rendered as the first: "could not read gh-pages" must not become "0 MB of previews". Same rule as check-ci-health.ts, restore-staging.ts and kg-qa.ts.

Enforced here, so a check added later cannot opt out:

  • a result in state unknown must carry a reason;
  • a result in state finding must carry at least one finding;
  • a result in state ok must carry none.
