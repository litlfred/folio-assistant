Description

The glossary sense verdicts of a layer: for a use of a glossary term in the narrative that no rule decides, whether the term is used in its glossary sense — the store translation:glossary:check reads and its --record writes.

Owner, 2026-11-07: "If a glossary term is used in the narrative in the correct context, then the glossary translation is used in the translated narrative." The context is decided by rule first (the catalogue's subject area is the term's), then by an untainted adjudicator agent, who is given the term, its definition and the source sentence and rules:

| verdict | meaning | the check | |---|---|---| | in-sense | the sentence uses the glossary concept | the fixed translation is enforced | | other-sense | the same word, another concept | nothing is checked | | exception | in sense, but the fixed translation is wrong here; reason says why | passes, and is listed |

Sense is a property of the English source, so one verdict serves every locale. A verdict is keyed by the term's id (its IRI, or <subject area>#<label> for a terminology pin) and the SHA-256 of the msgid: a changed msgid has another hash, so its old verdict no longer applies and the use is asked again. decidedBy names the agent or person that ruled, never the model behind an agent.

The file is <translation-sources>/glossary-sense.json, committed in the layer.
