Folio Assistant Core

Indexing is adding an index to a document or a graph: keywords for each section, glossary terms, subject terms from a controlled vocabulary, a search that finds what is said in other words, and near-duplicate proposals.

Each indexing method rests on papers held in the library. A skill applies the method (uses-methodology), Tools carry the skill out (satisfies), and processes call the skill from an activity. Read a row left to right and you have the evidence for that piece of indexing work. A skill with no method has no held evidence yet, and is listed at the end.

The keywords these methods extract from every library entry are on the Keywords page, with the extraction variants side by side.
