Description

What each term in the folio namespace MEANS — one brief gloss apiece.

A term's IRI must dereference to a definition, since READMEs link words to their definitions.

What is NOT here

The graph typologies. tools, cat-harness, beans and the rest are GraphTypology individuals, not classes, and each already carries a summary in BASE_GRAPH_TYPOLOGIES, which ns-export reads. A gloss here for a term the registry already describes is a bug, and the completeness test says so.

Rich description. One sentence each: a gloss says what the term IS; rich descriptions and interpretation belong in the cat-harness docs.
