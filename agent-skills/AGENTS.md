# AGENTS.md — agent-skills

What binds everywhere is the repository's [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md); what
this layer *is* is [`README.md`](README.md). This says what to do here, and
the first thing is a warning.

## You are not the author of anything in `library/`

These are **other publishers' documents**, captured. Do not edit one to fix a
typo, update a claim, or make it agree with something else in this repository.
A corpus is evidence of what was published, and a corrected corpus is evidence
of nothing. If a document is wrong, that fact belongs in whatever reads it.

## Say which kind you are reading — every time

The distinction the README calls load-bearing is an instruction, not a
description:

| kind | what it is | what a rule taken from it is |
|---|---|---|
| **assertion** | a publisher describing its own product | a **convention** |
| **evidence** | a measurement somebody else can repeat | a **finding** |

**Nothing in the schema can tell them apart.** Vendor documentation is
revisable without notice, and one document here describes a product that no
longer exists. So an agent deriving anything from this corpus states which it
read, and a voice that does not is under-specified rather than terse.

Treating an assertion as evidence is how a vendor's marketing becomes this
repository's measured fact — with a citation, which makes it worse, because
the citation resolves.

## Before you add to the corpus

Captured documents carry their provenance. A page print is not the same
artefact as the publisher's own PDF, and the difference shows up later as an
unexplained discrepancy between two agents' readings. Record what you fetched,
when, and how.
