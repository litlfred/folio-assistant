Record a decision rationale (optional)

Skill id: record-decision-rationale · Package: authoring-who-smart-guidelines · Task Task_Rationale of act-on-decision-support.bpmn (Process_ActOnDecisionSupport), lane health-worker, reached only when Gateway_AddReason reads that the health worker tapped “add a reason”.

Opt-in, skippable, and skipped by default. It is never required to finish the review, the visit or the process: the diagram puts it on a branch of a parallel split that is never joined, so the care plan is updated without waiting for it. A DAK must not make it mandatory; a programme that needs a reason recorded every time is asking for a different, blocking step, and should draw one.

Performed by a person: the health worker who made the decision.

Input and output

The contract is input.schema.json and output.schema.json.

  • In: the decision (one action’s outcome, from review-decision-support-guidance), the practitioner, and the planDefinition the guidance came from.
  • Out: recorded (false when skipped) and, when true, one provenance.

The burden

  • At most one extra tap: “add a reason”. Choosing a coded reason is the same tap; free text is optional on top.
  • No field is required beyond the tap. A rationale with neither code nor text records only that the health worker chose to mark the decision.

Why a Provenance

FHIR R4 Provenance is the lightest standard artefact that says who decided what, about which guidance, and why: target the request the decision is about (or the RequestGroup, on a decline), agent the practitioner, entity (role source) the PlanDefinition, reason the coded or free-text reason (a CodeableConcept, whose text carries free text), recorded the time. It adds no workflow state, unlike a Task; it is not a clinical finding, unlike a DetectedIssue; and it needs no recipient, unlike a Communication.

When the guidance came as CDS Hooks cards, the system also sends the service the feedback its card asks for (accepted or overridden, with the overrideReason when one was given): that is the CDS service’s copy, the Provenance is the record’s.

Success criteria

  • Skipping it changes nothing anywhere, and the process never waits for it.
  • When recorded, the Provenance validates and names the decision’s target, the practitioner and the PlanDefinition.
