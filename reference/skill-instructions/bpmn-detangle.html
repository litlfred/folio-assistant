bpmn-detangle

Owner, 2026-10-31, on the rendered content-change-review page: “make the font a bit bigger, the spacing a bit more compact so dont need to zoom as much”, then “bpmn detangle skill including swap lanes to help issues like [this]”, and “add as QA and author check”. The screenshot showed the gateway question “Satisfied with changes?” written over its own “No” answer, a Stage → Review flow running up through two lanes and across the loop-back, and a 2610-wide pool whose text the page shrank to about 6px.

This is the ArchiMate rule (archimate-models §”Untangle every view you touch”) for BPMN: a change never adds a tangle, and the diagram you touch leaves cleaner than it came.

The check 

bun run cat bpmn:detangle <file.bpmn|dir>            # report
bun run cat bpmn:detangle <file.bpmn> --write        # also swap lanes when it helps
bun run cat bpmn:detangle <file.bpmn|dir> --check    # exit 1 on any finding

render:bpmn prints the same findings as warnings for every diagram it draws, so the QA side sees them without running anything. The criteria live in cat-harness-tools/src/workflow/diagram-fit.ts:

findingmeansfix
diagram-too-widewider than 1400close column gaps; stack steps of different lanes in one column; split by phase
diagram-too-talltaller than 900shrink each lane to its rows; split by phase
label-overlaptwo labels on each othergive one a BPMNLabel position clear of the other
label-on-edgea label on a flow that is not its ownmove the label, or the flow
label-on-shapea label on a box, its own shape, or a lane’s name stripmove the label
edge-diagonala flow runs at a slant--write routes it at right angles through the gap
header-overflowa pool or lane name spills out of its 30-wide strip--write makes the lane taller; or shorten the name (never repeat the process id in the pool name)
text-overflowa name spills out of its box--write widens, then heightens the box; or shorten the name
edge-crossingtwo flows crossre-route one around the other, move a step, or swap lanes
lane-orderanother lane order crosses fewer lane boundaries--write applies it

Labels are checked where bpmn-js draws them, so the check uses the browser when one is installed. A file without BPMNLabel bounds leaves the placement to bpmn-js, and only the drawing shows that a gateway’s question, wrapped to three lines under the diamond, lands on the flow leaving it.

Why 1400 × 900

Arithmetic, not taste. The renderer draws text at 16px (bootstrap-tools TEXT_STYLE; owner, 2026-10-31: “maybe make tasks a bit taller and font a bit bigger?”). A page shows a diagram at most about 1200px wide once the nav rail is taken off a 1440px screen, so a diagram W wide shows its text at 16 × 1200 / W px: about 14px at 1400. About 760px of a 900px screen is left under the header and the figure toolbar, so 900 keeps a diagram on about one screen. Change the budget and the text size together.

The procedure

  1. Run the check before you edit, and note the counts.
  2. Fix the lane order first. --write tries every order of the lanes (up to 8) and keeps the one whose flows cross the fewest lane boundaries, only if it adds no flow crossing. It moves shapes with their lane and re-draws each flow between lanes as one elbow, since its old bends were for the old order. Look at the result: it is a start, not a layout.
  3. Make it compact. Tasks 120 × 90 (three lines of 16px text), gateways 50, events 36, and big enough for the name at 16px (text-overflow). About 40–70 between neighbours in a row; wider only where a flow label needs it. A column can hold one step per lane: a hand-off straight down from one lane to the next is the shortest flow there is. A lane is as tall as its rows plus room for labels, no more.
  4. Right angles only (owner, 2026-10-31, on dth-pc: “straight angled arrows ugly”). A flow between lanes leaves the side facing its target, turns once in the gap between the two columns, and enters the target’s side: never one slanted line from box to box.
  5. Route the loops below. A loop-back leaves through the bottom, runs in the channel under the row and comes up into its target (bpmn-processes §”Edge routing”). A flow between lanes leaves on the side facing its target.
  6. Place every label. A gateway’s question goes above the diamond, its answers beside their flows; an event’s name below it, clear of the lane’s name strip; a flow label beside its own segment. Write BPMNLabel bounds for each one bpmn-js would put somewhere bad. Give a multi-line label room for the lines it wraps to: bpmn-js wraps external labels at about 90 wide whatever bounds you give, so a three-word question is three lines: about 60 tall at 15px.
  7. Run the check again: zero findings on the diagram you touched, or say in the PR which remain and why.
  8. Look at the rendered SVG, at 100% in a 1200px band. The check finds collisions; only looking finds a diagram that is clean and still confusing.

When it still does not fit

Split by phase (bpmn-authoring §”Split by phase: how”). Past about 20 flow nodes a diagram rarely fits 1400 × 900 at a readable size, and the phase split is the fix, not a smaller font.
