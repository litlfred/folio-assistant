External registry — confirming a source’s authority

external-registry (declared below this layer) is played by an external actor: trusted registries and observatories outside this instance’s control. A role played by something we do not run is still a role, and its skills are its contract: what we rely on it to do (owner, 2026-10-16). This skill is the part of that contract this layer’s evidence diagram relies on, and nothing more. The registry list, its endpoints and the API bindings are not settled (the diagram is a STRAWPERSON), so no registry is named here.

Where the diagrams lean on it

Lane_Trusted in evidence-retrieval names this role and holds Task_VerifyAuthority and Gateway_Authoritative. What that lane’s documentation and the task’s say is the whole contract.

What it must do

  1. Resolve the identifier at the publisher, through the standard API of the body that publishes the candidate.
  2. Return the record it holds, so the citation’s claim, its version and its date can be compared with what the publisher says (the task’s documentation lists the checks).

What happens when it does not answer

The decision is a readout of what the external API returns, not this instance’s call. When that answer is uncertain or absent, Gateway_Authoritative takes the no branch and Lane_WorkPlan opens a bean for the unverified citation. The lane does not invent a verdict, and the citation is neither dropped nor silently kept. Whether a publisher is one this folio has declared trusted is the folio’s judgement, not the registry’s.

Tests

The test in tested-by: drives the evidence process with the harness’s workflow engine and checks that a no from Gateway_Authoritative leads to the bean for the unverified citation and that no step in the registry’s lane is a decision of this instance’s. Lines 1 and 2 are the registry’s own behaviour, on endpoints not yet chosen, and are not exercised.

Needs the owner’s review: this contract is read off one lane of one strawperson diagram.
