WHO Editorial Style Monval

Interferons

Interferons are represented by the symbol IFN to which is attached by
a hyphen the Greek letter representing its type, for example IFN-a
for alpha interferons. Non-allelic variants can be indicated by an
arabic number or capital letter (sometimes subscripted) attached to
the Greek letter. The prefixes “Hu” and “Mu” may be used to represent
“human” and “murine” respectively. For example:

MulFN-y HulFN-B — HulFN-c4.

Further reading
Interferon nomenclature. Journal of immunology, 1980, 125:2353.

Interferon nomenclature. Journal of general virology, 1984, 65:669-670.

Interleukins
Designations have been officially adopted for 17 interleukins:
interleukin-1 to interleukin-16, including interleukin-lo and
interleukin-1B. These terms are generally abbreviated IL-1a, IL-5,
IL-13, etc.

Forther reading

IUIS/WHO Standing Committee on Interleukin Designation. Nomenclature for
secreted regulatory proteins of the immune system (Interleukins): update. Bulletin
of the World Health Organization, 1997, 75:175.
