man complement component C2. Revised nomenclature for human

HLA system

TUIS-WHO Nomenclature Subcommittee. Nomenclature for clusters
of differentiation (CD) defined on human leukocyte populations.
Bulletin of the World Health Organization, 1984, 62:809-811.

Nomenclature for factors of the HLA system 1977. Bulletin of the World
Health Organization, 1978, 56:461-465.

Nomenclature for factors of the HLA system 1980. Bulletin of the World
Health Organization, 1981, 58:945-948.

Nomenclature for factors of the HLA system 1984. Bulletin of the World
Health Organization, 1985, 63:399-405.

Later recommendations of the WHO Nomenclature Committee con-
cerning the HLA system have been published elsewhere — see, for
example, Immunobiology, 1988, 177:465-476 and Immunogenetics,
1990, 31:131-140.
