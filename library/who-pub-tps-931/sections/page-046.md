Abbreviations and symbols

Units of measurement

Detailed lists of the symbols to be used for units of measurement can
be found in the reference sources listed on page 38. A list of the most
commonly used symbols (including those used for the SI base units) is
given below. These symbols should only be used after a quantity
expressed in figures, in tables, and in graphs.

A compound abbreviation may sometimes be used to indicate a
relationship between two different units of measurement: mg/kg.
