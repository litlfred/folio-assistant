descriptionA built FHIR Questionnaire shown the way a person will meet it — a mid-fidelity wireframe at web and mobile width, and a live LHC-Forms form view — checked field for field against the L2 wireframe it was derived from, reviewed as a design, staged with deep links, and changed through requests that say which side was edited. Operating skill: fhir-questionnaire-wireframe. Owner decisions, 2026-10-14: LHC-Forms, vendored; the checker lives in this layer; either the Questionnaire or the wireframe may be the source of a change, and the request says which. WHERE IT SITS. After SUSHI has compiled the Questionnaire to JSON (l3-fhir-pipeline, Task_Sushi), and wherever a layer above reviews a Questionnaire's fidelity to its wireframe. It reads JSON only, so it can run on any IG that produces a Questionnaire. WHAT IT DOES NOT CLAIM. A faithful Questionnaire is not therefore a good form, and an accepted form is not therefore conformant: wireframe-design-review judges the first and fhir-validation the second.
