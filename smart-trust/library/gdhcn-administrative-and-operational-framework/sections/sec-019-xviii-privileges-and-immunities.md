Nothing contained in or relating to the Framework for the Administration and Operations of the 
GDHCN (this document) shall be deemed to constitute a waiver of any of the privileges and 
immunities enjoyed by WHO and its officials under national or international law and/or as 
submitting WHO to any national court jurisdiction.
