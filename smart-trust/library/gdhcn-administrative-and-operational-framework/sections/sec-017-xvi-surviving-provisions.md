SURVIVING PROVISIONS 
Those rights and obligations as set forth in the Framework for the Administration and Operations 
of the GDHCN (this document) that are, by their nature, intended to survive its expiration or 
earlier termination for whatever reason shall survive indefinitely. This includes, but is expressly 
not limited to, paragraphs XII, XIII, XIV, this paragraph XVI, paragraphs XVII and XVIII, and 
the corresponding definitions contained in paragraph II.
