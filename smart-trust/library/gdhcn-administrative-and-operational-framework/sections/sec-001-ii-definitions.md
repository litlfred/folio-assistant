DEFINITIONS 
The following definitions apply herein: 
Business Owner Representative: The individual identified by a GDHCN Participant as having 
the primary business and programmatic responsibility for the GDHCN Participant for the 
implementation of the use cases covered by a Trust Domain. 
Certificate Authority: An entity that stores, signs, and issues cryptographic certificates and 
certifies the ownership of a public key by the named subject of the certificate. This allows 
GDHCN Trust Network Gateway participants to rely on signatures or assertions made in relation 
to the private key corresponding to the certified public key. 
Certificate Authorities may be operated by different types of entities, including, but not limited 
to, a private-sector Certificate Authority, a ministry or agency responsible for ICT/eGovernment 
services, a Ministry of Health, or another competent authority within the jurisdiction. It is the 
responsibility of each GDHCN Participant to determine the appropriate arrangement for its 
Certificate Authority, in accordance with applicable laws and policies within its jurisdiction, to 
guarantee security and proper data management. Each GDHCN Participant must identify, 
establish, or liaise with the relevant Certificate Authority, as applicable and impose on the latter 
all requirements of this document. 
Encryption Key Certificate Governance: The process and technical specifications regarding the 
management and use of encryption keys for Verifiable Digital Health Certificates, Public Key 
Infrastructure, and for securing connections with Trusted Services and the Trust Network 
Gateway.
