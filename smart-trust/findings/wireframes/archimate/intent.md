# archimate — as-is intent

## Covers

The `archimate` visualiser this instance declares (`smart-trust.json` → `visualisers`, `renderedBy: archimate-pages`), route key `smart-trust/archimate`, published at `en/smart-trust/archimate/`. Its pages are written at publish by Tool `archimate-pages` (cat-harness-tools `archimate/scripts/gen-archimate-pages.ts`) from the one model the `archimate/` directory holds, `1.7.5/smart-trust.archimate`, which `archimate/build.py` generates. Each page is a thin shell; `assets/archimate.js` draws its body from `model.json`.

The pages, in the order a reader meets them:

- `index.html` — **ArchiMate models**: one row per model (here one: *WHO SMART Trust*, `1.7.5`, 1 view, 14 elements).
- `1.7.5/` — the model: its counts, its purpose, its views, and its elements grouped by ArchiMate layer.
- `1.7.5/view/<v>/` — the one view, *The GDHCN trust network*: the drawn SVG in a zoomable figure, then the elements it shows.
- `1.7.5/element/<e>/`, `1.7.5/relationship/<r>/` and `1.7.5/metamodel/` — one page each; not drawn here, they repeat the model page's list-and-links pattern.

### How this was drawn

From the model file (read with `zipfile`; the `.archimate` is a zip holding `model.xml`) and the page templates in `gen-archimate-pages.ts` and `templates/archimate.js`, not from a built site. The index, the model page and the view page are drawn on one sheet, separated by their routes. The view's SVG is drawn as a box carrying the three steps and their actors, as `render-view.ts` lays them out from the model's own diagram.

## Who it is for, and what they need to do

- **Readers:** an implementer or a policy reader of the GDHCN who wants the trust network's moving parts, and an architect who wants them as ArchiMate they can reuse.
- **Tasks:**
  - see the trust network as one picture: who onboards, what is distributed, who verifies
  - reach any one actor, data object or step and see what it is related to, and its page in this guide
  - take the model away (the Archi file, or any node as JSON-LD)

## What it must show

1. The model's identity: its title, version id (`1.7.5`) and its counts (1 view · 14 elements · 28 relationships).
2. Its purpose, as the model states it (generated by `archimate/build.py`; not edited in Archi).
3. The view, drawn, with the three groups *1. Onboard*, *2. Distribute*, *3. Verify: the trust network*.
4. Every element, by layer: Business (3 processes), Application (5 components, 3 data objects), Other (3 groupings).
5. Links out: each node as JSON-LD, the drawing as SVG, and the Archi model file.

## Findings

1. **The model page says nothing until the script runs.** The shell holds only "Loading the model…"; with scripts off, or while `model.json` loads, a reader sees no element and no view. The index page is static and is fine.
2. **The view is a wide drawing.** At 390 px the SVG is scaled down inside its figure until the box labels are unreadable; the figure's zoom control is the only way to read it on a phone.
3. **The groupings are listed as elements.** *1. Onboard*, *2. Distribute* and *3. Verify* appear under "Other (3)" beside the actors, so the three steps read as things rather than as the structure of the picture.
