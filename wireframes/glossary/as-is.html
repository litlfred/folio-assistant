Adjudicator
cat-harness/glossary/glossary-ledger.json#role/adjudicator		Settles a criterion whose reviewer entries disagree, and records WHY. Inherits `reviewer` because an adjudicator is a reviewer with one added entitlement — the `adjudication` permission — and the separation is the point: a reviewer reports, an adjudicator settles, and a process where every reviewer could settle has no way to say who decided.
