Two questions, not one. catalogues / templates asks whether a .po exists at all for each extractable .pot; the string columns ask how much of the catalogues that DO exist is done.

“no basis” is not zero. A share over an empty denominator is undefined, and rendering it as 0% would report a measurement this run did not make.
