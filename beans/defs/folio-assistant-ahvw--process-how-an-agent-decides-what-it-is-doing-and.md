How an agent works out what it is doing, and what governs the step it is on.

`hylg` makes the workflow interpreter descend into call activities, so a
subprocess gives increasing context rather than a flat step name. `3190` makes
coding conventions a process-SCOPED context kind, so a rule applies where it
belongs instead of everywhere.

`xfoh` and `vjbl` are the measurement half: CRDM detection has a baseline and
known missing exclusions, and a single annotator's eval corpus cannot be
checked for agreement. A classifier nobody has re-measured is a claim.
