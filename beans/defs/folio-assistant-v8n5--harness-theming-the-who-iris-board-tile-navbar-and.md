Owner, 2026-09-30, answering the GOAL 3 goal review: keep GOAL 3 **open**, and theme the harness itself. The goal's words are *"showing who-iris w/ existing materialized assets with themed harness"*, and the owner reads "themed harness" as more than the pages.

## What is true today (measured 2026-09-30)
- The who-iris **pages** (`/who-iris/`, generated by `who-iris/scripts/gen-iris-pages.ts`) render on `--iris-*` custom properties from `who-iris/themes/themes.ts`. That file holds two WHO themes, `iris-web` and `who-wpro-publication`, measured from the captured IRIS stylesheet and the style guide, and resolved through the platform's `resolveTheme` with `instance: "who-iris"`.
- The **harness surfaces** do not use them. The board tile, the navbar entry and any sticky resolve a `theme` name against the platform's own themes (`cat-harness/schemas/theme.ts`; `harness-tiles.ts` via `resolveThemeBackdrop`). The who-iris harness entry in `docs/_data/harness.json` carries only `tone: 199` (a WHO-blue hue) and `genericAvatar: false`. `who-iris/who-iris.json` declares no `theme` on any sticky or tile.

## The shape of the fix, to be checked rather than assumed
An instance-declared theme has to be resolvable wherever a surface names a theme. That means resolving the who-iris themes through the same `resolveTheme` lookup the surfaces use, not copying the palette into the platform. `who-iris/themes/themes.ts` already says why it must not live in `cat-harness/schemas/`: a palette read off a WHO style guide is subject matter. The platform learns to *find* an instance's themes and never *holds* them.

## Done when
- [ ] The who-iris board tile and navbar entry render on `iris-web` (or a theme the owner names), resolved from `who-iris/themes/`, with no WHO palette value in `cat-harness/`
- [ ] A test fails if the surface falls back to the platform default for an instance that declares its own theme
- [ ] `check:voices`, `iris:pages:check` and the SC 1.4.1 rule from `j66n` (state carried by word as well as colour) still hold
- [ ] Checked on a rendered build, `preview:site` **plus** the `mount-instance-docs.ts` step (see `yg29` §"A correction worth keeping"), not inferred from generator output
