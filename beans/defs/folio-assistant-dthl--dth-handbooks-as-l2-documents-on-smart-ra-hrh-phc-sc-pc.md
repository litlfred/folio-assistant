Groundwork: litlfred/smart-ra#133, litlfred/smart-base#242. Pilot: litlfred/dth-hrh.

Children: the beans that each handbook's workplan files, starting with folio-assistant-max2 and folio-assistant-llxa (the CRDM sources the PHC handbook cites).
