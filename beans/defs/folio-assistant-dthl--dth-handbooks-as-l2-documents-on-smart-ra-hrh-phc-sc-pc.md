Owner, 2026-10-31: each WHO Digital Transformation Handbook becomes an L2 DTH that depends on smart-ra. Its narrative is validated against the glossaries and linked to ArchiMate, with BPMN for its business processes, roles as DAK persona blocks, and DMN tables extracted.

Workplans: litlfred/dth-hrh#47 (HRH pilot), litlfred/dth-phc#1 (PHC), litlfred/dth-sc#2 (SC), litlfred/dth-pc#2 (PC), litlfred/dth-sfp#1 (SFP), and the per-handbook workplans in the project folder dth-l2-ingest-2026-10-31/. Groundwork: litlfred/smart-ra#133, litlfred/smart-base#242. Pilot: litlfred/dth-hrh.

Children: the beans that each handbook's workplan files, starting with folio-assistant-max2 and folio-assistant-llxa (the CRDM sources the PHC handbook cites).
