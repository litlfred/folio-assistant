#### 13.3.3. Asset States

If a validator reports that the portions of the asset that are covered by content bindings have not been modified since the active manifest was produced \[[Section 14.12, “Validate the Asset’s Content”](#_validate_the_assets_content)\], and its active manifest is either [Valid](#_valid_manifest) or [Trusted](#_trusted_manifest), then the asset itself is a Valid asset.
