###### 16.4.1.4.7. Changes

The action may be specific to only a portion of an asset - such as a range of frames in a video or a specific area on an image. In v1, the value was a simple text string. For v2, they are identified using a `changes` field, whose value is an array of `region-map` objects (as defined in [Section 16.9.2, “Regions of Interest”](#_regions_of_interest)).
