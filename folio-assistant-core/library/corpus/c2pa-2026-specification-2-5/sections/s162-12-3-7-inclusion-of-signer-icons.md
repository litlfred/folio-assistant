#### 12.3.7. Inclusion of signer icons

A C2PA Manifest Consumer may wish to display an icon or logo for the signer. To locate such a graphic, it shall look inside the embedded certificate for a logotype as defined in [RFC 9399](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9399). If no logotype is present, the Manifest Consumer may use icons or logos from other sources in an implementation-dependent manner.
