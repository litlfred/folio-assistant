#### 2.3.2. Assertion store

The logical collection of [assertions](#_assertion) referenced by a [claim](#_claim) within a [C2PA Manifest](#_c2pa_manifest), stored together as a single construct. For the full structural description, see [Assertion Store](#assertion-store-arch).
