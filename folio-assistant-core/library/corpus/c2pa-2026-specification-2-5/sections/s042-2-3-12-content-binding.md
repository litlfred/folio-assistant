#### 2.3.12. Content binding

Information that associates [digital content](#_digital_content) to a specific [C2PA Manifest](#_c2pa_manifest) associated with a specific [asset](#_asset), either as a [hard binding](#_hard_binding) or a [soft binding](#soft_binding_assertion). For the full structural description, see [Binding to Content](#_binding_to_content).
