#### 2.2.5. Asset rendition

A representation of an [asset](#_asset) (either as a part of an [asset](#_asset) or a completely new [asset](#_asset)) where the [digital content](#_digital_content) has had a ['non-editorial transformation'](#editorial-transformation-definition) [action](#_action) (e.g., re-encoding or scaling) applied.

**EXAMPLE:** A video file that is re-encoded for reduced screen resolution or network bandwidth.
