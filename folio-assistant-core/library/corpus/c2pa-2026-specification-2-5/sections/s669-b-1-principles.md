### B.1. Principles

UX designed for the consumption of C2PA provenance shall be informed by the context of the asset. The recommendations are built around four primary user groups — Consumers, Creators, Publishers and Verifiers (or Investigators), as defined in the [C2PA Guiding Principles](https://c2pa.org/principles/) — and describe exemplary interfaces for each across common contexts. These are recommendations, not mandates, and are expected to evolve; see the UX Recommendations document for full detail.
