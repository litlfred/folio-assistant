#### 2.3.15. Trust signals

The collection of information that can inform a [Manifest Consumer’s](#_manifest_consumer) judgment of the trustworthiness of an [asset](#_asset). These are in addition to the [signer](#signer-definition) upon which the fundamental trust model relies.
