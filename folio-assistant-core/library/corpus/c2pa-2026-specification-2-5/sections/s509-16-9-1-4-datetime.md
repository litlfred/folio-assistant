##### 16.9.1.4. DateTime

If a `dateTime` field is present, its value shall be a date time string that complies with CBOR date/times ([RFC 8949](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8949), 3.4.1). This field is used to indicate the date and time when the assertion was generated.
