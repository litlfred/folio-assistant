##### 16.8.2.2. Details

The AI Disclosure assertion shall have a label of `c2pa.ai-disclosure`.

The value of the `modelType` field is an enumeration of AI model types defined in [Table 8, “Model type values”](#model-types) and it shall be present in the `ai-model-disclosure-map` object. If present, the value of the `scientificDomain` field shall conform to the [arXiv taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy), e.g., "cs.AI", "physics.optics". All other fields are optional, but if present, they shall conform to the types specified in the CDDL schema defined below.
