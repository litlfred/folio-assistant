## 4\. Standard Terms

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in [BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14), [RFC 2119](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119), and [RFC 8174](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174) when they appear in any casing (upper, lower or mixed).

CDDL Rules and Type Definitions in the CDDL schemas for C2PA constructs are normative. [Schema validation](#schema-validation) is not necessary, nor it is recommended. Comments within CDDL schemas are non-normative. If there is a conflict between the CDDL schema and the normative text of this specification, the normative text takes precedence.
