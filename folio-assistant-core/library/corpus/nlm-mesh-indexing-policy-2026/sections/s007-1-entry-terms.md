#### 1. Entry Terms

Entry terms, sometimes called "See cross-references", are synonyms, near-synonyms alternate forms, and other closely related terms in a MeSH record. Entry terms are not always strictly synonymous with the preferred term in the record or with each other. However, Entry terms are generally used interchangeably with the preferred term and are equivalent to the preferred term for purposes of cataloging, indexing and retrieval. For example, the Entry terms for the preferred term *Heart Arrest* range from *Arrest, Heart* to a simple inversion or less obvious synonyms such as *Heart Arrest* and *Asystole*.

Entry Terms are displayed as the **Entry Term** in the [MeSH Browser](/mesh/MBrowser.html). Entry terms exist as the \<[Term](/mesh/xml_data_elements.html#Term)\> element in XML MeSH. Note, however, XML MeSH does identify strict synonymy between subsets of terms within a record. See the \<[Concept](/mesh/xml_data_elements.html#Concept)\> element in XML MeSH and [Concept Structure in XML MeSH Data](/mesh/concept_structure.html).
