Doc-Researcher (in Figure 2) consists of two main modules: deep
parsing and deep research. The deep parsing pipeline transforms
multimodal documents into structured, multi-granular, and search-
able units while preserving multimodal semantics. The deep re-
search adopts a multi-agent collaborative framework to solve com-
plex questions with iterative search and refinement.
