##### 11.5.3.2. Toggles

All JUMBF Description boxes (ISO 19566-5:2023, A.3) used in a C2PA Manifest require a label and so the _Label Present_ toggle (`xxxxxx1x`) shall be set. In addition, because JUMBF URIs are used to refer to boxes throughout the system (e.g., listing assertions, references to ingredients, etc.), the _Requestable_ toggle (`xxxxxx11`) shall be set.

When including a salt in a _PRIVATE_ box as described in [Section 9.4.2.3, “Hashing JUMBF Boxes”](#_hashing_jumbf_boxes), the _Private_ toggle (`xxx1xxxx`) shall also be set.
