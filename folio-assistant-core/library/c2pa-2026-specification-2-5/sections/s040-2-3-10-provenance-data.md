#### 2.3.10. Provenance data

The set of [C2PA Manifests](#_c2pa_manifest) for an [asset](#_asset) and, in the case of a [composed asset](#_composed_asset), its [ingredients](#ingredient_assertion). For the full structural description, see [Manifests](#_manifests).

> **NOTE:**
> A [C2PA Manifest](#_c2pa_manifest) can reference other [C2PA Manifests](#_c2pa_manifest).
