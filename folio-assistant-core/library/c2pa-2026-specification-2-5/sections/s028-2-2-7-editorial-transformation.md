#### 2.2.7. Editorial transformation

A type of transformation that alters either the intent or meaning or both of the [digital content](#_digital_content).

> **NOTE:**
> Every editorial transformation is a [perceptible transformation](#perceptible-transformation-definition), but not every [perceptible transformation](#perceptible-transformation-definition) is editorial
