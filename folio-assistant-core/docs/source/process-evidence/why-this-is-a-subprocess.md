It runs inside the editing loop, between claiming the bean and drafting the
change — see [Editing and the HCI validation
gate](publication-workflow.html#editing-and-the-hci-validation-gate). **The
evidence is gathered before the sentence is written, not assembled afterwards
to support one.**

It is a *call activity*, with its own file, lanes and diagram, so the editing
diagram stays readable. Content that carries no recommendations — a
mathematics folio — skips it rather than satisfying it vacuously.
