The grade attaches to the **body** of evidence for one PICO question, so
appraisal sits after the join, not inside the fan-out. A single citation does
not carry a certainty rating.

Then one gateway: **is this sufficient for a recommendation?**

**Insufficient evidence is a result.** It is recorded as a gap the guideline
can state — *"the evidence does not support a recommendation here"* — never
closed by weakening the recommendation until the evidence supports it.

On the "yes" branch the PICO, the graded body of evidence, and every verified
citation with its resolved authority record are written onto the
recommendation block, so a reader can retrace the chain from the sentence to
the source.
